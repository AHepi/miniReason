# E016 original criticism: independent instrument and content review

E016 completed all six arms and all ten provider calls without an operational alarm. The recorded requests, occurrences, resources and both Mini logs verify. All four requested use responses give the stipulated numerical answers and preserve the difference between reporting and planning. Their proposed accounts also contain specific attribution errors and unresolved tensions about capacity and admissibility. The completed experiment is evidence about these occurrences, not a creativity verdict or an established advantage of Mini.

This review follows the prepublished [interpretation contract](../../experiments/materials/reason-use-v1/INTERPRETATION.md). It inspected the frozen plan, actual requests, all ten public responses and terminal records in [E016-reason-original](../../experiments/records/E016-reason-original/plan.json). Early inspection occurred while the run was active; final verification occurred only after its terminal summary existed. The reviewer made no provider calls and changed no observations, plans, source modules or material files.

## Content findings requiring preservation

### E016-INT-1: capacity is promoted from a reporting coordinate to an admissibility filter

Native's proposed account says a capacity-only composition relation regards combinations as jointly possible whenever their sum satisfies capacity. Matched native defines named constraints as including capacity, restricts its availability set to assignments satisfying those constraints, and then says negative availability is allowed. Under the ordinary hard-cap interpretation of that named constraint, every retained assignment has sum at most capacity, so every resulting availability is nonnegative. The literal filtering interpretation and the allowance of negative output cannot both govern that same set without an additional distinction.

This is stronger than J's original tentative internal criticism that capacity already supplies a cross-booking constraint. It is not a finding that J had already installed a deletion policy. The new responses are responsible for their stronger formulation. A reporting-capacity interpretation could resolve the conflict, but then the admissibility restriction must not silently mean `sum <= capacity`.

All use responses nevertheless calculate Case A as -2 and explicitly require extra premises before concluding that either entry is invalid or physically impossible. Matched native grounds that decision in the finite task and treats a hard capacity limit as an additional operational premise. Thus no capacity-based deletion occurred in the actual use answers. The returned-account tension remains, but it did not force that operational mistake in this trial. The use response does not explicitly diagnose the contradictory filter in its parent; a claim of fully repaired account semantics would exceed the evidence.

### E016-INT-2: attribution of supplied distinctions is unstable

Mini says its sub-commitment distinction is absent from the supplemental criticism, yet R explicitly states that P7's examples challenge different commitments rather than merely different objects and calls that a contribution. Mini may be adding the particular phrasing of a composite-account decomposition, but it does not make that narrower difference precise before claiming absence. Its account of R as fully absorbing J's insight also overlooks R's express reservation of the commitment distinction as independent of the target/locus objection.

Bare attributes to J a note from P9, quoted as “if availability is allowed to be negative, then capacity is not a hard constraint.” Neither “negative” nor “hard constraint” appears anywhere in the frozen J text. Negative availability is supplied elsewhere in C0, so the underlying concern is available; the attribution to J is inaccurate. This is an error in the generated historical account, not a mutation of J or an instrument provenance defect.

Several replies repeat R's claim of contribution beyond the packet without a passage-level demonstration of the narrower added organization. P7 already distinguishes the three example claims; that fact alone does not refute every further organization, but repetition of R's praise alone does not establish one either. The frozen collateral-attribution obligation remains unresolved where the reply provides no such comparison.

### E016-INT-3: reporting a pair is sometimes confused with endorsing its physical feasibility

Mini's use response says free per-alternative composition licenses treating all four pairs as evaluable and reporting their arithmetic values. That inference is too strong for the stipulated task. The reporting procedure expressly forms all pairs and computes `23-u-v`; the inspection can deny a pair's physical performability without preventing evaluation of that pair. The same response later correctly preserves the four-value report and excludes only the planner's infeasible pair. The numerical result is correct while this explanatory sentence conflates computation and physical admission.

Matched native similarly says the snapshot-only reader can “at most” support the four values conditionally on a free-composition assumption, after already correctly stating that the declared reporting procedure computes all four. Physical free composition is not an extra premise for this stipulated calculation. Later paragraphs distinguish arithmetic from joint feasibility accurately, leaving a local inconsistency rather than a wrong returned table.

The other replies sometimes require a caveat even for a purely arithmetic reporting account. That can be proposed communication advice, but the hypothetical reviewer has not thereby refuted an explicitly stated arithmetic procedure. The frozen interpretation contract permits a reply to locate the concern in an implied feasibility claim or intended use. These outputs must be read at that claim level rather than treating every use of “rule criticism” as proof that arithmetic has failed.

## Disposition

E016 is a completed, intact original-criticism comparison. It supplies no recoding, reason-omitted or account-absent observation by itself. Those prospective contrasts must retain their own frozen inputs and terminal records.

The review is ready for a separate publication checkpoint. No new experiment, source edit, optional test campaign or semantic authority change is authorized by this review. Its findings should remain attached to these original occurrences when later comparisons are interpreted.
