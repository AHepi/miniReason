# E002 independent explanatory review

E002 delivered one executable reservation account that survived every recorded case: the direct native-reasoning arm. The other outcomes concern absent final output, malformed submissions, or failed executable realization. The records do not establish a Mini advantage, an expressive impossibility of the supplied language, or a counterexample to ECS. Several failed executable submissions nevertheless contain substantive explanations and criticisms.

This review inspects the frozen [E002 records](../../experiments/records/E002-completion-allowance/REPORT.md), their complete public response contents, request-carried feedback, results and errata. It compares them with [E001](../../experiments/records/E001-reservation-critic/REPORT.md). Diagnostic parsing below preserves duplicate keys and inspects complete leading values without accepting repaired prefixes as submitted programs. No model calls, source changes, output repairs or new task executions were performed for this review. Native reasoning text was not available and is not assessed.

The provider marks all delivered E002 public responses `COMPLETE`. This status describes transport and completion delivery; it does not assert that either the outer answer or its embedded program satisfies the downstream syntax contract. Conversely, Mini native supplies no public account to assess. Its entire reported completion allowance is attributed to reasoning tokens. Nothing about the content of an unseen argument follows from that receipt.

Duplicate object members are prohibited by this repository's strict reading. The Mini critic and revision describe them as something most JSON parsers reject or as simply illegal JSON. That broad description is inaccurate: a permissive parser can accept duplicate names while discarding an earlier member. The relevant observed fact is strict-contract rejection, and retaining duplicate members is necessary to diagnose the actual submitted organization without silently choosing a replacement value.

There is one realization per condition, no deterministic-seed guarantee and no matched actual-token expenditure. The E002 native answer is a new sample rather than a resumed E001 computation. Its success supports the practical value of the larger ceiling for this observed run; it does not prove that E001 would have produced the same answer after more tokens, that every native run needs this budget, or that native reasoning generally exceeds Mini. Cache-hit counts also differ between runs. The causal statement justified directly by the old receipts is that their configured ceilings terminated generation; the later content remains a fresh observation.

## The successful account and what it establishes

The native direct response selects a unique list of booking identities, then selects a maximum-revision event from each booking's full bag of records. It filters those selected events by a short-circuiting hold test and strict expiry, aggregates quantities by stock item, and emits stock items in ascending name order with subtraction from capacity.

This organization gives a conditional explanation of the contract. Replaying a record cannot create another booking identity. Reordering does not change the maximum revision; tied maxima have identical contents by the explicit nonconflict assumption. Checking expiry only after maximum selection prevents an expired latest hold from exposing a superseded older version. The hold test short-circuits access to expiry for releases. Summing one selected active quantity per booking implements version-total quantities. Enumerating stock items preserves event-free rows; unconstrained subtraction preserves negative availability.

The 17 recorded cases exercise those behaviors, including expiry at the boundary, oversubscription, a new hold after release, multiple items, replay and reordering variations. They establish survival on those cases under the named interpreter. Inspection also supplies the preceding conditional mechanism argument; that argument is distinct from, and stronger in scope than, merely counting passed examples. It still presupposes the contract's input invariants and a faithful implementation of the declared operations, and it does not prove execution within the resource envelope for arbitrarily large inputs.

The observed program is a concrete witness that the supplied expression language and direct native route can realize this task organization within these tested conditions. It refutes a local assertion that no program in this language could express the tested procedure. It supplies no comparison with Lean: E001 and E002 contain no Lean proposal or proof-checking condition. It also does not show that Mini's interfaces are incapable of carrying such a program; the unsuccessful Mini samples are failed constructions, not an exhaustive expressive-capacity argument.

## Criticism and repair in the unsuccessful workflows

The matched nonnative proposal consists of a `let` whose value maps over `input`, a dictionary, before attempting a lookup of `__never` on `input.events`; its continuation is `null`. The [critic request](../../experiments/records/E002-completion-allowance/matched-r01/calls/call-0002.request.json) contains all five public observations, each reporting `DSL_LIST_REQUIRED: map`. This is an executed operand-type failure, unlike the program parse failures in other arms. The critic correctly distinguishes this construction from the abstract reservation account and identifies the missing operative organization.

The matched revision's prose responds to that criticism, but its submitted outer JSON has surplus trailing material, so no completed final program is established. Its secondary diagnosis that reading `events` from the whole input is itself erroneous is unsupported: that is precisely where the contract places `events`. Applying `map` to the whole input and then looking up `__never` are the relevant defects. It also says an out-of-contract unknown item would make the program fail, then describes that item being omitted. Failure and omission are different behaviors; neither claim is established by this malformed final submission.

The matched arm's final `result.json` has an empty history, and its errata have an empty `candidate_failures` array because the final submission exception bypassed the normal return of accumulated history. Those empty arrays must not be read as evidence that no earlier candidate failures occurred. The request bytes preserve the five actual type failures and the criticism that followed. This is an evidence-organization limitation in the result summary, not missing evidence of those earlier observations.

The matched native critic correctly identifies surplus closing braces. Its proposal has a 2,110-character leading JSON value followed by nine closing braces. The revised commitments retain that exact leading value and remove eight of the nine surplus braces, leaving one. The leading bytes have SHA-256 `c7d06680487111722c0bc3c1dad5119181d8e7f21b9fdb0b8877a953f398b14f` in both submissions. The revision therefore makes a partial serialization edit while preserving the proposed program organization. Its claims to have restored brace balance and repaired the parse failure are directly contradicted by its final bytes and evaluation. No semantic program revision or completed repair follows from the changed prose.

Mini's first commitments contain a duplicated `do` in a nested value expression. Its critic correctly identifies strict parsing as the first failed boundary and keeps the prose account under consideration. The revision asserts that every object now has distinct keys, but its commitments contain two duplicate `do` members plus four trailing braces. It therefore fails the very construction obligation it claims to discharge. Additional conjectures in its prose about downstream capacity handling cannot substitute for the missing successful parse, and no particular post-repair runtime behavior was observed.

Mini's revision also introduces a substantive mistake: it treats a lower-revision release followed by a higher-revision hold as a condition the task excludes and says latest-wins would misclassify it. The contract permits this history and requires the higher hold to govern. The independently recorded `new_hold_after_release` case in the native result exercises that permitted relation. This error belongs to the prose interpretation and is separable from malformed program delivery.

Across these workflows, correspondence between the supplied criticism and the revised prose is visible. It is evidence that the response addresses a named issue at the text level. Without content-controlled recodings, reason-changing contrasts and the further representation premises, that correspondence does not discharge ECS's full active reason-use conditions. Even successful reason use would not guarantee a sound criticism or a completed repair.

## Bearing, scope and the language-proposal study

Most completed accounts explain the same relevant relation: latest revision must be selected before activity is classified. Their connections to duplicate-insensitivity, cancellation, no resurrection, exact totals and unused stock rows provide substantive bearing under the declared computational respect. Prose remains legitimate evidence for that account even when its attempted machine realization fails. Claims that the specific submitted bytes faithfully implement the account remain independently criticizable and, in several arms, false.

Many accounts still mix applicability boundaries with internal falsifiers. Conflicting contents at the same booking/revision or unknown stock items violate declared input assumptions; observing them changes or defeats an auxiliary premise. A valid-input case where replay changes totals or an expired latest hold revives an older version would instead defeat the stated behavior within its scope. Preservation language likewise needs restraint: failed programs supply proposed invariants, not observed previously successful behaviors.

E002 supports investigating the difference between a content's expression in prose, its attempted translation, and its operative use. It does not justify using successful formalization as an admission condition for a language proposal or treating these samples as a ranking of semantic classes. The next comparative study should freeze its initial Lean-oriented and non-Lean proposals with their interpreters, interfaces and resource conditions, retaining failed or partial proposals as informative specimens. E002 supplies an existing construction example and its failure mechanisms; it supplies neither a prerequisite that each new language already capture the problem correctly nor an authorization to repair frozen specimens retrospectively.

Under [the semantic guide](../SEMANTIC_GUIDE.md), no E002 result establishes historical newness, the complete owned-construction attribution, or created explanatory knowledge. Nor is there an independently justified ECS necessity or sufficiency counterexample. The accounting conditions, representation supplies, target respect and attribution boundary still require their own arguments. The damaged equations in the supplied ECS source cannot be filled in from these experimental outcomes. The strongest established contribution here is an observed executable witness alongside concrete evidence that relevant explanations, apt criticisms, and successful program delivery can come apart.
