# Independent audit of the language and problem-promotion derivations

This review covers [Relative expression, candidate languages and Mini](../LANGUAGE_PROPOSAL_THEOREMS.md) and [Problem promotion](../PROBLEM_PROMOTION.md). It checks their arguments against their stated premises and the legible clauses of the supplied ECS 2.0 and V1.3 sources. The review does not validate the sources' constitutive conjectures, reconstruct their damaged equations, run a proof checker, or assess an ungenerated language proposal.

The documents now support a scoped study of frozen Lean-oriented and non-Lean proposals without requiring prior adequacy, executable syntax or proof certificates as admission gates. They preserve prose as substantive material and distinguish a language's available representations from what Mini can carry, generate or use. No result proves that a proposed language captures the intended phenomenon, makes Mini creative, or exhausts possible improvements.

The most informative mathematical contributions are the explicit loss and preservation constructions: an encoding collision, failure under an untested composite edit, failure of monotonic improvement after adding expressive means, a context-limited route losing distinctions, and a commitment extension destroying a compatible account. Their validity is conditional on explicit representations, maps, contrast families and resource arrangements. They are useful constraints on interpreting experiments, not empirical findings about this model.

Several other results are deliberately limited consequences of definitions or engineering bridges. Inclusion of witnessed contents in reachable contents follows from the chosen study definitions. Finite protected-obligation repair follows from satisfying its named conjuncts. Proof transport requires independently justified checker soundness and a sufficiently strong interpretation map; merely preserving some relation symbols is inadequate for arbitrary quantifiers or negations. These deductions must not be advertised as new theories of creativity or independent validation of ECS.

The remaining substantive burdens include interpretation of each initial proposal, identification of its admissible routes and costs, content-sensitive reason-use contrasts, faithful translation where invoked, and independently fixed attribution boundaries. Theorems do not select those supplies. Frozen first specimens may expose partial or mistaken supplies, and the study can investigate them without silently amending the specimens. Corrections require successor identities and separate comparisons.

The damaged source also imposes a firm limit: this review can check the local proofs against the legible accounting and attribution clauses, but cannot certify conformance to formulas that are missing or to earlier exhibits supplied only by reference. The surviving claims are accordingly local, conditional and criticizable. No remaining clear logical defect was identified in the scoped arguments after these changes; that is a review disposition, not a completeness certificate.
