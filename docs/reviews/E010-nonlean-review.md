# E010: Rival-Story Semantics in the frozen language probe

This review concerns the completed [E010 non-Lean comparison](../../experiments/records/E010-sources-nonlean/summary.json). It reads the original problem, the model-produced corpus C0, the frozen RSS proposal, all three stages of both Mini arms, and a separate reading of the four direct control arms. It does not install a language repair or assign an automatic adequacy or creativity verdict.

The most informative observations are about how components are used: provenance affects the written duplicate relation; a local commitment affects RSS's stated open-status criterion; account-relative refutation can mean rejected assumptions rather than demonstrated falsehood; and preserving competing readings does not itself compare their support. The critics sometimes expose these distinctions and sometimes introduce new misreadings of their own. Their diagnostic headings are claims to inspect, not authoritative classifications.

## What was actually delivered

Both Mini arms completed one cycle with three model calls and no recorded port-empty, format, refusal or budget alarms. Each log contains two deterministic material seed artifacts and three model artifacts. The archived packet and selected-language source files equal their frozen plan inputs byte for byte. The original Mini briefs passed complete route validation. Reconstructing each provider request with the shared stage renderer and that arm's actual prior answers gives exact message equality; public answer text and its UTF-8 hash also agree with the retained history.

The expression and criticism stages receive the complete packet as its frozen JSON serialization. Decoding that packet recovers the original problem, C0, both candidate languages and their identifiers. Reinterpretation receives the selected RSS text and actual expression, with the original corpus field excluded. This is source-text-withheld, not independent of source information: both the source-conditioned language and the expression convey substantial source content. [Mini requests and logs](../../experiments/records/E010-sources-nonlean/mini-r01/requests/001-reinterpret.json), [native Mini requests and logs](../../experiments/records/E010-sources-nonlean/mini_native-r01/requests/001-reinterpret.json).

The body/commitments duplication is host transport adaptation. It is not two independent commitments, two deliveries endorsing a claim, or an implementation of RSS's duplicate semantics. The model's substantive proposals remain the original prose. RSS's accounts, open questions and refutations are discussed in that prose; the current adapter does not execute an RSS status transition.

## Four component questions supported by the material

### 1. Provenance changes which repetitions receive the stated inertness guarantee

C0 says that delivery of the same booking, revision, kind, quantity and expiry adds nothing. RSS defines exact duplication using those fields **and provenance**; its near-duplication relation instead requires a kind, quantity or expiry difference. RSS also makes provenance a set of delivery events associated with a story, then speaks of comparing delivery provenance. The final limitation list says exact provenance cannot be expressed unless provenance is added, despite its earlier presence as a primitive. These are specific tensions in the [frozen RSS proposal](../../experiments/records/E010-sources-nonlean/packet.json), not evidence that every provenance distinction is inexpressible.

A separating case is two distinct delivery events containing identical record fields, with distinct associated provenance sets. Under that reading of the stated definitions, the pair is neither exact nor near duplication. RSS's explicit exact-duplicate inertness rule therefore does not cover the case. This does **not** entail that RSS adds authority to it; an account or reading could supply a further treatment. The absent guarantee and the treatment still owed are the concrete issue.

The native Mini critic identifies this gap with a concrete case. The direct native expression, however, calls same-field records with different provenance “a near-duplication in RSS,” which does not meet the written near-duplication condition. The matched expression makes provenance optional, and its critic incorrectly attributes that conditional to RSS itself. [Native Mini criticism §1](../../experiments/records/E010-sources-nonlean/mini_native-r01/calls/call-0003.response.json), [direct native expression](../../experiments/records/E010-sources-nonlean/native-r01/express.artifact.json), [matched expression and criticism](../../experiments/records/E010-sources-nonlean/matched-r01/criticize.artifact.json).

### 2. Local commitment can close the written global open criterion

RSS says, “A conjecture is open if no account has committed to it or refuted it.” It separately says commitment is local to an account. Read literally as the stated criterion, if

\[
Open(c)\iff\neg\exists a\,[Commit(a,c)\lor Refute(a,c)],
\]

then one account's commitment makes `Open(c)` false even if another account remains undecided or objects. That follows from this conditional formal reading; it is not a measured Mini state change. The wording “if” could instead be intended as sufficient rather than exhaustive, which is a further interpretive question rather than permission to silently replace the definition.

The bare expression repeats the rule. The direct native expression also constructs an account A0 with a commitment while retaining an inquiry-state framing. The record therefore makes it useful to distinguish the existence of an unresolved issue from this particular predicate of a conjecture. A successor investigation could construct two disagreeing accounts and ask which open-question uses remain available after one local commitment. No global correctness condition is needed to investigate that question. [Bare expression, Combinations](../../experiments/records/E010-sources-nonlean/bare-r01/express.artifact.json), [native expression](../../experiments/records/E010-sources-nonlean/native-r01/express.artifact.json).

### 3. RSS refutation already includes a dependency disagreement

RSS allows a refutation to be a counterexample, an inconsistency under a reading, **or** a demonstration that a conjecture depends on an assumption another account rejects. These are different argumentative roles. If a conclusion follows under assumption A and another account rejects A, that occurrence can challenge the argument's use for that account without establishing the conclusion's falsity or eliminating other arguments. The frozen proposal explicitly makes these refutations local and does not demand global agreement.

The matched critic nevertheless attributes to RSS the supposed quotation “it does not encode a notion of assumption dependence. This is an expressive limitation.” That quotation is absent from the frozen RSS text and overlooks the existing dependency/rejection clause. Whether RSS supplies enough structure to distinguish independent supports remains a substantive question; the invented prohibition cannot answer it. [Matched criticism items 10–11](../../experiments/records/E010-sources-nonlean/matched-r01/criticize.artifact.json), [problem-promotion analysis PP1 and PP5](../PROBLEM_PROMOTION.md).

### 4. Unranked readings preserve alternatives without supplying a common preference

C0 says structural supersession is “better supported” while the expiry-deletion account remains a live conjecture. RSS permits different readings and says it does not rank them. The expressions retain the source's tilt in several ways: as a local commitment, as a preference in prose, or as an explicit prose escape. These are inspectably different carriers of the preference. Preserving its wording does not derive a common preference across accounts. Conversely, RSS's lack of a global ranking does not prohibit a local account from giving reasons or accepting a reading.

This distinction is narrower than the nonnative Mini critic's claim that treating the tilt as a reading necessarily erases its evidential role. The actual expression retains both the tilt and source reasons. The question is how that retained material can become operative in inquiry under the proposed organization, not whether an explicit ranking primitive exists. [Mini expression, second disagreement and closing escape](../../experiments/records/E010-sources-nonlean/mini-r01/calls/call-0001.response.json), [matched native expression](../../experiments/records/E010-sources-nonlean/matched_native-r01/express.artifact.json).

## Operative changes and criticism of the critics

The nonnative Mini expression relocates C0's criticism of possible-world construction to “a specific projection, not … the semantics itself.” That is a concrete narrowing of the criticism's target. The original occurrence can question the representational move, including the selected language's own possibility vocabulary. The later critic notices this narrowing, showing that the objection can return in prose through the current route. It does not prove that the earlier narrowing established a permanent barrier. The native Mini expression instead explicitly says the criticism targets the possibility vocabulary RSS supplies. [Nonnative expression, first disagreement](../../experiments/records/E010-sources-nonlean/mini-r01/calls/call-0001.response.json), [nonnative criticism §5(a)](../../experiments/records/E010-sources-nonlean/mini-r01/calls/call-0003.response.json), [native expression, first disagreement](../../experiments/records/E010-sources-nonlean/mini_native-r01/calls/call-0001.response.json).

The nonnative expression also explicitly says RSS turns the source's conjecture that every single-number projection discards information into a fact about projections. This assertion originates in the frozen RSS proposal, whose Projection primitive says every projection loses information. It is a candidate-language commitment being adopted during expression, not a theorem proved by the run. Whether a particular projection loses a relevant distinction depends on its domain, answer contract and comparison. Definitional status does not make that account immune to substantive criticism.

## Expressibility and preserved conjectures

The native expression marks convexity, partial orders, hard/soft constraints and word-level criticism as prose escapes because RSS lacks named relations for them. This records the route the expression actually chose. It does not prove that no composition of RSS's conjectures, accounts, assumptions or open questions could express those contents. The native critic itself acknowledges this limitation for convexity and partial orders. The nonnative reader's stronger claim that non-Lean semantics “cannot state a theorem” likewise does not follow from lacking a proof-assistant implementation. [Native expression obstruction notes](../../experiments/records/E010-sources-nonlean/mini_native-r01/calls/call-0001.response.json), [native criticism §11](../../experiments/records/E010-sources-nonlean/mini_native-r01/calls/call-0003.response.json), [nonnative reading](../../experiments/records/E010-sources-nonlean/mini-r01/calls/call-0002.response.json).

A prose escape remains a legitimate substantive occurrence. If a reader recovers a quoted distinction through that escape, it demonstrates delivery through the combined configured route; it does not by itself show that a distinct RSS relation performed the work. Conversely, labeling prose “outside RSS proper” cannot disqualify it from the experiment or from criticizing RSS. The source explicitly permits that criticism.

Cross-revision cancellation, summing tied holds and expiry deleting supersession are preserved as conjectures or rival readings, including proposals that may be false under the stated supersession account. Their continued presence satisfies the instruction to retain false and incomplete material; it is not a delivery failure. Newly added assertions about the language, quotations of nonexistent prohibitions, and attribution of the wrong stage's access require their own support. The same distinction applies to inherited arithmetic or conceptual mistakes in C0: preservation does not vindicate them, and their reappearance alone does not establish a new translator error.

## Bounded next inquiry

The record supplies a concrete next construction task: choose one preserved issue, retain its raw occurrence and reasons, and ask for an account that discriminates the competing treatments. Strong candidates are same-field deliveries with different provenance, two accounts whose commitments differ while a question remains unresolved, or the distinction between withdrawal of one argument and falsity of its conclusion. A revision can propose a new component or an operative prose convention while leaving the original RSS package intact. Promoting that issue allocates investigation; it does not certify bearing, refutation or improvement.

These observations do not isolate a Mini benefit. Under identical prior answers the matched and Mini policies send the same messages, while the observed answers differ and each arm has one repetition. The direct controls also preserve disputes, introduce account constructions and produce both substantive objections and unsupported diagnoses. The current contrast supports instrument calibration and a set of specific component questions. It supplies neither a general creativity verdict nor a theorem that further configurations cannot help.
