## A substantive worked construction, with its inheritance exposed

Mini native supplies capacity 100 and two bookings with alternatives `{0,10}`. In its hypothetical resource model, each high alternative consumes 10 units of a second resource whose capacity is 10. The initial snapshot does not name this resource. The construction explicitly labels the example as its proposal and retains the difference between that hypothesis and an observed warehouse fact.

| Interpretation of that fixed example | Joint reservation sums | Exact scalar availabilities | Status of the old interval `[80,100]` |
|---|---|---|---|
| All combinations of the two local alternatives admitted | `{0,10,20}` | `{100,90,80}` | Encloses all values; as a full real/integer interval it also includes unattainable interior values. |
| Added hypothetical resource excludes the two highs together | `{0,10}` | `{100,90}` | Still a correct enclosure, but its lower endpoint 80 is no longer attainable. |

The construction correctly concludes that “availability can be 80” is false under the added resource premise while “availability is between 80 and 100” remains true but loose. This case links the criticism to the claim actually made without requiring rejection of the records or the capacity subtraction. It provides a positive witnessed expression of the target/use distinction on the ordinary prose route.

The pattern of two `{0,10}` bookings, an unnamed shared resource and an impossible high/high combination is already supplied by E009. The later response must not receive credit for originating that pattern. What it works out here is a specific resource-capacity binding and the different consequences for attainability and enclosure. That is an inspectable elaboration relative to this initialization, not evidence about the model's entire earlier repertoire or an ECS `New` finding. Changing the numerical capacity is not itself the relevant contribution.

The account's broader phrasing remains imperfect. A “convex summary” can intentionally enclose a non-convex set, and a declaration of independence alone does not make every point of the interval attainable. The explicit example supplies stronger support than a blanket demand to replace intervals. A compatibility restriction also need not change either endpoint: it can remove only interior possibilities. Those are reviewer distinctions, not additional model results.

## Criticism and revision do different things in the four staged arms

The strongest shared criticism is that P7 gives examples of targets, not a closed menu. An aggregation rule can be a reconstruction rule, and an independence assumption can be an assumption target even though P7 names logical time as its example. A proposed finer distinction can still be useful; it does not need to prove that a broad existing category is incapable of covering it. The critics generally challenge necessity more successfully than they establish uselessness.

### Matched: reversal presented as preservation

The [matched construction](../../experiments/records/E015-joint-prose-retry/matched-r01/construct.artifact.json) says its contribution is to add a “level-relation target” and identify it as P9's target. It explicitly acknowledges a rival interpretation under which this would be a re-description. The [critic](../../experiments/records/E015-joint-prose-retry/matched-r01/criticize.artifact.json) challenges the argument for a necessary new category because P7's examples are not exhaustive. That is a supported objection to the exclusion argument. Its earlier suggestion that a conjecture already being disputed prevents a further target distinction does not follow; contested content can motivate new distinctions.

The [revision](../../experiments/records/E015-joint-prose-retry/matched-r01/revise.artifact.json) opens “I preserve J,” but ends by assigning J the opposite answer: the objection is “not a new target.” It also says the criticism inferred that failure to target the rule requires a new target. That was J's move; the critic challenged it. The revision has adopted the critic's principal conclusion while misreporting the earlier organization and who argued for it.

This is a concrete commitment and provenance error, not merely an unsettled taxonomy. J's retreat clause permits reconsideration; it does not make categorical non-addition identical to J's earlier proposal. A revision could explicitly withdraw or suspend the addition while preserving J as a historical occurrence. Instead it conflates preservation of the occurrence with preservation of its claim. The raw J is nevertheless intact in the host record, so this is not archival corruption.

The [promotion](../../experiments/records/E015-joint-prose-retry/matched-r01/promote.artifact.json) retains a legitimate question about whether the finer target classification contributes anything, but repeats the mistaken preservation account. Its branch “if no such case can be given” also leaves a burden ambiguous: failure to produce a case in a finite continuation would not establish that no case is available. That future distinction needs to be specified before treating non-production as decisive.

### Mini: a sound challenge followed by an unsupported strengthening claim

The [Mini critic](../../experiments/records/E015-joint-prose-retry/mini-r01/criticize.artifact.json) correctly notes that aggregation can be a reconstruction rule in P7's existing vocabulary. It distinguishes locating an objection more precisely from proving that an additional target category is required. This criticism does not show that every target/locus distinction is useless, and it does not need to do so to challenge J's overbroad exclusion.

The [revision](../../experiments/records/E015-joint-prose-retry/mini-r01/revise.artifact.json) keeps both readings open and adds a proposed distinction between stated rules and presupposed commitments. That is an admissible new proposal. It then claims that stating the rule is necessarily stronger because it adds content about a resource absent from the snapshot. This inference is unsupported. Stating an already operative presupposition explicitly can preserve its content. Stating a conditional as an explicit rule need not assert its antecedent. The response moves between making an independence assumption explicit, stating a conditional incompatibility rule, and asserting an extra resource constraint, although these are different changes.

For example, the same conditional content can say that if a specified resource condition holds, a particular combination is inadmissible, either as an ordinary sentence or under the heading “Rule.” Neither form entails that the condition actually holds. An additional assertion that the resource condition obtains would strengthen the premises. This counter-reading defeats the claimed necessary strengthening by formulation alone; it does not prove that every actual reformulation preserves meaning.

The [promotion](../../experiments/records/E015-joint-prose-retry/mini-r01/promote.artifact.json) usefully questions what the newly alleged cost measures. It does not resolve the antecedent/assertion conflation and makes a further partly false dichotomy between vocabulary and epistemic status. A vocabulary omission and uncertainty about an actual resource can coexist. Its proposed question is useful material for a controlled continuation, not an established barrier of the source vocabulary.

### Matched native: an explicit narrowing with limits retained

The [matched-native critic](../../experiments/records/E015-joint-prose-retry/matched_native-r01/criticize.artifact.json) identifies a specific mismatch: J describes P7's fifth example as an assumption relied on rather than carried, then announces that P7 has no slot for uncarried commitments. It also distinguishes the assertable conditional resource claim from the claim that a particular snapshot representation lacks the resource relation. That challenges the inference from a missing field to wholesale unassertability.

The [revision](../../experiments/records/E015-joint-prose-retry/matched_native-r01/revise.artifact.json) explicitly creates J′, withdraws the broad carried/not-carried novelty claim and narrows its organization to assumptions versus expressive limits. It also weakens “P9 runs the losses together” to the more specific claim that P9 does not work through the proposed repair asymmetry. The response identifies both what changed and what it retains; this is a more accurate change account than matched's preservation claim.

The narrower category still requires an interpretation and a specified boundary. The snapshot data fields, the account's surrounding prose and the whole configured system are different expressive routes. The [promotion](../../experiments/records/E015-joint-prose-retry/matched_native-r01/promote.artifact.json) appropriately asks for a criterion distinguishing presupposition from expressive limit and whether the new category is needed. It does not show that such a criterion must be a new mandatory type or that the proposed organization has been used in another case.

### Mini native: a qualified correction, followed by cross-episode misattribution

The [Mini-native critic](../../experiments/records/E015-joint-prose-retry/mini_native-r01/criticize.artifact.json) supplies two accounts that compute the same interval but make different claims: enclosure only versus complete possibilities. This supports its objection that J's headline over-selects independent-product aggregation as P9's target. J's later qualifications already recognize the alternative, so the criticism is best read as correcting the headline and its scope rather than refuting the entire account.

The critic's stronger description of the bound/set distinction as merely inherited understates J's worked sound-enclosure versus attainability argument. P9 names bounds and correlation loss; that is not by itself the complete argument J supplies. One quotation about non-convexity also comes from C0's P3, not the selected P9 paragraph. The [revision](../../experiments/records/E015-joint-prose-retry/mini_native-r01/revise.artifact.json) notices that source-location difference explicitly. It moves independent-product aggregation into a conditional subcase, lowers its novelty claim and preserves the numerical example. These are identifiable textual responses to the critique, with their reasons stated.

The [promotion](../../experiments/records/E015-joint-prose-retry/mini_native-r01/promote.artifact.json) then asks a substantive next question: has J′ removed the over-specific target or merely renamed it? It also permits scrutiny of whether the originally selected E009 loci warranted this pairing. Those are legitimate promotion targets. Its opening, however, calls the objection to J's headline “the E009 criticism's charge.” E009 precedes this J and contains no such charge; the relevant objection is E015's own criticize occurrence. Later paragraphs distinguish them more accurately, but the opening is a concrete cross-episode provenance error.

## Preservation, use and attribution

All six constructions explicitly preserve the hypothetical status of the unnamed resource at their principal statements, and the staged responses retain that qualification. All continue to distinguish rule and record criticism and keep the exposure-versus-decision dispute available. This does not establish complete preservation of every nuance. Overbroad statements about what P7 permits, the hard/soft role of capacity, source locations and historical commitments require the qualifications above.

The host retains C0, L0 and every generated occurrence separately. All arm records report `standing_effect = none` and `proposed_changes_installed = false`. Each staged arm queues one whole promotion occurrence; bare and native queue none. Those fields accurately describe the host's actions. They do not certify that the model's prose refrained from endorsing a claim or that a model correctly described its own change.

The read–criticize–revise history supplies exposure and inspectable correspondence: some later claims directly address an earlier objection. It does not yet isolate ECS reason use through content-preserving recoding and relevant-content change. Nor does an accurately described J′ demonstrate operative repair, because no separate use stage installs and tests its organization. The constructive example and possible corrections are bounded witnesses; hidden provider history leaves historical `New` unresolved.

No carrier comparison is concluded here. Prose was selected while both candidate languages and WHL-focused criticism were visible. The model may use that whole initialization. Likewise, one run per arm under matched conditional policies supplies no independent Mini advantage. A stronger engine attribution would need an actual model-facing intervention and the corresponding control.

## Concrete successor questions supported by this run

The most immediate reason-use candidate is matched's misreported reversal. Freeze its original J and C_J and ask a separately identified continuation to respond while preserving the original claim and source attribution. Compare the actual criticism with a meaning-preserving recoding and a relevant change in its argument, retaining identical J and source material. Predeclare which original commitments and actor attributions must be distinguished. A renderer that explicitly juxtaposes the prior claim and proposed response can be tested as a distinct intervention; the host must not silently decide that a prose response has answered a criticism or changed standing. The question is whether semantic change can be acknowledged and correctly attributed, not whether the model uses a preferred phrase.

A second supported inquiry concerns the Mini revision's alleged strengthening by explicit formulation. Freeze that whole claim and compare an equivalent conditional in ordinary prose and under a “Rule” label, then separately add the antecedent as an asserted premise. Keep the resource example and requested use fixed. The first contrast concerns formulation with preserved content; the second changes content. A response that treats the first as establishing an actual resource but ignores the second would expose a specific commitment-mapping failure. A response may reject the original claim with reasons. This does not make a formal grammar mandatory or demote prose.

Mini native also supplies a concrete proposed organization for a later use comparison. Before testing it, fix whether the requested answer is an exact possibility set, tight endpoints, a safe enclosure or a decision. An interval can preserve one of those uses while losing another; a successful restriction case must not change the question afterward. The current worked example can be development material, but a later preservation or transfer test needs its own identified cases and its installation/use bridge. Repeating the supplied example would not independently test acquisition of its organization.

These follow-ups arise from specific observed content and remain separate from the already frozen E012/E013 carrier comparisons. Those plans should not be silently changed in response to E015. No claim of exhausted inquiry is supported: this run supplies several concrete, discriminating continuation questions, including criticism of its own selection and interpretation.
