# A001 v1 → v2: disposition of every review finding

`REVIEW.md` returned 29 findings, F1-F29, seven of them blockers (F1, F2, F3,
F4, F5, F6, F16, F18 — the review marks eight as `[blocker]`; the commission
named seven). Every one is dispositioned below as **fixed**, **withdrawn** or
**declined, with reason**. v1 (`STAGING.md`) and `REVIEW.md` are retained
unedited; the revision is `STAGING-v2.md` with `CELLS-v2.md`.

**Headline.** Four v1 claims are withdrawn outright: that Case A-S's Anchoring
holds; that FW5's projection theorem is "the reason" A-S's attribution is
false; that Case B-N's determinacy step is owed by (E); and that the executable
leg can reach an Account verdict. A001 now offers **no candidate
counterexample** of either kind.
