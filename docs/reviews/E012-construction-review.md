# E012: construction with the frozen WHL carrier

This independent review concerns [E012-joint-lean](../../experiments/records/E012-joint-lean/plan.json), plan `d2cf3cc284e751283e4d76f1c76932a24958fd081065ad22e024f32c157d7413`. It retains the content distinctions fixed for [E011](E011-construction-review.md#distinctions-fixed-for-this-review) and applied to [E015](E015-construction-review.md): criticism target and consequence, hypothetical versus observed compatibility, exact possibilities versus bounds, inherited versus constructed organization, preserved source meanings, validity of criticism, and faithful revision/promotion attribution.

The selected carrier is the original Warehouse History Language (WHL), presented as Lean-compatible. C0, both initial language proposals, the full E009 Mini-native criticism and the P7/P9 question remain visible and unchanged. Ordinary prose remains a legitimate route for both proposals and criticism. Selecting WHL does not establish compilation or require every proposal to have executable form.

This review additionally distinguishes use of existing WHL expressions and ordinary compound definitions from changed domain assumptions, altered existing definitions or added primitives. A proposed extension can be substantive material without becoming an installed component. A definition's presence in a reply is not evidence of Lean elaboration, operative installation or a proved correspondence to the warehouse question.

## Result and scope

E012 supplies substantive compound uses of WHL as well as prose proposals that mostly quote its vocabulary. Matched native repairs a genuinely contradictory proposed condition and constructs a WHL `Refutation`. Mini native separates losing a joint choice from losing an attainable total and supplies sound implications between those notions. These are identifiable construction and criticism-responsive revision in the public text. They do not demonstrate compilation, an installed language extension, historical novelty, or a Mini advantage.

Some later statements introduce fresh mistakes. Matched's promotion treats `P ∧ True` and `P` as different represented propositions; Mini's promotion infers that there is no availability account from the absence of an actual downstream consumer; Mini-native promotion reverses the polarity of the compatibility objection. Later-stage status does not make those claims correct. A particularly concrete successor concerns the interaction between matched-native's new selection structure and WHL's existing zero default for an empty alternative list.

The separate [instrument review](E012-instrument-review.md) verifies exact material and request bindings, complete response receipts, occurrence text hashes, Mini replay and resources. The artifacts sometimes acknowledge that alternative and elsewhere speak as though exclusion were established.

The source problem and E009 already supply per-booking alternatives, a possible unnamed resource, an incompatible high/high combination and suggestions for a resource/possible-world relation. Reusing them is legitimate initialization use. The additional work to inspect is a discriminating relation, consequence or argument, not the appearance of a new name for compatibility. Neither this review nor the run establishes ECS historical `New` or creativity relative to an actor's earlier repertoire.

## What WHL use establishes

WHL's frozen `Conjecture` is `Snapshot → Prop`, its `Commitment` is `Prop`, and its `Refutation` contains a snapshot and a proof that a conjecture fails there. The absence of a named domain predicate does not by itself show that this propositional route cannot express a target or assumption. For example, an independently specified proposition can be held constant across snapshots, and definitions can compose existing material. Whether such an expression has the intended interpretation, and whether its parameters add assumptions, are further questions. A type declaration alone does not answer them.

This matters because several responses repeat WHL's own self-description that it cannot express compatibility or assumption-target relations. Such self-description is source material, not an independently established impossibility theorem. Matched's promotion improves on the blanket claim by acknowledging that a `Snapshot → Prop` could have the content “this record's quantity is implausible,” while still asking where its interpretation comes from. That is the right distinction to preserve: absent named vocabulary, possible compositional expression and an adequate interpreted expression are different conditions.

The native staged arms give positive witnesses of compound use. Matched native eventually maps a particular endpoint claim into the existing `Conjecture` and constructs its existing `Refutation`. Mini native defines `LocalChoice` by membership in the existing `possible_reservations`, then relates choices, totals, feasible totals and reports. Those are more than aliases or a Lean fence around prose. Their logical consequences can be inspected independently of whether the warehouse interpretation has been completed.

The added scaffolding remains visible. Matched native stipulates a numerical reading of opaque `Quantity`, introduces a selection type and unsupported compatibility/summation/order machinery, and explicitly disclaims compilation and installation. Mini native uses `Total` and `R` as parameters and marks new target constructors as proposals. Parameterizing a predicate does not establish a particular warehouse constraint or assert that an extra physical resource exists. Conversely, calling these expressions proposals does not discharge the interpretation obligations.

There is no Lean elaboration receipt. Frozen WHL itself leaves opaque arithmetic/type bridges unresolved and its named lower/upper availability functions subtract the per-booking minimum/maximum sums respectively, requiring care about their numerical orientation. Matched-native's scaffold discusses that orientation explicitly. The review therefore credits inspected logical implications and explicit references to carrier components, not an executable warehouse development. Compilation would be an additional observation, not an admission condition for these conjectures or criticisms.

## A numerical consequence in the direct native arm

The native construction uses capacity 10 and two bookings with alternatives `{0,10}`. Under unrestricted combination, total reservations are `{0,10,20}` and scalar availability is `{-10,0,10}`. Under the hypothetical high/high exclusion, reservations are `{0,10}` and availability is `{0,10}`. The interval `[-10,10]` remains a sound enclosure although its lower endpoint is no longer attainable.

This correctly retains negative availability and distinguishes an enclosure claim from exact possibilities. It does not infer that the resource actually exists. The statement “This example is mine” needs an inheritance qualification: E009 supplied the two `{0,10}` bookings and the forbidden high/high pattern. Working through different claim consequences is the inspectable elaboration here. Its broader suggestion that decision-relevant endpoints are thereby refuted is too strong: a conservative bound can remain suitable for a particular decision. A downstream decision rule must be specified before that consequence follows.

## Matched: a target argument weakens, then the shadow is misdescribed

The [matched critic](../../experiments/records/E012-joint-lean/matched-r01/criticize.artifact.json) correctly distinguishes a criticism's target from an edit that would remove a symptom. J argues that an aggregation constraint can change while rule and records remain fixed, then treats that as identifying the unique target. It also claims that changing a rule or record could not remove the incompatible combination. A record revision can remove one of the alternatives, even if that has additional costs or is a poor repair. The edit-location argument therefore does not establish the proposed target classification.

The critic also turns P7's illustration into a general “act-separability” criterion. P7 distinguishes accepting/rejecting a rule from accepting/rejecting data, but does not state a complete sorting theorem. The supported objection is to J's inference; the critic's alternative organizing principle remains proposed. Similarly, lacking a concrete coupling witness need not make the conditional objection or investigation inert. It limits an actual-world conclusion.

The [revision](../../experiments/records/E012-joint-lean/matched-r01/revise.artifact.json) accepts the sorting problem, retains the aggregation locus conditionally and gives a proposed carrier shadow:

```lean
def rule_data_separable_shadow (snap : Snapshot) : Prop :=
  ∃ (r₁ r₂ : ReservationRule),
    rules_disagree r₁ r₂ snap ∧
    True
```

It acknowledges the placeholder. This expression is compound use of an existing name, but it expresses a rule/rule disagreement, not P7's independent rule/data acceptance contrast. It supplies no record-side judgment or relation between the two sorts of acceptance. Acknowledging that limitation is preferable to treating the sketch as completed evidence.

The [promotion](../../experiments/records/E012-joint-lean/matched-r01/promote.artifact.json) usefully asks whether any adequate carrier expression can complete the intended contrast and which current vocabulary is actually doing the work. It then says removing the `True` placeholder produces a shadow of a different thing. Logically `P ∧ True` and `P` are equivalent; removing a comment or placeholder can change how a display advertises missing work, but not the represented proposition here. The issue is the missing record-side relation, not logical content supplied by `True`.

The promotion also treats a future failure to produce a completed shadow as support for inexpressibility without specifying the search boundary. Failure of one attempt is not a proof that no expression can be composed. A good successor would fix a intended rule/data contrast and then inspect a candidate expression and interpretation. It should distinguish an undefined primitive, an uninstantiated parameter, an actual assumption and a failed construction attempt.

## Mini: explicit narrowing, followed by an unsupported absence claim

The [Mini critic](../../experiments/records/E012-joint-lean/mini-r01/criticize.artifact.json) challenges the need to add an environmental-model category when P7 already includes assumption criticism. That is a supported reason to suspend the claim of necessary addition. Its opening calls the defect established, while later text acknowledges that the alternative interpretation remains unproved. P7's open list does not establish that the proposed refinement is useless either.

The [revision](../../experiments/records/E012-joint-lean/mini-r01/revise.artifact.json) explicitly drops the “sixth target” commitment, records J as historical, and separates a narrow logical-time reading from a broader assumption-class reading. This is an identifiable, accurately described change. It retains conditionality about the resource and the distinction between a snapshot's arithmetic and a stronger realizability claim. Its assertions of whole-carrier inexpressibility remain underargued; quotations of WHL names and comments supply no witness that all compositional routes are unavailable.

The [promotion](../../experiments/records/E012-joint-lean/mini-r01/promote.artifact.json) asks a useful question about a claim made by an account versus a demand introduced by its interpreter. It supports that question with the false generalization “No availability account appears in the corpus at all.” C0 contains a reconstruction account and disputed claims about availability, and the packet also contains the candidate languages. Absence of a particular deployed consumer or an observed demand does not mean there is no account to criticize. A hypothetical or explicitly conditional account can have assessable commitments.

The response also implies that different interpreter-dependent answers remove a stable target and create a category error. That does not follow: a target can be indexed to an account's stated role or an interpreter's claim. The useful successor is to freeze that role, not to assume that a consumer observation is necessary for all criticism. Its Lean fence contains only a comment about missing vocabulary; it is not a formal expression of this proposed relation.

## Matched native: a real local repair and a new scaffold interaction

Matched native's initial `endpoint_claim` conjoins attainment of two endpoints. Its proposed `p9_refutes_endpoint` then conjoins that claim with negation of the first attainment conjunct. Writing the first endpoint assertion as `A` and the second as `B`, the condition is `(A ∧ B) ∧ ¬A`.

The [critic](../../experiments/records/E012-joint-lean/matched_native-r01/criticize.artifact.json) correctly identifies the contradiction. A false conjecture remains admissible material; the particular defect is treating this unsatisfiable conjunction as an available condition witnessing the endpoint objection. The [revision](../../experiments/records/E012-joint-lean/matched_native-r01/revise.artifact.json) explicitly accepts the defect and separates the roles:

```lean
theorem obstruction_refutes_endpoint
    (snap : Snapshot) (bookings : List Booking)
    (h : endpoint_obstruction snap bookings) :
    ¬ endpoint_claim snap bookings :=
  fun he => h he.1

def endpoint_conjecture (bookings : List Booking) : Conjecture :=
  fun snap => endpoint_claim snap bookings

def endpoint_refutation (snap : Snapshot) (bookings : List Booking)
    (h : endpoint_obstruction snap bookings) :
    Refutation (endpoint_conjecture bookings) :=
  { snap := snap,
    not_c := obstruction_refutes_endpoint snap bookings h }
```

Here `endpoint_obstruction` is separately defined as the absence of an admissible selection attaining the pessimistic endpoint. The implication is sound: the endpoint claim supplies exactly the witness that the obstruction excludes. The WHL wrapper uses an existing carrier structure in a meaningful way. The revision does not assert that an obstruction actually obtains or install its additional selection machinery.

The [promotion](../../experiments/records/E012-joint-lean/matched_native-r01/promote.artifact.json) finds a concrete issue in that machinery. `Selection.local_mem` demands a chosen member of each listed booking's `possible_reservations`. For a listed booking with no matching record, that list is empty, so no such selection exists. WHL's booking bounds nevertheless default to zero on the empty list. Consequently the new endpoint obstruction can hold even under universally permissive compatibility, for a reason unrelated to cross-booking exclusion.

This is a useful successor because it fixes a specific interaction between new construction and frozen semantics. The broad “none” alternative question already appeared in E009; its interaction with this particular selection structure and endpoint-refutation condition is the additional relationship. The promotion's literal claim that WHL never supplies zero as a live alternative when records exist is too broad: release records and expired holds can yield zero effects. It later distinguishes zero effect, the empty-list default and a “none of these records is true” interpretation more carefully. Adding a null alternative to every nonempty list would be a stronger semantic change than repairing the empty-list mismatch.

## Mini native: projection repaired, then objection polarity reversed

Mini native's initial construction correctly proves that an independently inclusive reported set cannot also be exact if some independently possible scalar total has no feasible realization. With `Total` and `R` as parameters, that is a conditional logical claim. It does not implement availability subtraction or establish a physical interpretation for `R`.

The [critic](../../experiments/records/E012-joint-lean/mini_native-r01/criticize.artifact.json) introduces A/B alternatives `{0,10}`, C alternatives `{0,20}`, and a relation that excludes only the choice `(10,10,0)`. All totals `{0,10,20,30,40}` remain possible because `(0,0,20)` also realizes total 20. Thus exclusion of a choice does not entail loss of its scalar total. That is a correct and discriminating argument against identifying the two conditions.

Two scope qualifications matter. First, J describes the total-level condition as “R is universal or irrelevant.” The example refutes universality, but can leave the restriction irrelevant to the scalar-total question. Since “irrelevant” was not defined, the critic does not establish that the whole disjunction is false. Second, `(10,10,20)` remains permitted: this is a contextual three-way exclusion, not an A-high/B-high prohibition independently of C's choice. It shows the general projection distinction without being a direct counterexample to P9's stronger pairwise high/high scenario.

The [revision](../../experiments/records/E012-joint-lean/mini_native-r01/revise.artifact.json) responds substantively. It defines `ChoiceCorrelationLoss` as an excluded local choice, `UniversalCompatibility` as admission of all local choices, and distinct `TotalCorrelationLoss`/`TotalIndependence` predicates. It proves that absence of choice loss is equivalent to universal compatibility, and that total loss implies choice loss. The latter proof takes a choice realizing the lost total and observes that admitting that choice would contradict loss of the total. Both inspected implications are sound. The previous exactness theorem remains, with its narrower total-loss premise exposed.

The revision still describes a bounds-only account as receiving a warning about tightness too broadly. An arbitrary choice restriction can leave the entire total set and both endpoints unchanged. The recorded three-booking example already demonstrates that possibility. It remains necessary to ask what observable or claim the restriction changes; the existence of an excluded choice alone does not answer it.

The [promotion](../../experiments/records/E012-joint-lean/mini_native-r01/promote.artifact.json) asks a useful next question: can `R` remain an abstract feasibility parameter, or must it be interpreted through a specified physical resource to serve the intended use? It also makes the concrete polarity error that P9's objection is “the condition that there is no `ChoiceCorrelationLoss`.” The absence of such loss is the defended universal-compatibility condition. An exclusion objection would supply a loss witness. This reverses objection and defended assumption despite the preceding revision having explicitly distinguished them.

The promotion again calls the critic's example an instance of high/high exclusion with preserved totals, without retaining its context dependence. A later continuation should distinguish the abstract predicate, a particular resource-derived interpretation and actual warehouse evidence, and should retain the polarity of objections and assumptions across those changes. Missing a resource interpretation does not alone prove that new primitives are mandatory for the abstract conditional result.

## Protected meanings and attribution

The principal constructions retain the hypothetical status of the unnamed resource and preserve P7's distinction between criticism of rules and data. They keep the exposure-versus-downstream-decision dispute available. Native's worked example also retains negative availability. The records preserve all source and generated occurrences separately. This does not erase the overbroad taxonomy claims, missing arithmetic bridges, account-absence claim, context change or polarity reversal identified above.

All arm records retain `standing_effect = none` and `proposed_changes_installed = false`. Each staged arm queues one whole promotion occurrence. A queue entry marks material for subsequent inquiry; it does not establish bearing, adoption or an implemented return. The text itself nevertheless contains actual, inspectable revision: matched native repairs its contradictory condition, Mini native narrows its projection claim, and Mini explicitly withdraws the necessity of its sixth category. These are textual responses to particular reasons, without a demonstrated downstream installation or resulting warehouse correction.

Both initial languages remain in the common packet, the whole E009 criticism supplies extensive suggested organization, and the selected WHL is presented again. This is a carrier-conditioned experiment under common supplied information. It cannot isolate exclusive expressive access to WHL, establish an intrinsic language ranking or attribute a result to WHL alone. Matched/Mini initial prompts are identical within thinking mode, and later differences follow their sampled prior occurrences under the shared conditional renderer. Observed differences therefore establish no additional model-facing Mini effect.

## Concrete successors after the frozen carrier block

These proposals are reviewer follow-up designs, not further E012 observations and not changes to the frozen E013 setup.

1. **Selection versus the empty-list default.** Freeze matched-native's revised selection and endpoint definitions. Use a named booking included in `bookings` with no matching record, hold compatibility universal, and inspect whether the endpoint obstruction is due to selection emptiness. Compare that with a booking having an explicit zero-effect record. Ask what minimal change, if any, is justified and which meaning it changes. Do not silently add a “none” alternative to nonempty histories.
2. **Objection versus defended assumption.** Freeze Mini-native's exact revised definitions and promotion sentence. Ask a continuation to determine which polarity represents an exclusion objection and what revision follows. Preserve the public earlier occurrence and distinguish withdrawing a sentence from falsely claiming its original meaning was unchanged. Equivalent names or packaging can later test sensitivity, with the underlying proposition held fixed.
3. **Choice, total and bound consequences.** Freeze an explicitly stated account claim and distinguish a contextual excluded choice whose total remains attainable from a true pairwise high/high exclusion. Ask which observable changes under each relation. Do not supply an observed physical resource or promote an abstract parameter to an actual-world premise. A later resource interpretation can be a separate case with disclosed additional assumptions.

These questions follow concrete content produced in this run. They do not require optimizing a scalar score or cycling through unguided prompt variants. The already frozen prose/WHL/non-Lean comparison should finish with its sources intact; subsequent intervention should state its own selected occurrence, reason, protected meanings and discriminating use before any live response is inspected.
