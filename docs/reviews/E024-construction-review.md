## What the selected occurrence actually did

The [construction](../../experiments/records/E024-chain-construction/mini-r01/calls/call-0001.response.json) proposes relocating the compatibility objection from arithmetic to the snapshot's claimed sufficiency. It adds a distinction between the target of criticism and the place a repair would be made. It preserves arithmetic-as-account, representation and no-change-until-evidence readings as competitors. These are proposed organizations of the source. They are not observations of a warehouse or established source commitments.

The [criticism](../../experiments/records/E024-chain-construction/mini-r01/calls/call-0002.response.json) challenges the construction's claim that calling the issue a rule defect mislocates criticism “in exactly the way P7 warns against.” That challenge has a real textual object: P7 does not uniquely select the construction's relocation. But the critic overstates its own case. It describes P7 as merely descriptive, although P7 says preserving the distinction matters; and it says P7 enumerates four accept/reject combinations, although the quoted passage explicitly names two mixed combinations. Distinguish the warranted objection to exclusive source support from those additional claims.

The [revision](../../experiments/records/E024-chain-construction/mini-r01/calls/call-0003.response.json) accepts the alleged misattribution and proposes a one-paragraph amendment to construction section 2, leaving the rest standing. It inherits the critic's overly sharp descriptive/prescriptive dichotomy and four-combination attribution. Its claim that no protected use is lost is not an independently established whole-bundle result: the construction's categorical opening, rule-versus-input framing and later organization also remain available to B. A local wording amendment does not by itself settle those wider tensions.

The [promotion output](../../experiments/records/E024-chain-construction/mini-r01/calls/call-0004.response.json) quotes the proposed amendment's exact sentence: “P9 does not refute the arithmetic, but it does suggest that the arithmetic is being asked a question whose inputs it does not have.” It asks whether this is a flat description of input scope or quietly attributes design intent and misuse, thereby subordinating holist and deflationary readings. The alternatives have stated consequences: further amendment if the normative implication persists, or retention of the amendment and open questions if it does not.

This is a traceable proposed follow-up about the revision's own commitment. It is more specific than the occurrence merely containing the word promotion. Its diagnosis is still contestable. A claim about an argument's input requirements need not ascribe authorial intent. The output also proposes that the wording must be “received as flat across all three readings”; requiring that reception is itself an additional criterion, not something proved by the sources. B must be free to reject the diagnosis, challenge that criterion, suspend, or decline another promotion.

## Consequence for the chain and formal-feedback research

Proceed with the already fixed mini-r01 bundle and a distinct locate/discriminate B episode, one cycle. Freeze the actual original material and all four A public outputs only after their complete record is verified on main. Do not inject this review's diagnoses, the control examples or E023 findings into B. Publish the handoff, plan and zero-call preflight before dispatch. Preserve any failure and do not retry or substitute an arm automatically.

The present observations sharpen the separate [formal-feedback protocol](../FORMAL_FEEDBACK_PROTOCOL.md): finite calculations can be checked while claims about source meaning, necessity versus sufficiency, and diagnostic scope still require scrutiny. A future executable formal-feedback experiment should record explicit premises and prose mappings, return localized checker evidence, and inspect whether the next public response repairs the relevant claim or merely changes assumptions. E024 did not execute that formal-feedback intervention and supplies no live causal result for it.

Independent review read all public outputs; root checked the selected occurrence and recording totals. Source text and existing frozen plans remain unchanged. No proposed repair has been installed. Review conclusions remain bounded to these recorded occurrences.
