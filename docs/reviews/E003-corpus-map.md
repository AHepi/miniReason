# E003 frozen prose: source map and a later construction pair

This map reads the actual E003 prose without correcting or endorsing it. Its purpose is to preserve concrete distinctions for the first carrier studies and to identify a justified later construction question. A conjecture need not be correct to remain in the specimen. This document supplies neither a language proposal nor an adequacy gate.

The source is [E003 corpus.json](../../experiments/records/E003-frozen-corpus/corpus.json), corpus identity `41efaae132de8b176c85b109b39ab39738708355742354d2e9714aeaf87ecb55`, from preparation plan `842e2fc309a1dd76503a7d275460c906571732ac69d513fc15a097998e15d417`. The model is recorded as `deepseek-flash`. The UTF-8 SHA-256 of its `text` value is `6350476770ae813c186d8e2e4b434f7481216f146e5a5f3db5689c982665e6c3`; the inspected corpus file hash is `0c906446277f8be74041e53b1a157033a527d5d2d1068b1850028b07f9ebed55`.

Paragraph references below are reader navigation only. They number the fifteen paragraphs separated by blank lines in the original text. They are not model-authored identifiers, compulsory semantic types or a new ontology imposed on C0. Quotations reproduce source fragments exactly. Interpretive remarks are this reviewer's proposed readings and remain contestable.

## The main organization of the source

The corpus starts with a reconstruction of a reservation snapshot, then develops ten families of disagreement and a closing claim about keeping false or incomplete proposals available. It repeatedly shifts among a reporting question, a decision-policy question and questions about the assumptions behind reconstruction. A carrier that compresses all those passages into one endorsed reservation algorithm would lose material the source deliberately keeps in play.

The paragraph “Now the disagreements begin.” is paragraph 4. The other paragraphs carry the substantive material mapped here.

## Dependencies and unresolved readings

The reconstruction in P2 depends on the per-booking supersession account in P1. Its later aggregation also depends on a reading of what an unresolved alternative permits. P2 leaves room for “none” under an unresolved meaning of truth; a formalization that assumes exactly one current version changes that part of the source unless it presents the assumption as its own proposed reading.

P3's extremes and P9's correlation objection raise different questions. Calculating extremes under a specified compatibility relation is different from claiming that the snapshot supplies that relation. The text itself asks whether possible-world semantics adds a notion not already contained in the bag. The source therefore contains both a reconstructive device and criticism of its source interpretation. Preserve both.

P8 says independent bookings interact because capacity is shared. That sentence does not itself distinguish dependence of the final arithmetic result on several bookings from restrictions on which booking alternatives can jointly occur. P9 raises the latter possibility through an unnamed physical resource. A carrier should be able to retain the possible distinction or the ambiguity. This map does not settle which independence claim the source intended.

P6 and P13 discuss nearly the same expiry conflict. P6 calls the structural-supersession reading better supported while preserving the rival. P13 says the description does not resolve the issue and then calls the structural reading safer. These need not be logically contradictory: better support is compatible with unresolved inquiry. They do differ in emphasis and degree of commitment. A renderer that summarizes them as either complete certainty or total equivalence would erase that difference.

P7 makes the target of criticism an explicit subject. The relevant target might be a record, a reconstruction rule, a history, the whole snapshot or the logical-time assumption. This is a concrete reason for a later language component capable of distinguishing targets **if the proposed scheme has such a component**. It is not permission to impose those targets as a mandatory closed menu before the initial proposal is examined.

P11's gap example and P14's bag-scope example both concern source assumptions, but they are distinct. A gap could reflect numbering conventions without any lost delivery; a union of replica views could be complete relative to that union while representing no single replica's state. Keeping them as one generic “missing evidence” token could lose what each criticism concerns.

The final paragraph contains a substantive commitment to retaining false or incomplete proposals and also the strong rhetorical conclusion that visible disagreement is the reconstruction's “only honest form.” Both belong to the source. The study can preserve and later criticize the latter without treating the paragraph as an external instruction that determines every result.

## A defensible pair for later joint construction

The primary pair is **P7's target-sensitive criticism conjecture** together with **P9's criticism of lost correlations in global bounds**. This pairing is justified by a specific unresolved relation already present in the source: if a reported availability range changes when compatibility between booking alternatives is questioned, what exactly has been criticized and what should remain available?

| Member | Exact source passage | Why it participates in the pair |
|---|---|---|
| A, P7 | “many apparent disagreements about availability are really disagreements about which target a criticism addresses.” | Proposes that criticism needs a target-sensitive account |
| A's supporting distinction, P7 | “one can reject a rule while accepting the data, or accept the rule while rejecting the data as corrupt or late.” | Makes preservation of unaffected material part of that account |
| B, P9 | “booking A's high alternative might be incompatible with booking B's high alternative if they share a physical resource not named in the snapshot.” | Raises a concrete possible objection to an aggregation assumption without supplying the missing evidence |
| B's unresolved background, P9 | “The snapshot does not name physical resources, so this incompatibility is invisible.” | Prevents treating the proposed incompatibility as an established observation |

Neither passage by itself states how a criticism of a compatibility assumption should affect a range calculation while retaining the underlying booking records and their local supersession relationships. That is a concrete space for a new relational construction. It goes beyond asking a model to repeat the highest-revision algorithm in another syntax.

A proposed new-use question is: **Construct an account of how a challenge to cross-booking compatibility should affect the global availability claim, the individual records and the unresolved alternatives, while distinguishing an established criticism from a conjectured missing constraint. What different observations or interpretations would direct criticism to a different target?**

The new artifact may propose a dependency organization, a distinction between kinds of change, a criticism of the question or a discriminating inquiry. It need not implement a solver or certify which warehouse history is true. It can also reject the pairing with a substantive reason if it finds that the apparent relation is ill posed. Its novelty and bearing remain subjects of later assessment.

## Protected content for this construction

The original A and B occurrences stay unchanged. A new artifact must preserve the difference between criticizing a rule and criticizing its data, the source's qualification that cross-booking incompatibility is only a possibility, and the fact that the physical resource is not supplied by the snapshot. It must keep disagreement over reporting versus decision policy available. Those are preservation of the source's content, not obligations to endorse each conjecture.

Any new compatibility relation is explicitly the new artifact's proposal. Installing it in an operative model is a separate later step. A revised global report does not automatically imply that a booking record was corrupt, nor does retaining records force retaining every aggregation assumption. Those are possible consequences to argue for, not built-in labels supplied by this review.

A concrete later contrast can keep the delivered bag fixed while varying a **declared hypothetical compatibility premise**. Another can keep that premise fixed while changing a particular record's claimed reliability. The point is to distinguish target and propagation of criticism. Neither contrast should be inserted into the first frozen expression study as if it were part of C0. Its status is a new experimental construction with its own identity.

The same selected source passages, existing criticisms, hypothetical premises and protected conditions must be supplied to the direct, native and Mini construction arms. A no-return condition can retain a proposed dependency revision without installing it, once an explicit update bridge exists. The current expression-and-reading study does not yet perform that integration.

## An alternative pair if the first proves uninformative

P6's distinction between revision authority and expiry effect can be paired with P14's question whether the bag is a union, intersection or one replica's view. The concrete new issue is whether the reconstruction's meaning is preserved when its bag and logical-time coordinate come from different replica views. This is a separate mechanism from cross-booking compatibility and should be a separate inquiry, not an unannounced extra change in the primary pair.

Use it only if the completed frozen studies expose an unresolved temporal or provenance distinction that the pair can investigate. Do not run both merely because the corpus contains enough passages to enumerate combinations. The [research agenda](../RESEARCH_AGENDA.md) requires a reason for a construction pair and preserves its inapplicability as a possible outcome.

## What this map establishes

The corpus supplies concrete material about uncertainty, criticism targets, source assumptions, alternative support and differences between inquiry and decision. It therefore supports a deliberately chosen later construction question. It does not establish that any initial language can express those distinctions, that any interpretation is adequate, or that a later construction will be creative.

The immediate use of this map is to make losses and added commitments inspectable against exact original prose. Its reader labels and proposed pair are separate review artifacts; they do not replace the model-origin corpus or become hidden instructions to the first language proposal.
