# 15g Supplement 6 — after the Atria half of the skill experiment

What this file is: the project-story additions after supplement 15f, verbatim. The report is file 26c; the per-reply notes are appended in 26b.

## Log entries

43. **The Atria half of the skill experiment (file 26c).** Atria's gateway
    fought the whole way: ninety-four attempts across three passes gave
    fifteen replies, and three of the eighteen items never came back at all
    (two under hard-to-vary, one under story-critique). Running one call at
    a time was cut just as often as running three, so it was not the
    crowding; whether the long skill text itself made the cuts more likely I
    cannot separate from the gateway's own bad hour, and I say so. On what
    did come back, the verdict of file 26 holds: neither skill incapacitated
    Atria, every reply followed its skill's full shape, and per item
    answered Atria found at least as many of the known defects as in the
    plain run, plus twenty-two more groups I ruled on. The best of them: the
    rule that ties an explanation's parts to the real mechanism only applies
    when the physics supplies a history, so without one the decoy the
    document itself worries about gets through; "being on the actual route"
    is a third sort of kind the theory's two-sort story does not name; three
    of the selection witness's anti-cheating clauses follow from a fourth
    and do nothing on their own; and a one-round staged contest satisfies
    the whole selection witness. Two false claims in fifteen replies, both
    under hard-to-vary (one a spliced quotation); none under story-critique,
    which refused four false candidates on the item built to catch it. The
    sharpest lesson: on the contract-adequacy defect, the same witness under
    the two methods reached opposite repairs (move the condition out;
    keep it in), each stating the other's cost. The method shapes the
    remedy, not the finding.
