**Note 6. ## 3. Pass 2 — Sequences, the chain of questions, and what fails to collect

**Note 8. 1. **\(K\) has no translation or meaning rule** (Note 8). It is the first-ranked item because it is the one place where the semantics is *undefined* at exactly the case it advertises as its hardest open one ("(i) elimination of a physically impossible rival ... handled only through a declared counterfactual set whose meaning is stipulated"). One clause in Part III, plus a line in Part XII, fixes it; part (iv) of Part 0's scope should then be re-read with that clause.
2. **Account is two-place only as a definition, and use plus restriction make the verdict witness-relative** (Note 9). It also softens Part 0's "**The first grade is what can be assessed within a question**": it can be assessed within a question only once the bundle is named.
3. **The orientation gloss in Part IV contradicts the formalism of (R)** (correction 1): "(R) ... _held to_ c by a transport ... \(t:c\to D\)" against the paragraph's own "**the carrier's organization \(D\) ... is held to the content \(c\) it represents by \(t:c\to D\)**", and against "**This one orientation is used everywhere**." The formalism is the self-consistent side (the bundle runs \(c\to D\); the gloss runs \(D\to c\)), so this is a sentence to rewrite, not an architecture to change — but it must be rewritten, because it sits *before* the reader meets (R).
4. **Surprise-entails-loss-of-Deploy versus the selection response** (Note 13), scoped as in the fix.
5. **Of the writer's list, the remaining items are detail or not faults:** the baseline exclusion is inverted in direction and discharged by (F1) (Note 5); the grain is a declared index doing its job (Note 4); the historical index agrees with Derivation 7 (Note 12); Derivation 10 is a mode conflict, not a membership contradiction (Note 6); the count is the document's own arithmetic (Note 10).
