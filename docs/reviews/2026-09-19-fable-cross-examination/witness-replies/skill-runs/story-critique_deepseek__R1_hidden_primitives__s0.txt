## Pass 2 — sequences and the chain of questions

I grouped the definitions into the document's own clusters (base schemata; roles/kinds; fidelity and account; anchoring; representation and provenance; build/deploy/origin; EK/Part XI; recursion/universality). **Note 11 — The main cycle: Build → repertoire → Deploy → (R) → Con → Build.**

- Build: "**Build_{β,ℓ}(s,c,h,e) holds when … contains a nontrivial binding construction required for that use, and is not a composition of content-preserving transfers.**"
- Build's nontriviality: "**a binding construction is nontrivial when the binding is neither a relabelling of a binding already in the repertoire nor obtainable from the incoming carriers by ≡_ℓ.**"
- Deploy defines the repertoire: "**The repertoire R_{β,ℓ}(s,ξ) is the set of contents deployable in some nontrivial use respect.**"
