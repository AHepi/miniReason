# Skill-run replies

Public reply texts from the skill experiment (files 26 and 26c): battery `battery_rev.json` (R1–R9) on file 20, with a method skill as the system prompt. File names are `<method>_<model>__<item>__s<sample>.txt`. `usage_summary_skill_runs.json` lists every record of every pass, including the calls that failed at the gateway (empty reply, first line of the error kept), with a `pass` field: DeepSeek ran one pass; Atria ran three passes for hard-to-vary (3, 2 and 1 workers) and two for story-critique (3 and 1 workers), each pass rerunning only the items still failed, into separate folders, so earlier failed records are preserved. The harness variant is `../cross_examine_skill.py` with the framing texts `../frame_htv.txt` and `../frame_sc.txt`; the rerun drivers are `../run_htv_atria_retry.sh` and `../run_final_reruns.sh` (keys from the environment only); the skills themselves are under `../method-skills/`. Hidden reasoning text is not published. Final coverage: DeepSeek 27 of 27 per skill; Atria 7 of 9 items under hard-to-vary (R3, R8 lost) and 8 of 9 under story-critique (R1 lost).
