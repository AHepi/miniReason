## Findings

**1. The elimination clause is logically redundant, yet is called "the difference-making condition."**
(1) Claim attacked: *"and which eliminated at least one member that differed from t at a pair of H on which that member failed and t did not; and t is a member that survived. That last clause is the difference-making condition, stated in the module's terms."*
(2) Why it fails: the preceding conjunct already records that "the members that survived were exactly the members faithful on H," and t survived. So t is faithful on H, every eliminated member is unfaithful on H, and hence every eliminated member already differs from t at a pair of H on which it failed and t did not. The clause's residual content is only "the population contained at least one member unfaithful on H." It does no difference-making work.
(3) Serious.
(4) Fix: rewrite as "the population contained a member unfaithful on H and the condition eliminated it," and delete the "difference-making" gloss; or drop the exact-coincidence conjunct (see 2), in which case the elimination clause becomes non-redundant and does real work.

**2. The coincidence fact is simultaneously a definitional conjunct of Sel and a "hypothesis" of Derivation 3.**
(1) Claim attacked: *"a survival condition ... of which the witness records the checkable fact that, in this population and in this history, the members that survived were exactly the members faithful on H (a fact about the run, not a definition of survival, and the hypothesis Derivation 3 uses)"*; and Derivation 3: *"in a run in which, as Part IV's witness records, the survivors were exactly the members faithful on H."*
(2) Why it fails: if the coincidence is a conjunct of Sel, then Derivation 3's hypothesis is a corollary of "t is selected," not a hypothesis, and the derivation's framing as a conditional result is misleading; if it is not a conjunct, then the elimination clause is not redundant (Finding 1) and Sel no longer implies fidelity on H. The parenthetical asserts both readings at once.
(3) Serious.
(4) Fix: remove the coincidence from Sel; keep it as Derivation 3's standing population hypothesis. Then fidelity-on-H is a fact about the run, Derivation 3 is a genuine conditional, and (R) supplies the fidelity requirement independently.

**3. Sel as written excludes ordinary natural selection, so the three provenances are not exhaustive.**
(1) Claim attacked: the coincidence conjunct, plus Part IV's *"Three provenances"* framing and *"Declared. neither witness exists."*
(2) Why it fails: real selection usually culls by fitness, not by transport-fidelity on H. Where the cull does not coincide exactly with fidelity-on-H, the resulting transport satisfies neither Sel (coincidence fails), nor Con (no construction witness), nor Dec (not author-entered). Provenance is undefined, (R) fails, and the primitive layer's tracking is not "selected." The document gives no fourth category and does not state the consequence.
(3) Serious.
(4) Fix: either state the scope explicitly ("selected" here means fidelity-tracking culls only) or weaken the coincidence to "eliminated at least one member unfaithful on H."

**4. "Non-empty and not chosen" is undefined and does not close the contract-relativity of H.**
(1) Claim attacked: *"the finite history H of all edit–boundary pairs within C_t — the contract on which t's content is claimed — that occurred as events of Σ_h, non-empty and not chosen."*
(2) Why it fails: since H is *all* occurred pairs within C_t, membership is already fixed, so "not chosen" must mean something else (no foresight? not modeller-post-selected?) and is never defined. And because C_t is a declared contract, H's content is a function of a modeller's declaration; this is the gerrymandering route the clause is meant to close.
(3) Serious.
(4) Fix: define "not chosen" (e.g., fixed by the module's process record before the survivor's occurrence), and require the witness to state C_t alongside the largest contract on which the content is claimed — the relation (R) already uses for unqualified representation.

**5. Blindness is indexed to a grain that is nowhere declared or fixed.**
(1) Claim attacked: *"lies on any active route, at the grain at which the route is described, to the event that produced t's occurrence"*; plus *"Provenance is thereby indexed to the grain of the route, as everything else is indexed to a grain."*
(2) Why it fails: Part IX's active routes carry no grain index, and Part XIV's declared parameters list a single grain ℓ for organization attribution — no route grain. A process can be blind at one admissible grain and sighted at another, and grains are declared by the modeller, so a partisan can declare a route grain that makes a sighted process blind. The admissibility constraint does not prevent this: it constrains an organization's components, not what counts as a defect-record occurrence.
(3) Serious.
(4) Fix: add a route-grain index to the witness, fixed by the module rather than declared; or require blindness at every admissible grain for the route.

**6. The aggregate exemption is gerrymanderable: magnitudes can leak state identity.**
(1) Claim attacked: *"an aggregate summary — a function of the multiset of failure magnitudes alone, with no access to which state failed, such as how many failures and how large — is explicitly permitted and does not make the process sighted."*
(2) Why it fails: "failure magnitude" is never defined or typed. If magnitudes are state-specific and distinct across states, the multiset, together with other information on the route, can identify the failing state; the process is then sighted in fact but blind by definition. Whether magnitudes are per-pair or per-component is also unstated.
(3) Serious.
(4) Fix: declare the magnitude function and require it to factor through a state-blind summary type; or state plainly that blindness is a structural condition about the record's *pairing*, not an information-theoretic one, and accept the leak as a stated limit.

**7. The distributed-record loophole: blindness quantifies over a single occurrence instantiating the pairing.**
(1) Claim attacked: *"an occurrence instantiating a record that pairs the state at which fidelity failed with the value the target took there."*
(2) Why it fails: if the failing state is carried by one occurrence and the target value by another, and the comparison is computed downstream, no single occurrence instantiates the pairing, so blindness holds although the route is sighted.
(3) Serious.
(4) Fix: quantify over routes jointly ("no active route contains occurrences that jointly determine the pairing"), not over single occurrences.

**8. Non-triviality is not well-typed as regards H.**
(1) Claim attacked: *"no event of Σ_h instantiates an organization held to t, to H, or to the survival condition by a faithful transport."*
(2) Why it fails: t is a transport and the survival condition is a condition, but H is a set of edit–boundary pairs, not an organization or content; "held to H" has no referent under the definition of content in Part IV.
(3) Minor.
(4) Fix: reify H as an organization (ports indexed by pairs of C_t, values the outcomes) and say so, or rewrite the clause to name that organization.

**9. The event domain of Σ_h is unstated, which determines whether ordinary selection passes non-triviality.**
(1) Claim attacked: the non-triviality clause, plus *"The population, the operator and the survival condition are the ones the module identifies as enacted in Σ_h before the survivor's occurrence: a witness describes events, it does not choose among descriptions."*
(2) Why it fails: if Σ_h includes the population members' internal processing, a tracker that models its task plausibly instantiates an organization held to the survival condition and non-triviality fails for ordinary selection. If Σ_h is population-level only, non-triviality is nearly vacuous. The text never says which events Σ_h contains, yet blindness requires routes (process occurrences) inside Σ_h.
(3) Serious.
(4) Fix: state the event domain of Σ_h explicitly — population-level variation and survival events, plus the process routes blindness quantifies over — and say whether members' internal processing is included.

**10. The separator's exclusivity rests on two unstated identifications.**
(1) Claim attacked: *"a selection witness is blind on it, and a construction witness has the itemised defect record used on it at the acceptance of the binding (Part X). ... One transport cannot satisfy both for the same route."*
(2) Why it fails: the universal (blindness: no record on any active route to the event that produced the occurrence) and the existential (Build: a record used on an active route to the acceptance) are contradictory *only if* "the event that produced the transport's occurrence" is the acceptance of the binding, and only at one fixed grain. Neither is argued. If the occurrence is produced at the conjecture rather than at acceptance, the two routes differ, both witnesses can be minimal, and a paradigmatic conjecture-then-criticize episode gets *mixed* rather than constructed provenance.
(3) Serious.
(4) Fix: state that the transport's occurrence is produced at the acceptance of the binding (the binding is what makes the organization held to the content), and index both clauses to the same route-grain.

**11. "Used at acceptance" is not well-defined; the boundary of the whole distinction rests on undefined terms.**
(1) Claim attacked: *"the record is used, in the sense of Part IX, in the criticism the binding survives before it is kept"*; and Part IX: *"A response uses a reason when a structural map from the represented objection into the response suborganization preserves role bindings, sends content-preserving recodings ... to the same transition, sends content changes to the changes specified by the operative deliberative rule, and lands on an active route."*
(2) Why it fails: "role bindings" and "the operative deliberative rule" are themselves listed in Part XIV as informal and undefined, and "lands on an active route" depends on "the declared contrasts," a declared parameter. So whether a record is used — and therefore whether a process is selecting or constructing — is not determined by the text as written.
(3) Serious (this is the load-bearing joint of the provenance distinction).
(4) Fix: define the operative deliberative rule as a declared rule type with an explicit sensitivity condition; or replace "used" with a structural condition in the document's own witness style — e.g., a map from the record's paired content into the acceptance event's organization that is nonconstant in the record's paired value.

**12. Build has no outcome-sensitivity requirement, so sighted selection can be rewritten as construction.**
(1) Claim attacked: *"the binding may be conjectured by any process, blind or not, and what makes the episode construction is that the record is used ... in the criticism the binding survives before it is kept."*
(2) Why it fails: an oracle-driven conjecture plus a correct-but-ignored accounting criticism satisfies Build as written. Bearing (K1) requires only that the criticism be an account of the defect question, not that the keeping depend on it; reason use's "nonconstant dependence" is dependence of the *route* on the *distinction*, not of the outcome. So the collapse attack runs in the reverse direction too: any record-carrying process with a pro-forma criticism counts as construction, which over-ascribes creativity rather than under-ascribing it. (Whether this is closed turns entirely on the undefined "operative deliberative rule" of Finding 11.)
(3) Serious.
(4) Fix: add a sensitivity conjunct: there is a variant of the record's paired value under which the binding would not have been kept (or the criticism's verdict would have differed) — a counterfactual witness in the style already used for non-circular dependence.

**13. "Required" is vacuous for novice systems and weak relative to capability.**
(1) Claim attacked: *"it is required for the transport when replacing it by any binding already carried by s or obtainable from the carriers fails (F1) or (F2) for that transport on c's contract,"* with the preceding *"so an empty prior repertoire does not make every binding nontrivial, because the carriers still count."*
(2) Why it fails: with an empty prior repertoire and carriers carrying no bindings, the universal is vacuously true and "nontrivial" reduces to "not a recoding of the carriers" — so any error-informed novel binding by a novice satisfies Build, and New is automatic for a novice. The quantification domain is *actual carried* bindings, not bindings realizable by the system, so a system that could trivially have used an alternative anchoring still satisfies "required." Build is therefore cheapest exactly where creativity is most claimable.
(3) Serious (it undercuts the sufficiency claim behind attack (A)).
(4) Fix: quantify over bindings realizable by the system's retained capabilities (Part XII's Can) or over bindings obtainable by the module-admitted variation operators for s.

**14. The mixed-provenance exclusion is asserted for (G) but (G) does not enforce it.**
(1) Claim attacked: *"If they are of both kinds, t has mixed provenance, which is declared as such and does not count as constructed for (G)"* against (G): *"Origin ≡ Attempt ∧ New ∧ Build."*
(2) Why it fails: Build is a property of the owned subhistory and never consults t's provenance; Con(t) is existential over an episode with a construction witness and can hold of a mixed transport; so (R), Deploy, New and Build can all hold for a mixed-provenance content and (G) holds. Worse, mixed provenance makes New *easier*, not harder. The prose and the formal clause disagree.
(3) Serious.
(4) Fix: add to (G) (or to Build) the conjunct that the ≺_h-minimal transports witnessing (R) for c on its contract are all constructed.

**15. The minimal-witness rule can yield no provenance at all.**
(1) Claim attacked: *"Among the witnesses whose events produced the occurrence that carries t, take those that are ≺_h-minimal (an event of a witness precedes an event of another)."*
(2) Why it fails: on the existential reading, the witness relation is a non-antisymmetric preorder: with W1 = {e1,e4}, W2 = {e2,e3} and e1 ≺ e2, e3 ≺ e4 (acyclic), each witness precedes the other, so neither is minimal and provenance is undefined. The quantifier over event pairs is never stated, and existence of minimal witnesses is never proved (Derivation 6 addresses the definition order, not this).
(3) Serious.
(4) Fix: define W1 ≺_w W2 iff every event of W1 precedes every event of W2 (or max W1 ≺ min W2), and prove minimal witnesses exist given a unique production event; or select the witness whose production event is earliest, which also makes the "later histories are maintenance" clause do the work directly.

**16. Intermediate processes (record-informed, non-accounting) have no provenance.**
(1) Claim attacked: the three-provenance taxonomy, plus *"A criticism occurrence can exist when (K1) fails. An adverse signal is not a criticism until an organization represents how it bears."*
(2) Why it fails: a reinforcement-learning process that shapes a binding from an error signal has a defect record on the route (Sel fails), no accounting criticism (Bearing fails, so Build fails), and is not author-declared (Dec fails). The resulting transport represents nothing under (R), and the gap is unremarked anywhere in Parts IV, X, or XV.
(3) Serious.
(4) Fix: add a fourth provenance ("tuned"/"maintained"), or relax Build so the record need not be used in an *accounting* criticism, and state which predicates such contents can satisfy.

**17. The final clause of Build has an unclear antecedent and is redundant.**
(1) Claim attacked: *"and is neither a content-preserving transfer nor a composition of them."*
(2) Why it fails: the subject is grammatically ambiguous (the record? the episode? the binding?); on the only coherent reading (the built transport/binding) it duplicates the non-triviality clause (recodings excluded) and New (copies excluded).
(3) Minor.
(4) Fix: attach the clause to the binding explicitly and either delete it or fold it into non-triviality.

## Direct answers

**Still gerrymanderable or vacuous?** Yes, in four places. (i) H is "all encountered pairs *within C_t*," and C_t is a declared contract — the anti-gerrymandering guard is the undefined phrase "not chosen" (Finding 4). (ii) The elimination clause is implied by the recorded coincidence, so the witness's apparent difference-making content is illusory (Finding 1). (iii) The aggregate exemption is defeatable whenever magnitudes are state-specific (Finding 6). (iv) "Required" is vacuous for novices and quantifies over actual rather than realizable bindings (Finding 13).

**Is the separator genuinely exclusive?** Formally yes, per route and per grain: blindness is a universal over active routes to the production event, Build is an existential over an active route to the acceptance, and these are contradictory *if* the production event is the acceptance and the grain is shared. Both identifications are unstated (Finding 10), the grain is undeclared (Finding 5), and the boundary is drawn by the undefined "used" (Finding 11). It is not exclusive per transport — mixed provenance is permitted — and the mixed case is mishandled (Finding 14).

**Is "used at acceptance" well-defined through Part IX's reason use?** No. Reason use depends on "role bindings" and "the operative deliberative rule," both listed as informal in Part XIV, and on "the declared contrasts," a declared parameter (Finding 11). Whether a rubber-stamp criticism counts as "used" is undecidable from the text, and Build has no independent outcome-sensitivity condition (Finding 12).

**Does the minimal-witness rule work?** Not as stated. The witness-precedence relation is under-quantified, non-antisymmetric, and can leave no minimal witness (Finding 15); and its declared effect — excluding mixed provenance from (G) — is not enforced by (G) (Finding 14).

**Can construction still be rewritten as selection, or vice versa?** Construction→selection is no longer trivially available: the rewriting must place the itemised record on a route to the production event, which violates blindness, and non-triviality's "survival condition" clause plus the module's identification of enacted conditions give a second line of defence. But that defence is only as strong as the grain index (Finding 5), the aggregate typing (Finding 6), the single-occurrence restriction (Finding 7), and the undefined "used" (Finding 11). Selection→construction is now *easier*: an oracle conjecture plus a pro-forma accounting criticism satisfies Build (Finding 12), and a novice's any-new-binding satisfies "required" (Finding 13). So the distinction is not robustly preserved in either direction.
