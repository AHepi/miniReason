## Referee report: experiment 2 (files 13, 14)

I am reporting as the external adversarial pass the document admits was never run (14 §10.18). Findings are ordered by what they attack, not by severity.

### A. The definition of "construction" is not implemented

**1. M6(ii) is a counterexample to the paper's own definition of construction.**
Design §1: "A **constructed** piece was produced by operating on a **representation of the defect** — which states, which predictions, what actually happened — and that representation lies on the route from failure to new machinery."
Results §6: with the outcome column *replaced by the prediction* — i.e., defect content deliberately removed — C "built `IF (n_i == n_j) AND (c_j != n_j) THEN reverse_i`, three times." So C produces machinery from records containing no defect whatsoever. The provenance semantics is therefore not faithfully realised by the code: "operates on defect records" ≠ "operates on defect content," and the code satisfies only the former. *Serious.* Fix: make "fixed" require old prediction ≠ outcome, re-run M6(ii) and M4, and restate the definition so that it is the *content*, not the carrier, that must be on the route.

**2. The fix-counting pathology contaminates the central test.**
Results §8: "With the outcome set equal to the prediction, 'new prediction equals outcome' is true whenever the rule does nothing." Results §10.3: "the stopping rule 'positive net' is fooled the same way (the scrambled run built three junk rules with small positive nets)." Since the scrambled run's rule set was generated by the same exploitable accounting, the headline S4 number (0.906 → 0.355) is a composite of "content destroyed" and "accounting exploited." The direction is safe (scrambled C ≈ A0, 0.355 vs 0.352), but the magnitude and the inference "C uses defect content" are confounded by a known bug. *Serious.* Fix: correct the accounting, re-run M4, and report whether the 0.1 threshold is met without the bug's help.

**3. "Never computes an aggregate fitness" is false as stated.**
Design §5: "**C never computes an aggregate fitness. Its only inputs are itemised records.**"; results abstract: "using no fitness number at all." C's condition score is |V∩c| − |N∩c| and its action score is fixes − breaks — both are aggregates over records. The real contrast is *per-step supervised labels* vs *per-candidate rollout returns*. *Minor, but it is the load-bearing rhetorical contrast of the paper.* Fix: say "C consumes itemised per-step labels; B consumes a scalar per candidate."

**4. The scramble's B-arm cannot fail and carries no evidence.**
Design §9 S4: "B's output under the scramble is byte-identical to B's without it." Results §5: "re-run with the scrambled records passed in as an argument its code never reads: byte-identical output." A deterministic program re-run with an unread argument is guaranteed byte-identical. This is a tautology presented as half of a discriminating test; all of S4's evidential weight rests on C's degradation. *Minor.* Fix: drop the B-arm, or make it informative by giving B a *fitness table* built from records and scrambling that.

**5. The operationalisation of "route" is satisfiable by any program that reads a file.**
Design §1: "**remove or scramble the defect representation and the constructing process breaks, while the selecting process — which never read it — is unaffected.** That is the operational content of 'the mapped objection is on an active dependency route.'" Differential ablation sensitivity is a property of *implementations*, and §1 concedes the output is the same. So the operational content is: two programs with different inputs depend on different inputs. The non-trivial part is only that the *pairing* of state to outcome matters (aggregate-preserving permutation), which is finding 2's good half. *Serious for interpretation.* Fix: restrict the claim to content-dependence under preserved marginals, and drop the philosophical vocabulary unless a contract is found where the two routes yield *different machinery*.

### B. "Needed" (S1) and the retired margin

**6. The closedness margin was moved after a number was seen, and S1 is evaluated against the revised criterion.**
Design §12.4: "As first written it applied a 0.1 margin to *any* G0 architecture on collision steps. The best G0 architecture on collision steps turned out to be the lookup table at 0.819 (hand-built rules: 0.906; ceiling: 0.923)… The check now has a structural part (slot-only G0 on collision steps) and an overall part (any G0, all steps)." Consequence: A0+C (0.906) beats best-G0-any (0.819) by **0.087**, which fails the original 0.1 margin; that margin was retired from the collision-step comparison and S1 is marked ✅ against a marginless inequality. *Serious.* The fair restatement, which is stronger and should have been used: on the *overall* check the lookup fails by 0.128 > 0.1 (0.707 vs 0.835), so extension was needed overall; on collision steps a memoriser recovers roughly 84% of the achievable gain (0.819→0.906 against a 0.923 ceiling), so pairwise structure is needed only for the last 16%; and it is definitely needed for *structured* machinery (0.366). Fix: restore a margin, report S1 as "needed overall and needed for structured machinery, partially needed at the sensory level."

**7. The memoriser is not a neutral baseline.**
Results §1: lookup(k=2) collision 0.819 but non-collision **0.681**, below A0's 0.702, and overall 0.707. So the only G0 competitor on collision steps *degrades* the base organism elsewhere; the comparison "0.906 > 0.819" is against an architecture that is not Pareto-competitive. This helps the paper but is not stated as such. *Minor.* Fix: report the Pareto framing explicitly.

### C. The crossing law / F3

**8. The "pre-registered expectation" (change 2) cannot be violated, so it is not a prediction.**
Design §12.2: "C, which builds from sensory defects, is **expected not to build the crossing rule**, because there are no defects from crossings." Since C's induction has zero signal from crossings, building it is impossible. Confirming a structurally unavoidable outcome is not evidence for Derivation 2. Results §4 then reports the 0.000 as "F3 ✓." *Serious.* Fix: test Derivation 2 where it can fail — make the invisible kind visible (add identity to sensation, or a downstream task that depends on it) and check that C *then* builds it while a contract-blind process does not.

**9. S3's "faithful component" is satisfied only after the success set was narrowed.**
Design §1(iii): "whether that extension is a faithful component with the world's kind-signature, not a patch." The world's collision law has two cases; C built one. M3 gives 0.000 on 221 crossing pairs for A0+C vs 1.000 for the hand-built pair. The threshold ≥0.9 was moved to non-crossing pairs by change 2. *Serious.* Fix: state S3 as "faithful on the visible sub-kind"; and note that the only architecture in the study that carries the world's full kind-signature was hand-built by the author, not produced by any process.

**10. The vacuity argument for B's junk atoms uses the wrong state space.**
Results §3: "`c_i == c_j` is never true — positions are distinct; the rest are almost never false." The rules read *slot* terms, not world positions, and A0's two slots are independent pass-through movers that can coincide. Vacuity must be established over the encountered slot-state distribution, not the world's distinctness condition. Empirically the junk never bit (B ≡ C to 16 digits), so the claim survives by data, not by the stated reason. *Minor.* Fix: justify vacuity from slot-state logs, or from the observed identity of predictions.

### D. Protocol

**11. Phase 2 ran despite a failed pre-registered gate.**
Design §11 step 5: "Phase 2 only if S1–S6 hold." Results §9: S6 is "⚠️" with "M6(ii) ✗." Phase 2 ran anyway, and the gate breach is nowhere flagged. *Serious.* Fix: re-run phase 2 after fixing C's accounting, or explicitly relabel phase 2 as exploratory and exclude it from the support count.

**12. Self-scoring is generous in both directions.**
Results abstract: "Phase 1 held on every supporting outcome but one clause." Actually S6 failed a sub-clause and S7 failed a clause (0.013 > 0.01) *plus* produced an un-pre-registered collapse. And in results §7, A0+phase-1+C's-rules scores **0.431** on the original world versus **0.636** for the hand-built wall rule — C's accumulated machinery is strictly *worse* than the hand-built equivalent, which the "any correct sticky rule collapses" argument does not explain (it predicts equality of collapse, not a 0.205 gap). *Serious.* Fix: mark S7 failed by its letter; attribute the extra gap to C's wrong action and patch, not to the design confound alone.

**13. The two substantive pre-freeze changes were responses to data, and hashing cannot undo that.**
Design §12 flags changes 2 and 4 for hostile probing; change 4 was made *after* the pilot produced 0.819. The frozen hash covers the revised design, including the revised success criterion. *Serious (integrity-level, though transparently reported).* Fix: keep both criteria in the outcomes table and report S1 against the original margin as well as the revised one — which is exactly the "both parts pass" claim in §12.4, except that the collision-step part passes only in its rewritten form.

### E. Measurement and statistics

**14. M3 conditions on a post-treatment variable.**
Design §7 M3 and results §4: "unbound = the predictor was not tracking both things exactly at step 2." Being bound at step 2 is *caused* by the rule, so the reported fractions are over treatment-selected, architecture-dependent denominators (A0: 99 unbound on same-target pairs; A0+C: 21). The headline "0.973 from 0.000" compares fractions over different sets. *Serious.* Fix: report the unconditional tracking fraction over all pairs with the unbound count alongside, and apply the 0.9 threshold to the unconditional figure. (This would likely *strengthen* the result, which makes the current presentation puzzling rather than self-serving.)

**15. No variance, no intervals, no multiplicity control.**
C is deterministic: the scramble, M5, M6(i)–(ii), M3 and the phase-1 fidelity are each n=1. Roughly twenty thresholds (0.1, 0.05, 0.01, 0.9) are evaluated against point estimates on a single world with no correction. *Minor-to-serious.* Fix: give binomial intervals on the key fractions (e.g., 0.906 over the collision-step predictions) and state whether the 0.013 S7 miss is inside noise.

**16. The ceiling is in-sample and the gap-to-ceiling comparisons are not like-for-like.**
Results §1: "Sensory ceiling (in-sample, for scale) 0.900 / 0.923 / 0.895," with collision-step ceiling (0.923) *above* the all-step ceiling (0.900), unexplained. All other figures are on the held-out half. *Minor.* Fix: compute the ceiling on the evaluation half, or mark every ceiling comparison as scale-only.

**17. M8's cost comparison uses incommensurable units.**
"Zero fitness evaluations" for C vs "1,720–1,788 full-history fitness evaluations" for B. C's 2,830+4,778 labelled records and 320 condition–action scorings are not zero work; B's rollout is not the same unit. *Minor.* Fix: report a common cost (world-step simulations, or wall-clock) for both.

**18. M5 is near-tautological, and M2 double-counts one mechanism.**
M5: order-invariance of a sum over records, and i↔j symmetry of the language under relabeling, guarantee an identical rule for a symmetric rule like `n_i == n_j`; it is a sanity check, not evidence for "content not carrier." M2's non-collision improvement (0.702→0.819) is the *same* causal pathway as the collision rule (post-collision binding), reported as a separate protected obligation. *Cosmetic.* Fix: downgrade M5's evidential role; merge M2 into the mechanism story.

**19. Round-2 arithmetic does not reconcile.**
Results §2: round 1 had 2,830 defect records; the chosen action "fixed 820, broke 0"; round 2: "1,266 defects remain." 2,830 − 820 = 2,010, not 1,266. The 744-record gap is presumably knock-on from `hold` on later steps, but it is unexplained, and "C correctly declines to invent one" is only as trustworthy as the accounting that finding 2 shows is exploitable. *Minor.* Fix: show the reconciliation, or define "remaining defects" as a fresh collection after the rule is appended.

### F. Design–world co-design

**20. The search space was engineered to contain the target, and the world was reshaped to fit the language.**
Design §1: "(ii) whether a **general-purpose** process operating on defect records builds the extension." Design §6.1: "Hand-build the two collision rules in the language… If the language cannot express the law, **revise**." Design §12.3: "The world is now defined as kinematics first, then the pairwise law on the kinematic next-states… world and language have the same shape." The term set {c, v, n, w} is the world's own state vocabulary; the world was redefined after 16% of collision events proved inexpressible; `swap_next_v` sits in the action set (§10.9). *Serious.* "General-purpose" is unsupported: the constructor was handed the world's ontology. Fix: either test C on a second world whose law is not used to design the language, or restate the claim as "a process operating on defect records *in a language co-designed with the world*."

**21. The task is easy for both processes, which bounds what the comparison can show.**
The target is a single atom (`n_i == n_j`) with a large fitness/defect gradient; B's six independent seeds all converge to the same law with identical fidelity to 16 digits. That C and B reach the same place is then unsurprising, and the design's own F2 ("C needs more rounds than K=3") — the regime where the routes might separate — did not occur. *Minor.* Fix: add a harder world (deeper conditions, K=3 required) as a pre-registered extension, or state that the two routes are indistinguishable on this task.

**22. C's one-step lookahead and arbitrary tie-break make phase 2's outcome a coin flip.**
Results §8: `stop_both + hold` and `swap_next_v + hold` tie at net 952; the tie goes to enumeration order. Failure modes 1 and 2 are exactly this. So "C accumulates, but with a guaranteed error" is a property of the constructor, not of the world. *Minor (already admitted).* Fix: break action ties by simulated two-step effect, or report phase 2 under both tie-breaks.

**23. Cosmetic.**
Results §11's "one world, one rule language, one constructor. Nothing about brains" is good scope discipline and I have no quarrel with it. §10.19 (API keys in a transcript) requires rotation before any citation; §10.18 (blocked adversarial review) means this report is the first external pass.

## The honest conclusion, and what resists it

**The deflation, and it is largely the document's own.** C is a greedy version-space learner over 50,176 rules, scored by per-step supervised labels; B is a mutation–selection search over the same 50,176 rules, scored by rollout returns. Both find the same rule. The scramble shows C's success depends on the correctness of the state→outcome pairings and that B cannot depend on them because it holds no labels. That is *exactly* "informed search and blind search differ in what they depend on." The paper concedes the rest itself: S5 is titled "provenance is not output," F1 declares the two "one kind on this contract," and the closing sentence of file 14 says the constructor "is a version-space learner with a small grammar, not a theory of conjecture." So the deflationary conclusion is not imposed from outside; it is the paper's own conclusion wearing different clothes. The overclaim lives in the intermediate vocabulary — "provenance," "construction," "the mapped objection is on an active dependency route" — which is operationalised as a data-flow dependency that findings 1–2 show is not even correctly implemented, and in the abstract's "same machinery, two routes, and the route is detectable by ablation," where the detection is half tautology (finding 4) and half confounded (finding 2).

Nothing about **creativity** follows: the target was placed in the search space by the expressibility check, the world was redefined to fit the language, and the term set is the world's own state vocabulary (finding 20). Nothing about **explanation** follows: the world's law has two cases and C built only the sensorially visible one; the sole architecture carrying the full kind-signature was hand-built by the author (finding 9). And the "route" difference has no output-level signature at all in this world — not in fidelity, and not even in M3, since B's junk-wrapped rule is not reported on M3 (a missing control for a claim about machinery rather than output).

**What resists the strongest version of the deflation ("it is all just curve-fitting"):**

1. **Cross-contract transfer of the built component.** C's training signal was sensory-defect records only. Its rule was then evaluated on *thing-identity persistence through collisions* (M3) — a strictly finer contract never present in its training data — and passed at 0.973 on held-out configurations. The lookup memoriser, by contrast, degrades non-collision fidelity below the base organism (0.681 vs 0.702). So the built rule is a composing component with the world's kind-structure, not a patch, and that distinction is measured, not assumed. This is a genuine generalisation claim, though it is a claim about *generalisation*, not about creativity.
2. **Content-dependence under preserved marginals.** The permutation keeps every state, keeps the defect count, and keeps every aggregate, yet drops C to the base organism's level (0.355 vs 0.352). A process depending only on marginals would be unmoved. That is a non-trivial dependency result — once the accounting bug is repaired and it survives the re-run (finding 2).
3. **M6(i)** — an empty defect set builds nothing. A genuine dependency check, and the one part of the "content, not carrier" claim that is currently clean.

None of these three reaches beyond "informed search depends on its labels, generalises, and does nothing without them." They are worth keeping; they do not license the word "construction."
