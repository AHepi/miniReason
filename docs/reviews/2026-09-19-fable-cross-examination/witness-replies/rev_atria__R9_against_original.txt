**1. [new] The "proper sub-block" witness contradicts the one-component concession that motivates the second grade.** Claim attacked: "The witness: there exists an edit τ(a) on E … that removes or replaces a nonempty **proper** sub-block B⊊Γ … Removing all of Γ changes the profile of any candidate and witnesses nothing." Fails against: "The one-component candidate whose single relation is Sol_D satisfies (E) on every contract and fails conditions 1 and 3"; "The one-component candidate that repackages Sol_D satisfies (E). It is an account at C and not anchored"; and Part XV: "A candidate meeting (E) alone and explaining nothing is already known to exist — the one-component repackaging of Sol_D — and is why the second grade is stated." If |Γ|=1 there is no nonempty proper sub-block, so NonCircular is false and (E) fails. More, every single-commitment candidate now fails (E), including the pole–shadow forward account of Part VII. Severity: serious (fatal for that concession; one-line fix). Fix: require B⊆Γ nonempty, and handle |Γ|=1 by adding "or its single component's relation is definable as Sol_D or a projection of it" (which is anchored condition 3).

**2. [new] Part VI's (S) is ill-typed under the new arity of Account.** Claim attacked: "Account(E,p) ⟺ ∃t,Γ Account(E,p,t,Γ)" together with "S_{E,p}={W⊆Γ:Account(E|W,p)}". Γ is free in the definition of a predicate subscripted only by (E,p), and because t and Γ are existentially quantified inside Account, membership in S records which deletions leave *some* transport and commitment set that works — not which of the fixed candidate's commitments are load-bearing. The finite monotone theorem survives as a theorem about that family, but its reading as criticality for 𝓔 does not follow, and Part VI's own claim that "the anchored grade's active-route condition uses it" is thereby weakened. Severity: serious. Fix: subscript S by (E,p,t,Γ), or take Γ as a parameter of Account.

**3. [new; replaces a 10 defect with another] The selection-side discrimination requirement is vacuous and the "no target instantiated" claim is not in Sel and is false of the paradigm case.** Claims attacked: "Non-triviality is a condition on the events of Σ_h: no event in the selection process is itself a faithful transport to t, to H, or to the survival condition"; and "What keeps the two apart: a selection witness contains no itemised representation of a defect and no target instantiated before the variation; a construction witness contains both." Fails three ways: (i) an event cannot *be* a transport, and transports' targets are organizations, so "a faithful transport to t, to H, or to the survival condition" is type-confused and the clause is satisfied trivially — 10's version ("No member of the history represents t, H, or the survival condition") was also vacuous, so the repair substitutes one vacuity for another; (ii) the "no target instantiated before the variation" half is not a conjunct of Sel; (iii) under the Orientation rule ("A transport from D to E is the relation by which E is held to D: D is what E answers to"), the target of the paradigm selected transport is F, and "P is held to F by a transport that is typically selected" with F instantiated throughout — so the claim is false of the file's own example. Severity: serious (this is the selected/constructed distinction creativity rests on). Fix: add to Sel an explicit clause that no occurrence in Σ_h instantiates the survivor's target organization before the variation is applied, and reword non-triviality as "no event carries an itemised representation of the target, the history, or the survival condition."
