**Finding 1 — Transported query is declared, not translated.**
- **Quoted passage:** Part IV: “When the transport serves a question, it carries a fifth datum, the **transported query** \(\mathcal Q_E\), an operation on \(E\) of the same type as \(\mathcal Q\), declared with the transport”; Part III: “What a question asks … is fixed by the type of \(\mathcal Q\) and the shape of \(C\), not by a label.”

## 14. Part VI’s finite monotone theorem is internally inconsistent

**Quote:** Part VI: “If \(\Gamma\) is finite, \(\mathsf S\) is upward closed, and \(\Gamma\in\mathsf S\), then the critical singletons are exactly \(\bigcup\min\mathsf S\) and the globally indispensable ones are exactly \(\bigcap\min\mathsf S\). *Proof.* … Deletion of \(d\) from \(\Gamma\) preserves support exactly when a minimal support omits \(d\).”

**Claim attacked:** The critical singletons for \(\Gamma\) are \(\bigcup\min\mathsf S\).

**Seriousness:** Serious.

**Fix:** State explicitly whether “critical singletons” are relative to \(\Gamma\) or to some support \(W\). If relative to \(\Gamma\), replace \(\bigcup\min\mathsf S\) with \(\bigcap\min\mathsf S\).
