## A. Transport orientation and the transported query

**1. "Agree with 𝒬 through the transport" is not what (A) checks.**
Part IV: "Ans_E(τ(a),σ(b))=𝒬_E(E,τ(a),σ(b)); no inverse of π is taken, and (A) is the requirement that the transported query agree with 𝒬 through the transport on C." But Part V's (A) is "Ans_E(τ(a),σ(b))=Ans_p(a,b)" — it contains τ and σ and no π at all. Agreement is between two declared operations (𝒬_E and 𝒬), not "through the transport"; π enters only via (F2). *Why it fails:* the sentence misdescribes the mechanism of its own central adequacy condition. *Serious* (it is the load-bearing link between orientation and question-fidelity, and as written a reader cannot tell whether π is doing work in (A)). *Fix:* either define 𝒬_E explicitly as the pushforward of 𝒬 along (π,τ,σ) — and state the injectivity/surjectivity needed for that to be well-defined — or say plainly that 𝒬_E is declared with the transport and that (A) is agreement of two declared schemata.

**2. 𝒬_E being "declared with the transport" makes question-fidelity partly stipulative, and nothing constrains it relative to (F2).**
Part IV: "it carries a fifth datum, the transported query 𝒬_E, an operation on E of the same type as 𝒬, declared with the transport". Since (F2) fixes Sol_E from Sol_D and π, but 𝒬_E is independently declarable, a candidate can satisfy (A) by fiat of the declaration whenever 𝒬_E is chosen to match Ans_p. *Why it fails:* the condition that is supposed to ensure the candidate *answers the question asked* is satisfied by a declaration, not by structure; the only structural backstop is (F2), which constrains solution sets, not which port the query reads. *Serious.* *Fix:* require 𝒬_E to be the operation induced on E by 𝒬, λ and the port translation, and prove (or assume) that (F1)+(F2) then entail (A), making (A) a corollary rather than an independent declared conjunct.

**3. The 5-tuple is never reconciled with the 4-tuple notation used everywhere downstream.**
Part IV introduces t=(π,τ,σ,λ) and then appends "a fifth datum"; Part V's ℰ=(E,p,t,Γ), Part VI's t|W ("t with its anchor map restricted to W"), and Derivation 2's "same anchor map" all treat t as the 4-tuple. *Why it fails:* it is undecidable from the text whether t|W also restricts 𝒬_E, and hence whether Account(E|W,p,t|W,W) is even well-typed. *Minor.* *Fix:* define the serving-a-question transport once as a 5-tuple and restate t|W accordingly.

## F. Selection witness versus Derivations 3 and 10

**14. Derivation 3's closure hypothesis is not shown to be compatible with the selection witness's applied-operator clause.**

Derivation 3 requires 𝒯 to contain, for every (a,b)∈C∖H, every component j, and every admissible L′, a member agreeing with (E,t) except at that one relation. *Why it fails:* the witness is a claim about the actual run's population, so the closure members must exist in the run; whether a single physically admitted μ generates that entire off-H closure is unargued. The result's scope ("selection provides no guarantee") is thus narrower than a reader might infer, and Derivation 10 immediately uses a population that is *not* closed ("If the simulation layer's population can only predict from occupancy … no member survives"). *Minor* (the document does scope it: "this derivation is silent"). *Fix:* state that the closure population need not be μ-generated, and that the result concerns population-level determination, not the witness's satisfiability.

**15. Grievance 9's blindness route differs from Part IV's.**

Part IV: "no itemised representation of a defect … lies on any active route, at the grain at which the route is described, to the event that produced t's occurrence." *Why it fails:* one clause quantifies over the feedback route (failures → variation), the other over routes to the production event; a process could satisfy one and not the other (e.g. a defect record on the production route but not on the feedback route). **17. The historical-kind witness is ill-typed.**

**18. The earliest-witness rule can misreport how a content was in fact obtained.**
