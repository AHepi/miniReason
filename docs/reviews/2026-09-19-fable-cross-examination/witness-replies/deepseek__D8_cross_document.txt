**Finding 2 — S1 does not test Part IV provenance or Build.**
**Claim attacked:** `"S1 needed — 0.906 > 0.819 (best G0, a memoriser) > 0.366 (best structured G0)."`
**Why it fails:** The intended target is presumably Part IV’s three provenances and/or Part X’s Build. It does not supply the population, variation operator, history, survival condition, represented target, or construction witness. A performance advantage is not a provenance fact.
**Severity:** Serious.
**Fix:** Provide a formal `Sel(t;T,μ,H)` witness for the baseline and a formal `Con(t;h,e)`/Build witness for C’s rule, then verify `Account` and (F1)/(F2).

**Finding 3 — S2 is not a Repair instance.**
**Claim attacked:** `"S2 protected — non-collision 0.819 ≥ 0.702."`
**Why it fails:** File 10’s Repair is: `"Repair_{O,P}(ξ,ξ';Δ) iff ∃o∈O[¬o(ξ)∧o(ξ')]∧∀r∈P[r(ξ)⇒r(ξ')]∧ProducedBy(Δ,ξ,ξ';O)."` The experiment never states \(O\), \(P\), \(\xi\), \(\xi'\), \(\Delta\), or `ProducedBy`. Non-collision accuracy is at best a proxy for a protected obligation, not the obligation.
**Severity:** Serious.
**Fix:** State the obligations and protected set, show the old obligation failed, show the new one holds, show every protected obligation preserved, and show the change was `ProducedBy(Δ,ξ,ξ';O)`.

**Finding 4 — S3 is not a fidelity or signature test.**
**Claim attacked:** `"S3 faithful — 0.974 and 0.973 given bound on non-crossing pairs (threshold 0.9), from 0.000 for A0. Crossing pairs 0.000, as pre-registered under F3."`
**Why it fails:** File 10 (F1) requires, for every active component \(k\), every anchor \(\lambda(k)\), and every \((a,b)\in C\): `"the relation obtained by imposing the constraints of \(\lambda(k)\) and projecting away its hidden ports equals the component relation of \(k\) under the translated edit."` The experiment computes a scalar “given bound” fraction. It never identifies \(E\), active components, anchors \(\lambda(k)\), hidden ports, translated edits \(\tau(a),\sigma(b)\), or the relations \(L_k\). “Thing-signature” is not the signature set `sig_C(j)={(a,b,L_j(a,b)):(a,b)∈C}`. It is a tracking statistic.
**Severity:** Serious.
**Fix:** Specify \(D,E,t=(\pi,\tau,\sigma,\lambda),C,\Gamma\); verify (F1) and (F2) pointwise; compute signatures `sig_C(j)`; separate the sensory contract from the finer M3 contract.

**Finding 5 — S3’s crossing failure contradicts any unqualified “faithful” claim.**

**Why it fails:** If the M3 contract contains crossing edits, then C’s transport fails (F1) on those edits. The results treat the zero as expected because C’s sensory contract cannot see crossings. **Severity:** Serious.

**Finding 6 — S4 is not the Part IX reason-use witness.**
**Claim attacked:** `"S4 on the route — scrambled C worse by 0.55 (threshold 0.1); B byte-identical."`

## Bottom line

The construction experiment tests predictive accuracy and ablation of two algorithms in a toy world. It does not instantiate the formal predicates of file 10. S1, S2, S3, S4, and S6 are loose analogies at best. S5 only shows output equality, which is consistent with Part IV’s claim that provenance is not output, but it does not test provenance. The M6(ii) failure is not a minor diagnostic; it shows the S6 operationalisation does not enforce content or nontrivial binding construction.
