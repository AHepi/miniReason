## §2 Findings, ranked

### F1 — Part XV (B)(i) contradicts Part VII on whether (F2) constrains symmetry explanation. **Serious**

**(1) Claim attacked.** Part XV's sorting of the symmetry case: *"explanation by symmetry, whose force is an entailment among the components of \(E\) that neither (F1), a per-component condition, nor (F2), an assembly condition, constrains; no construction exists here and none is known to follow from present machinery."*

**(2) Why it fails.** Part VII now says the opposite: *"(F2), which constrains the assembled solution set, carries some inter-component entailments, and whether it carries this one depends on the edit set, which is the door and is not walked through here."* One part of 24 asserts (F2) does not constrain the entailment; another asserts it may. 23 §3 ruled this partly valid and explicitly required "Part XV should say so". Part VII says it; Part XV denies it. The four-case sort in Part 0 (B) inherits the contradiction, since it sends the reader to Part XV for the sorting.

**(3) Serious.** This is a substantive inconsistency about the status of one of the four open cases under the necessity attack, in the document's own map of its defeats.

**(4) Fix.** Rewrite Part XV (B)(i) to match Part VII: (F1) is per-component and does not constrain it; (F2) carries some inter-component entailments and whether it carries this one depends on the edit set; the door is identified and not walked through.

### F2 — The artefact case is declared moved to attack (D) but is still listed under attack (B). **Serious**

**(1) Claim attacked.** Part 0 (B): *"explanation of an effect as a selection artefact is a missing condition rather than a missing transport and is listed under (D)"*; Part VII: *"and it is listed under attack (D)."*

**(2) Why it fails.** Part XV (D) contains no artefact clause — it offers only *"a selection history can guarantee fidelity off the history … or that construction reduces to selection … or that the layers described in Part IV are not what explanation operates on."* The artefact case is still at Part XV (B)(ii): *"(ii) closable, not done — explanation of an apparent effect as a selection artefact: what is missing is not a second target but a provenance clause on the target's own components …; the construction is not written."* So two sentences in 24 are false, and the misclassification 23 §3 ruled valid (a genesis/condition case sitting under the necessity attack) survives in the very place it was supposed to leave. The ledger marks item 12 Done.

**(3) Serious** as a false self-reference plus an unimplemented re-sort; the substantive theory is untouched.

**(4) Fix.** Either move the bullet into (D) as a fourth disjunct ("or that the semantics lacks a condition on the provenance of the target's own components"), or change Part 0 (B) and Part VII to say it remains under (B)(ii) as closable, with the missing item a condition rather than a transport.

### F3 — The translation to \(C_{\mathrm{adm}}\setminus C\) is named, not defined, and conflicts with τ's declared totality. **Serious**

**(1) Claim attacked.** Part V, objectivity: *"\(C\) is protective when there is an admitted pair \((a,b)\in C_{\mathrm{adm}}\setminus C\) that touches some anchor of an active component of \(E\) … and on which the target's answer is defined and the candidate's transported answer differs from it: \(\operatorname{Ans}_E(\tau(a),\sigma(b))\neq\operatorname{Ans}_p(a,b)\), the transport being extended to such pairs by \(\lambda\) (the edit acts on an anchored component, so its translation is the corresponding edit on the anchored component of \(E\))."*

**(2) Why it fails.** The extension mechanism does not exist. \(\lambda\) assigns each component of \(E\) a subnetwork of \(D\) — it runs from \(E\) to \(D\), so it cannot map an edit of \(D\) to an edit of \(E\); and "the corresponding edit on the anchored component of \(E\)" is not a function of \((a,\lambda)\), nor is \(A_E\) required to contain such an edit. Meanwhile Part IV declares \(\tau:A_D\to A_E\) with no scope qualifier — total — which makes the parenthetical unnecessary and makes the old "undefined translation" loophole impossible by typing. World-kind condition 3 then evaluates \(L_k(\tau(a),\sigma(b))\) over all of \(C_{\mathrm{adm}}\), presupposing exactly the totality the objectivity section says needs extending. So 24 is self-inconsistent about τ: if τ is total, the parenthetical is false; if τ is partial, condition 3 and the objectivity condition are ill-defined off \(C\). This is the load-bearing piece of the item-3/item-23 repair, and the silence fix only closes the λ route, not the translation route.

**(3) Serious.** The objectivity claim and the anchored grade's third condition both quantify over \(C_{\mathrm{adm}}\) using a translation whose status is ambiguous.

**(4) Fix.** Declare τ total on \(A_D\) (and σ: \(B_D\to B_E\)), delete the parenthetical, and state that silence arises only through λ, never through τ, with \(\mathcal Q_E\) required defined on all of \(A_E\times B_E\). If partial transports are intended, add a well-formedness condition on transports: for every admitted pair touching an anchor, \(A_E\) must contain the edit induced on the anchored component, and τ maps to it — stated as a requirement, not a parenthetical.

### F4 — Part X's mirror adjudication collides with Part 0 (A) and Part XV over the Origin of the target's copy. **Serious**

**(1) Claim attacked.** Part X, Recoding: *"So the target's own faithful copy, built from error records, is an anchored, originated account that is \(\equiv_\ell\) to the target: New fails for it if the target is already in the repertoire, and holds if it is not — in which case building the target's mirror from its errors is discovering it, and the semantics says so without embarrassment."*

**(2) Why it fails.** Part 0 (A) says: *"The target's own copy and the faithful modular table satisfy the anchored grade and fail Origin, and the semantics says so rather than hiding it."* Part XV says: *"Candidates meeting the anchored grade and explaining nothing are already known — the target's own copy, the faithful modular table — and the class does not claim they explain: they fail Origin."* Part X's object is "the target's own faithful copy, built from error records" and it is *originated*; Part 0/Part XV's object is "the target's own copy" and it *fails Origin*. The phrases are not distinguishable, the verdicts are opposite, and the disambiguation is load-bearing: attack (A) asks for a candidate that is anchored **and** originated and explains nothing, and Part XV's list is meant to exclude such candidates. If the built mirror with the target absent from the repertoire is included in "the target's own copy", Part 0 (A) and Part XV are false. Part V's own object is different again — *"The target itself, \(E=D\) under the identity transport, is an anchored account of \(D\)"* — which is unbuilt and does fail Build.

**(3) Serious.** The collision sits inside the sufficiency attack and the document's statement of its own position.

**(4) Fix.** Name the two objects apart in Part 0 (A) and Part XV — "the identity copy (unbuilt)" vs "the built mirror" — and add: a built mirror satisfies Origin iff it is new to the repertoire; if it is, the semantics counts it discovery, not a sufficiency counterexample.

### F6 — (K1) uses a definite description over a relation the document says is not a function. **Minor**

### F7 — Mixed provenance is invisible to (R), and 24 does not say so. **Minor**

**(1) Claim attacked.** Part IV: *"If they are of both kinds, \(t\) has **mixed** provenance, which is declared as such and does not count as constructed for (G)."* And (R): *"…a transport faithful on \(c\)'s contract whose provenance is selected or constructed."*

**(2) Why it fails.** Mixed is neither selected nor constructed, so a mixed-provenance transport witnesses (R) nowhere. An occurrence whose only representing transports are mixed represents nothing under (R), and Deploy and (EK) fail with it. 24 states only the (G) consequence. The "Three provenances" heading is then also one short: four statuses are introduced (selected, constructed, mixed, declared).

**(3) Minor.**

**(4) Fix.** Add one clause: mixed provenance does not witness (R) either, and say whether that is intended or an omission; or allow mixed to witness (R) while remaining barred from (G).

### F8 — "the largest contract on which some witnessing transport is faithful" need not exist. **Minor**

**(1) Claim attacked.** Part IV, after (R): *"what it represents without qualification is \(c\) on the largest contract on which some witnessing transport is faithful."*

**(2) Why it fails.** (F1) and (F2) are pointwise in \((a,b)\) (plus the contract-independent homomorphism conditions on τ), so per-transport maxima exist. But the phrase says *some* witnessing transport: if \(t_1\) is faithful on \(C_1\) and \(t_2\) on \(C_2\), no single transport need be faithful on \(C_1\cup C_2\), and maxima may be incomparable. Item 25 promoted this from a consequence in 22 to a definition in (R), so the gap is now load-bearing in the definition Deploy and (EK) rest on.

**(3) Minor.**

**(4) Fix.** Define unqualified representation by the per-transport largest contract, or by the family of contracts on which some transport is faithful, without asserting a maximum.

### F9 — (A) now rests on a declared query rather than a structurally transported one. **Minor**

**(1) Claim attacked.** Part IV: *"the transported query \(\mathcal Q_E\), an operation on \(E\) of the same type as \(\mathcal Q\), declared with the transport"*; and *"(A) is the requirement that the transported query agree with \(\mathcal Q\) through the transport on \(C\)."*

**(2) Why it fails.** 22 defined \(\mathcal Q_E\) structurally (\(\mathcal Q\) on \(\pi^{-1}[\operatorname{Sol}_E]\) and the λ-anchored components), which had the injectivity defect 2.9 flagged; 24's fix removes the structure and leaves \(\mathcal Q_E\) as a datum whose only constraint is (A) itself. So (A) is satisfiable by declaring a \(\mathcal Q_E\) that returns the target's answers, and "the query the transport carries" is declared rather than transported. Non-circular dependence (which requires the answer profile to move when a sub-block is removed) and the extensional stance contain this, but 23 asked for agreement "through π", and "through the transport" is (A), not a structural condition.

**(3) Minor.**

**(4) Fix.** Require \(\mathcal Q_E\) to be given by a fixed rule from \(E\)'s solutions and component structure in the manner \(\mathcal Q\) is from \(D\)'s, and note that (F2) is what makes the comparison well-posed; or state plainly that (A) is extensional and that (F1)/(F2)/non-circularity do the work.
