# Word list

This file matches the plain words used in this skill to the terms of the source theory, so that anyone can trace a step of the skill back to where it came from.

## Where the sources are

- **The source theory:** "Claude Fable Semantics - a structural class of explanatory creativity, with selected and constructed correspondence". Supplied by the user as the file `10_Claude_Fable_Semantics_-_standalone_theory.md`. It is a frozen source: this skill reads from it and does not change it. Part numbers below refer to that file.
- **The original idea:** "hard to vary" as the mark of a good explanation comes from David Deutsch, *The Beginning of Infinity* (2011), and his 2009 talk on explaining explanation. The seasons example is his. It is retold here in fresh words.

## Traps

- The source theory is formal and this skill is not. If the two seem to disagree, the source is the authority on what was meant; say so and check it.
- Several source ideas are deliberately left out because they are not needed for this test: the physical module, capability, recursion and universality (Parts XII and XIII), and the definitions of newness and origin (Part X). Do not assume the skill covers them.
- The source marks its own weak points (Part 0, "Where to attack this", and Part XV). The treatment of absences is one. Pass that caution on when it applies.
