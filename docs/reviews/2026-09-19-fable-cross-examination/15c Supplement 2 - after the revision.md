# 15c Supplement 2 — after the revision of the semantics

What this file is: the parts of the project story written after supplement 15b, verbatim, so the published story stays unchanged. The revision itself is file 20; the mapping from the revision list to its sections is file 20a.

## Where things stand

The theory has been revised. File 20 is the semantics rewritten against the
whole consolidated list from the cross-examination, still presented as a
standalone theory with no history in it; file 20a says where each item of
the list landed and what was not done. The main changes: the circular
definition is broken by requiring a construction to work on a target that is
physically instantiated, not "represented"; the hidden decomposition oracle
is named as part of the physical module's package; kinds come in two sorts,
forward (how a part responds to changes) and historical (how it came to be);
adequacy comes in two grades, an account at a contract and an anchored
account whose parts match the world's under every physically admitted
change with a proper decomposition; "selected" now requires that selection
eliminated something; a contract must not be drawn to hide the embarrassing
change; two derivations are downgraded (one to a conjecture, one to a
remark) and the two-layer story is downgraded from a witness to a scenario.
Four things the theory cannot represent are listed as open: explanation by
symmetry, explanation of an artefact, inventing a new dimension of
variation, and mathematical explanation under a declared admission
relation. File 20 has not been cross-examined. The experiments' headlines
remain withdrawn as recorded in files 16 and 17. The repository branch
carries everything as draft pull request #1.
