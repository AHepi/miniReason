# FW5 versus harness spec v1.3: does the spec help or hinder the programme?

Review prepared for root. FW5 is the designated semantic source; harness spec
v1.3 is a mechanism reference (`docs/SEMANTIC_GUIDE.md:13`). Six readers
produced 39 findings; six refuters checked every citation at source, and every
FW5 line cited here was re-read at the line. No scalar scores; no claim about
creativity or model merit; the conclusions are root's. **Measured** marks what
is readable off a file or countable from the frozen H005 tree.
**Interpretation** marks a reading that could be wrong.

## 1. What FW5 requires of any study of explanatory construction

**R1 — Adequacy is a property of a construction, not of an approval event.**
`Account(𝓔)` over `𝓔=(E,p,π,τ,σ,λ)` conjoins anchoring, fidelity, question
fidelity, non-circular dependence and non-vacuity (FW5:167-170, :217-224); it
"contains no predicate that already means 'really explains'" (:226); "No
separate approval event creates an account" (:244).

**R2 — A criticism is four-part, and bearing is itself an account.** Target,
defect, grounds, connection (:609); `Bearing(c,z,p) ⟺ Account(𝓔_c, p_δ)`
(:614-620); an occurrence can exist when (K1) is false, and "A mere adverse
signal is not made into a criticism by giving it a negative label" (:622).

**R3 — Reason use is causal organization under a three-case contrast
contract.** The witness "must preserve internal role bindings, not merely the
endpoint string" (:628), on an active route with nonconstant dependence on the
represented distinction (:601); the contract needs a content-changing case, a
content-preserving recoding, and a carrier disturbance held apart from a
change in the objection (:630); absent data leaves the attribution
"unresolved" (:634).

**R4 — Standing is enacted, appraisal-indexed and violable.** `Usable_j(u)` is
indexed to an appraisal and an application (:638, :642-651) and the actor can
violate it (:653); prompt appearance is a delivery fact, while actual use is
"not automatically machine-maintainable" (:640).

**R5 — Elimination is not falsity; routes stay distinct; non-evaluability is
not refutation** (:657, :682, :688).

**R6 — A failed test contradicts a conjunction.** From `T∧B∧I ⟹ O` and `¬O`,
only `¬(T∧B∧I)`, "not ¬T without additional premises about B and I"
(:663-676); a passing execution immunises neither specification nor relevance
(:661).

**R7 — Support is a family, not a conjunction.** `S_{E,p}` has no upward
closure and no assumed minimal member, and there can be a support with no
singleton deletion witness (:254-267, :350).

**R8 — Progress is repair against a fixed protected set, with losses
exposed.** (P) needs a failed obligation discharged, every protected
obligation preserved, and `ProducedBy`, "not satisfied by temporal succession
alone" (:787-800); "Losses outside P must be exposed" (:802); progress can be
net withdrawal (:810).

**R9 — No count is an automatic warrant; reach needs a stated anchor.** Hard-
to-vary is "not a numerical warrant" (:381); no merit predicate exists (:849);
no quantity enters (G), (P) or (EK) "as an automatic warrant", though counts
"are not outlawed as information" (:851); reach needs a core participating
"through a stated anchor" (:383).

**R10 — Scrutiny must reach the operative target; endpoints cannot fix the
accounting predicate.** A result "must be able to affect the operative target"
(:63), with a path to the parent's use, method, interpretation or attention
state (:1052). No function of an input–output projection agrees with the
accounting predicate across models differing in active route (:1218),
including "semantic use inferred from delivery logs" (:1222) — though the
theorem "identifies missing information in a projection" rather than closing
the question (:1224). Equivariance holds only for genuine recodings (:154,
:1202-1206).

## 2. Where the spec helps

**Recomputed closures give R10 a mechanism (CA-8, intact).** Measured: the
three §1 closures are "computed, not curated" (spec:107-114); `theory(id)` is
deterministic (spec:328); `why` prints the chain (spec:497). Interpretation:
the one thing v1.3 gives FW5 that FW5 lacks — a non-editorial record of *why*
a criticism stopped counting, which is R10's operative reach and R5's "must
not silently lose its actual dependencies" (FW5:690). Qualification: operative
consequences are gated on labels (spec:181, 251, 269, 280).

**Refl plus N1 make the assessment apparatus attackable (EC-6).** Measured:
adjudication semantics, standards and guard procedures are registered
artifacts (spec:173); nothing "is ever marked final" (spec:233); refuting a
standard collapses every ν citing it in pass 1 (spec:108) — R10's first clause
satisfied structurally. Struck: the finding imported FW5:628 into FW5:1052's
return condition, which admits "attention state".

**The validity node mechanises R6 (EC-7).** Measured: spec:103 parks soundness
and relevance in an attackable node; §7's ν enumerates four auxiliary claims
in advance (spec:307); "a bare verdict is never an edge" (spec:115) is R2's
refusal of the adverse-signal-plus-label. Corrected: the spec does propose
that B or I may be at fault (§3:183, spec:389, §11.3/§11.4); the residual is
that this machinery is windowed while spec:179 charges T per verdict.

**Budget honesty and oracle isolation state R5's last clause (XD-7).**
Measured: `overrun` means "unobtainable within the declared deterministic
budget… never 'the machine was slow'" (spec:79) and a containment kill "MUST
NOT mint a warrant" (spec:80-88).

**The judge-audit triple reconstructs R3 (LI-3, XD-3).** Measured: paraphrase
invariance and premise-deletion sensitivity (spec:383-384) are the recoding
and content-change legs; "verbosity pairs (same content, padded vs. terse)"
(spec:386) is the carrier-disturbance leg. Interpretation: all three legs
exist, aimed at the judge, none at the responder, where R3 defines reason use.

**Three more, rescued by the refuters.** Pareto's partial order — "Never a
status; an artifact off the frontier is merely unfunded" (spec:469) — is the
refusal of common currency FW5:804 demands (MC-05); spec:226's "orphaned ≠
false" is K2 plus FW5:657, mechanised (CU-3 refuted); and §10.1's forbidden
cases are conditional commitments over unperformed cases (spec:366, :393),
matching FW5:208 (LI-2).

## 3. Where the spec hinders or is silent

### 3.1 Use, uptake and repair versus attack and support

Measured: v1.3 has two adjudicative relations, `att` and `dep` (spec:229), one
inert ref role, `mention` (spec:62), no relation meaning "a later organization
took this content up", no `Repair` transition, and — by full-text search — no
protected-obligation vocabulary and no loss register (CA-7, EC-8, XD-4).

Interpretation: the abstention is FW5-conformant, since R4 denies that use is
machine-maintainable; the defect is that nothing is *recorded* either, and the
programme has read the gap backwards. Measured: `docs/reviews/engine-path-
decision-2026-09-14.md:27-31` calls the missing machinery "no mechanical
notion of a criticism *landing*: no att edge, no warrant, no label, nothing
that distinguishes a criticism taken up from one written down beside the thing
it criticises." Under R3 and R10 an adverse edge is neither necessary nor
sufficient for uptake, and one cycle shows both failures: `response` record
k1 ("The objection is right that…") produces no edge, while the import
reinstates `A_rival` only because `A_carry` attacked `A_response` (CU-1).

Measured, on `daily/mini_fcl/cycle01`: 42 records (claim 15, objection 9, use
9, problem 5, commitment 4); 24 `target` refs across 14 records; 17 `depends`
across 11; `grounds` and `bearing` on 9 each; `revises` and `withdraws`
unused. Declared uptake per document: 5/6, 6/8, 9/9, 8/8, 4/11.

Measured: v1.3 has no sub-artifact address. Interpretation (CU-4, re-scoped):
record-grain artifacts are not forbidden, since content is Σ* (spec:31), but
the absence forces an all-or-nothing granularity election, and the repo's
node-grain election discarded the represented target FW5:609 names —
`response#k7`, which cites `rival#r9` as conceding the point it concedes about
its own k2, landed as a one-directional attack on `A_rival`. R8 has no
counterpart: the carry document's FW5:810 case gets the label a bare assertion
gets.

### 3.2 Status is not an FW5 quantity

Measured: §4 computes one label per artifact id from `att` and `dep`
(spec:206-229). FW5's nearest objects are appraisal-indexed `Usable_j(u)`
(:642-651), receipt sets that "are descriptions of available arguments, not
four kinds of reality" (:684), and engagement decisions that do not prove a
problem solved (:777). Interpretation (CA-1): the label carries no application
and no respect index, and §4's normative text never says what its labels are —
spec:410 and §17:578 say it, in other sections. (CA-2): `accepted` and
`Account(𝓔)` are logically independent both ways, since R1 makes adequacy
prior to appraisal. Struck: that a computed fixpoint cannot represent an
appraisal error (spec:173, :233); that labels masquerade as ground truth
(spec:32); and the schema limb, since §0:30 and §10.1 make an account
declaration a content convention.

Two granularity defects survive. `dep` is a flat conjunction (spec:219), so
R7's distinct sufficient routes cannot be declared and refuting one route's
premise suspends a conclusion an independent route still supports (CA-3,
EC-3). And ν bundles soundness and relevance (spec:103), so a criticism cannot
land on bearing alone, which R2 requires (CA-4, downgraded to "hinders on
granularity": grounds *are* carried, by spec:115, :102, :199-204).

### 3.3 Measures

The strongest charge failed (CA-5, MC-02, XD-6, refuted): §0:33 names the
commitment route as licensed, `ŝ` is not a §4 input, FW5:851 forbids only
*automatic* warrants, the `hv-floor` warrant carries four attackable ν clauses
(spec:303, :307), and §17:580 states the spot-check limit first. What survives
is narrow: §4's single `refuted` label puts an eight-sample estimate and a
program refutation in the same state; `HV_MIN` is a bare config constant
(spec:541) whose margin carries no account; and µ's identity is not content-
addressed although k, `HV_MIN` and B₀ are (spec:301), so the variation family
FW5:358 requires to be *fixed* is frozen only post hoc.

The load-bearing defect is upstream. Measured: `≈_B` is extensional and sits
under `mod` (spec:245), `s(a)` (:255), `hv-floor` (:304) and the registration
gate (:193). Interpretation (MC-08, sharpened): not extensionality as such —
FW5:232 admits a table encoding the organization under its interventions — but
that battery adequacy is declared in a ν on the *warrant* path (spec:303) and
unaccounted on the *gate* path. A stage-3 block yields no artifact, hence no ν
and no attack surface, so N1's "every status admits an exit" has nothing to
apply to; FW5's parallel/priority pair (:1402) is the fixture whenever the
battery is endpoint-only. Two smaller items: §6:259's "a rename is the same
explanation" asserts content identity from a verdict projection, denied by
FW5:144 (EC-4); and the spec's reach omits R9's anchor (MC-04, XD-1).

### 3.4 Language and carrier

Interpretation (LI-7, re-aimed): §8:330 is scoped "for skeleton-codec
artifacts", and for `codec:utf8` the prose *is* the content (spec:31), so the
collision with FW5:69/:857 is not the view rule but §10.1's pinning of
`skeleton-wf` on every informal problem — an admission form every informal
contribution must complete. `docs/PROBLEM_PROMOTION.md:7` bundles the two and
should separate them. (LI-6): FCL-1's `depends`/`mentions` distinction is R4's
separation, but the engine's reduction maps `depends` straight to a `dep`
edge, promoting a fallible self-report into a §4 pass-2 input — FW5:653 makes
that declaration a criticism target, not an established dependence.

## 4. Net judgment

Interpretation. As offline bookkeeping over authored criticism — an index of
which record targets which, a recomputed record of why a warrant stopped
counting, and a discipline separating delivery failure from content evidence —
v1.3 helps, and helps where FW5 supplies nothing for itself: the three
closures, ν, N1, Refl, `overrun`, oracle isolation, the audit triple and
Pareto's shape are worth keeping. As an admission rule it hinders: `skeleton-
wf` pinned on every informal problem is the admission form FW5:69 refuses, and
anti-relapse stage 3 refuses registration on an endpoint criterion FW5:1218
says cannot fix the accounting predicate, leaving no artifact to attack. As a
progress meter it hinders most: `accepted` is silence, not standing; there is
no repair, no protected set and no loss register, so FW5:810's progress-by-
withdrawal is indistinguishable from a bare assertion. The spec is not the
wrong tool; it is the wrong *primitive*. Operative consequence — what the next
invocation receives and what is de-licensed — is what FW5 makes primary; the
label should be a summary of that, never its cause.

## 5. Proposals to widen the aperture

**P1 — Use-relation instrument. Delegable to build, root-only to read.**
Inputs: the five `daily/mini_fcl/cycle01` FCL-1 documents plus the matched and
prose surfaces; the importer side table used only as an index of which record
names which target; no labels, no `att`, no `dep`. Procedure: for each cross-
document reference, record whether the later node's *content* re-deploys,
qualifies, rejects-with-reason, repairs or retains the earlier content, citing
the passage; record declared `uptake` and its divergences separately. No
scoring. Evidence: a passage-level correspondence a reader can check. Ceiling:
a declared, witnessed-by-reading relation, never FW5:628's witness — a
transcript supplies no active-route map, and an unresolved cell stays
unresolved (FW5:634). Offline: yes.

**P2 — Three-case contrast triple at one node, redirected from judge to
responder. Delegable to execute, root-only to read.** Inputs: one frozen
objection; a recoding certified content-preserving by a correspondence table
published beforehand; a carrier variant at fixed content; a no-objection
control. Procedure: deliver each under otherwise identical payloads; read what
commitment the successor changed. Evidence: differs against control, not under
recoding, not under carrier disturbance (FW5:630). Ceiling: one system, one
problem, one respect; consistent-with, never a witness. Offline: no — one cell
exists accidentally (the matched arm's two OPAQUE coordinates).

**P3 — Criticism-ablation arm for occurrence-02. Root-only design, delegable
execution.** Inputs: fork5 topology and views fixed; the criticism node's
content replaced once by a content-preserving recoding and once by a
substantively different objection. Procedure: run all three; compare the
successor's operative commitments. Evidence: a difference under content change
and none under recoding is the only thing that can discharge `ProducedBy`
(FW5:800). Ceiling: one construction account, not repair in general. Offline:
no.

**P4 — Loss ledger; ignores the spec entirely. Root-only.** Inputs: two
situations ξ, ξ' (ends of consecutive cycles); sets O and P written as prose
predicates over readable artifacts and published with a pinned sha256 *before*
the later cycle is dispatched (FW5:787). Procedure: evaluate each o and r by
reading, passage cited; then a mandatory "losses outside P" section, present
even when empty (FW5:802), and a construction account naming the binding.
Evidence: (P)'s three clauses. Ceiling: local progress on one comparison, "not
an all-things-considered verdict". Offline: as a calibration dry run only,
over the H004 conversion18/19 pair, labelled as violating its own pre-
declaration condition and so establishing nothing.

**P5 — Custody and grain preconditions. Delegable.** Inputs: (a) frozen
request bytes per coordinate, pinned by receipt sha256; (b) the occurrence
imported at two scopes, plus a merge and a split of one commitment surface at
fixed prose. Procedure: for each pair the design calls a route contrast, diff
the payloads and state which represented distinction is present in one and
absent in the other (labels and headings do not count); then produce P1's
projection for each grain variant and have root re-read one fixed pair,
comparing readings, not counts. Evidence: the contrast exists in the material;
the reading survives re-partition, or it does not. Ceiling: preconditions on
the comparison and on the instrument — never evidence of use, never a test of
the model (FW5:154, :1202-1206). Offline: yes.

**P6 — FCL-1's fields against FW5. Root-only.** Measured: FCL-1 supplies
`target`, `grounds` and `bearing` as separate authored fields, exercised on
all 9 objection records, plus `depends`/`mentions` with an explicit "not
inferred from citation" rule — three of FW5:609's four constituents and R4's
distinction, none of which the §10.1 skeleton has a slot for. Interpretation:
three gaps. (i) No δ slot — the defect lives in free `text` — so FW5:622's
four failure modes are not separately addressable. (ii) `use` is prospective,
whereas R3's use is retrospective and organizational; split it into
`proposed_use` and `used`, the latter carrying a witness status with
`unresolved` as a legal value (FW5:634). (iii) `revises` and `withdraws` are
the only repair vocabulary in either document and went unused in
occurrence-01; they should carry P4's O/P declaration rather than be dropped.
Procedure: root reads the 9 objection records against FW5:622's enumeration.
Ceiling: a language-adequacy reading of one cycle. Offline: yes.

**P7 — Pre-registered reading set. Root-only.** Inputs: the coordinates root
will read in full, published before dispatch; additions only with a recorded
reason and the original list retained. Evidence: every negative finding states
the set it was searched over. Ceiling: constrains the observer, not the
subject. Offline: the retrospective check of H003/H004 is available now.
Corrected: the grounds are FW5:208, :787 and :634 — not FW5:1101, which
governs enabling conditions in a capacity claim.

## 6. Spec critique in FW5 terms

Ten lines FW5 would rewrite.

1. **spec:213** (`refuted if exists b in G…`). Add to §4: "`accepted` means no
surviving warranted attack exists in this graph and entails nothing about
explanatory adequacy; a label carries no application and no respect index."
FW5:226, :244, :642-651.
2. **spec:219** (`supported(a) = all(...)`). Replace with `supported(a) = ∃
route r: all deps in r accepted`, adding an optional `route` label on
dependence refs and defaulting to today's behaviour. FW5:657, :682, :267.
3. **spec:103** (ν "sound & relevant"). Split into `soundness_node` and
`bearing_node`, the latter naming p_δ. FW5:242, :620.
4. **spec:193** (stage 3 blocks on verdict-vector identity). Replace with "log
the near-miss, admit, and Spawn a discrimination problem", or require a
structural witness at a declared grain. FW5:1218, :1402; N1 (spec:233) cannot
reach a blocked candidate.
5. **spec:259** ("a rename is the same explanation"). Replace with "is treated
as equivalent at the declared grain ℓ, a surrogate asserted in the validity
node". FW5:144, :154.
6. **spec:269** ("a hit raises standing"). Replace with "raises scheduler
funding, never status; recorded as a coupling candidate, becoming a reach
record only when a bridge artifact stating the anchor is registered". FW5:383,
:851; spec:469's own formula.
7. **spec:307** (`fail ⇒ … refuted`). Give estimate-derived defeat a distinct
non-terminal marker, so a k≈8 spot-check never occupies the state a program
refutation does. FW5:381, :851; §17:580's concession.
8. **spec:301** (freezes k, `HV_MIN`, B₀). Extend to µ's identity — role
endpoint, model version, kernel selection. FW5:358, :1300.
9. **spec:366** (`skeleton-wf` pinned on every informal problem). Make it one
admissible convention among others, satisfiable by an explicit
limitation/underdetermination record, and make failure `suspended_unresolved`,
never `refuted`. FW5:69, :857, :438, :810.
10. **spec:555** (`PARETO_AXES` as a config default). Register as a Refl
artifact, fix before the window it governs, require each axis to name the
obligation it tracks. FW5:605, :787.

Three places FW5 is silent and the spec's precision is useful. **Non-
evaluability:** FW5:688 states the principle; `overrun` plus `sandbox_abort`
(spec:79, :86-88) operationalise it, including "must not be written as
evidence". **Criticism of a standard reaching the operative record:** FW5:63
demands it and gives no mechanism; the case-law closure (spec:108) makes
collapse and reinstatement fall out of pass 1, not curation. **Pre-
registration:** FW5:787 requires O and P not to shift inside an assessment;
§11.8 (spec:471-480) executes that discipline — arms, replicates,
distributions not means, oracle withheld from the loop, falsifier committed
before first look — and is adoptable verbatim.
