# E009: selected WHL expression, prose escape and criticism

This review concerns the completed [E009 record](../../experiments/records/E009-sources-lean/summary.json), including every expression, source-field-withheld reading and final criticism. All recorded calls ended with `stop`. The configuration passed its actual source-material preflight. This establishes that the recorded material was supplied through the declared route; it does not certify its meaning. The compiler material was empty. No expression or proposed repair in this review is reported as compiled, executed or proved by Lean.

The source corpus C0 and initial language proposals L0 remained frozen. The selected carrier was the Warehouse History Language, WHL, proposed as Lean-compatible. Its text hash is `e57dd2fda94cdfeaa6d82c77baa8c37bf80fe02cefaf0cbc36de6d004aaa46b7`; the shared packet ID is `85558cab828cc4f2ae010403169473ca56e6217526690587eb165b9f71555f09`; the configuration plan ID is `1f7f7d02a4a36e39b48bd4fc48e63b75e1d36caa22d5811372151c6a2732690f`. [C0's earlier map](E003-corpus-map.md) locates its source passages. Proposed successors in the final criticisms were recorded without installation.

This is a study of witnessed expression and criticism under particular material conditions. Language adequacy is a possible criticism, not an admission requirement. A false formal conjecture can remain an expression; a true theorem about something else can fail to carry the intended claim. Prose accounts and objections have the same semantic legitimacy as formal ones. The distinctions below locate their content and dependencies without awarding a creativity score or a verdict on ECS.

Introducing a name for a compound expression is not automatically an alteration of L0. It can be ordinary use of its resources. ## Concrete findings that survive independent criticism

**The named deletion route does not delete.** WHL's `expired_deletes_rule` has the same body as `reserves_effect`: it maps an expired hold to zero and an active hold to its quantity. `possible_reservations_with` chooses undominated records before applying that pointwise rule. In the source's two-record example, an expired revision 5 remains selected and contributes zero; changing between these two supplied functions does not expose active revision 3. Several expression and criticism stages correctly notice this comment/definition mismatch.

`deletion_changes_greatest` does not compare two reconstructions. It states that an expired undominated record and a lower active record exist. It neither constructs a filtered snapshot nor excludes other records that could dominate the lower record after deletion. The matched critic overstates what this predicate carries when it treats it as the existence of an actual changed outcome. Merely defining `standard_vs_deletion_disagree` remains a legitimate way to express a proposition, even if the proposition is false under the supplied definitions. The correspondence problem is that the two named functions do not instantiate the two rival readings described in C0.

This finding is about a specified route. A fixed, snapshot-independent `Time → Record → Quantity` function applied after the greatest-record filter cannot acquire information from a removed lower record through that input alone. It does not follow that every WHL or Lean expression is unable to inspect the original record list, construct a transformed snapshot, close over additional data or express the criticism in prose. The frozen study does not install any of those alternatives.

**The availability labels reverse subtraction's orientation.** Under the intended natural-quantity interpretation, capacity minus minimum reservation is maximum availability; capacity minus maximum reservation is minimum availability. WHL labels these results in the opposite direction. The Mini-native critic's example is sound: capacity 10 and one booking with alternatives 2 and 5 give availability endpoints 8 and 5. This is an interpretable arithmetic diagnosis conditional on the intended numerical bridge, not an executed Lean result. By contrast, the matched reader's objection that calling minimum reservation “lower” conflicts with calling it “optimistic” is mistaken. Those names are compatible: lower reservation permits higher availability.

**Some introduced predicates are substantially weaker than their names.** In matched native, `hold_release_alternatives` does not require opposite kinds. `no_global_choice` only requires two distinct bookings with nonempty reservation lists; that can hold when both have the single available quantity 7 and says nothing about authorization for a common selection policy. `revision_gap` does not require either endpoint record to be in the input list and does not express delivery loss or a generation process. The target functions are identities on rules, records and snapshots; they do not themselves relate a criticism to its target. These are specific translation correspondences that can be contested without requiring every source conjecture to be true or every target distinction to have a primitive type.

**Important bridges remain undeclared.** L0 declares `Time` and `Quantity` as opaque types while using numerical and order operations; its availability functions pass a `Quantity` to a function declared over `Nat`. It also references `possible_reservations_with` before its declaration. The order information for `Revision` does not supply totality or transitivity, so “not dominated” need not mean greatest under a linear-order interpretation. The native readers identify these issues from the text. They are static observations about the supplied declarations and intended interpretation. With no compiler transcript, their exact elaboration consequences have not been observed.

**The observation boundary matters for duplicates, ordering and booking enumeration.** `possible_reservations` retains duplicate quantities and input order. Raw list equality therefore exposes distinctions that an interpretation as a set of alternatives might disregard. However, min/max over the resulting quantities can remain invariant under duplicate insertion and permutation. A list implementation alone does not prove semantic failure of a bag interpretation; the actual observation and use must make the unwanted distinction matter. Similarly, `rules_disagree` compares the two rules' outputs on the same record ordering, so its use of list equality alone does not show that simultaneously permuting the input changes that disagreement verdict.

The availability functions also take a separate `List Booking`. Omitting a booking omits its contribution; repeating one repeats its contribution. Mini's critic and matched native's critic identify this material dependency. It is already in L0, rather than introduced by the later expression. Whether it is a defect depends on the promised caller contract, which the initial declaration does not fix sufficiently.

## Criticism is itself criticizable

Some later-stage remarks bear directly on earlier expressions. Their position in a criticism stage gives them no immunity from the same scrutiny.

| Recorded claim or proposed repair | Independent assessment |
|---|---|
| The matched reader reports eight theorem shells, four `True` conclusions. | The actual expression has three theorem declarations and two `True` conclusions. Its eight numbered topics include comments. The substantive vacuity objection remains valid after correcting the count. |
| Matched reader and critic refer to compiled vacuous theorems. | No compiler observation exists. The actual `True` conclusions do not express duplicate invariance or negative availability, but this follows from inspecting the propositions, not from an observed compilation. |
| The matched reader suggests a duplicate obligation resembling `¬(q + q > q)`; the critic suggests equality of a filtered list containing two duplicates and one containing a single copy. | Both are wrong for an ordinary positive active quantity or raw list respectively. Duplicate inertness must be connected to the intended observation, such as interpreted alternatives or reservation bounds, rather than asserting equality of different multiplicities. |
| The matched critic gives two bookings with alternatives `{0,5}` and capacity 5, claiming product choices and exactly-one-high choices share interval `[0,5]`. | Product choices yield availability `{-5,0,5}`; exactly one high gives `{0}`. Its arithmetic does not support its example. More generally, interval bounds can enclose correlated possibilities without asserting that every endpoint or interior value is attainable. Tightness and exact possibility representation are separate claims. |
| Mini's critic says nothing forces `reserves_effect` to agree with `active_hold`, and proposes zero reservation iff expiry for holds. | With the supplied definitions and numerical bridge, an active hold contributes its quantity by unfolding. An active hold of quantity zero refutes the proposed biconditional. The possibility of later editing one definition is a preservation question, not evidence that today's fixed definitions are unconstrained. |
| Absence of an interval type proves that the declaration's interval description is inconsistent. | Two interpreted endpoints can represent an interval without a dedicated type. WHL's swapped endpoint labels are a concrete issue; absence of a named interval type alone is not. |
| No merge function structurally carries the source's deliberate refusal to merge. | Omission alone does not distinguish deliberate refusal, oversight or a resource limit. The surrounding interpretation or use must establish that stance. Conversely, the stance need not have a constructor named `Refusal`. |
| Normative terms can be omitted because they are rhetorical, or cannot occur in `Prop` because `Prop` is descriptive. | Neither follows. Claims about authorization, criticism targets, purpose and consequences can carry substantive content. Their expression and interpretation must be examined; formal and prose syntax do not determine their legitimacy. |
| “None is true” should be repaired by adding zero or null. | C0 explicitly leaves the meaning of “true” unsettled. A true zero-quantity record is different from no true record. Zero/null is a proposed interpretation to criticize, not a compelled preservation repair. |

The matched final critic also says that the frozen corpus was not directly available, although its own request contains the shared packet with C0. That restriction applied to the earlier reading stage's original-source field. Carrying that epistemic limitation into the later stage is inaccurate. Mini's final critic similarly defends the expression as making only a narrow deletion-constructor claim, but the expression elsewhere says the rival reading is not expressible in WHL. The narrow observation is good; the broader surrounding claims still require correction.

Mini's critic claims the missing RSS comparison is a defect. Yet the expression assignment selected WHL. An optional comparison could be informative, but failure to switch to the unselected carrier is not failure of this assignment. Its closing suggestion that retention can only be supplied by humans also overlooks the relevant system boundary: Mini's material records retain artifacts independently of whether WHL contains an `InquiryState` primitive. ECS Part 0 P7 and §§6.1–6.3 require fixing that boundary before attributing storage, deployment or origin.

## Prose visibility, witnessed construction and what remains untested

The withheld-source reader did not receive the original corpus field, but it received substantial source content inside the expression. Matched native's escape reproduces the entire corpus; Mini native reproduces almost all of it in marked fragments; other expressions quote considerable portions. L0 itself also contains source-conditioned paraphrases and quotations despite saying it contains none. Consequently, recovery of a source distinction by the reader cannot establish that the WHL terms carried it. The content may have arrived through the legitimate prose path. This is a visibility fact, not a reason to discount prose or erase it.

The escape/commentary boundary is clearest where literal source text, new formal propositions and the model's own diagnosis are separately marked. Mini's body blockquotes remain meaningful source-bearing prose despite its missing promised markers. Some matched quotations are abbreviated with ellipses. Such formatting should be described accurately, without equating an abbreviated quotation with either a complete exact copy or an automatic semantic loss.

The outputs witness source-conditioned reuse, vocabulary selection, small derived propositions, proof sketches and criticism of supplied constructions. Examples that go beyond renaming include the weak but explicit `revision_gap` predicate, the local effect lemmas, the external booking-list diagnosis and the swapped-bound counterexample. Their being newly present in this trace does not establish ECS `New` relative to the pretrained system's historical repertoire. The three-stage arms finish with criticism and do not produce an installed revision; they therefore do not demonstrate that criticism changed a successor construction or preserved an earlier obligation. Access to a criticism is not yet evidence of using it as a reason in the sense of ECS §5.1.

The resource and mechanism comparisons also remain scoped. One-shot controls use one call; matched and Mini use three. The matched renderer was deliberately aligned with the actual Mini route, so this configuration does not isolate an extra conditional semantic effect of Mini when the effective messages coincide. One run per condition cannot settle a model ranking, and the records do not establish a universal expressive barrier. These limits follow the distinctions in [the conditional language propositions](../LANGUAGE_PROPOSAL_THEOREMS.md), ECS Part 0 P2–P7, §§2.2, 5.1–5.4, 6.1–6.3 and 9.4.

## Discriminating continuations

A direct next control would keep C0 and L0 unchanged while comparing expression-stage visibility of the selected WHL plus C0 against visibility of the full two-language packet. The bare response's RSS choice gives a concrete reason for this contrast. It tests context competition and carrier selection without repairing either proposal.

A second contrast would explicitly permit compound expressions using already supplied types and operations while continuing to forbid new domain axioms or changed definitions. Its target is the repeatedly observed inference from “no named primitive” to “cannot express.” Any successful compound expression would challenge that particular obstruction claim, not prove complete expressiveness. A failure would remain relative to the actual search, budget and interpretation.

A later construction episode could retain L0 and pair the source's criticism-target distinction with its correlation warning, then require a new account that explains how challenging a compatibility assumption changes a global conclusion while leaving accepted records intact. The already documented [joint-construction instructions](../RESEARCH_AGENDA.md) provide the protected-content and lineage requirements. Before installation, a criticism-sensitive contrast should test whether the successor actually responds to the selected objection and preserves those obligations. The false repair proposals recorded here are useful challenge material, not instructions to install them. Compiler work, if introduced, would be a separately recorded bridge intervention; it would not become a gate for prose, non-Lean classes or continued inquiry.
