## What will count as evidence

Root will distinguish a warranted criticism from a manufactured error, a source assumption from a model-added premise, and an actual correction from a relabeling. Finite cases check consequences under their declared contract; correct arithmetic does not adjudicate prose. Track which earlier sound commitments remain usable, which criticism targets were misidentified, whether a criticism of a method changes that same method's later use, and whether a new problem is connected to the actual unresolved difficulty.

Twenty finite cycles can reveal long-run failure modes or sustained local capability. They do not establish all finite target chains, constructor-theoretic limiting capability, historical New, CreateEK or universal creativity. A separate content-changing/recoding contrast and matched staged non-Mini control remain needed for the stronger corresponding attributions. No scalar novelty ranking or automatic language selection is planned.
