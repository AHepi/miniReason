# One episode per template, with an explicit handoff

Status: source design review under ledger D026. This is a proposed implementation and experiment contract, not an executed chain or an adopted template disposition. It changes no source, frozen plan or observation. Root must finish and review the frozen E021/E020 comparisons and the separate template-disposition review before adopting the next experiment.

The minimal useful pair is the existing `joint_construction_v1` followed by a new `successor_discrimination_v1`. The first template constructs, criticizes, proposes a revision and suggests an unresolved question. The second examines the actual question and its parent material, attempts to distinguish its live interpretations, then either formulates a warranted investigation or explains why to suspend or retain the current problem. Each template runs once, has its own `max_cycles=1`, manifest, source archive and Mini log. No template is repeated under a renamed identity.

This makes the next inquiry about problem promotion without requiring promotion to occur. The first template's `promote` stage already produces a fallible suggestion. Passing that suggestion to another template is an allocation to inspect it; it does not endorse its diagnosis. A suggestion that says no successor is justified is still a legitimate input to the second template's examination, and the second template may agree.

## What the source already does

[`inquiry_study.make_plan`](../../src/minireason/inquiry_study.py) binds the packet, exact selected issue, parent occurrences, carrier, instructions, settings and source identity. Its template is `joint_construction_v1`, with one cycle. `stage_prompt` supplies all original source material at every stage and the exact prior generated texts. `_output_occurrence` records supplied occurrence identities without manufacturing semantic dependency edges. `_arm` queues the whole `promote` artifact and records `automatic_successor_started=false`. There is no current cross-template dispatcher.

[`inquiry_mini.make_manifest`](../../src/minireason/inquiry_mini.py) compiles machine-copy source stages followed by construct, criticize, revise and promote, then an end stage. The Mini allowance is one cycle and four calls. `_ProseResponder.reply` rejects a cycle other than 1 and validates the complete routed brief before using the direct comparator's prompt renderer. Its host-generated body/commitments duplication adds no model-visible reasoning step.

[`reason_use_study`](../../src/minireason/reason_use_study.py) and [`reason_use_mini`](../../src/minireason/reason_use_mini.py) provide the stronger pattern for a new two-stage adapter: validate exact material before provider construction, bind the effective compiled runtime fields as well as its header, reject duplicate stage dispatch, compare replayed public prose against the actual provider output, and preserve partial history. Their distinct respond/use visibility contract demonstrates that a genuine template difference can be expressed using the existing Mini engine. It does not by itself make reason-use the right successor to every construction question.

The underlying [`run_mini`](../../src/creib/forge/mini/runner.py) and [`RunPlan`](../../src/creib/forge/mini/manifest.py) can retain each episode independently. Changing the engine's `max_cycles` to 2 is unnecessary for this task and would obscure the requested A-to-B boundary. Likewise, calling `inquiry_study.make_plan` again with the previous promotion as its issue is already mechanically possible, but would execute the same four-stage template a second time; it is the wrong continuation here.

## Proposed template boundary

| Property | A: `joint_construction_v1` | B: `successor_discrimination_v1` |
|---|---|---|
| Primary question | What account or distinction addresses the frozen relationship in the original packet? | What issue, if any, survives in these actual outputs, and what would distinguish its live interpretations? |
| Model stages in Mini/matched arms | `construct`, `criticize`, `revise`, `promote` | `locate`, `discriminate` |
| Input | Frozen source packet, selected carrier, issue and declared parent material | Same frozen original material plus the exact A construct, criticism, revision and promotion occurrences |
| First output | A proposed construction | A grounded reading of the candidate issue, its target, uncertainty, source relations and alternatives |
| Final output | A candidate successor question or suspension | A case argument addressing those alternatives and a reasoned investigation proposal, suspension or no-promotion outcome |
| What is forbidden by its contract | Silent source rewrite or installed language change | Treating a candidate diagnosis as established, inventing an observed test result, or generating another joint construction instead of investigating the encountered issue |
| Allowed unresolved result | No construction or next question presently supported | No distinct successor justified; request missing evidence; retain competing readings |
| Mini execution boundary | Exactly one cycle, then end | Exactly one cycle, then end |

The names are insufficient evidence of distinction. Freeze the stage graph, instructions, input-port exposure and output role under a content identity for each template. The coordinator should only accept a registry pair whose semantic contracts differ in this declared way. It should reject A=A even if test IDs, labels or manifest hashes differ. A digest proves identity of a contract; substantive review supplies the account of why two contracts are distinct.

Suggested `locate` instruction: “Examine the exact proposed successor and its actual parent occurrences. Identify the question or uncertainty it raises, what claim or use it could affect, and which source passages and assumptions its diagnosis depends on. Preserve the distinction between the original construction, its criticism, the proposed revision and the later suggestion. If the suggestion misattributes an earlier claim, changes the question, or supplies no distinct question, explain that possibility using the provided content. Do not presume a defect is present. A question about whether an objection bears is admissible; ordinary prose is sufficient.”

Suggested `discriminate` instruction: “Using the frozen material and the actual preceding examination, attempt a substantive argument or case that distinguishes the live accounts. State which facts are supplied, which premises you add, and which observations remain unavailable; a proposed test is not an executed observation. State what different outcomes would change about the original use and which claims or uses must remain distinguishable. Then give a successor investigation only if there is a reason to allocate work to it. You may instead retain the current question, suspend pending named evidence, or conclude that no further problem is presently warranted. Do not turn allocation into acceptance of a diagnosis, install a language change, or manufacture a progress verdict.”

These instructions are proposals, not frozen bytes. Root should finalize their exact language with the disposition and actual task. Existing interpretation defects can motivate the operation, but the prompts must not insert an answer that the new response is then credited with discovering.

## Handoff contract and exact integration

Add a small coordinator module, for example `src/minireason/template_chain.py`, and a dedicated B data/study/Mini adapter. The coordinator should expose preparation and execution as separate operations rather than one opaque “run everything” command. A remains the unchanged inquiry implementation. B can follow reason-use's prepared-material and raw-prose pattern without altering old experiment schemas or records. Avoid a broad generalized template engine until a second concrete handoff requires it.

Before running A, freeze a chain plan containing the two template contract IDs, A's plan identity, the chosen A arm/repeat, the required stage tuple, B's non-output-dependent instructions and settings, the field-to-port mapping, and the allocation rationale. For the first diagnostic chain, predeclare A `mini-r01` as the source. This choice is not a judgment that its answer is best. If it fails to complete, record the chain as unavailable; do not silently substitute a more successful arm. The remaining A arms still provide their own controlled observations.

After A is complete and verified on main, create a new immutable handoff package. Copy the original selected issue and parent material and all four actual A output occurrences in full, retaining their original identities and exact public text. Also retain the complete original packet and selected carrier required to assess quotations and interpretation. Do not replace them with the first model's summary. Canonical JSON serialization of an envelope is permissible if the original signed occurrence object remains intact and its text hash is rechecked; retain the original file bytes and hash separately so the transformation is explicit. Never parse the prose to select the “most useful” sentence.

Each field mapping should say which parent file and occurrence supplied it, the parent's experiment/arm/repeat/stage/cycle, its original file hash, its public-text hash, the source plan and template identities, and the precise B source slot that receives it. Preserve the completed parent record and verified publication commit/tree. Require a signed root envelope whose digest covers every mapping and the B contract. The relation means “these exact bytes were supplied to this input”; it does not mean the child semantically depends on, answers or improves the parent.

The freeze operation must independently load the parent result, check its success state and required stages, verify every occurrence signature and text hash, and confirm they match that result's actual public outputs. For Mini, confirm the corresponding artifact body in the replayed completed log and its identity. Direct parents, if later allowed, require the adjacent recorded provider response and prompt coordinate instead of a Mini artifact. A bundle reconstructed only from arbitrary user-supplied JSON claiming a parent path is not an authenticated handoff.

B preflight then verifies the handoff, compiles the actual Mini manifest with those exact bytes, binds all effective runtime fields, and produces a concrete B plan. B gets a separate experiment directory and Mini run root. Publish the handoff, B plan and preflight before B dispatch. The coordinator must not start B as a side effect of serializing a promotion occurrence. Root's ledger already provides the allocation reason and publication checkpoint; no extra user confirmation is needed for an authorized, fully specified continuation.

Freeze B's first input from the handoff, then render its second input from that same material and exactly one actual `locate` output. Use the same pure renderer for direct matched and Mini. Require the complete routed brief to match the frozen sources and declared prior artifact before provider dispatch. Preserve public text without a model-facing JSON requirement. Retain host wrappers, source-copy records, actual requests, responses, settings, usage and replayable logs as already done in reason-use.

## Controls and what can be learned

All B arms should receive the same selected A bundle. Mixing Mini A's reply into Mini B and matched A's different reply into matched B would study whole-chain policies, which is possible later, but would not isolate the downstream template. This first common-input comparison establishes the narrower boundary cleanly. It still contains an actual A Mini episode whose public outputs become actual B Mini inputs.

The B bare and native baselines should answer the complete followup question in one call from that common bundle. Do not implement them as `locate` only and compare that partial answer with B's final `discriminate` output. Their one-call instruction needs the same substantive final obligation, allowing no-promotion. The matched, matched-native, Mini and Mini-native conditions use two stages. All routes use the same declared provider and mode pairing; actual prompt/completion tokens, native settings and unequal total costs remain explicit.

Two matched/Mini calls with identical conditional messages remain an execution-parity comparison. Under the common-provider and host-admissibility assumptions already stated in [the route analysis](reason-use-route-identifiability.md), the routes have the same public-content trace law. A successful chain cannot alone show that Mini adds creative reasoning over the same chain implemented directly. Its explicit custody and recovery may still be useful engineering properties.

If root elects to test the contribution of the A-to-B link, predeclare a separate no-handoff condition: retain and spend A exactly as specified, but remove its four output ports from B while keeping the original task/source material, B stage count, resource ceilings and general followup question. Use identical empty output fields and do not leak the removed candidate's wording through B's question, instructions or metadata. This is a model-visible information intervention. It compares availability of encountered issues with inquiry from the original problem alone; it does not test whether a stored edge label itself helps. A no-handoff condition can be prepared now and executed only under a separately ledgered experiment allocation, after the linked record is durable.

Read actual outputs for source-faithful target identification, preserved uncertainty, distinguishing cases, unsupported premises, changed question, and named unavailable evidence. These are situated arguments, not a scalar ranking. Record whether the final suggested question differs in a substantive way from A's suggestion and what that difference would change. An unchanged question can be defensible; a new question can be worse; a justified no-promotion is a valid outcome. Do not count the string “promote,” a terminal kind, or queue presence as evidence of problem promotion.

## Minimal verification obligations

| Concrete risk | Meaningful offline check |
|---|---|
| Same template repeated under different run IDs | Chain preparation rejects repeated registry contract identities and an A-to-A pair, including differently named aliases of the same graph/instructions. |
| A second cycle accidentally enabled | Actual compiled A/B plans each have `max_cycles=1`; fake-provider execution enters each model stage once and stores separate genesis/log identities. |
| Arbitrary or changed parent material admitted | Mutate one public byte, occurrence ID, parent coordinate, result status, required stage, or Mini blob and show handoff creation fails before B provider construction. |
| Signed envelope hides a changed mapping | Re-sign an envelope after rebinding its source slot to a different parent and show independent source/result checks reject it. |
| Runtime plan differs from preflight header | Mutate a compiled port, source, stage, bound, format or effective header and show dispatch is refused at zero calls. |
| Model no-promotion is treated as failure or activation | Fake outputs explicitly say no justified successor; both routes retain exact prose, finish normally, leave `standing_effect=none`, and start no third episode. |
| Downstream failure reruns A or erases completed output | Fail B's second request and verify A bytes, B's first output and failure receipt survive; re-execution refuses existing output roots. A retry uses a new identity. |
| Route parity falsely inferred from visible labels | Conditional fake output histories yield byte-identical actual provider messages and settings for the corresponding matched/Mini stages. |
| Baseline stops before the endpoint | Inspect fake-provider bare/native messages and assert the complete discrimination/followup obligation is present, then validate exactly one call. |
| An optional removed handoff leaks through another field | If implemented, inject parent sentinel text into a B source/instruction/brief outside allowed ports and show preflight or complete-route validation rejects it; original parent files remain archived. |

These checks concern custody and execution. They do not automatically test the bearing of prose or decide whether a proposal deserves attention. Use adversarial fixtures that contain Unicode, trailing newlines, quotations and explicit uncertainty so a summary or normalization cannot pass unnoticed. Existing frozen source and reasoning records remain untouched.

## Recovery and the next allocation

The durable states should be distinguishable: chain declared; A running; A complete awaiting publication; A published and verified; handoff/B plan prepared awaiting publication; B prepared and published; B running; B complete or partial awaiting publication; chain reviewed. No state should imply that a future call already occurred. An interrupted process leaves its original record; recovery adds a separate receipt with hashes and absent stages rather than overwriting a start file with imagined terminal status.

Completion of B does not auto-start C. A later investigation requires a separate allocation receipt naming the exact candidate occurrence, target, relevance, unresolved alternatives, discriminating contrast, protected material and resources, as set out in [problem promotion](../PROBLEM_PROMOTION.md). That receipt can defer all proposed problems. If a new window fails, README/STATUS should identify the last remotely verified state and the next exact plan, and the ledger should explain why the A-to-B choice was made.

The practical next step after disposition is therefore to adopt or amend this concrete pair, implement only the coordinator and B adapter, verify the binding and failure boundaries, freeze the new chain, and run A once followed by B once with a main checkpoint between them. The successful outcome is an inspectable connected inquiry and a defensible reading of its result, whether or not a new problem is promoted.
