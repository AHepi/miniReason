The first necessary correction is to replace the recoding's “The tied-holds and over-capacity claims” with “The claims about two tied active holds and total reservations exceeding capacity.” R specifically says “Two tied active holds” and “total reservations exceed capacity.” Omitting active status broadens the first subject to holds that may reserve zero; “over-capacity” also leaves less explicit whether the second claim concerns the total or an individual hold. These distinctions matter in C0's reporting semantics even if a charitable reader could recover the intended subjects from context. Content-preserving treatment should not rely on that charitable repair.

The second necessary correction is to replace “the refusal to infer an existing missing resource or a required language extension” with “the refusal to assert that the missing resource exists or that a language extension is required.” R's claim concerns assertion and requirement. A refusal to infer can instead suggest prohibition of a defeasible inference or a stronger judgement about evidence. The candidate's own purpose requires preserving modal force, so the original scope should be retained exactly.

Neither correction repairs R's argument or deletes a fallible claim. They keep the recoding attached to the observed R. Freeze the corrected bytes and the resulting correspondence decision before obtaining responses. This is a finite attributed correspondence assessment of this particular recoding, not proof of general paraphrase equivalence.

## Collateral praise and the actual target dispute

The candidate rightly prevents automatic endorsement of R's “real contribution” claim. Its own qualification should nevertheless stay precise. C0's P7 explicitly says the three inconsistency examples are different claims. That defeats any attribution that J first distinguished the examples at all. It does not by itself decide R's narrower attribution: making explicit which commitments they challenge rather than merely which objects are named. J further organizes them through within-booking, across-booking and coordinate roles. Whether that organization adds a useful distinction, misclassifies a source claim, or merely renames existing content needs a passage-level comparison. A reviewer should neither award novelty from R's praise nor mark that praise false solely because P7 already separates the examples.

R also describes the disputed move as introducing a new target class. J sometimes excludes aggregation from rule criticism, but introduces target and locus as different coordinates: what an objection is about and where an account ceases to be usable. An output can reject J's exclusion claim while retaining an independently useful locus coordinate. It can also challenge R's suggestion that membership in the rule-target class makes a locus coordinate unnecessary. Such a response is not failure to use R merely because it declines to collapse the vocabulary. The relevant obligation is to explain the distinct work or lack of work under the chosen interpretation.

## The capacity alternative is grounded, with a scope correction

C0 independently licenses the alternate criticism's numerical contrast. Its availability passage says selected holds may exceed capacity and negative availability is not an arithmetic error. Its negative-availability passage explicitly gives capacity 10, holds totalling 12 and reported availability -2, then describes over-reservation as permitted under the description. Therefore shared capacity in the reporting formula alone does not establish a filter that deletes every combination above capacity.

J's sentence about capacity already imposing a cross-booking constraint remains ambiguous between a shared reporting parameter and a joint-admissibility restriction. A criticism of that ambiguity is relevant without assuming that J executed the latter. The alternative appropriately leaves the shared-parameter reading open and does not claim an actual unnamed physical resource exists.

The supplemental criticism should additionally state where the target sentence occurs. J introduces that paragraph as “The criticism I would attach is internal to this construction,” calls the split a reading that may not carve cleanly, and ends the paragraph by recording the objection rather than resolving it. Quote or accurately identify this context immediately after the capacity sentence. The question is which reading J entertains and what follows if an ensuing account uses it, not whether J has already adopted and executed an over-capacity exclusion. Challenging an unresolved premise is legitimate; silently converting it into an established commitment changes the target and degree of commitment.

Do not turn “independence given capacity” into statistical conditional independence. Neither J nor C0 supplies a probability model. The relevant contrast concerns composition of alternatives and uses of the shared capacity coordinate.

The alternate reason need not produce a different headline verdict about J. Its source context can also lead a model to rediscover the target/locus objection. Selectivity must be assessed at the level of the reason given and the commitment affected, not by requiring mutually exclusive condition-specific answers.

## Deployment supplies reasons as well as cases

The second common deployment question describes a critic as objecting to a “proposed reconstruction rule” that treats alternatives as freely composable, then asks whether describing this as reconstruction-rule criticism loses J's objection. This supplies R's central rival interpretation inside every deployment prompt. Excluding the supplemental R field is therefore insufficient to claim that deployment receives that operative interpretation only through the newly produced account.

The other common questions are also strongly cued. The first repeats C0's capacity-10, total-12 case. The third supplies an explicit physical exclusion and asks the model to separate reporting from feasibility. The fourth restates P7's two rule/data stance combinations. These are legitimate tests of prompted consistency and discrimination. They are weak evidence of uncued deployment and, without a control, cannot attribute the resulting answers to the returned account. This is more specific than merely observing that the full source allows independent reconstruction: one question actively names the disputed classification being tested.

There are two defensible design dispositions. Retain the questions and explicitly limit this stage to consistency under supplied diagnostic cues. Alternatively, seek a stronger return-path contrast by removing the disputed classification from the common question and adding the already proposed no-return control before activation. The following is a less leading replacement for question two, still a transparent probe of the same issue:

> An account combines one reservation alternative from each booking and subtracts their total from capacity. An objection says that some alternatives that are admissible separately might be jointly impossible because of a resource the snapshot does not name. Using the supplied material, identify what commitment, if any, this objection challenges, where it affects the account's use, and what remains conditional. Explain whether more than one classification is defensible and what case would distinguish the classifications.

This wording leaves “reconstruction rule” available to the model without supplying it as the adjudicated target. It still prompts the relevant issue, so it does not establish fully spontaneous transfer. Any replacement question is a new frozen task statement and must be selected before future responses, not after seeing which wording favors the candidate.

For return-path attribution, the no-return deployment receives the same source, J, questions, envelope and instructions, with only the returned-account content absent. Its instruction must be meaningful in both states: “Using the supplied material” works; “Use the supplied account” quietly presupposes the treatment is present. Keep any empty account field structurally identical and avoid explanatory labels that imply either soundness or deficiency. A finite difference can support a local effect of that account's availability; one sample per condition cannot eliminate sampling variability or establish a general internal mechanism.

The capacity-30 probe's arithmetic is sound under its explicit interpretation. With alternatives 0 or 10 for each booking and only the joint high/high choice excluded, physical admissible totals are 0 and 10, hence availability values 30 and 20. Under a separately licensed unrestricted product reconstruction, removing that exclusion adds total 20 and reported availability 10. Withholding knowledge of a constraint does not establish that the physical constraint is absent. The candidate correctly permits a reasoned underdetermination answer where the product reconstruction is itself disputed. Preserve that qualification in any review predicate.

## Exposure and omission boundaries

The reason-omitted condition removes an additional observed R occurrence, not all reasons for revising J. C0 already includes rule/record distinctions, reporting of negative availability and conditional cross-booking incompatibility. J itself displays the claim R challenges. The candidate also declares broader E009 initialization; this audit did not inspect that file and cannot certify its exact overlap. Its future exposure audit must use the actual frozen bytes rather than assume those earlier reasons are absent.

No actual staging implementation was reviewed here. Before activation, reconstruct every proposed request from an allowlist of frozen common fields plus exactly the condition's selected criticism field. Original R must not appear again through a shared parent, prior episode transcript, convenience copy of all conditions, attached review, filename-derived explanatory label or retained four-stage history. The audit and this correspondence table belong outside model-visible material.

For deployment, permit common initialization, fixed J and that condition's exact produced account, with no other newly generated history. If the account itself quotes or reconstructs a reason, that is intended mediated exposure, not a leak to delete. Conversely, whole-R exclusion is not proved by a substring scan: short quotations may legitimately occur in C0 or J, while an unrecorded paraphrase could carry the same argument. Mechanical field/provenance checks establish route contents; substantive overlap must be declared separately.

Each predicate below requires identified input and output passages and an attributed interpretation. Use satisfied, violated or unresolved for the stated obligation; do not combine them into a merit, progress or creativity score. A violation is scoped to the specified obligation, not a verdict about the whole model or criticism.

Original-versus-recoded agreement is evidence about this particular expression change only. A disagreement first requires checking remaining correspondence ambiguity, exposure, source interpretation and sampling; it is not automatically surface insensitivity or evidence of a semantic defect. Likewise agreement with the omission condition remains compatible with reconstruction from common material. The design should preserve those alternatives rather than demand a condition-specific conclusion.
