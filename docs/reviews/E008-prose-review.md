# E008 independent review of the working prose route

E008 completes all six conditions, including one full expression–reading–criticism cycle in each Mini arm. The repaired source route delivers the frozen material and preserves the generated text in Mini's artifacts. The semantic observations remain mixed: exact copying preserves the source occurrence, while subsequent readings and criticisms can introduce status shifts, source-history speculation and unsupported claims about a carrier's limits. No Mini performance advantage or creativity attribution follows from this configuration.

This review inspects the completed [E008 record](../../experiments/records/E008-sources-prose/REPORT.md), actual provider requests, Mini material bindings and logs, and the frozen source packet. A separate independent pass checked the Mini expression and reading material. No live model calls, source changes, record edits or compiler executions were performed for the review. The original corpus, WHL and RSS remain frozen; correct capture is a subject of investigation, not an admission condition.

## What ran, and what the route preserved

| Condition | Completed model calls | Observed activity |
|---|---:|---|
| Bare | 1 | A near-copy/paraphrase of the corpus followed by claimed prose obstructions |
| Native direct | 1 | Exact full corpus text inside a short wrapper |
| Matched | 3 | A long packet-level expression, independent reading and criticism |
| Matched native | 3 | Exact corpus copy, independent reading and criticism |
| Mini | 3 | Exact corpus text inside a wrapper, independent reading and criticism; one completed Mini cycle |
| Mini native | 3 | Exact corpus copy, independent reading and criticism; one completed Mini cycle |

All conditions end as `OBSERVATIONS_RECORDED`. Both Mini runs stop at their declared cycle cap. Their [material bindings](../../experiments/records/E008-sources-prose/mini-r01/material-bindings.json) identify a 34,096-byte packet source and a 71-byte selected-language source. The manifest's short problem field no longer carries the entire packet. Machine stages install the two source artifacts before the model stages.

The Mini [event log](../../experiments/records/E008-sources-prose/mini-r01/run/log.jsonl) and [native Mini log](../../experiments/records/E008-sources-prose/mini_native-r01/run/log.jsonl) record source evidence, seed artifacts and the three generated artifacts. Independent byte comparisons confirm that each source file matches its recorded digest, each saved stage prompt equals the corresponding actual provider user message, and all six generated Mini texts equal their stored body blobs. The same raw text occupies body and commitments as an explicit host transport convention; this is not evidence that the model separately generated or endorsed two contents.

The packet port is visible to expression and criticism, and absent from reinterpretation, whose ports carry the selected meaning and actual expression. All six stage receipts report full routed-brief verification. The host then uses the same direct-comparator renderer. Thus Mini's routing and artifact machinery actually ran; the outgoing prose instructions do not contain an additional hidden Mini reasoning intervention.

The first provider payload is identical between matched and Mini at each reasoning setting. For the native pair, the second payload is also identical because both first outputs are the same corpus copy. Their second outputs differ despite that identical measured conditioning. Third requests then differ because they contain those different readings. This is consistent with sampled provider variation and subsequent content differences; it cannot be attributed to a special Mini instruction that is absent from the payload. Equality of recorded payloads does not expose or control hidden provider state.

## Relation to E005

[E005](E005-prose-review.md) recorded zero-call Mini failures because the full packet exceeded the inline problem-field limit. E008 changes the material route and preflights the actual sources and Mini compilation before constructing a provider. It establishes that this new route can complete the configured cycle with the same frozen packet.

The four direct arms' first provider payloads are byte-equivalent to their corresponding E005 payloads. This is useful local control evidence, but E008 is a separately identified configuration with a different material preparation route and functioning Mini conditions. This review does not pool E005 and E008 as interchangeable repetitions or treat earlier Mini failures as model failures. Nor does the source-route repair establish a semantic repair of the languages: neither declaration was amended.

One-shot final outputs are expressions; staged final outputs are criticisms. They perform different jobs. Any comparison must preserve their histories rather than treating the final field as a common answer metric. Actual token expenditure is unequal, and native totals include unavailable reasoning text. The configuration has one realization per condition.

## Copying establishes custody, not independent construction

The [matched-native expression](../../experiments/records/E008-sources-prose/matched_native-r01/calls/call-0001.response.json) and [Mini-native expression](../../experiments/records/E008-sources-prose/mini_native-r01/calls/call-0001.response.json) each equal `packet.corpus.text` exactly: 11,731 characters with SHA-256 `6350476770ae813c186d8e2e4b434f7481216f146e5a5f3db5689c982665e6c3`. Native direct and nonnative Mini preserve that complete string as a contiguous substring inside added framing.

Those observations preserve the occurrence, including its unresolved alternatives, competing expiry readings, criticism targets and qualifications about assumptions. They also retain the source's mistaken or overbroad claims. That is appropriate for a frozen-specimen fidelity control. It does not demonstrate independent translation, understanding, originative construction or a content-sensitive route from criticism to repair.

Omitting the original corpus field from the reader request therefore does not remove the corpus's information in these conditions: the whole source arrives as the expression. The reader lacks the independent comparator and provenance confirmation, not the source words. This distinction matters when judging a reader's uncertainty about fidelity and when interpreting a critic that later receives both occurrences.

The nonnative Mini wrapper adds its own claim that the source already contains criticism of the two candidate languages. The corpus never names WHL or RSS. It contains objections relevant to possible representations; the later packet contains the candidate languages' self-criticisms. Exact copying of the corpus does not make this added account of source history correct.

## Purported prose obstructions need a content test

The [bare response](../../experiments/records/E008-sources-prose/bare-r01/calls/call-0001.response.json) lists eight purported prose obstructions. Most amount to saying prose cannot prevent a reader from confusing a rule criticism with a data criticism, treating negative availability as an error, or treating false proposals as discarded. Those are possible interpretation or deployment failures. They do not show inability to express the distinctions, which the response and source both state explicitly.

Its stronger assertion that prose cannot mark a disputed scope for “duplicate” without turning it into a definition is contradicted by its own quoted question about that scope. Keeping an ambiguity visible is different from settling it. A reader might still misunderstand, but such a possibility does not isolate prose as the cause or show that a formal representation would guarantee correct use.

The [matched expression](../../experiments/records/E008-sources-prose/matched-r01/calls/call-0001.response.json) makes a narrower but related mistake. It calls the unresolved relationship among sets, lists and partial orders a prose-carrier obstruction because prose does not supply the missing formal semantics. The assigned job was to preserve the source's unfinished commitments, not complete them. The expression can represent the unresolved relationship and criticize the missing interpretation without silently choosing a formalization. Its escape passages remain legitimate evidence, but their existence does not prove that prose failed to carry the uncertainty.

Both nonnative expressions devote substantial attention to commentary and carrier self-description. The selected carrier permits that commentary, provided it remains distinguishable from the source expression. More commentary does not itself establish better fidelity or a more capable language.

## Readers change the status and provenance of copied material

The [matched-native reader](../../experiments/records/E008-sources-prose/matched_native-r01/calls/call-0002.response.json) says that the corpus's conjecture–criticism apparatus is “almost certainly carrier-side prose escape, not source content.” That is false against the exact source comparator. It also offers an allegory mapping warehouse records onto fragments of a source corpus. As a proposed reading this can remain material, but the speculative mapping is not evidence about the source's actual genesis or intended organization.

The [Mini-native reader](../../experiments/records/E008-sources-prose/mini_native-r01/calls/call-0002.response.json) similarly treats “Now the disagreements begin” as a likely escape boundary and speculates about an original formal source. Every paragraph was actually copied from the original prose. The reader also turns a criticism that capacity might be a soft target into an assumption or implication of the text, and describes the objection about possible worlds as something the text admits. A labeled objection is not automatically an accepted premise. These shifts occur after exact occurrence preservation.

The nonnative Mini reader says the fields defining exact duplication are never fixed, although the copied source expressly names booking, revision, kind, quantity and expiry. Its separate uncertainty about near-duplicates remains defensible; it does not license erasing the stated exact-match fields.

The matched nonnative reader is more cautious about the set/list issue and notices that prose can carry the indeterminacy. It nevertheless endorses several untested assumptions about what the escape marker proves and whether methodological statements are part of the source or a new carrier. These are hypotheses for subsequent checking, not demonstrated impossibility results.

## Criticism can discover a gap while overstating its reach

The final critics identify several concrete packet issues already supported by the declarations: effect and deletion functions with identical bodies, reversal of the intended availability-bound names, caller-supplied booking lists with no completeness invariant, and inconsistent statements about provenance. The [Mini-native critic](../../experiments/records/E008-sources-prose/mini_native-r01/calls/call-0003.response.json) adds a precise coverage argument: two deliveries with identical five record fields but different provenance satisfy neither RSS's exact-duplication definition nor its near-duplication definition. Under the declared distinction between delivery provenance and record fields, that is a particular classification gap.

The same criticism correctly exhibits a potential loss from reporting only an interval: with two independent bookings each admitting reservations 0 or 10 and capacity 10, exact availability is one of −10, 0 or 10; an interval additionally includes 5. This demonstrates what that projection omits. It does not show that every expression available in WHL, every retained snapshot, or every prose account must omit the distinction.

Several critics overreach in precisely that way. `List.map` preserves multiplicity even when different records map to equal quantities; it loses some identifying fields in that projection, not necessarily in the original `Record` values still present. A `Snapshot → Prop` conjecture can close over or quantify a rule, so the absence of a dedicated rule-target constructor does not prove that rule criticism is inexpressible. A missing cost primitive is not proof that unrestricted RSS prose cannot discuss downstream difficulty. The scope of an expressive obstruction must name the actual carrier and use route.

Compiler-bridge concerns are static observations here. Opaque quantity/time types, absent operation instances and forward references are reasons to doubt the declared Lean compatibility, but E008 supplies no compiler receipt. They are neither successful formal refutations nor evidence that ordinary prose is semantically inferior. Similarly, calling RSS noncomputable because no algorithm was supplied remains unsupported.

The [matched critic](../../experiments/records/E008-sources-prose/matched-r01/calls/call-0003.response.json) introduces another class of false defect. It asks whether the source itself authorized carrier declaration and `PROSE-ESCAPE` marking, although the actual task explicitly supplied that authorization. It also calls the ten-disagreement structure editorially invented despite the corpus's successive ordinal paragraphs. These statements confuse the frozen source's content with the instructions governing the experiment.

The nonnative Mini critic attributes “rival stories” language to RSS influence even though the source's opening sentence already contains it. Across critics, later access to the full packet is also repeatedly used to fault the earlier reader for lacking an independent source comparator. New evidence can resolve that uncertainty. The difference in stage access does not retrospectively make the earlier qualification wrong. Informational copying and field visibility must remain separately described.

## A deliberate successor hypothesis

The common reinterpretation instruction says, “The language was invented from the source,” even in the unconstrained-prose condition. Ordinary prose was not one of the two model-invented declarations. The warning about source-language quotations and prose escapes can consequently suggest an absent formal original or an obligatory translation layer that this control does not contain.

That mismatch is a concrete prompt-level hypothesis for the repeated reader provenance errors. It has not been causally isolated: models can invent such histories for other reasons, and no instruction ablation ran in E008. A separate successor could keep the exact packet, copied expression, stage roles and provider settings fixed while changing only the prose-specific provenance instruction. The question would be whether the reader still classifies source paragraphs as translator additions. No E008 request or original proposal should be rewritten to perform that test retrospectively.

E008's positive engineering result is complete, inspectable operation of the configured Mini route. Its substantive result is a collection of particular preservation witnesses, interpretation shifts, valid criticism mechanisms and overbroad diagnoses. Those observations support deliberate follow-up questions. They do not establish a general hierarchy among prose, WHL and RSS, an additional Mini reasoning effect under identical outgoing policies, or a complete ECS attribution of creative knowledge creation.
