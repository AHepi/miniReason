The explicit tentative-context obligation is most directly witnessed in matched native's opening and Mini native's capacity discussion. Native rejects an inference from the phrase without attributing execution to J. No response supplies a record of J actually executing a filter; no such execution is present in the fixed construction.

Mini's wording deserves a precise scope note. It says: “If it means (2), then the construction has quietly adopted an exclusion rule,” but then says, “I do not choose for the construction” and retains the ambiguity. The antecedent prevents reading this as an unconditional finding that J adopted a filter. On a charitable reading, it describes the commitment an ensuing account would take on by adopting that interpretation. On a stricter reading, “has quietly adopted” collapses interpreting a tentative premise into endorsing it. The historical-commitment scope is therefore unresolved in that passage. The capacity distinction itself is clear, and its use output does not apply a capacity-only filter.

## Residual claims that the capacity response does not settle

Bare retains the categorical claim that aggregation is not reducible to P7's vocabulary. Matched is especially explicit: its answer says the target is “Not a rule” and then says P7 lacks a target for “the composition rule that an account supplies.” P7 expressly allows criticism of a reconstruction rule. Calling a commitment an aggregation or composition rule does not by itself show why it falls outside that class. Matched records an exhaustive/non-exhaustive reading of P7's list, but offers only “snapshot as a whole” as an alternative classification and does not answer the reconstruction-rule rival. This is a scoped unestablished exclusion claim; it does not show that a separate locus coordinate is useless.

Mini begins by retaining the claim that P9 lands on aggregation rather than any enumerated target. Later, under “What the criticism in P9's sense targets,” it calls the same objection criticism of an “aggregation rule,” using P7's vocabulary “extended” by locus. The account does not explain whether “rule” has acquired a restricted or extended meaning, or why the locus coordinate changes rule membership. This is an unresolved internal classification tension, not automatically a formal contradiction under every possible interpretation.

The native variants generally weaken the universal exclusion to a distinction from records or local supersession rules, and allow coarse snapshot/source-assumption classifications. They do not establish a necessary new target class. All six retain some organizing vocabulary from J. That shared retention is compatible with selective response to the supplied capacity reason; E018 alone does not determine how much the different supplemental reason caused it.

There are also concrete mathematical wording defects outside the finite use answers. Mini's standalone account calls capacity the “shared subtrahend,” whereas the supplied arithmetic subtracts reservations from capacity: capacity is the minuend. In its “cross-booking complementation loci,” Mini also refers to “minimum-reservation lower availability” and “maximum-reservation upper availability.” With fixed capacity, subtraction reverses that order; minimum reservation gives maximum availability. This repeats a source-side naming difficulty as standalone account language without locating it as a disputed convention. The finite use response nevertheless computes the correct four values and does not depend on these mistaken labels.

Native's standalone account says “a min/max interval may be insufficient and may be nonconvex.” Under the ordinary scalar-interval interpretation used here, an interval is convex; the attainable availability set can be nonconvex. The sentence conflates the interval with the set it encloses. Native has no use stage, so E018 provides no later observation of whether that wording would affect its numerical deployment. These defects should remain attached to their actual occurrences without being inflated into verdicts about the entire model.

## Evidential disposition

The capacity-reason condition is operationally complete and supplies concrete instances of responding to the reporting/admissibility distinction and applying it consistently on the isolated finite task. It also preserves unsupported target-exclusion claims, an unresolved scope ambiguity and mathematical wording defects. Correct task use therefore does not certify the complete proposed accounts.

This single block does not establish recoding invariance, causal transmission of a reason, necessity of the returned account, a general Mini advantage or creativity. One sample per arm leaves stochastic variation; response lengths and native reasoning expenditure differ; the common source already supplies negative reporting and conditional compatibility reasons; the use task remains explicitly cued. The review creates no rank or scalar score. It is ready for separate publication as a later interpretation of immutable E018 observations.
