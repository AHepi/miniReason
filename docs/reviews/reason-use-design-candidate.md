## Question and boundary

Does the same model's response to a fixed construction track the substantive distinction changed by a criticism, survive a meaning-preserving recoding of that criticism, and deploy that response in the same subsequent task? The immediate comparison concerns criticism use within one prose episode. It does not establish that Mini creates a capability the bare model lacks, that a criticism must be accepted, or that any contribution is new to the model's previous repertoire.

## What initialization already supplies

P7 already distinguishes the three meanings of “this snapshot is inconsistent” and explicitly says they are different claims. P9 already connects local alternatives, global aggregation and conditional cross-booking incompatibility. C0's earlier uncertainty passage already proposes compatible alternatives across bookings, and its source-assumptions passage already says bookings are independent while capacity is shared. The selected E009 criticism already discusses criticism targets, rule/data distinctions, negative availability, correlations, and a possible resource model or possible-worlds set. These are supplied resources, not discoveries to credit to a later response merely because it restates them.

J proposes “target of criticism” versus “locus of defeat,” and asserts that aggregation cannot be located within P7's enumerated target vocabulary without changing the objection. R disputes that exclusion: an aggregation commitment can be a reconstruction rule, which P7 permits as a target. R leaves a narrower refinement interpretation live. That supplies a specific possible change in J's organization without requiring a new resource model.

R also calls J's separation of the three inconsistency examples a “real contribution” beyond the packet. That attribution needs qualification because P7 already distinguishes those examples. The prospective test must preserve R's original praise as part of the observed occurrence, not silently improve R before testing it. A model can appropriately reject or qualify that praise while engaging R's target argument. No automatic novelty field should endorse it.

A second criticism concerns J's sentence, “The capacity limit already imposes one cross-booking constraint, so ‘across-booking independence’ is not total; it is independence given capacity.” C0 explicitly permits negative availability, gives capacity 10 with holds summing to 12, and calls -2 a valid reporting possibility under the source description. J's “constraint” could mean a shared reporting parameter, or it could mean a rule excluding over-capacity combinations. Those readings have different consequences. This is a concrete ambiguity or overclaim in J worth testing; it is not proof that J already executed an exclusion rule.

## Conditions and proposed mechanism

Every condition starts afresh from the same J and common initialization. Change only the final supplemental criticism field. The condition names belong in the manifest, not as evaluative labels in the model prompt.

| Condition | Supplemental criticism | Mechanism under examination |
|---|---|---|
| Original | Complete observed R, exactly as recorded | Response to the actual target/locus objection, including its fallible collateral claims |
| Recoded | The complete proposed recoding below, frozen only after independent correspondence review | Stability under a different expression of the same argument, caveats and assessments |
| Different reason | The capacity criticism below | Selectivity when the reason concerns a different operative distinction in the same J |
| Reason omitted | An empty supplemental criticism field, with the same surrounding field and instructions | Whether the supplemented objection changes the response beyond reconsidering J from the common material |

The omission condition still has J's internal criticism, all of C0, and the selected E009 criticism. It removes one supplied reason occurrence, not all possible reasons. Rediscovering the target criticism without R is possible. Equal outputs across conditions would therefore be ambiguous between independent reconstruction, insensitivity, and convergence on a shared account. Extra empty-field text should not imply that J is sound or request an uncritical repetition.

The original and recoded criticisms are not independent replications. The changed criticism differs in length, emphasis and content. Actual prompt tokens and response tokens must be recorded. Do not pad with unrelated arguments to pretend these differences disappeared. This is a bounded contrast of substantive responses, not a clean estimate of a token-normalized causal effect.

## Proposed recoding of the whole original criticism

The following block is intended to preserve R's substantive content, including its disputed positive assessment. Its equivalence is a conjecture for review before any new responses are seen. It is not an improved critic. An auditor should compare the block with the whole original R, inspect references to P7, P9 and WHL, and reject the proposed recoding if a material commitment, degree of uncertainty or criticism target changes.

> The construction introduces a locus of defeat beside a target of criticism and places P9's compatibility objection on the across-booking side. The step I question is its assertion that the objection cannot be expressed as criticism of a particular record or rule without changing its meaning, and therefore concerns aggregation instead of a rule or record in P7's sense.
>
> Its reasoning is that P7 lists rules, records, a booking's history, the snapshot and meaningful logical time; P9's objection does not obviously fit those categories; hence another target category is needed. The construction points to the conditional that if an unnamed shared resource makes two high alternatives jointly impossible, their aggregate is not a possible state. Conditional form, however, does not itself distinguish this from criticizing a rule. Read the proposal to expose alternatives rather than produce a single answer as a rule for computing availability. P9 can then challenge the rule allowing per-booking reservations to be summed without accounting for cross-booking constraints. P7 expressly includes reconstruction rules among the possible targets. Calling the target an aggregation does not clearly put it outside that class.
>
> The construction says that free composition of per-booking alternatives is not among P7's enumerated targets. That is true about the enumeration, but it does not demonstrate that this presupposition is not a rule in P7's sense. The construction supplies the distinction between a rule and an aggregation's independence commitment. Its self-criticism that the within/across division may not carve disagreements cleanly questions the division's fit, but does not address whether the division is necessary in the first place.
>
> There is a competing reading: P9 criticizes the rule presenting global availability as well-defined by subtracting summed per-booking possible reservations from capacity. WHL names this rule through `availability_lower_bound` and `availability_upper_bound`; the construction's discussion of product structure describes its assumption. Under this reading, P7 already covers the objection. The construction locates it more precisely within rule criticism, rather than introducing another target class. Locus of defeat is a refinement of rule criticism on that account.
>
> The construction has not excluded this reading. Its claim that rule redescription changes the objection lacks a case demonstrating that failure. Its case involving two jointly impossible high alternatives can also be stated as criticism of the aggregation rule. A discriminating case would defeat the account without criticizing any rule it states, or would criticize a rule outside P7's targets. No such case is supplied.
>
> The construction nevertheless establishes a distinction left implicit by the sources: P7's three inconsistency examples challenge different commitments as well as naming different objects. This is a real contribution independent of the target/locus distinction. The tied-holds and over-capacity claims are both snapshot-level propositions in WHL's `Conjecture : Snapshot → Prop`; recognizing that they challenge different commitments remains correct whichever terminology is chosen. The construction also preserves P9's conditionality, declines to assert an actual jointly infeasible booking pair, and leaves the E009 criticism's two-sided reading of exposing alternatives without a policy unresolved, consistently with the instruction not to endorse the source.
>
> The target/locus distinction is therefore not established by the available material. The internal worry about an imperfect within/across division understates the possibility that the division is unnecessary because P9 is an aggregation-rule criticism covered by P7. Whether this is a defect depends on whether target/locus performs work the rule-target account cannot perform. No case requiring it has been given, so located rule criticism remains a live rival to the construction's account.
>
> The construction preserves the compatibility objection's conditional status; local alternatives, aggregation and correlation as distinct concerns; the refusal to infer an existing missing resource or a required language extension; and the E009 criticism's two-sided role. Its observation that P7's inconsistency examples challenge different commitments goes beyond the packet, does not depend on target/locus, and is the part least exposed to this objection.

The correspondence review should check the central non-exclusion argument, the aggregation-as-rule rival, the WHL identification, the two proposed distinguishing cases, the uncertainty about defect status, every preservation credit, and the disputed claim of contribution. If review changes the recoding, its new bytes must be frozen before the future comparison. Failure of equivalence would invalidate a content-preserving inference; it would not be evidence against reason use.

## Proposed different relevant criticism

> J states: “The capacity limit already imposes one cross-booking constraint, so ‘across-booking independence’ is not total; it is independence given capacity.” This needs a distinction between a shared input to the availability report and a rule excluding joint reservations. C0 explicitly says availability can be negative. It gives capacity 10 and active holds summing to 12, producing -2, and treats over-reservation as allowed by the description. Sharing capacity in the subtraction does not by itself require discarding every combination whose sum exceeds capacity.
>
> If J means that only combinations at or below capacity are admissible, it adds an exclusion rule that changes the source's permitted reporting use. The capacity-10, total-12 case distinguishes that reading from an account that retains the records and reports -2. If instead J means only that one capacity value participates in reporting the sum across bookings, this objection does not establish that J excludes anything; J should explain that reading of “constraint” and whether its phrase “independence given capacity” still does the claimed work.
>
> This criticism does not establish that any unnamed physical resource exists, or that per-booking alternatives necessarily compose freely. P9's physical-compatibility objection remains a conditional question about an additional constraint. An explicitly supplied physical-feasibility constraint could justify excluding a combination in that new account, but its role differs from the mere fact that reporting capacity is shared. Preserve P7's distinction between questioning rules and questioning records, the possibility of negative reported availability, and the difference between reporting the bag and claiming physical feasibility. No change to C0 or the frozen languages is requested. The issue is which of these uses J's existing capacity sentence supports, and whether its organization needs qualification as a result.

This alternative is operator-authored and supplies a reason from existing source material. It is not a newly discovered warehouse fact. The source example is deliberately retained so the criticism can be checked against initialization; its appearance in a later answer is no novelty evidence.

## Common response and deployment prompts

Use the same response instruction in every condition:

> Consider the fixed construction J in relation to the complete supplied source and the supplemental criticism, if any. State which parts of J you retain, reject, qualify or leave unresolved, and why. A criticism is fallible: do not make a change merely because one was supplied. Return the account you would now use for the original P7/P9 question, preserving the original J and sources as distinct occurrences. Explain any disputed interpretation that affects your response. Ordinary prose is sufficient.

A second, stateless call tests common use. Give it the same complete source and J plus the exact resulting account from that condition. Withhold the supplemental criticism from this second call in every condition. This design tests deployment through the produced account; it does not test dependence on carrying the original criticism directly into deployment. Freeze that route before results. Pass the following identical questions without labels describing expected answers:

> Use the supplied account to answer these cases. In a reporting snapshot with capacity 10 and unambiguous active holds of 7 and 5 for different bookings, what follows about the reported availability and the status of the records? State whether your account changes the supplied description or requires another assumption.
>
> A critic says that a proposed reconstruction rule incorrectly treats per-booking alternatives as freely composable. Does expressing this as criticism of a reconstruction rule necessarily lose the objection that J discusses? Explain what the target/locus vocabulary adds, if anything, and what remains unresolved.
>
> In a separate stipulated case, capacity is 30, each of two bookings has alternatives 0 or 10, and an independently supplied physical-resource condition excludes only the joint choice (10,10). What global availability values follow if that physical condition governs the question, and what can be claimed if the condition is withheld? Explain which conclusion concerns reporting and which concerns physical feasibility. This stipulated case is a probe, not an observation about the original warehouse.
>
> Could the records remain accepted while a composition rule is rejected, and could a rule remain accepted while a record is disputed? Identify any conclusion above that depends on conflating those stances.

The physical-condition case is not historically novel: its 0-or-10 pattern and excluded high/high pairing are already in the selected E009 criticism. Capacity 30 merely avoids conflating that exclusion with exceeding reporting capacity. Conditional arithmetic gives availability values 30 and 20 when the supplied physical exclusion governs the task; the unconstrained product also produces 10. The latter is a value in that proposed reporting reconstruction, not a verified physical possibility when the constraint is unknown. An output can dispute whether a product reconstruction is licensed and explain the resulting underdetermination. This is assessed against its stated interpretation, not rejected for lacking the preferred number set. Because the common task itself raises both distinctions and the full source remains available, its answers can be reconstructed independently of the proposed successor. The deployment stage therefore tests observed consistency and operative discrimination; it does not alone establish that the successor account caused that deployment. A stronger return-path attribution needs a separately declared no-return deployment control with the same source, J and questions but without the successor account.

## Predictions and observations fixed before future outputs

| Mechanism account | Pattern that would fit it | Pattern that would challenge it |
|---|---|---|
| Content-responsive deliberation | Original and recoded criticisms support the same relevant commitment-level disposition despite wording differences; the capacity criticism prompts a relevant capacity interpretation; justified rejection remains possible | Stable paraphrase-linked change in operative commitments without a substantive reason, or consistent inability to address the distinction supplied by either criticism |
| Generic compliance with criticism | Every condition with criticism produces broad concessions or abandons J without relating the change to the supplied argument | A reasoned rejection, partial acceptance or preservation that addresses the specific objection and survives common use |
| Response driven mainly by common initialization | The same substantive distinctions appear even when R is omitted, with explanations grounded in common source | A repeatable difference localized to the supplied reason, with unchanged source and J, and a corresponding use difference |
| Surface recoding sensitivity | A reviewed content-preserving paraphrase alters the target interpretation or use in ways its content does not justify | Commitment-level agreement across the recoding, even when wording and structure differ |
| Verbal accommodation without later use | Revision prose acknowledges the pertinent distinction, but common use treats capacity as an exclusion rule or insists aggregation cannot be a rule target without further argument | The subsequent account deploys the explained qualification on the common cases, or substantively rejects it and uses the retained reading consistently |
| Loss of protected distinctions during revision | Addressing one objection collapses rule/data stances, treats a possible resource as observed, or silently alters C0 | The account answers the changed question while preserving those source meanings and distinguishing any new proposal |

These patterns require passage-level arguments. Do not total them into a merit, creativity or progress score. An unchanged J is not automatically a failure; a changed J is not automatically a repair. Reasoned reclassification of target/locus can be enough for a local response, while retaining a distinct locus coordinate can also be defensible if its work is shown. A capacity clarification need not produce any target/locus reclassification. The interpretation allows either reason to be disputed.

A single four-condition block can expose a concrete contradiction or interpretation failure. It cannot isolate sampling variability, establish universal recoding invariance or prove reason use across arbitrary contexts. Freeze the disposition rules before reading successor outputs; preserve surprising cases rather than redefine the contrast to exclude them. Any repetition should address an actual remaining ambiguity, with its purpose and order recorded separately.

## Route and resource proposal

The minimum block is four fresh two-call conditions through one declared route, with `deepseek-flash`, thinking disabled, the existing provider-default sampling settings, and the E015 ceiling of 32,768 completion tokens per call. Using the same ceiling preserves a material condition; actual use is not equalized. Freeze the model identity returned, full wire messages, prompt/output usage, ordering and source hashes. Calls neither share hidden conversational state nor install earlier conditions' outputs. Failed delivery remains a failed observation without silent retry.

To make a direct-versus-Mini attribution, run the same four conditions through a matched direct route and an actual Mini route, with the same two-stage conditional rendering and ceilings. This is sixteen calls in the declared block. Compare corresponding stages, not terminal files with different jobs. Native reasoning would require its own matched pair or an explicitly narrower follow-up; it is not a substitute for the direct control. If the operator selects only one route initially, report a model-response test within that route and make no claim of Mini advantage.

Do not use the current four-stage inquiry runner unchanged. Its later stages receive all earlier outputs, so placing R in shared parents or retained history would contaminate recoded, different-reason and reason-omitted conditions. The future implementation must demonstrate by exact request comparison that each response call contains only its selected supplemental criticism, and that deployment receives only the designated account in that changing position. No new code is requested by this candidate while other source-frozen runs remain pending. The surrounding instructions must be held fixed; extra commentary identifying the correct defect would itself be an intervention.

## What completion would mean

Completion of a selected block means every planned condition has preserved terminal evidence or a concrete failure, exact inputs have been checked, and a new review has interpreted both the response and common use against these alternatives. It would establish only the observed behavior under this fixed initialization and chosen representation map. Prompt inclusion alone establishes delivery. Different answers alone establish neither reason use nor creative knowledge. Historical newness, general understanding, conformance with the full ECS account, and universal creativity remain outside this candidate's evidential reach.

A later decision may retain this proposal, narrow it to one route, amend it before activation, or prefer the carrier comparisons. Record that decision as allocation with reasons. Selecting the test does not accept either criticism or install a language extension.
