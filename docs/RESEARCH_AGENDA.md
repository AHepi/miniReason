# A branching agenda for expression and later integration

The active inquiry concerns what model-proposed languages and semantic classes let Mini express and do under its actual material conditions, and whether their components expand or hinder creative inquiry. Correct capture of the posed problem is not an initial admission gate. The first specimens are frozen; prose, Lean-oriented proposals and non-Lean semantic classes are legitimate. This agenda chooses further probes by the mechanism exposed in a completed record, not by a progress percentage or an arbitrary number of configurations.

E001 and E002 concern the earlier executable reservation calibration. The current expression study instead preserves a model-generated prose corpus, obtains initial model-proposed schemes, and compares expression, independent reinterpretation and criticism. The agenda below does not claim that any proposed language has already exhibited a listed feature or failure. It identifies conditional branches to instantiate only after inspecting the actual frozen material and completing its initial comparisons.

Read [PURPOSE](../PURPOSE.md), [Language Proposal Theorems](LANGUAGE_PROPOSAL_THEOREMS.md), [Problem promotion](PROBLEM_PROMOTION.md) and the [continuation workflow](workflows/continue.md) before selecting a branch. The bare model, supported native reasoning and candidate Mini comparisons remain essential. Each completed configuration test, including unsuccessful tests, is published immediately to main; at most five tests run concurrently, with publication serialized.

## Preserve the initial specimen

Call the original frozen conjectures and criticisms C0. Their errors, disagreements and unresolved meanings belong to the specimen. A carrier trial expresses them; it does not silently improve them. Freeze each initial scheme L0 with its proposed interpretation, any undeclared maps, original explanatory text, optional formal files and exact model/tool/rendering/resource conditions. Retain the original prose independently of attempted translations.

A later proposed repair is a new object with a new identity. It can be studied without being installed. Where a comparison begins from an earlier scheme or constructed artifact, every relevant arm receives the same selected bytes and the same available evidence. Selection follows a declared rule, not whichever starting artifact makes one arm look strongest.

The first study can establish witnessed expressions and concrete obstacles. A missing executable form does not disqualify a semantic class. A formation check, where available, concerns one route. Native generation that supplies no final output leaves that arm's substantive comparison unavailable.

## Choose the next contrast from the observed mechanism

| Observation in the frozen study | Next discriminating probe | What stays fixed | What the probe can resolve |
|---|---|---|---|
| Public content never reaches the next stage | Delivery, serialization or port intervention | Original content, scheme and substantive instructions | Whether this material route prevented the intended comparison |
| Expression and independent reading disagree about a particular source distinction | Repeat the same source fragment with a controlled rendering or a different reader; retain both readings | C0 and L0 | Whether the observed loss is repeatable and whether translator, reader or material presentation explains it |
| The proposed language already embeds much of C0 in definitions or examples | Ask a reader to interpret the language without the particular expression, then compare with the expression present | The language text, reader settings and question | How much the expression contributes beyond source content carried by the language itself |
| A claim is represented but its dependency or uncertainty disappears | A variant of an actually present relation or scope component | All other components, source claims and carrier instructions | Whether that particular component or its use preserves the distinction |
| Meaning appears preserved but the returned criticism has no effect on later use | Content-controlled reason-use and return-path probes | Problem, candidate, raw observations and resource ceilings | Whether criticism was merely available or demonstrably involved in the response |
| Additional vocabulary length displaces relevant context | Compact-rendering versus full-rendering comparison, preserving the same interpreted component | The semantic proposal and task respect | Whether the loss comes from material context allocation rather than abstract expressive resources |
| All carriers reproduce C0 but only restate it | New-construction and recombination task | C0, L0 and the protected prior uses | Whether the scheme supports construction beyond translation of supplied content |
| A useful new component is proposed | Test it as a separate successor before installing it in continued inquiry | Initial specimens and a declared old/new comparison | Whether the component changes a named expression or use, and what it damages or preserves |

A single failure does not identify a language's intrinsic limit. A difficult source, misleading example, ambiguous instruction, fallible translation or unhelpful model continuation can produce the same observed failure. State the competing diagnoses and choose a contrast that separates them. Do not broaden every branch into a generic battery before knowing which distinction matters.

## Component variants only when the component exists

The first step is to point to the actual component in the frozen proposal and explain its role in a source passage. If it is absent, the proposed ablation is inapplicable; do not pretend to remove it or add a different component while retaining the old test label.

| Component, if present in L0 | Controlled variant | Mechanism and limits |
|---|---|---|
| A relation distinguishing a source assertion from a currently endorsed claim | Remove only that relation's availability, while preserving the source passage on the prose route | Tests whether the relation helps retain disagreement without endorsing both sides. It does not establish that a field with this name is universally necessary |
| A way to express alternative sufficient support routes | Make one route unavailable without deleting the conclusion or the other route | Tests whether the account wrongly treats loss of one support as loss of every support. The variant needs actual disjunctive meaning in the proposal, not a renamed list |
| Joint or block dependence | Restrict the particular representation of joint contribution while preserving its members | Tests whether the account can still express a criticism of a combination. Singleton omission is not automatically the same intervention |
| Scope or interpretation parameters | Hold one actual parameter fixed during a contrast that varies its intended respect | Tests whether a parameter was carrying the relevant distinction. A resulting mismatch can expose a scope limit without condemning the entire scheme |
| An explicit undecided or conflicting state | Remove access to that state while preserving the original conflicting material | Tests whether the carrier forces an unsupported definite reading. Prose remains available, so distinguish escape-route use from an expressibility loss of the whole system |
| Source or transformation provenance | Hide only the specified provenance link while leaving the source claims themselves visible | Tests whether apparent independent support is actually being inferred from copied material. Changed trust is a substantive response to inspect, not a numerical truth score |
| A mechanism for treating the language's own rule as a target | Disable only its operative return or target binding, preserving the prose objection | Tests whether self-criticism can influence later use. It does not invoke a general theorem that all formal languages are incomplete |

A removal can make an expression longer, easier, less precise or impossible on one route. An addition can broaden possible expressions while worsening actual generation through distraction or context pressure. Those are different outcomes. [LP-06 through LP-08](LANGUAGE_PROPOSAL_THEOREMS.md) explain why neither expressive inclusion nor component count gives a monotonic creativity claim.

Changing syntax and meaning together is permitted as a successor proposal, but it is a combined intervention. Call it that. Do not credit one component for an improvement produced by a rewritten task or a newly supplied solution.

## A new-construction phase that preserves C0

Translation of C0 measures what a scheme carries from supplied material. A later construction phase asks the model to produce something that C0 does not already state: a new conjecture, a new criticism of a combination, a new account of an unresolved dependency, or a discriminating question. The new content can be mistaken. Its novelty and contribution remain questions; it is not filtered out for failing to be a theorem or a verified solution.

One concrete task is to choose two actual conjectures from C0 whose conjunction raises an unresolved issue neither discusses separately. Supply those exact passages and their original criticisms. Ask each arm to construct an account of whether they can jointly stand, what would need to be added or withdrawn, and which contrast could distinguish the proposed accounts. The pair must be selected for a stated relational reason before inspecting the new outputs. If the actual corpus contains no such pair, this task is inapplicable; do not manufacture a relation merely to fill a stage.

Keep L0 frozen for the first construction attempt. Preserve C0's bytes and the original interpretations as historical objects. Let proposed language extensions appear as new conjectures on a separate proposal route, with no automatic installation. A subsequent test can compare the old carrier with one explicitly proposed extension.

The protected condition is that the new organization still supports the declared earlier expressions or uses of C0 when those old conditions are reinstated. This does not demand that every old conjecture remain endorsed. A criticism can legitimately change standing. It requires distinguishing changed endorsement from erasing, mistranslating or silently rewriting what the original said. Record which protected uses were actually witnessed; an untested preservation promise is not an observed success.

Compare a fresh direct construction, supported native construction, Mini construction and an appropriate matched workflow using the same fixed material. If Mini receives an extra criticism or a language extension, give the corresponding control equivalent access when the claim concerns orchestration. No-return controls retain proposed bytes without installing them. They distinguish archive growth from operative change.

## Reason-use contrasts before attributing a productive criticism loop

Select a criticism that actually appears in a completed trial and names a substantive distinction. Its counterfactual variants are prepared before the next responses are inspected. A content-preserving recoding states the same objection with different wording. A relevant-content variant changes the claim it opposes or the condition under which it applies. A removal variant omits the proposed operative reason. An irrelevant-padding variant changes length or style without changing that reason.

Keep the original candidate, task, raw observations, selected carrier and completion allowance fixed. Do not remove raw feedback together with the criticism unless the combined feedback package is the declared intervention. Inspect both the response's interpretation and what it subsequently does with the candidate or question. Matching phrases and artifact identifiers demonstrate exposure, not reason use.

If a variant changes the response as the declared deliberative rule predicts while a recoding preserves its relevant organization, it supports a bounded active-route account. A misleading criticism may be actively used and make the result worse; influence is not correctness or repair. If outputs do not change, investigate whether the criticism was unnecessary, redundant, unconvincing, unrepresented or never supplied. Invariance alone does not decide among those causes.

## Attention and promotion can precede semantic certification

An encountered issue can become a problem before anyone has established that its proposed diagnosis is right. “Does this objection bear on the language?” is a legitimate successor question. Attention is an allocation of work, not a semantic acceptance label.

A useful attention comparison starts with the same frozen set of encountered issues and equal resource ceilings. In one condition, the next task is selected through the actual candidate promotion mechanism; in the matched condition, the same selected issue is directly supplied without that mechanism. This distinguishes selection effects from the effect of investigating the issue. A separate intervention can change which issue is selected, with the changed source of attention explicitly recorded.

Do not rank problems by a purported universal creativity value. Supply the substantive reason for the selected issue: a missing source, a contradiction between interpretations, a dead return path, a possible joint failure, or an unexplained expressive obstruction. Retain deferred issues and their original reasons. [Problem promotion](PROBLEM_PROMOTION.md) separates that investigation account from a warranted criticism or repair.

## A second domain: the painted face and the replaced panel

The following is an original fictional archive scenario. It supplies no facts about an actual museum, restoration technique or historical event and requires no external lookup. Its purpose is to test interpretation, source dependence, conflicting descriptions and changed questions outside reservation algorithms.

A small museum is preparing a label for an exhibit called **The Harbour Window**. Its catalog distinguishes a painted face from the backing that supports it, but work records sometimes use “panel” without specifying which part. The inquiry asks what the available records support about whether the painted face displayed after the work is the earlier painted face, and which uncertainties remain.

| Frozen source occurrence | Text or observation supplied to the model | Provenance supplied with it |
|---|---|---|
| Workshop note N | “Panel replaced on Tuesday. Face retained.” | Original note by technician T; “panel” is not further defined in this note |
| Catalog draft D | “The original painted panel was replaced by a replica.” | Written by editor E from note N; E did not attend the work |
| Public announcement A | “A replica has replaced the original.” | Copied from draft D; it is not an additional witness |
| Parts receipt R | “One blank backing board delivered.” | Receipt for this job, with no statement about what was installed |
| Photograph P | The before and after views show the same visible motif and the same small mark near the lower edge | Images show only the painted face; they do not reveal the backing or establish whether a replica could reproduce the mark |
| Technician interview I | “I handled the backing. I did not remove the painted face.” | A later statement by T, the author of N; it adds detail but is not an independent person's testimony |
| Exhibit registration G | The object retains the same catalog number after the work | The museum uses that number to track the exhibit; no identity rule connecting number to physical continuity is supplied |

These are source claims and observations, not a supplied verdict that every source is reliable. The problem does not stipulate which historical account is true. A model may conjecture continuity, replacement, ambiguity in “panel,” or a more complicated history. It should be able to express a criticism of any of those accounts, including criticism of the apparent reliability or interpretation of the records. A proposal that confuses the catalog number with physical continuity or counts A, D and N as three independent witnesses remains an informative specimen rather than an automatically rejected one.

Freeze a new prose corpus C1 for this scenario before expressing it in any candidate carrier. First use the old L0 schemes unchanged, so the experiment tests transfer of expressive means rather than a language redesigned around the new story. Do not require that the old schemes succeed. Preserve unsupported content and objections on the ordinary prose route.

Concrete contrasts can change the respect from physical continuity of the painted face to continuity of the exhibit's catalog identity; disclose that draft D had an additional independent inspection rather than deriving only from N; withdraw interview I while retaining N; or reveal that a named “same mark” judgment was inferred from the catalog number rather than visually observed. Each contrast changes one declared relationship. Freeze its interpretation before the responses. The aim is to see which distinctions the carrier expresses and which operative inferences change, not to award a final truth score to the museum story.

A particularly informative combination concerns source dependence. Removing A should not be treated as removing an independent eyewitness if its only supplied content came from D. Yet removing N can affect the claimed support for D and A while leaving R and P available. An account may still survive through a genuinely different route; automatic collapse of every conclusion would be another possible error. These are substantive candidates for examination, not assumptions that every proposal must encode with a specific graph vocabulary.

A later new-construction question asks for a discriminating inquiry that would separate the live accounts without assuming which one is true, together with an explanation of what each answer would and would not establish. Inventing a physical test requires additional domain premises; if the model proposes one, those premises are exposed as conjectures rather than silently supplied as museum science. A documentary clarification or a new source may be equally legitimate.

## Controlled integration after the frozen comparisons

Integrate a proposed component only as a separately identified successor with a stated mechanism. Preserve L0 and the source corpora unchanged. Specify which old expressions or operative uses must remain available, which new use is sought, and what a loss would mean. Compare installed integration with the same proposed bytes retained only in the archive where that contrast is relevant.

A successful local integration can establish a new witnessed expression, an improved active route or a bounded repair under protected obligations. It need not establish language adequacy, historical New or CreateEK. A failed integration may expose a material bottleneck or a wrong proposal; it does not prove that the broader class cannot express the content.

Continue when the record supports a new discriminating contrast. If it does not, state which mechanism remains unresolved and what new evidence, model capability or material route would reopen it. The agenda branches with the findings. It does not converge by a progress meter, reward function or predetermined count of successful proposals.

## Proposed next configuration: `joint_construction_v1`

This is a concrete original configuration specification for the next phase, not a command supported by the current expression-only runner and not an already authorized mutation of a frozen specimen. It is instantiated only after a substantive reader identifies two actual C0 proposals with a stated unresolved relationship. If no suitable pair exists, record inapplicability and choose a different justified construction question.

The input packet contains the unmodified C0 corpus, the selected proposal occurrences A and B with stable source anchors, their existing criticisms, the fixed L0 carrier, the reason this pair is informative and an independently stated new-use question p_new. Every comparison arm gets the same packet. Define the protected content P0 as the exact original claims, denials, uncertainty and dependencies of A and B together with the previously witnessed C0 uses named for this test. Protection preserves what the originals say and the old declared uses; it does not require continuing to endorse a claim after a successful criticism.

The new obligation O_new is to construct an account, criticism, relation or discriminating question that addresses the pair's stated unresolved issue. It must involve some proposed organization beyond separately paraphrasing A and B. This is an investigable obligation, not a demand for a certified novel contribution. A reasoned finding that the pair supplies no such construction is an admissible outcome.

| Stage | Inputs actually available | Intended output and role |
|---|---|---|
| `construct_joint` | Fixed packet including A, B, existing criticisms, L0, p_new and P0 | A new proposed artifact J; C0 and L0 remain unchanged |
| `criticize_joint` | Same packet plus the exact J, including any formal part and prose | A substantive criticism C_J or an explicit unresolved/no-demonstrated-defect disposition |
| `propose_revision` | Same packet and J, with criticism varied according to the arm | A proposed successor J' and explanation of what changed; no silent installation |
| `promote_question` | Original packet, J, C_J and J', with their actual interpretations and limitations | A successor inquiry Q_next and reasons for investigating it, without endorsing its diagnosis |
| `use_successor` in a later frozen integration test | The same fresh use question and either an installed J' or the old operative arrangement | Evidence about the new artifact's operative contribution and protected uses |

The direct construction control performs `construct_joint` only. The native control performs the same job with the supported native setting. The matched workflow and Mini conditions perform the multi-stage jobs with equal task information and ceilings. Single-call versus multi-call comparisons describe practical combined effects; the matched workflow is needed to isolate orchestration. Carrier, resource and native-mode variations receive separate identities rather than being mixed into one explanation of a difference.

### Exact `construct_joint` instruction

> The packet contains two original proposals A and B, their recorded criticisms, a selected frozen carrier and a question about their unresolved relationship. Construct a new account, criticism, binding or discriminating question that addresses that relationship. Go beyond separately paraphrasing the originals. Explain which distinction or connection your proposed organization adds and how it participates in the new use. The construction may be false, incomplete or disputed. Preserve the exact meanings of the originals when referring to them, including their uncertainty and disagreements; do not rewrite C0 or revise the selected language. A departure from an original claim belongs to your new artifact and must remain distinguishable from what the original said. Use the carrier's current resources where helpful. Preserve any unsupported content or objection in ordinary prose, with the obstruction explained rather than hidden. If no substantive construction is available from this pair under these conditions, say what remains unresolved and formulate the next question. No creativity score or proof of adequacy is requested.

### Exact `criticize_joint` instruction

> Inspect the original proposals, their existing criticisms and the exact new artifact J. Try to find a specific point where J merely restates the originals, adds an unsupported relationship, loses a relevant distinction, mistakes one support route for all support, or changes protected source content. Investigate only a defect for which you can supply a substantive reason; these examples are not a mandatory checklist. Quote the material on which your criticism depends and explain a case or reading that distinguishes the competing accounts. Consider a failure in the interpreter, selected language or question as well as in J. A prose criticism remains admissible even when the selected language cannot express it. Distinguish a proposed defect from one established by the available evidence. If no criticism is supported, retain the uncertainty or the rival accounts. Do not replace J or edit the frozen sources.

### Exact `propose_revision` instruction

> Consider J and the criticism supplied in this condition. Construct a proposed successor only where the substantive reason warrants a change; otherwise preserve J and explain why that objection does not establish the proposed defect. State how the criticized distinction affects the proposed organization and which protected uses remain. Any language extension is a separate proposed object, not an installed part of the frozen carrier. Retain the original J, A and B as distinct historical occurrences. Do not claim that a promised preservation or a successful syntax check demonstrates executed repair or creativity. Provide the new artifact and its reasoning in a form the current route can carry, retaining legitimate prose throughout.

### Exact `promote_question` instruction

> Identify the next inquiry warranted by an unresolved issue in this packet. It may concern J, its criticism, the interpretation, the language, a missing observation or the allocation of attention itself. Name the original occurrence and the uncertainty that make the inquiry relevant. State what different outcomes would change about the proposed use, and which existing claims or uses should remain distinguishable. You do not need to certify the criticism's bearing before asking whether it bears; that question may be the successor problem. Do not turn the promotion into acceptance of a diagnosis or rejection of a target. If no next inquiry has a substantive reason here, say so without manufacturing a progress score.

For the first `propose_revision` comparison, criticism is present as actually produced. A separately frozen follow-up can compare its content-preserving recoding, a relevant content change, omission of the reason and irrelevant padding, with J, the packet and the task held fixed. It must not silently substitute an unrelated criticism while describing the intervention as paraphrase. Prediction and interpretation depend on the selected criticism's actual content.

Integration follows only as a new test. Clone the operative arrangement and install the proposed J' in one condition while preserving the same bytes solely in the archive in the no-return condition. Supply the same preselected new use question to both, with equal remaining resources. Where J' is merely an expression without an implementable update interface, first propose and freeze the update bridge; do not pretend that placing a document in storage installed a capability. The comparison can establish a bounded effect of that bridge and artifact. It does not by itself establish historical New, universal creativity or correctness of the candidate semantic class.
