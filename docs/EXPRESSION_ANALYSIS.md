# Reading evidence about expression

This protocol supports the frozen language study. Read PURPOSE, LANGUAGE_PROPOSAL_THEOREMS and PROBLEM_PROMOTION first. Its categories organize observations; they do not constrain what the model may propose or what can count as a substantive criticism. An unclassified passage remains evidence.

## Unit of analysis

Use a source fragment, its attempted expression, the source-text-withheld reading, and a specific criticism as the smallest reviewable chain. Cite immutable file paths plus exact quoted fragments and content hashes. State the interpretation under which the claimed distinction matters. Preserve competing readings and challenges to the reviewer's interpretation. Do not replace a model's mistaken original with a repaired paraphrase when judging fidelity.

Each observation records its carrier, run and stage; the source fragment and expression; a claimed preservation, omission or addition; a distinguishing case or reading; the proposed cause; competing causes; the relevant language component; and what observation would challenge the diagnosis. There need not be one outcome for the whole answer. One expression can preserve a dependency while obscuring its scope.

## Distinctions that must remain separate

| Observation | What it supports | What it does not establish |
|---|---|---|
| A fragment is reproduced as ordinary prose outside the selected language | The combined language-plus-prose route preserved those bytes | The selected language alone expressed the distinction |
| A reader reconstructs a distinction from the expression and language | A witnessed reading under those inputs | Unique interpretation, faithful capture, or general model competence |
| Two fragments receive the same expression | A collision in that attempted translation, if the source difference is defended | That every expression of the language must collide |
| A defended factorization argument shows every admitted expression identifies two cases | A conditional limitation of that language and interpretation | An impossibility for broader Mini configurations or prose escape |
| The model reports inability | A failed attempt or declared obstruction under recorded resources | Mathematical inexpressibility |
| A parser/compiler rejects an artifact | The submitted bytes failed that checker and environment | That accompanying prose is false or has no critical bearing |
| A new conjecture connects previously separate material | A candidate construction relative to the retained record | Novelty relative to the pretrained model's unknown prior repertoire |
| A criticism is logged or a problem is promoted | Availability for investigation and an attributed unresolved target | Refutation, endorsement or actual downstream reason use |

## Withholding and contamination

The middle call receives the selected language declaration and the actual expression, without the separate original corpus field. This is source-text withholding, not complete information blindness. The language was invented from the corpus and may already encode its content. Before any live probe, inspect the setup output for copied corpus fragments, item-specific translations, named answers or precomputed criticisms. Record them as possible leakage; do not secretly edit the frozen language to remove them. A successor setup can deliberately address the cause.

The expression may legitimately reproduce source fragments on a prose escape path. Such a reproduction can make reinterpretation easy. Retain that success for the combined route and attribute the information source accurately. No mechanically perfect detector exists for semantic copying; exact fragment checks are limited operational alarms and human interpretation remains fallible.

A direct matched control and Mini should have the same stage-specific information. Check actual request bytes for both inclusion and exclusion. Mini's wrapper, ordering, artifact IDs and context cost may still differ. If they do, identify the comparison as the actual orchestration packages under those differences. One-call bare and native controls answer a practical resource question; their results do not isolate the effect of Mini's extra calls.

## Creativity and component changes

The frozen translation block studies expression and criticism. It cannot by itself answer whether a language makes new constructions more creative. A later task must invite a new conjecture or explanatory connection, retain its antecedents, and test a stated obligation and preservation set. Inspect whether a cited criticism changes the content of the successor, rather than merely adding an ID or repeating a slogan. Do not interpret longer text, symbols, proof count or reviewer agreement as a creativity score.

Only intervene on a component actually present in the proposed language. A request to remove a named negation constructor does not establish that denial disappeared: ordinary strings, implication, encodings or another constructor may still carry it. State the semantics of the restricted channel and test equivalent expressions before claiming a limitation. Vocabulary closure, uncertainty, negation, dependencies and provenance need separate treatments unless a deliberately bundled comparison is named.

Distinguish the possibility of expression, what the renderer actually makes available, and what a finite generation witnesses. More available distinctions can increase representational possibilities and still impair search under the actual context and token allowance. Conversely, a restrictive language can aid a particular construction without justifying exclusion of criticisms it cannot carry.

## Review and successor selection

Review raw public prose independently of parse or task outcome. Record failures in errata at their observed locus; proposed causes remain revisable. Add transferable findings to the relevant lessons file with scope and evidence. Select a successor because it could distinguish an unresolved mechanism, and state that mechanism before calling. Repetition addresses sampling uncertainty only when the question and resource conditions stay fixed.

Publish every completed configuration, including failed setup or probe configurations, before starting its successor. Preserve originals when revising an interpretation. An exhausted token allowance, provider timeout or unavailable compiler is a resource or environment boundary, not an exhaustion of research options.
