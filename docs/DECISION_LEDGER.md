# Decision ledger

This is the append-only recovery ledger for AHepi/miniReason. Each dated receipt records the choice, its reason, its contribution to the project goal, and evidence or completion state. These are concise decision justifications, not private model deliberations. Append corrections and outcomes; do not rewrite earlier receipts. A future agent reads README, PURPOSE, STATUS and this ledger before work. Every new design, implementation, experiment, interpretation, delegation, publication or stopping choice needs a receipt. Mechanical executions of an already recorded choice belong to that receipt’s evidence.

The end goal is to discover how model-proposed languages and semantic classes expand or hinder expression, construction, criticism and use through Mini, compared with bare, matched-call and native-reasoning controls. Prose remains legitimate. Operational completion never certifies creativity.
