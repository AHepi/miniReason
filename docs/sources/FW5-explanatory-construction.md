## A structural, recursive semantic class of creativity

**Archive name:** FW5. **Date:** 8 September 2026. **Standing:** a new proposal, not an assertion of adoption and not an amendment that silently preserves every claim of its predecessors.

## The proposal in ordinary language

A creative thinker does not merely produce an unusual answer. It constructs or reconstructs an organization of ideas through which a difficulty can be understood, investigated, or transformed. That organization can be mistaken. When it actually removes an explanatory defect, the achievement is knowledge creation in the relevant respect. When the thinker can turn the same activity on its own interpretations, methods, and restrictions, with consequences for how it proceeds, the organization is recursively critical. When no subject-specific barrier permanently confines that capacity, the stronger attribution is explanatory universality.

The central proposal of this document is that an explanation represents a **question-relevant organization of dependencies**, not merely a set of true sentences or a successful input–output mapping. What makes a dependency explanatory is not a label attached to it. The explanation must preserve the target's relevant organization under the distinctions and changes the question concerns. Producing an effect, identifying an unknown quantity, deriving an obstruction, constituting a status, and making an artistic feature serve a purpose are different questions. A representation that silently substitutes one for another is inadequate even when its arithmetic is correct.

Creativity concerns the construction and use of such representations. Constructor theory constrains their physical realization and the capabilities their instantiation makes available. Creating knowledge can change an organization's accessible capabilities or its use of existing ones; it does not change the laws of physics or convert a physically impossible task into a possible one.

The mathematics used here is ordinary set theory, relational structures, counterfactual interventions, and modal transition semantics. Neither computability nor uncomputability is a membership condition. There is no objective function, ranking of thinkers or conjectures, probability of truth, mandatory formal submission, or final judge.

**Constitutive conjecture.** Explanatory creativity can be characterized by the construction of question-relevant dependency representations, their content-sensitive use and criticism, and their physically grounded provenance. The definitions below make that conjecture precise. The conjecture is not a theorem about every possible mind. A genuine explanatory achievement that cannot satisfy the structural conditions would count against it.

## What “no gaps” can responsibly mean

Every defined predicate in this class has a stated interpretation and terminates in declared mathematical data. There is no residual primitive named `Because`, `ExplanatoryWork`, `GoodExplanation`, or `Creative`. The central explanatory predicate is defined through structural conditions rather than through a synonym for explanation.

That does not remove the need for substantive theories of the world. A physical law, the meaning of an expression, and a normative reason are not derived from a transcript by set theory. Their interpretations are declared inputs to a model, and applications of the model are conjectures. The distinction is essential: a complete mathematical definition of a model class is not complete knowledge of which model describes reality.

In particular, objective aesthetics cannot honestly be obtained by defining beauty as approval, symmetry, compression, surprise, or successful persuasion. This class gives normative and artistic questions an explicit semantic place without pretending to have discovered all their true substantive premises. It makes that dependence visible rather than leaving an undefined aesthetic module or claiming that a new notation has solved aesthetics.

# The commitments

## Explanatory realism and fallibility

Whether a representation preserves a target's relevant organization is independent of whether anyone accepts it. A system can be wrong about its interpretation, its observations, its criticism, its progress, or its own capacities. A mathematically valid derivation can be applied to the wrong target. Conversely, a correct explanation can remain unrecognized.

Knowledge need not be an entirely true theory. A theory can contain an accurate account of a scoped dependence together with erroneous claims elsewhere. An approximation can correctly describe an explicitly approximate feature. What cannot count as successful explanation is an error in the very dependence alleged to account for that feature. This separates partial truth from truth-certification, and fallibility from the claim that falsehood itself performs explanatory work.

Deutsch's account of knowledge as conjectured explanations, typically containing both truth and error, motivates this treatment. The present formal definitions are not his formulas. [R1]

## Conjecture, criticism, and action

An idea may be entertained without first being justified. Its generation neither proves it nor obliges a thinker to use it. Conjecture precedes criticism in the sense that a criticism has a target; that target can be an inherited or inexplicit expectation. A criticism is itself conjectural.

A thinker may act on an appraisal without having certified it. Withholding a premise, retaining a theory, revising a question, or moving on are possible actions. They are not truth-conferring events. Refusing an infallible evaluator must not be turned into a requirement that no criticism can ever affect practice.

Observation, deduction, computation, selection, feedback, communication, and biological inheritance may participate in creativity. None is sufficient merely by occurring. The invalid inference from “not sufficient” to “cannot participate” is explicitly excluded.

## Recursive scrutiny without prior authorization

A problem, value, standard, observation model, interpretation, inferential practice, attention policy, attribution boundary, or conjecture-generating restriction can itself become a problem. A result of that inquiry must be able to affect the operative target. Scrutiny that can never change anything is not the recursive capacity described here.

No inquiry must first complete a proof of the legitimacy of all its methods. Present methods can be used provisionally while particular difficulties in them are examined. A system can represent a method without rewriting the physical laws that implement it.

## Substrate independence with physical obligations

A sentence, diagram, internal model, imagined movement, artistic discrimination, or distributed practice may carry relevant organization. No complete translation into a formal language is required of the thinker. The mathematical representation is an account of the relevant organization, not an admission form that every thought must complete.

Physical realization is nevertheless substantive. The claimed distinctions, transformations, and retained capacities must be permitted by the adopted physical theory. Abstract consistency alone is not evidence of physical realizability.

# Mathematical foundations

## Ambient mathematics and size

Work in classical set theory. Every individual model has set-sized domains. A class of models may be proper. The domains, relations, histories, and sets of commitments may be infinite. No enumeration, finite axiomatization, compactness property, minimal support, or decision procedure is assumed unless a result explicitly states one.

Classical truth in the metatheory does not require classical deductive closure in the thinker. A model may describe a person entertaining mutually inconsistent claims without asserting those claims as truths. The system's appraisals and the model's satisfaction relation are different objects.

## Organizations

An organization is a structure

\[
D=(V,(X_v)_{v\in V},J,B,A,L,\operatorname{role}).
\]

Here \(V\) is a set of ports or variables, and each \(X_v\) is its nonempty value domain. A valuation is an element of \(X_D=\prod_{v\in V}X_v\). The set \(J\) indexes components. A component may be a physical assignment, a measurement relation, a mathematical constraint, a constitutive rule, or another explicitly interpreted relation. Its footprint \(V_j\subseteq V\) specifies the ports it connects.

The set \(B\) contains boundary conditions. The set \(A\) contains admitted edits and their composable sequences, including an identity edit \(1\). Composition is associative wherever defined, with stated typing conditions. Edits may change inputs, replace a component, delete an assumption, alter an observation interpretation, or change a rule. Not every conceivable edit must be physically possible.

For each \(j\in J\), edit \(a\in A\), and boundary condition \(b\in B\), the interpretation supplies a relation

\[
L_j(a,b)\subseteq\prod_{v\in V_j}X_v.
\]

The compatible valuations are exactly

\[
\operatorname{Sol}_D(a,b)
=
\{z\in X_D:\forall j\in J,
\ z|_{V_j}\in L_j(a,b)\}.
\tag{O}
\]

Deleted components impose the full relation on their ports. A causal intervention that sets a variable replaces its assignment; it does not merely add an observational equation while retaining an incompatible assignment. A change of a rule is a change of a component, not evidence that the original rule had already changed.

The role interpretation fixes which ports are independent inputs, produced outputs, observations, control states, rule applications, and so forth. It also fixes what component edits mean. These distinctions are part of the substantive target model, not supplied by the direction in which someone happens to solve an equation.

Entire paths, functions, fields, proof structures, or histories may be values of ports. Equation (O) therefore does not restrict a domain to finite graphs, instantaneous states, linear systems, or acyclic dependence. Cyclic equilibrium constraints are admitted without treating them as backward historical causation. When a model has several solutions, they remain several solutions; a preferred one is not silently selected.

## Questions and their contracts

A question is an indexed structure

\[
p=(D,\Sigma,b_0,\kappa,\mathcal C,\mathcal Q,O_p).
\]

The target is \(D\). The scope \(\Sigma\subseteq A\times B\) fixes which edits and boundary conditions are in the claim. The distinguished baseline is \((1,b_0)\in\Sigma\). The respect \(\kappa\) fixes what is asked: for example, production, inferential identification, impossibility, rule-governed status, achievement of an aim, or aesthetic value. The contrast contract \(\mathcal C\subseteq A\times B\), with its associated role and edit specifications, fixes the input changes, component changes, and distinctions material to that question. It includes the baseline and is not identified with a collection of performed experiments.

The query operator \(\mathcal Q\) is a specified set-theoretic operation on the relevant organization, its solutions, and, where needed, its component structure. Its codomain \(Y_p\) is specified. It may return a feature value, a set of compatible values, a reachability relation, the truth value of an obstruction, a derivation property, or a normative application. The answer profile is

\[
\operatorname{Ans}_p(a,b)
=
\mathcal Q(D,a,b).
\tag{Q}
\]

The set \(O_p\) contains the obligations being addressed or protected by an inquiry about \(p\). Each obligation is a specified condition on a resulting situation, not an automatically optimized objective.

The contract states which quantities may be supplied as independent boundary facts and which quantity or dependence is to be accounted for. A question about what produces an output cannot be answered by installing that output as an unexplained input and relabelling an inverse calculation as a production process.

A question can be posed mistakenly. The occurrence of its formulation is still a real event. It may fail to pick out the alleged target, assume an impossible baseline, confuse two respects, or combine incompatible requirements. Exposing that defect is another question with its own target and contract. “Why did the nonexistent event happen?” and “Why does this question presuppose an event that did not happen?” are not the same question.

The question contract is not immune. It can be criticized and replaced. What is prohibited is changing it during an assessment without recording the resulting change in what is claimed.

## Contents and occurrences

An occurrence is a particular physically or historically located carrier. A content is an interpreted organization, including its commitments, connections, reference, and scope. Occurrences are not identified merely because they carry the same words. Contents are not identified merely because they have the same truth value or final output.

An interpretation at grain \(\ell\) may assign a content to a token, a disposition, or an organized collection of occurrences. The representation relation

\[
\operatorname{Rep}_{\beta,\ell}(o,c,h)
\]

is declared model data about reference and representation at boundary \(\beta\). It is not a host's claim that the token was supplied to a prompt. Its admissibility requires preservation of the relevant bindings and transformations in the realization conditions below.

A grain fixes which structural distinctions count as differences for the attribution at issue. A genuine recoding is an isomorphism of the retained structure at that grain. Coarsening is not automatically an isomorphism. Bundling two commitments into one can change the answer to a question about individual contribution while preserving an answer about their joint organization.

The representation relation is an explicit semantic primitive. Removing the word “explanation” from a formula does not derive reference from uninterpreted matter. Here reference has a different job from explanatory adequacy: a system can represent a false explanation, misidentify a target, or understand a criticism that is wrong. Those possibilities would be lost if representation itself meant successful explanation.

# Explanatory adequacy without a `Because` primitive

## A structural answer

An explanatory candidate for \(p\) supplies an organization \(E\), an answer profile \(\operatorname{Ans}_E\), and an interpretation of its active commitments. The commitments actually claimed to account for the target must be distinguished from incidental remarks. This distinction is part of the interpreted claim, not permission for an assessor to rescue a failed argument by selecting whichever fragments happen to be true.

A structural account consists of

\[
\mathcal E=(E,p,\pi,\tau,\sigma,\lambda).
\]

The map \(\pi\) sends target valuations in the stated scope to explanatory valuations. It may be many-to-one. The maps \(\tau\) and \(\sigma\) translate edits and boundary conditions. The map \(\lambda\) anchors explanatory components to target components or specified subnetworks. All maps have declared domains. Their meanings are held fixed across the comparison.

The following conditions define \(\operatorname{Account}(\mathcal E)\).

**Anchoring.** Every active explanatory component has a stated target interpretation under \(\lambda\). Its port roles and edit semantics are preserved. A causal assignment must be anchored to a causal assignment or a derived causal suborganization, not merely to an observed association. A measurement relation must be anchored to a measurement organization. A constitutive rule must be anchored to the rule it purports to apply. A derived suborganization is evaluated using (O), including its hidden ports and boundary conditions; the word “derived” does not authorize an unexplained explanatory arrow.

More formally, the anchoring data include a port translation for each component, depending only on the ports of its anchored subnetwork and the explicitly declared boundary. The target subnetwork's relation is obtained by imposing its component constraints and projecting away its hidden ports. That projected relation must equal the explanatory component relation at the declared abstraction. Port-role translations must agree, and every component edit in the contrast contract must translate to the corresponding replacement of that subnetwork relation. Together with (F), which checks the assembled organization, this prevents local relation matches from silently losing constraints shared between components. These are equalities and typing conditions on supplied relations, not an additional unexplained relevance predicate.


**Structural fidelity.** For the comparisons specified in \(\Sigma\cap\mathcal C\),

\[
\pi[\operatorname{Sol}_D(a,b)]
=
\operatorname{Sol}_E(\tau(a),\sigma(b)).
\tag{F}
\]

The translation preserves identities and every composition for which a claim is made:

\[
\tau(1)=1,
\qquad
\tau(a_2a_1)=\tau(a_2)\tau(a_1).
\tag{C}
\]

Component surgeries must commute with their anchors. Replacing an explanatory component cannot be interpreted as changing an unrelated part of the target solely to obtain the desired answer. Equations (F) and (C) are applied at the stated abstraction and scope, not to every microscopic feature of the world.

**Question fidelity.** For the same comparisons,

\[
\operatorname{Ans}_E(\tau(a),\sigma(b))
=
\operatorname{Ans}_p(a,b).
\tag{A}
\]

This includes the respect \(\kappa\), not just a matching number. The contract ranges over the declared class of changes, including unperformed changes, not merely over the trials on which the candidate succeeded. An account of identifiability must answer an identifiability question. An account of an audience's response does not, by that fact, answer a question about objective aesthetic merit.

**Non-circular dependence.** The answer follows by evaluating the anchored organization under its declared independent boundary conditions. The target answer is not an unanalysed boundary input or a copied target assertion among its premises. Moving the same target assertion from an input slot into a component called a law does not remove this prohibition. Identity of that assertion is structural at the declared grain, not the indiscriminate identification of all logically equivalent mathematical truths. There is at least one admitted contrast that removes or changes a nonempty block of active organizational commitments, while preserving the other declared boundary conditions, for which the answer profile changes or ceases to be determined in the claimed way. The contrast may concern a counterfactual law or rule rather than a physically executable intervention. Its formal meaning must still be stated.

**Non-vacuity.** The baseline organization has a compatible state or history. A claim of impossibility may correctly assert that a specified goal has no compatible realization, but not infer a substantive result merely from an inconsistent baseline. A contrast family containing only notational variants, or one defined to exclude every change that could matter, does not meet non-circular dependence.

Thus

\[
\operatorname{Account}(\mathcal E)
\iff
\operatorname{Anchor}\land\operatorname{Fidelity}
\land\operatorname{QuestionFidelity}
\land\operatorname{NonCircularDependence}
\land\operatorname{NonVacuity}.
\tag{E}
\]

The right side is determined by the specified structures and maps. It contains no predicate that already means “really explains.” Anchoring is a claim about the kind and organization of the target's components; fidelity is a collection of mathematical equalities; question fidelity is an indexing condition; non-circular dependence is a contrast property.

This is a substantive proposal about explanation, not a logical consequence of realism alone. Its strongest claim is that these structural requirements, correctly interpreted, capture explanatory accounting. If a genuine explanation requires a distinction no such structure can preserve, the proposal must change. An unsupported exemption saying that the example nevertheless satisfies a primitive `Because` is not available.

## What the definition excludes—and what it does not

A table of observed answers need not have an anchoring to the target's causal, constitutive, or inferential organization. Matching the table therefore does not establish (E). A table that really encodes an entire relevant organization under its interventions is a different case: the representation's tabular notation is not a reason to exclude it. Anti-behaviorism is a distinction about organization, not a ban on a data structure.

“\(p\), because \(p\)” fails when the conclusion is simply installed as the independent premise and no separate organizational constraint explains it. A true mechanism guessed for bad reasons can nevertheless meet (E); the guesser's grounds can be defective without making the mechanism false. Circular support for accepting a mechanism must therefore be distinguished from a circular explanatory structure.

Explanatory depth is question-relative. A derivation may answer “why does this follow under these rules?” while failing to answer “what caused this event?” A proof can be adequate to a specified mathematical question without being the deepest or most illuminating account available. Equation (E) is not a universal measure of elegance or merit.

Nor is all explanation globally asymmetric. Production can be directed; equilibrium constraints can mutually determine quantities; different respects can support different directions. What must be preserved is the particular dependency organization, not an untyped axiom that every explanatory relation has the same directionality.

## Why the structural bridge is not a certificate

A mathematician can establish that (F) holds for two described structures without establishing that either describes the intended physical target. A thinker can propose an anchor that is wrong. A question contract can omit a distinction the original difficulty requires. These are different criticism targets.

No separate approval event creates an account. The maps can exist before anyone discovers them. An investigator's successful argument for their existence is evidence for the attribution, not its truth-maker by decree. A thinker need not possess the maps in explicit notation to instantiate the relevant organization.

The previous separation into a mathematical claim, an application claim, and a relevance claim was pointing in the right direction. The further commitment here is explicit: a complete account also preserves **component-level organization under the appropriate kinds of change**. There is no assertion that any mathematical theorem plus any independently testable application claim automatically constitutes an explanation.

# Work, redundancy, interference, and infinity

## Support families without a minimality assumption

Fix an interpreted candidate, a question, its background, and a declared restriction operation. For \(W\subseteq\Gamma\), let \(E|W\) be the organization obtained by retaining the commitments in \(W\) in the specified roles while keeping the named background fixed. Removing a commitment does not assert its negation. Removing a sentence from an archive does not automatically perform this semantic restriction.

Define

\[
\mathsf S_{E,p}
=
\{W\subseteq\Gamma:
\operatorname{Account}(E|W,p)
\}.
\tag{S}
\]

The suppressed maps in this notation are the restricted, declared maps of the original interpretation. A new map or a changed role assignment is a different route, which must be identified as such.

No upward closure is assumed. Adding an active, incompatible premise can spoil a route. No minimal member is assumed. The whole family, rather than a selected minimal subset, is the basic support object.

## Individual and collective contribution

For \(W\subseteq\Gamma\) and a nonempty block \(D\subseteq W\), define

\[
\operatorname{CriticalBlock}(D;W,p)
\iff
W\in\mathsf S_{E,p}
\land W\setminus D\notin\mathsf S_{E,p}.
\tag{B}
\]

An individual commitment is contributory in this deletion respect when it is a critical block of size one in some support. A block may contribute without any singleton in it doing so. The absence of a singleton witness must not be called absence of explanatory organization.

Replacement contrasts need not be deletion contrasts. For a declared family \(\mathcal V\) of organization edits, define

\[
\operatorname{Boundary}_{E,p}
=
\{(v,w)\in\mathcal V^2:
\operatorname{Account}(E_v,p)
\ne\operatorname{Account}(E_w,p)\}.
\tag{D}
\]

Each pair retains its edited components, background, and interpretation. The boundary records where an account survives or fails; it does not assign a probability or a score. Positive contribution, interference, substitution, and jointly necessary organization can all be represented in this unrestricted family.

## The finite monotone theorem

Suppose \(\Gamma\) is finite, \(\mathsf S\subseteq\mathcal P(\Gamma)\) is upward closed, and \(\Gamma\in\mathsf S\). Let \(\min\mathsf S\) be its inclusion-minimal members. Then

\[
\{d:\exists W\in\mathsf S,
\ d\in W,
\ W\setminus\{d\}\notin\mathsf S\}
=
\bigcup\min\mathsf S,
\tag{M1}
\]

and

\[
\{d:\Gamma\setminus\{d\}\notin\mathsf S\}
=
\bigcap\min\mathsf S.
\tag{M2}
\]

**Proof.** A member of a minimal support is critical for that support. Conversely, if \(d\) is critical for \(W\), finiteness supplies a minimal support \(U\subseteq W\). If \(d\notin U\), upward closure would make \(W\setminus\{d\}\) a support, a contradiction. For (M2), deletion of \(d\) preserves support exactly when a minimal support omitting \(d\) exists. The original support assumption prevents the intersection statement from relying on an empty family. ∎

The theorem is a conditional result about supports, not a theorem that every explanation has a minimal proof or every working detail is indispensable.

## Redundant routes

Let \(\Gamma=\{a,b\}\) and

\[
\mathsf S=\{\{a\},\{b\},\{a,b\}\}.
\]

Both commitments have an individual contribution witness. Neither is indispensable to the full organization. Removing both defeats every route. Redundancy can make a realization resilient; it does not make each route meaningless or automatically make the explanation bad.

Two systems with the same output table can have different active routes. In parallel wiring, both routes may be active. In priority wiring, the first may prevent the second from firing. A token-level authorship or causal attribution requires the internal event structure. Endpoint agreement establishes neither identity of active causes nor identity of contributors.

## Interference

Let success at a fixed scope require \(a\) and the absence of an interfering active commitment \(b\). Then

\[
\mathsf S=\{\{a\}\}
\quad\text{on}\quad
\Gamma=\{a,b\}.
\]

The full active candidate fails even though a restricted candidate succeeds. It is wrong to infer success of the full candidate from existence of a successful subset. It is also wrong to repair the mathematics by silently declaring \(b\) “not really a commitment.” Its interference is part of the modeled problem.

Equations (S), (B), and (D) already handle this case. The monotone theorem simply does not apply. This is a complete semantics for the non-monotone case, not a promise that minimal-support reasoning will eventually be generalized to it.

## Infinitary organization

Take \(\Gamma=\{d_n:n\ge1\}\), where \(d_n\) asserts \(|x|\le1/n\). A subset of these bounds determines \(x=0\) exactly when its indices are unbounded. Every unbounded set remains unbounded after removal of one index. Hence there is a support but no minimal support and no singleton deletion witness.

The universal family here is a stated mathematical premise, not a conclusion inferred from finitely many observed bounds. The whole family still does work. Removing an unbounded block that leaves only bounded indices destroys the determination. Equation (B) records that collective contribution without inventing a minimal block. At a coarser grain, the single content “for every \(n\ge1\), \(|x|\le1/n\)” can be individually critical. The change is in the attribution grain, not in the mathematical implication.

**Proof of the determination.** Zero satisfies every bound. If \(|x|>0\), an unbounded index set contains \(n>1/|x|\), and the corresponding bound fails. A bounded subset has a largest relevant bound index, or is empty; it leaves a nonzero interval of compatible values. ∎

## Hard-to-vary and reach

Fix an organization, interpretation, variation family \(\mathcal V\), and a family \(F\) of explanatory jobs. Let

\[
\operatorname{Pres}(F)
=
\{v\in\mathcal V:
\forall f\in F,
\operatorname{Account}(E_v,f)\}.
\]

Then

\[
F\subseteq F'
\ \Longrightarrow\
\operatorname{Pres}(F')\subseteq\operatorname{Pres}(F).
\tag{H}
\]

**Proof.** Preserving every job in the larger family includes preserving every job in the smaller family. ∎

That is the valid structural connection between additional reach and restrictions on variation. The containment need not be strict. It says nothing by itself about two different theories using different interpretations or different variation families. Counting jobs is not a replacement for the missing comparison.

“Hard to vary” is therefore an articulated pattern of constrained changes, relative to an explanatory job. It is not the requirement that every detail of a complete theory be individually indispensable. Nor is it a numerical warrant. Two descriptions can divide the same work into different numbers of features; splitting one feature into ten cannot create knowledge.

Reach occurs when an unchanged organizational core participates in an account of another question through a stated anchor and additional background. The new bridge is itself content and can be a creative contribution. No insistence that exactly the same minimal support survive is imposed.

# Exact domain constructions

## Production and the direction of an explanation

Consider the stipulated structural organization

\[
H:=U_H,
\qquad \theta:=U_\theta,
\qquad L:=H\cot\theta,
\qquad 0<\theta<\pi/2.
\]

The role assignments designate \(H\) and \(\theta\) as upstream in the production of \(L\). Intervening on \(L\) replaces its last equation and does not replace the equations for \(H\) or \(\theta\). Changing \(H\) while holding \(\theta\) fixed changes \(L\). The forward account preserves the production structure. Solving the last equation for \(H\) does not reverse that structure.

A calculation from shadow length to height can nevertheless be a correct account of **identification**. It fails only when presented as an answer to the different question of what produced the height. A pole intentionally constructed to cast a specified shadow would have a different causal organization. The direction is not settled by the nouns “pole” and “shadow.”

This use of structural equations is informed by causal-model work, but FW5 does not import a particular published definition of actual cause as a universal definition of explanation. Actual token causes, type-level dependences, inference, and explanation remain distinct. [R6]

## Nonlinear and constrained identification

Let \(Z\ne\varnothing\) be the set of states admitted by the background constraints. Let \(g:Z\to Y\) be a measurement map and \(f:Z\to F\) the feature at issue. Neither map need be linear, continuous, differentiable, computable, or finite-dimensional.

For an observed value \(y\), define its compatibility fibre

\[
Z_y=\{z\in Z:g(z)=y\}.
\]

The feature is identified at \(y\) exactly when

\[
Z_y\ne\varnothing
\quad\land\quad
|f[Z_y]|=1.
\tag{I1}
\]

An empty fibre is incompatibility of the stipulated observation with the model. It is not perfect identification by vacuous universal quantification.

**Factorization theorem.** The feature is identified on every attainable measurement exactly when a function \(\bar f:g[Z]\to F\) exists such that

\[
f=\bar f\circ g.
\tag{I2}
\]

**Proof.** If \(f\) is constant on each fibre, define \(\bar f(y)\) to be that fibre's unique feature value. The definition is unambiguous and requires no choice among competing values. Conversely, if (I2) holds, equal measurement values have equal feature values. ∎

This theorem supplies the nonlinear and constrained case directly. For example, \(g(x)=x^2\) on \(Z=\mathbb R\) does not identify the sign of a nonzero \(x\). On \(Z=[0,\infty)\), it identifies \(x\). The change is an additional domain constraint, not an improvement created by repeating the same observation. Local derivative information alone does not establish the global fibre property.

For a measurement **relation** \(G\subseteq Z\times Y\), define \(Z_y=\{z:(z,y)\in G\}\). Equation (I1) remains the exact criterion. There is a function assigning each attainable \(y\) a unique feature value exactly when every such fibre has a singleton image. This is the relational generalization; it does not assume a hidden deterministic measurement function.

For approximation in a specified pseudometric \(d_F\), the complete answer can remain the uncertainty set \(f[Z_y]\). A claim that all compatible values differ by at most \(\varepsilon\) means

\[
\forall z,z'\in Z_y,
\quad d_F(f(z),f(z'))\le\varepsilon,
\qquad Z_y\ne\varnothing.
\tag{I3}
\]

Equation (I3) does not automatically supply a preferred centre or an estimate with half that error. Those claims need their own premises about the space and the estimator.

## The two balances

The stipulated measurement model is

\[
\begin{pmatrix}m_A\\m_B\end{pmatrix}
=
\begin{pmatrix}1&1&0\\1&0&1\end{pmatrix}
\begin{pmatrix}x\\b_A\\b_B\end{pmatrix}
=
\begin{pmatrix}10\\12\end{pmatrix}.
\]

Its kernel is spanned by \((1,-1,-1)\). Moving along that direction changes \(x\) but leaves both readings fixed. It preserves \(b_B-b_A\). Thus the readings identify

\[
b_B-b_A=2,
\]

but not \(x\). This is an account of a limitation, not a failure to produce a preferred number.

For a linear measurement map \(A\) on a real vector space and a linear feature \(c^Tz\), fibre constancy is equivalent to

\[
\ker A\subseteq\ker c^T.
\tag{I4}
\]

**Proof.** Points in one nonempty fibre differ by a kernel vector. The feature is constant on that fibre exactly when it vanishes on every such difference. ∎

In finite dimensions, (I4) is equivalent to \(c^T\) lying in the row space of \(A\). Repetition of the same rows does not change the kernel. An independently applicable calibration can add a constraint that does.

“Set \(b_B=0\) because that gives the mass I favor” is not an inference from the readings. The proposition \(b_B=0\) might happen to be true, in which case the augmented model can identify the mass. Its alleged evidential support is still circular. The criticism removes that route for trusting the calibration, not the logical possibility that the calibration is correct.

This distinction repairs a recurring confusion between whether a content is correct and whether a particular argument for adopting it succeeds.

## Invariants and obstruction

Let \(Z\) be a state space, \(R\subseteq Z\times Z\) the allowed-step relation, and \(I:Z\to K\) a function satisfying

\[
zRz'\ \Longrightarrow\ I(z)=I(z').
\]

Let \(R^*\) be its finite reflexive-transitive closure. Then

\[
zR^*z'\ \Longrightarrow\ I(z)=I(z').
\tag{O1}
\]

**Proof.** Induction on the finite path length; the zero-length case is equality, and each additional step preserves \(I\). ∎

If every goal state has an invariant value different from the initial state's value, no allowed path reaches the goal. This is a sufficient obstruction criterion, not a claim that every invariant partitions the state space into exactly the reachable components. Equal invariant values alone do not establish reachability.

For twenty-three indivisible tokens, an equal division among three recipients would require an integer \(k\) with \(3k=23\). The obstruction is a property of the allowed integer configurations. Permitting division of tokens changes the state space and the operation contract. It does not refute the original scoped result.

## Constitutive rules

A constitutive system supplies states \(Z\), a rule relation \(C\subseteq Z\times S\), and a rule-identity and scope condition. The query “which status does this state have under this rule?” is answered by the fibre \(C[z]\). Existence, uniqueness, ambiguity, and inapplicability are separate cases.

A game rule can make a configuration a win. It does not follow that the move was wise, beautiful, intended, or morally justified. Those are different queries. A correct application of an unjust rule can be criticized through a normative question about the rule itself without denying which rule was applied.

A derivation from a rule is mathematically assessable. Whether that rule governs the practice is an application claim. Whether it should govern the practice is another claim. No vote, implementation status, or written label collapses the three.

# Transport and preservation

## Exact functional transport

Suppose a target process \(S_a\) and a represented process \(T_a\) satisfy

\[
\pi\circ S_a=T_a\circ\pi
\]

for every admitted generator \(a\). Then the same equality holds for every admitted finite composition, provided the intermediate scopes match.

**Proof.** For two steps,

\[
\pi S_bS_a=T_b\pi S_a=T_bT_a\pi.
\]

Induction gives the finite case. ∎

The proof is about a fixed map and compatible scopes. It does not establish that two different theories share an interpretation merely because their outputs sometimes agree.

## Relational transport

Functional projection is not mandatory. Let \(R\subseteq Z_D\times Z_E\) relate target and explanatory valuations. A relational structural account replaces the image equality in (F) by total coverage of the relevant target solutions, coverage of the relevant explanatory solutions, and preservation of the query value for every related pair. For every admitted transition, related initial states must have matching successors under the translated label; a reverse matching condition is required for any claim of exact equivalence rather than one-sided abstraction.

Concretely, forward preservation is

\[
zRy\land z\xrightarrow{a}z'
\ \Longrightarrow\
\exists y'\,[y\xrightarrow{\tau(a)}y'\land z'Ry'].
\tag{T1}
\]

The backward condition reverses target and explanatory roles. These are conditions on the supplied transition relations; they do not choose a branch without a premise.

If \(R\) relates \(D\) to \(E\) and \(S\) relates \(E\) to \(F\), their composite is

\[
(S\circ R)(z,w)
\iff\exists y\,[zRy\land ySw].
\]

Forward preservation composes: use the \(R\)-witness for the first match and the \(S\)-witness for the second. The backward condition composes by the same argument. Query preservation composes when the intermediate query and scope are the same. Mere overlap of two unrelated interpretations is insufficient.

Equation (E) permits either the functional case or this relational case. The choice must be stated; the stronger claims of equivalence are not inferred from a one-sided simulation.

## Approximate transport

Suppose three representations have maps \(f:X\to Y\), \(g:Y\to Z\), and an actual map \(h:X\to Z\). If

\[
d_Z(h(x),g(f(x)))\le\varepsilon
\]

on a stated scope, then that bound is the claim. It does not automatically survive repeated use.

For stepwise dynamics, suppose the discrepancy after one mapped step is at most \(\varepsilon\), and the represented next-step map is \(L\)-Lipschitz. Writing \(e_n\) for the discrepancy after \(n\) steps gives

\[
e_{n+1}\le L e_n+\varepsilon.
\]

With \(e_0=0\),

\[
e_n\le\varepsilon\sum_{k=0}^{n-1}L^k.
\tag{T2}
\]

**Proof.** Substitute the preceding bound into the recurrence and induct. ∎

Without a Lipschitz bound, a stated modulus of continuity can replace multiplication by \(L\). Without either, a general accumulated-error bound does not follow. Approximation is not exact equivalence, and an exact question may not be silently replaced by an approximate one.

## Historical truth and changed applicability

A proposition indexed to \((p,\Sigma,h,e)\) remains that proposition when a later theory changes the current scope. A new index creates a new claim; it does not retroactively invalidate every true proposition at the old index.

A later model may show that the earlier application was always mistaken. That changes the appraisal of an old claim, not the old world's truth. Alternatively, a later model may preserve an accurate approximation while explaining its limits. These are different cases. A transport must say which it is.

# Criticism, evidence, and operative decisions

## Histories, attention, and situations

A history \(h\) is a set of occurrences with an acyclic causal precedence relation \(\prec_h\), together with its physical process interpretation. Semantic support and opposition need not be acyclic. Two existing arguments can oppose each other without either having caused an earlier event.

For token-level claims, the physical interpretation of a history supplies process occurrences with their input and output ports, the port-to-port connections actually instantiated, and the component relations applied there. An **active route** is a connected subnetwork of those actual occurrences joining the named represented input to the named operative result. Its designation must satisfy the corresponding component relations and role bindings; it is not inferred from the presence of a similar sentence in a record. For reason-use and contribution claims the subnetwork must also have a nonconstant dependence on the relevant represented distinction under its declared contrasts. A dead branch, a cancelled signal with no downstream role, or a carrier detail that changes while the represented reason does not change cannot supply that dependence. Contingent alternatives and collective blocks remain available, so this condition does not equate contribution with single-variable necessity at the full endpoint.

A situation \(\xi\) comprises the contents currently represented or available, active questions, current uses, appraisal acts, attention engagements, purposes, and the relevant links to the history. These are role relations, not compulsory explicit databases.

An episode is a causally connected course of attention to an interpreted difficulty. It can contain overlapping subepisodes, change formulation, pause, resume, or become part of another episode. Attention is itself an activity in the history. Its organization and the values affecting it are potential criticism targets. The class imposes no maximizing attention function and no rule that the most recent objection receives priority.

## A criticism and its bearing

A criticism has a represented target \(z\), an alleged defect \(\delta\), grounds \(g\), and a proposed connection from \(g\) to \(\delta\) relative to a question. A defect is a specified failure condition on the target or its application: inconsistency, a false dependency, a missing distinction, an unsupported reference, a failed task, a misapplied rule, or another condition whose interpretation is supplied.

Write \(p_\delta\) for the question about that alleged defect. Define

\[
\operatorname{Bearing}(c,z,p)
\iff
\operatorname{Account}(\mathcal E_c,p_\delta),
\tag{K1}
\]

where \(\mathcal E_c\) is the criticism's interpreted structural account. The alleged defect must concern the stated target and respect. A counterexample to a universal claim is a simple structural case: the target's requirement and the counterexample cannot jointly hold.

A criticism occurrence can exist when (K1) is false. Its grounds may be mistaken, its target misidentified, its inference invalid, or its relevance wrong. A mere adverse signal is not made into a criticism by giving it a negative label. It can become grounds for a criticism when an organization represents how it bears on a target.

## Reason use is causal organization, not an output comparison

A response uses a reason when the represented content of the objection participates in the organization's deliberative transition. The response state includes interpretations, provisional uses, methods, and continuing questions, not just a terminal action label.

Formally, the realization supplies a response suborganization \(D_s\) with represented target, grounds, defect connection, and response-role ports. A reason-use witness is a structural map from the represented objection organization into that suborganization such that content-preserving recodings preserve the interpreted transition, relevant changes to the grounds or alleged defect have the changes specified by the system's operative deliberative rule, and the mapped objection is on an active dependency route of this response occurrence. The map must preserve internal role bindings, not merely the endpoint string.

The operative deliberative rule can be wrong. **Understanding and using an invalid objection does not make it valid.** The definition concerns whether the system used the content, not whether it responded ideally. Its contrast contract must include a content-changing case, a content-preserving recoding, and the distinction between a change in the objection and an irrelevant carrier disturbance wherever those distinctions are claimed. These are semantic comparisons, not a mandatory battery of physical tests.

Two objections can lead to the same decision for different represented reasons. A valid criticism can lead to retention when its alleged force has been misunderstood. An invalid criticism can lead to a mistaken revision. A refusal with no evidenced or modeled content-sensitive route must not be assigned a fictional reason.

An observer may lack the data needed to establish the witness. That makes the attribution unresolved; it does not prove either understanding or its absence.

## Standing and actual use

Standing is the system's enacted permission to use a content in a particular application and respect. It may be explicit or inexplicit. An appraisal can change it without having earned prior certification. Generation alone does not logically entail it, but generation and provisional adoption may occur together in one physical event. Separating their meanings does not require separate timestamps or separate machines.

A source's appearance in a prompt is a delivery fact. A named field asking for a criticism is an invitation fact. Actual use of the source as a premise, actual understanding, and actual criticism are semantic attributions. They are not automatically machine-maintainable facts merely because a host can maintain corresponding labels.

For an argument application \(u\), let \(\operatorname{Prem}(u)\) be its declared essential premises, including interpretation and scope premises. Let \(\operatorname{Live}_j(d;u)\) indicate standing in appraisal \(j\), and let \(\operatorname{Lic}_j(u)\) and \(\operatorname{Scope}_j(u)\) indicate the enacted license and scope judgment. Define

\[
\operatorname{Usable}_j(u)
\iff
\operatorname{Lic}_j(u)\land\operatorname{Scope}_j(u)
\land\forall d\in\operatorname{Prem}(u),
\operatorname{Live}_j(d;u).
\tag{K2}
\]

The actor can violate this coherence condition. The model then represents an error in its appraisal. A record declaring a dependency does not establish that the dependency is actually essential; an error in that declaration is another criticism target.

## Elimination without a truth machine

Suppose a system withdraws an essential premise of \(u\). Equation (K2) no longer licenses \(u\). Another argument \(v\) for the same conclusion can remain usable. The conclusion is not thereby false. Withdrawing a criticism likewise does not establish the truth of its target.

The thinker can remove a rejected conjecture from its current working use while retaining its occurrence for comparison or later criticism. It need not retain every rejected occurrence forever, and the absence of an archival copy does not undo a past creative event. Availability as a current target requires some representation of that target, not an append-only history of every earlier thought.

A machine check can establish a limited proposition such as equality with source bytes or validity of a derivation relative to formal premises. Its interpretation can have real consequences. A passing execution cannot make the program's specification, the model of the experiment, or the claimed relevance immune to prose criticism.

For a test whose reasoning is

\[
T\land B\land I\ \Longrightarrow\ O,
\]

an established \(\neg O\) yields

\[
\neg(T\land B\land I).
\tag{K3}
\]

It does not yield \(\neg T\) without additional premises about \(B\) and \(I\). Here \(B\) can include auxiliaries and \(I\) the observation interpretation. This is not an argument against testing. It states exactly what the test contradicts and where further criticism can matter.

## Evidence receipts without false certainty

Let an evidence leaf be a reference to an actual event and a particular interpreted claim about it. Its identity includes the event, the inspected relation, the interpretation, and the scope. Equal text in two events does not identify their provenance or metadata.

A receipt is a derivation tree whose leaves are these references and whose internal nodes specify the inference actually used. Distinct sufficient routes remain distinct receipts. Conjunction combines the premises needed by that route; disjunction permits a witness from either side. A refutation by an independent disjunct must not inherit the disputed assumptions of an unused disjunct.

For a claim \(\phi\), let \(P_j(\phi)\) be the set of currently usable receipts supporting it and \(N_j(\phi)\) those supporting its negation. The record can indicate that neither set, one set, or both sets are nonempty. These are descriptions of available arguments, not four kinds of reality.

Negating a claim exchanges its positive and negative receipt sets. It does not turn an absent receipt into a negative observation. Thus missing evidence remains missing under negation. An explicitly named missing predecessor remains that missing predecessor; a different baseline may not be substituted without changing the claim.

Eligibility is a separate predicate. An observation of a completed answer can be eligible for a content claim. A timeout may instead be evidence about delivery. The inability to evaluate a proposition is not a falsifying observation of the proposition.

This receipt construction is optional descriptive mathematics. Ordinary prose need not arrive with a tree, source fields, or a machine-verifiable witness to enter inquiry. The point is that an interpretation of its reasoning must not silently lose its actual dependencies.

# Understanding, provenance, and creative events

## Realized use rather than logical omniscience

For a content organization \(c\), a use task specifies requests to employ its represented relations in a declared family of problem contexts and the corresponding internal or external semantic outcomes. The requests can include interpreting a component, following a dependence, relating an implication to a question, or treating the content as a target of criticism. The outcomes are changes in represented organization, not necessarily sentences.

A system **understands \(c\) in a stated respect** when a represented realization of \(c\) is integrated into its own problem-directed activity and supports the corresponding content-governed uses. The realization must preserve relevant role bindings and at least the discriminations in the declared use contract. It must permit \(c\) to be treated as a conjectural target rather than merely enact a received instruction. The retained-capability definition in the constructor section gives the modal meaning of “supports.”

A system may understand a false theory. The use task can concern the theory's own organization while its application to the world fails. Understanding is not defined as agreement, correct prediction of the world, or actual success of the explanation.

Let

\[
\operatorname{Deploy}_{\beta,\ell}(s,c,\xi;U)
\]

hold when the attributed system possesses such a realization and an owned capability for the declared use task \(U\) at \(\xi\). The repertoire \(R_{\beta,\ell}(s,\xi)\) consists of the contents deployable in at least one nontrivial explanatory use respect. Storage, universal mathematical consequence, and possible acquisition after further learning are not sufficient for membership.

This is a deliberately structural understanding proposal. A generic simulator is not automatically an understander because it produces the right trajectories. Its represented reference, integration into problem-directed activity, and ability to make the simulated account a critical target must also be present. Conversely, no substrate or notation is excluded merely because it is a simulation or a program.

## Construction and transfer

A construction witness concerns an actual suborganization of a history. It identifies the controlled processes, incoming carriers and already operative organizations, the bindings constructed or reconstructed, and the resulting representation. Its physical and semantic maps must preserve the relevant component structure, as in (E).

An elementary **transfer** is content-preserving carriage of an already integrated organization at the stated grain. An elementary **binding construction** changes or establishes a relation between represented parts in the recipient's operative organization. These are classifications of actual transformations under the interpretation, not intrinsic types conferred by a model's invitation or by a host event label.

Define \(\operatorname{Build}_{\beta,\ell}(s,c,h,e)\) when an actual subhistory owned or controlled by \(s\), delimited at \(e\), prepares a represented organization for an attempted explanatory use of \(c\), contains a nontrivial binding construction relevant to that use, and is not merely a composition of content-preserving transfers of the already operative organization. A structural account of that subhistory must connect the constructed binding to the resulting use. Electricity supply, incidental causal ancestry, and ownership of an output file do not by themselves meet the condition.

“Owned or controlled” refers to the boundary's actual action-producing organization. In a collaboration it can be distributed. A list of contributors is not a construction witness. Overlapping attributions are possible when their respective causal-organizational claims are true; they do not create a numerical credit allocation.

A learner may receive a complete exposition and nevertheless construct the bindings through which it becomes usable to that learner. That is reconstructive construction, not historical firstness. A relay may deliver the same exposition without such construction. A physically faithful copy of an already functioning cognitive organization, if permitted by the relevant physics, must not be pronounced a fresh invention merely because the recipient's address is new. The interpretation must distinguish transfer of an operative organization from its reconstruction.

The construction predicate is not an assertion that every acquisition must be creative. It is an explicit provenance condition to be established. All claims about functional copying remain subject to physical realization, including restrictions on what information attributes can be copied.

## Newness, acquisition, and reacquisition

Fix a system boundary, grain, history, and continuity criterion before assessing an event. Let

\[
R_{<e}(s,h)
=
\bigcup_{\xi\text{ before }e}R_{\beta,\ell}(s,\xi),
\]

including the initial repertoire within the stipulated continuity. Define

\[
\operatorname{New}(s,c,h,e)
\iff
\neg\exists d\in R_{<e}(s,h),
\ d\equiv_\ell c.
\tag{N}
\]

The equivalence is structural at the stated grain, not string equality or similarity. Forgotten prior understanding belongs to \(R_{<e}\) when the claim is first creation by that continuing organization. Reacquisition can therefore be an achievement without being first creation.

Acquisition concerns absence from the immediately prior deployable repertoire and presence afterward. Newness concerns the whole stipulated historical baseline. Construction concerns provenance. These relations can come apart.

An incomplete archive leaves a historical claim uncertain. It does not make the absent content historically new. Initializing a new system boundary with an already functioning organization must include that organization in its baseline rather than manufacture novelty by forgetting its initialization.

## An originative explanatory act

An attempted account is a represented organization actually used to address a recognized difficulty, whether or not it satisfies (E). Write this relation as \(\operatorname{Attempt}(s,c,p,h,e)\). Its interpretation includes the active question and the content's operative role; a task label alone is insufficient.

Define

\[
\operatorname{Origin}_{\beta,\ell}(s,c,p,h,e)
\iff
\operatorname{Attempt}(s,c,p,h,e)
\land\operatorname{New}(s,c,h,e)
\land\operatorname{Build}_{\beta,\ell}(s,c,h,e).
\tag{G}
\]

No truth, universal capacity, permanent storage, or completed criticism of this very output is a conjunct. A false explanatory conjecture can be originative. An account of an inherited theory's defect can be originative even though the original theory was not authored by the critic.

Randomness, deterministic generation, deduction, or search can participate in the physical process. They neither establish nor disqualify (G) by themselves. The relevant issue is whether the event constructs the attributed problem-specific organization, rather than merely producing an output that an external interpreter finds useful.

## Critical and creative episodes

A complete critical episode contains a recognized difficulty, a represented target available before the criticism of it, a conjectural objection, and a content-sensitive response with a resulting situation. The objection need not have actual bearing, and the response need not improve the situation. An incomplete engagement is represented without being called complete.

A creative critical episode is a complete critical episode containing an originative contribution under (G) connected to its inquiry. That contribution can precede or follow criticism of a different, inherited target. Conjecture-before-criticism remains a target dependence, not a global three-stage schedule.

Closing an episode is an engagement decision. It may accompany acceptance, rejection, suspension, disregard, or a decision to investigate elsewhere. It does not prove that the problem is solved, and the episode can be reopened or absorbed into another.

# Progress and knowledge without an aggregate merit function

## Obligations rather than a hidden score

An obligation \(o\) is a specified predicate on a situation and its interpreted target. Examples are having an adequate account of a feature available for use, distinguishing two previously conflated possibilities, ceasing to use a demonstrated circular argument, or possessing an account of why an original demand is underdetermined. The predicate may depend on (E), deployability, or actual use, but not on an undefined generic predicate of “goodness.”

An obligation is not automatically appropriate because a participant adopts it. The legitimacy of the purpose or requirement can itself be a question. An assessment of improvement must expose that dependence. A newly discovered problem may supply a new obligation, but its connection to the original difficulty must be accounted for rather than assumed from a shared label.

Let \(O\) be the explicitly claimed repair obligations and \(P\) the protected obligations for a comparison, both fixed for that comparison. Their definitions need not predate the discovery historically; they must not shift inside its assessment. Define a local repair by contribution \(\Delta\) as

\[
\begin{aligned}
\operatorname{Repair}_{O,P}(\xi,\xi';\Delta)
\iff{}&
\exists o\in O\,[\neg o(\xi)\land o(\xi')]\\
&\land\forall r\in P\,[r(\xi)\Rightarrow r(\xi')]\\
&\land\operatorname{ProducedBy}(\Delta,\xi,\xi';O).
\end{aligned}
\tag{P}
\]

Here \(\operatorname{ProducedBy}\) is a construction account of the actual change in the relevant obligation-bearing organization, using the history and structural account conditions already defined. It is not satisfied by temporal succession alone. A transport supplies the interpretation of \(o\) and \(r\) across changed representations; without one they are not silently compared.

Equation (P) does not rank alternatives. It states that a particular defect was repaired, that declared protected achievements were not lost, and that the contribution produced the change. Losses outside \(P\) must be exposed. The result is local progress, not an all-things-considered verdict.

When important losses conflict with the gains, their reconciliation is a further problem about purposes and obligations. This class does not manufacture a common currency or smuggle in “sufficient merit” as a circularly defined predicate. A participant can still decide and act under a fallible account of that conflict.

## Why adding an account is not enough

A system that acquires an account of a trivial feature while destroying its only account of the main difficulty has acquired something. It has not thereby improved the whole situation. Adding a member to a set of accounts does not establish (P) for a comparison that protects the lost achievement.

Conversely, progress need not increase the number of accepted claims. Withdrawing an unsupported numerical mass and replacing it with a correct account of underdetermination can improve the inquiry. The numerical question remains unresolved. The improvement concerns the removal of a false claim to have identified it and the acquisition of the relevant limitation.

## Created explanatory knowledge

Practical repair alone is insufficient for knowledge creation. In particular, an obligation such as “increase our confidence” must not make a false belief into knowledge. An **epistemic obligation** in this definition requires a correct structural account to be deployable in a specified problem-use respect, or requires the correction of an operative use through such a deployable account. Accounts of a defect, a scope distinction, a mistaken question, or an impossibility are included by taking that relation as the specified question. An endorsement count or an ungrounded satisfaction report is not such an obligation.

A contribution creates explanatory knowledge in the stated respect when it belongs to a creative critical episode, produces a repair under (P) of at least one epistemic obligation, and contains an originative, adequate, subsequently deployable contribution that participates in producing that repair. A creative but false side-remark cannot borrow credit from an unrelated correction in the same episode. The new content can be an account, an account of a defect, a revised interpretation, or an explained limitation.

Formally,

\[
\begin{aligned}
\operatorname{CreateEK}_{\beta,\ell,O,P}
(s,\Delta,h,e)
\iff{}&
\operatorname{CreativeCriticalEpisode}(s,\Delta,h,e)\\
&\land\operatorname{Repair}_{O,P}(\xi,\xi';\Delta)\\
&\land\exists o\in O_{\mathrm{ep}}\ \exists c,p_c,e_c\,\bigl[\\
&\qquad e_c\preceq_h e\\
&\qquad\land\neg o(\xi)\land o(\xi')\\
&\qquad\land\operatorname{Origin}(s,c,p_c,h,e_c)\\
&\qquad\land\operatorname{Account}(c,p_c)\\
&\qquad\land c\in\operatorname{Result}(\Delta)\\
&\qquad\land\operatorname{Deploy}_{\beta,\ell}(s,c,\xi';U_c)\\
&\qquad\land\operatorname{ProducesVia}(\Delta,c,o;\xi,\xi')\bigr].
\end{aligned}
\tag{EK}
\]

Here \(O_{\mathrm{ep}}\subseteq O\) is the set of epistemic obligations just defined; \(\preceq_h\) includes equality as well as causal precedence; and \(\operatorname{ProducesVia}\) requires the construction account of the repair to contain the relevant binding of \(c\) on its active route. These are additional conditions, not inferences from temporal co-occurrence. The indices \(\xi,\xi'\) identify the episode's stated comparison situations. For a community attribution, \(s\) denotes that composite and its integrated use, not an unspecified future reader. A result merely stored for a hypothetical reader can be a valuable artifact without establishing this possession claim.

The definition is factive about the claimed repair, not about every sentence in the resulting theory. It is fallible in the epistemological sense that any attribution and any wider claim can be mistaken. It does not call an actually false repair true until someone notices its failure.

Creation is historical. Subsequent destruction of every copy can make present deployment impossible without making the creative event not have occurred. Historical creation, current possession, artifact custody, and operative standing are separate relations.

## Choice and criticism without “Merit”

A participant's choice is an event. An argument for that choice is content. Whether that argument accounts for the relevant reasons is assessed through (E) at the choice question. These distinctions suffice for a semantics of fallible action.

There is no further generic predicate defined as “withstanding criticism well enough to merit preference.” Such a phrase otherwise makes preference explain merit and merit explain preference. Particular reasons for choosing can be substantive and criticizable without requiring a universal order on all alternatives.

No quantity of endorsements, surviving tests, repeated observations, or partitioned features enters (G), (P), or (EK) as an automatic warrant. Mathematical counts can appear inside an explanation of a particular counting problem or a physical resource condition. They are not outlawed as information, and no special count of “independent reach” is licensed to choose explanations by itself.

# Inexplicit, artistic, and normative inquiry

## Inexplicit representation is not absent representation

A pianist can imagine a passage without playing it. A geometer can manipulate a spatial relation without naming every component. An investigator can notice an inconsistency before articulating its premises. The semantic question is whether the relevant distinctions and transformations are instantiated and used, not whether they are written in a particular format.

The realization can use a partial, distributed, or temporally extended representation. Its relevant bindings can be available through memory, imagery, action rehearsal, or interaction with an artifact. Not every representation must be simultaneously explicit. Retaining a critical target can consist in being able to re-present the relevant distinction, not in storing a complete verbal transcript.

An organization with recursively available scrutiny must be able to reify a relevant aspect of its practice as a target under suitable conditions. It need not have an exhaustive representation of itself or simultaneously contain descriptions at infinitely many depths.

## The artistic case in full semantic form

Let \(A\) be a set of artworks or artistic actions, \(K\) a set of interpretive contexts, and \(F\) a domain of structurally described effects. A proposed effect organization is a relation

\[
\mathcal R\subseteq A\times K\times F.
\]

It may concern recognition of a recurring motif, anticipation of a continuation, contrast between an established pattern and a later event, or the relation between a visual organization and an interpretation. Its actual relation to a viewer or listener is an empirical or interpretive application claim, not established by the symbol \(\mathcal R\).

A purpose is a predicate \(G\subseteq K\times F\). Achievement of that purpose by \(a\) in context \(k\) can be defined, for a robust-all-effects claim, by

\[
\mathcal R[a,k]\ne\varnothing
\quad\land\quad
\mathcal R[a,k]\subseteq G[k].
\tag{AR}
\]

An existential claim that the work can produce an effect uses nonempty intersection instead. Those claims must not be interchanged. No scalar utility or maximizing selection is required.

For example, stipulate a composition in which a motif must remain recognizable while its later harmonic role changes. The represented organization can preserve the motif's interval relations while changing its placement or surrounding harmony. A conjecture that simply replaces the motif with unrelated material may achieve contrast but fail recognizability. A conjecture that repeats it unchanged in every role may preserve recognition but fail the intended transformation. An artist can discriminate those failures inexplicitly and construct a different organization.

A structural account must show which relations carry identity and which carry changed function, how the intended effect depends on their combination, and whether the proposed interpretive context is applicable. Merely reporting that the artist liked the revision does not establish (AR). Nor does satisfying (AR) establish that the aim was aesthetically worthwhile.

## Objective aesthetic claims

To ask about aesthetic value rather than effect or aim-achievement, introduce a substantive normative structure. Its carriers include works, contexts, reasons, and aesthetic respects. Its relation

\[
\mathcal N\subseteq A\times K\times\mathcal Rsn\times\mathcal V_A
\]

states that a reason bears on a work in an aesthetic respect. \(\mathcal Rsn\) is a set of reason contents; \(\mathcal V_A\) is a set of aesthetic respects, not an ordered scale. A normative theory supplies the conditions of application and defeat of those reasons. Its application is interpreted using the same organizational semantics as other rule or constraint applications.

An **objective** interpretation asserts that these normative facts do not become true merely through approval or enactment. An agent's normative theory \(\widehat{\mathcal N}\) can misrepresent \(\mathcal N\). A criticism can challenge its interpretation, relevance, or content. There is no requirement that all such conflicts have a mechanically selected winner.

This supplies a complete model-theoretic location for the aesthetic case: effects belong to \(\mathcal R\), purposes to \(G\), normative bearing to \(\mathcal N\), judgments to appraisal events, and creative contribution to (G) and (EK). None is defined as another. The class contains both mistaken and successful aesthetic inquiries when these data are supplied.

**A substantive boundary remains explicit.** This document does not derive the true extension of \(\mathcal N\) from non-normative physics. It does not identify objective beauty with satisfying an arbitrarily chosen standard. Giving a normative relation a mathematical carrier makes the semantics exact relative to that relation; it does not explain away the philosophical task of discovering which aesthetic reasons are true. Deutsch himself distinguishes objective beauty from reliable access to it and describes our knowledge of it as limited. [R7]

This is not an exception under which artistic creativity is excluded from the class. An artistic attempt and its critical organization can be characterized before its ultimate aesthetic success is settled, just as a scientific conjecture can be characterized before its truth is settled. A purported complete reductive theory of **objective aesthetic knowledge** would owe more than the present structural class.

# Constructor theory as a constitutive part of the semantics

## Physical tasks and information

A substrate is a physical system. An attribute is a set of its states. A task specifies permitted input-to-output attribute transformations, with required resources and allowed side effects made explicit. Task occurrence and retained ability to perform the task are different claims. Constructor-theoretic possibility concerns the absence of a law-imposed limit, short of perfection, on performance and retention of the relevant ability; it is not merely the existence of one successful trajectory. [R2]

An information variable is, in the constructor-theoretic treatment, a clonable computation variable. Computation variables are characterized through possible permutation tasks; distinguishability is then characterized using information variables rather than being assumed to define them. The proposed interoperability principle relates information-bearing substrates. This does not imply that every physical state, or an arbitrary unknown quantum state, can be copied. [R3]

These are imported physical commitments. The particular protocols and realization maps used below are FW5 modeling choices.

## Protocols and retained capability

A protocol \(\pi\) is an interpreted organization of controlled physical activity. Mathematically it may be a relation from an accessible finite history to permitted next actions. It need not be computable, verbally expressible, or consciously consulted. A physical attribution requires that its control organization be realized under \(\Theta\), not merely defined as an arbitrary mathematical function.

Let \(\operatorname{Exec}_\Theta(\pi,z,i;\chi)\) be the set of maximal executions admitted by the physical process theory and task-completion contract when the protocol starts in constructor state \(z\), receives task input \(i\), and operates under enabling conditions \(\chi\). Those conditions specify resources, communication, maintenance, and the disturbances against which a capability is claimed. They do not filter executions according to whether the desired answer occurred.

For an idealized task \(T\subseteq I\times O\) with nonempty legitimate-input domain, a nonempty constructor attribute \(C\), and a fixed protocol, define retained realization by

\[
\begin{aligned}
\operatorname{RetReal}(\pi,T,C;\chi)
\iff \forall z\in C\ \forall i\in\operatorname{dom}T\
\forall\eta\in\operatorname{Exec}_\Theta(\pi,z,i;\chi),\qquad\qquad\\
\eta\text{ completes with some }o\in T[i]
\text{ and a continuing constructor state }z'\in C.
\end{aligned}
\tag{CT1}
\]

A maximal execution is either a completed task episode or an infinite or deadlocked episode with no permitted continuation; an arbitrary unfinished prefix is not a maximal execution. The execution families must be nonempty on legitimate inputs. Deadlock is not success through an empty universal quantifier. External resources expended by the task are substrates or explicitly replenished inputs, not secretly inexhaustible attributes of the constructor.

Equation (CT1) is the exact ideal schema for retained capability under a stated disturbance contract. It does not claim that real devices are perfect under every physical fluctuation. A physical module supplies task-accuracy and retention-accuracy notions, their limiting families, and the corresponding approximate realizations. Constructor-theoretic possibility is asserted only when the permitted family has no bound short of perfection in either respect. A single finite successful run, or a longer battery, does not establish that limiting claim.

The accuracy relations belong to \(\Theta\). They measure physical task performance, not the probability that a conjecture is true. FW5 does not replace the constructor-theoretic limit with a success frequency.

## The retention fixed point

For a fixed protocol, task, and enabling contract, let \(F(C)\) contain the states for which execution families on legitimate inputs are nonempty and every such maximal execution completes the task and returns a constructor state in \(C\). Then

\[
C\subseteq F(C)
\tag{CT2}
\]

is the invariant-witness form of retained capability. If \(C\subseteq C'\), then \(F(C)\subseteq F(C')\): a return into the smaller set is a return into the larger one. Thus \(F\) is monotone. The union of all post-fixed sets is itself post-fixed and, by monotonicity, is its greatest fixed point.

**Proof of the final statement.** Let \(G=\bigcup\{C:C\subseteq F(C)\}\). Every member of \(G\) belongs to some such \(C\), hence to \(F(C)\subseteq F(G)\). Therefore \(G\subseteq F(G)\). Monotonicity gives \(F(G)\subseteq F(F(G))\), so \(F(G)\) is itself post-fixed and is included in \(G\). Hence \(F(G)=G\). ∎

This fixed point concerns retention, not the generation or truth of explanatory ideas. Writing it does not turn recursive criticism into a scheduler or establish computability of the set.

## Owned capability

An attributed system has a capability only when a suitable protocol is presently realized in its organization, or can be constructed by an admissible continuation attributable to that organization under the stated achievement condition. An external theorist's ability to describe a protocol is not the system's ability to perform it.

Let \(\operatorname{Owned}_\Omega(\xi,\pi)\) be the physical-organizational fact that the continuing system at \(\xi\) contains the relevant control organization. The fact is interpreted through its physical realization and history, not conferred by a metadata flag. Define

\[
\operatorname{Can}_{\Omega,\beta}(\xi,T;\chi)
\]

to require an owned retained realization of \(T\), or an owned, physically admitted construction of one, within the same declared continuity and resource contract. The exact or approximate grade of the claim must be specified. No appeal to a favorable but uncontrolled execution suffices as the entire capability account.

A protocol for creative inquiry can permit false conjectures, mistaken objections, failed attempts, and provisional stopping. Retained ability to conduct inquiry is not a retained infallible ability to recognize when an explanation is true. A stronger task requiring a particular successful advance must be separately specified and established.

## A first discovery is not a repeatable first discovery

An originative event is indexed to a system's actual prior repertoire. Repeating the same first discovery by that same continuing system is not a legitimate repeatable task: after the first event the novelty condition has changed. Constructor-theoretic retention must therefore be applied to the **inquiry-enabling organization**, not to the claim that one historically unique event occurs again and again.

Let \(J_p\) be the independently specified nonempty family of legitimate starting configurations for an achievement on \(p\). It includes the repertoire and history indices material to the achievement. Let \(C_I\) be a physically realized attribute for continuing inquiry, with its independent task interface and retained-capability contract. It is not defined as the set of states reached after a successful answer.

For the exact ideal, an owned protocol has an achievement capability when, for each starting configuration in \(J_p\), its maximal execution family is nonempty, every such execution completes with a history satisfying the stated achievement predicate, and its continuing organization belongs to \(C_I\). For a declared approximate grade, the physical module gives the performance condition on these execution families; semantic success is still the specified predicate, never an output label. Write this relation as

\[
\operatorname{CanAdv}_{\Omega,\beta}(\xi,p;\chi,J_p,C_I).
\tag{CA}
\]

The permitted protocol is presently owned or prepared by a finite owned physical history. Its control decisions use the accessible histories fixed by its realization; they cannot depend on hidden target answers supplied by the definition. Its achievement predicate is (EK), or the separately stated weaker achievement, evaluated on the resulting history. \(C_I\)'s retained tasks concern continuing inquiry and content use. They do not require a return to \(J_p\), deletion of the new knowledge, or a fresh invention of the identical content.

This definition can describe an organization that makes failed attempts during a successful extended inquiry. A weaker capacity to conduct inquiry without a guaranteed advance is a different task. No claim that either capability is physically realized follows from writing (CA). A one-time event needs a lawful occurrence, not an arbitrarily accurate constructor that reproduces its historical novelty.

## Physical realization of semantic organization

For every attributed use task, there must be a relation between the physically admitted process and the semantic organization that preserves the relevant role bindings and their transformations. This is an application of the functional or relational fidelity conditions, not just a mapping between initial and final strings.

Changing a represented target, replacing an objection with an equivalent recoding, or withdrawing a premise must have the consequences claimed by the semantic interpretation. A realization may be distributed across people, tools, memory, and artifacts. The boundary must include the parts necessary for the attribution, and its continuity criterion must explain their integration.

Representational fidelity and content correction concern different possible defects. A damaged inscription and a false theory are not the same error. That does not entail two anatomically separate mechanisms or prove that one physical network cannot perform both functions. A lower-level mechanism can participate in a higher-level organization without being sufficient for it in isolation.

## Knowledge that preserves its instantiation

Marletto's constructor-theoretic account characterizes knowledge as information able to act as an abstract constructor and contribute to preserving its instantiation. The analysis relates accurate construction under no-design laws to replicable recipes and vehicles. It is stronger than the observation that some object happened not to decay. [R4]

For an FW5 application, write

\[
\operatorname{CTK}_{\Theta,\mathcal E}(k)
\]

for this physical attribution, displaying the realization ecology \(\mathcal E\): information-bearing media, interpreting or executing vehicles, resources, repair, and reproduction processes. The index exposes conditions often left implicit; it is not a proposed correction to Marletto's wording.

A conditional bridge to explanatory knowledge can now be stated without identifying the two notions. Suppose an explanatory organization is instantiated as information \(k\); suppose an integrated vehicle uses it in a retained task organization that maintains or reconstructs relevant instantiations of \(k\); and suppose the information's specific organization contributes causally to that maintenance, rather than merely being inert material that is indiscriminately copied. Then the realization supplies an instance of the constructor-theoretic preservation role. Whether a particular explanatory practice satisfies those premises is a physical and organizational question.

No unconditional converse follows. An adaptation can have a preservation role without representing a criticism. A self-propagating mistaken idea can have a preservation mechanism while failing its claimed account of the world. Conversely, an explanatory discovery need not persist forever. A later loss is not a refutation of a past counterfactual capacity.

It is therefore a mistake to infer “extensional independence” of explanatory and physical knowledge merely from the pair of examples “a spreading false belief” and “a lost explanation.” The examples mix content, instantiation, current capability, and historical events. A comparison needs a common domain, a time, and a realization ecology before any logical implication can be assessed.

## The bridge that matters for creativity

Let \(\mathsf{Admit}^{q,r}_\Theta\) contain the tasks physically achievable at the declared performance and retention grades \((q,r)\), and let \(\mathsf{Cap}^{q,r}_\Omega(\xi)\) contain those with an owned realization or owned preparation at those grades. Then

\[
\mathsf{Cap}^{q,r}_\Omega(\xi)
\subseteq\mathsf{Admit}^{q,r}_\Theta.
\tag{CT3}
\]

A capability at one finite grade does **not** imply constructor-theoretic possibility at arbitrarily demanding grades. Define the unboundedly improvable owned repertoire by

\[
\mathsf{Cap}^{\infty}_\Omega(\xi)
=\bigcap_{q,r\in Q_\Theta}\mathsf{Cap}^{q,r}_\Omega(\xi).
\]

With the same task and resource-class conventions as (CT4),
\(\mathsf{Cap}^{\infty}_\Omega(\xi)\subseteq\mathsf{Poss}_\Theta\).
The first inclusion follows by forgetting ownership of a physical realization. The second follows by applying the first at every grade, which supplies the witnesses quantified in (CT4).

Knowledge acquisition can change these owned repertoires while \(\mathsf{Poss}_\Theta\) remains fixed. An explanatory correction can also change how an existing capability is used without adding a new endpoint task. That is why the semantic description retains internal organization and does not identify progress with growth of the task set.

The constructor-theoretic and epistemological descriptions meet at the construction, use, and maintenance of organized information. They answer different questions about that same process. Neither is an optional decoration on the other.

A biological lineage can acquire a preservation-enhancing organization without representing objections. It can instantiate the physical knowledge relation without a critical episode. An explanatory thinker adds represented conjectures, content-sensitive criticism, and possible scrutiny of its methods. The distinction is not between “physical” and “nonphysical” knowledge creation; it is between different physical-semantic organizations.

# Recursive openness and explanatory universality

## Continuing organizations

A continuity specification \(\Omega\) fixes which histories count as continuations of the attributed organization. It includes the boundary policy for new resources, tools, collaborators, and replacements. It need not freeze every component or memory state. It must not select a different already competent author for each problem and call those different authors one continuing system.

A criticism of the continuity specification is possible. A revised attribution then states its changed boundary. It does not rewrite who acted in the original history.

## Target closure and return

At situation \(\xi\), an aspect \(d\) of the organization's represented activity is **scrutinizable** when there is an owned, physically admitted continuation in which a description of \(d\) becomes a represented target, conjecture and criticism can be directed at it, and the result can affect its subsequent operative use. The scrutiny concerns the same \(d\), under a stated interpretation, not a similarly named surrogate.

Return relevance is a capability of the transition organization. There must be a path from a subsidiary result to the parent's relevant use, method, interpretation, or attention state. At least one alternative subsidiary result in the contract must permit a different use-state, unless the contract itself establishes that only one use is applicable. A decorative transcript channel permanently disconnected from the operative state does not satisfy the condition. No requirement says a failed objection must change the conclusion.

Recursive critical capacity requires closure under every finite continuation of such scrutiny within its declared domain, with role preservation and return. Resources may depend on the finite depth. The quantifiers are

\[
\forall n<\omega\ \forall\text{ admitted target chains of length }n,
\quad\exists\text{ an owned enabling continuation}.
\tag{RC}
\]

The target chains include criticisms, interpretations, methods, and prior appraisal practices. They are not restricted to object-level claims. Their admissibility is fixed by the domain and the operation of taking a represented use, or a criticism of that use, as a target. It is not defined as “chains the candidate already succeeds at.” The condition includes every such finite chain in the declared domain; it does not authorize silently deleting a chain because it concerns the actual evaluator. Equation (RC) does not require an actually completed infinite regress or simultaneous infinite storage. No internal perfect truth predicate is required: a statement about the system's own adequacy remains a represented conjecture, not automatic metatheoretic disquotation.

## Restrictions that are not yet represented

A causal restriction on what a system can do is not necessarily a represented premise whose “standing” can be withdrawn. A fixed component, a learned disposition, an access restriction, an attention practice, and an interpretation convention can all constrain activity. They are not therefore the same kind of constraint.

An organization may come to represent a restriction through a new model of its own behavior. The relevant condition is an owned route from encountering the difficulty to constructing that representation and then changing, bypassing, or correctly accepting the restriction. Exact access to every underlying parameter is unnecessary. A person can overcome an inadequacy in unaided memory by constructing a notation without rewriting the laws governing neurons.

A physically fixed transition rule is not automatically a subject-matter barrier. Fixed rules may implement a general interpreter whose represented procedures can change. Conversely, permission to edit every current parameter does not establish that the organization can understand every domain.

For a fixed organization, an **explanatory barrier** is an independently characterized domain of questions for which every admitted, non-question-begging enabling condition leaves the relevant owned understanding or inquiry capability unavailable. The barrier is defined by what it blocks across the domain, not merely by its being implemented in hardware or in frozen weights.

A proof of such a barrier can be a finite argument. A finite list of failures is not, by itself, that proof. A successful bypass can refute a proposed barrier without changing the physical laws or every component of the generator.

## Extensibility is not a historical certificate of universality

Let \(\mathfrak D\) be an independently specified family of subject domains, each a subset of the explanatory-content domain. Let

\[
\mathfrak D_0
=
\{D\in\mathfrak D:
\exists c\in D\cap R(s,\xi_0)\}.
\]

A historical domain-extension event reaches some \(D^*\in\mathfrak D\setminus\mathfrak D_0\) through a contribution attributable to the continuing organization, with an account of the changed representations, methods, or tools. A weaker repertoire-extension event creates a new deployable content without requiring a new subject domain.

These definitions do not compare a set of contents with a set of sets of contents. They do not define “outside the original domain” after observing where the system happened to succeed.

A historical extension event does not prove the capacity to extend into every subject. A universally capable organization need not have a particular historical extension event in the chosen observation period. It may already possess the relevant general method. Accordingly, no inclusion of the universal class in a class defined by a required past extension is asserted.

## Enabling conditions and achievement conditions

A capacity claim names the background learning, evidence, time, memory, tools, communication, and maintenance it permits. It also names the achievement at issue.

Understanding a supplied explanation may permit its complete communication. Reconstructing an understanding absent from the recipient requires the recipient's construction of the relevant use organization. Originating an unsupplied solution must not be witnessed by silently providing a complete functioning solution or a competent replacement thinker.

The restriction is not that the environment contribute no knowledge. Inquiry always has background. The restriction is that the contribution credited to the system must actually be made by that system under the declared boundary. A receipt of a complete explanation can contribute to a later genuinely creative reconstruction or extension, but it cannot establish historical origination of what was supplied.

An enabling condition is inadmissible if its only description is “the conditions under which the system succeeds.” It must identify physically and semantically meaningful resources and contributions independently of that success claim. It may be discovered during inquiry; it cannot hide the desired achievement as a condition.

## The universal class

Let \(\mathfrak E_\Theta\) contain explanatory contents across the subject matters admitted by the adopted reality model and physically representable at the relevant abstraction. Let \(\mathfrak P^{\mathrm{adv}}_\Theta\) contain coherently posed challenges for which explanatory advance is possible in principle. Both domains are specified independently of the candidate's successes. An unfamiliar topic may not be removed solely because the candidate lacks its concepts.

Let \(U_c\) be the task of understanding and critically using content \(c\), with its use contract, and \(A_p\) the achievement task of a creative critical advance on \(p\). Define

\[
\operatorname{UU}_{\Theta,\beta}(s,\Omega)
\iff
\forall c\in\mathfrak E_\Theta\ \exists\chi,
\operatorname{Enable}(s,U_c,\chi)
\land\operatorname{Can}_{\Omega,\beta}(\xi_0,U_c;\chi),
\tag{U1}
\]

and

\[
\operatorname{UC}_{\Theta,\beta}(s,\Omega)
\iff
\forall p\in\mathfrak P^{\mathrm{adv}}_\Theta\ \exists\chi,
\operatorname{Enable}(s,A_p,\chi)
\land\operatorname{CanAdv}_{\Omega,\beta}(\xi_0,p;\chi,J_p,C_I).
\tag{U2}
\]

The use tasks in (U1) concern correct reconstruction of the content's organization, not endorsement of that content as true. The achievement tasks in (U2) use the actual repair and provenance conditions. Their success must not be redefined as producing a success label. The physical grade required by each task belongs to its enabling contract. The starting family \(J_p\) and continuing-inquiry attribute \(C_I\) in (U2) are fixed by the achievement contract, not selected afterward to exclude failures. Historical novelty is required at the achievement event, not on every subsequent use of its result.

The universal explanatory class is

\[
\mathsf{UECS}
=
\{(M,s,\Omega,\beta):
M\models\operatorname{RC}(s,\Omega)
\land\operatorname{UU}_{\Theta,\beta}(s,\Omega)
\land\operatorname{UC}_{\Theta,\beta}(s,\Omega)
\}.
\tag{U3}
\]

The same \(\Omega\), boundary policy, and attribution criterion remain fixed across the quantifiers. The contents and owned tools can develop. The definition demands neither that every attempt succeed nor that every explanation be simultaneously possessed, nor that a thinker solve an impossible or incoherent demand.

The conjecture that universal explanation arrives through a qualitative jump is compatible with this class. Equality between “has once extended itself” and (U3) is not adopted as a theorem. It would require a substantive argument that rules out every remaining independently specified barrier.

# The semantic class collected

## Primitive data

An FW5 model supplies physical and historical data \(\Theta,\mathcal H\), organization structures \(D\) satisfying (O), content and occurrence domains, the representation interpretation at declared grains and boundaries, question contracts, actual control and appraisal histories, a continuity specification, and any normative relations invoked by its questions.

More explicitly, the physical module \(\Theta\) includes substrate state spaces, attribute sets, allowed process histories, controlled-action interpretations, admitted resources and disturbance contracts, and a relation of approximate realization. Let its accuracy domain \(Q_\Theta\) be a directed preorder of performance grades, with increasingly demanding grades short of perfection. The primitive physical relation

\[
\operatorname{Approx}_\Theta
(\pi,T,C,\chi;q,r)
\]

states task performance to grade \(q\) and retained ability to grade \(r\), with the operational meaning of those grades fixed by the subsidiary physical theory. It is monotone toward weaker grades. The constructor-theoretic possibility assertion is

\[
T\in\mathsf{Poss}_\Theta
\iff
\forall q,r\in Q_\Theta\ \exists\pi,C,\chi,
\operatorname{PhysicallyConstructible}_\Theta(\pi,C;\chi)
\land\operatorname{Approx}_\Theta(\pi,T,C,\chi;q,r).
\tag{CT4}
\]

The resource and side-effect class for \(T\) is held fixed in this statement. An infinitely expensive or otherwise disallowed resource is not smuggled in by the existential quantifier. Equation (CT1) is the corresponding exact ideal schema, not a claim that every physical module attains perfection.

For an owned capability at a declared grade, the protocol and constructor attribute in (CT4) must either be present in the initial organization or be reached by a **finite, owned construction history** under the same enabling contract. That history must start with presently owned processes and admitted resources, not with an unexplained constructor for the desired achievement. This fixes the meaning of \(\operatorname{Can}\) without an infinite regress of available constructors. Physical preparation here is a composition of owned physical processes. It is not defined by the later semantic authorship predicate \(\operatorname{Build}\); whether the preparation is itself a creative achievement is a separate attribution. Higher grades may require further owned construction and further declared resources.

The controlled-action and process-port interpretation fixes what physical ownership and an active process occurrence mean. The model does not recover those facts from filename ownership or timestamps. These are physical primitives, not explanatory-success primitives. Their application is a theory of the physical system. Representation is a separate semantic primitive about what its states represent. Normative interpretation, when invoked, is a separate primitive about reasons in the relevant respect. All three are exposed to criticism through represented claims; none is made true by being entered into the model.

## Defined relations and dependence order

Organization solutions and query profiles are defined by (O) and (Q). Structural accounting is defined by (E), including its functional or relational fidelity clause. Supports, contribution, interference, and preservation are defined from accounting by (S), (B), (D), and (H).

Critical bearing is accounting for the specified defect question. Reason use is a representation-preserving map into an active response suborganization. Use and standing are properties of enacted applications and appraisals. Receipts describe the provenance of arguments for claims, not their truth.

Retained and owned capability are interpreted by the protocol, invariant, and physical-accuracy constructions. Deployability and understanding apply those constructions to represented content-use tasks. Construction identifies a nontrivial binding preparation in an actual attributed history. Newness compares that result with the stated earlier repertoire. Origin is (G).

Repair is the actual, attributable change in specified obligation satisfaction in (P). Created explanatory knowledge is (EK). Recursive openness is (RC); universal understanding and inquiry are (U1) and (U2).

There is no dependency of the basic definition of accounting on origin, progress, knowledge, or universality. There is no definition of knowledge by an appraisal that already presupposes knowledge. A represented claim about a definition is an object of inquiry, not a recursive call that must terminate before the definition can be used.

## Membership

The **base class** \(\mathsf{FW5}\) consists of interpretations supplying the data above, respecting their typing, and satisfying the physical-realization and reference-coherence conditions whenever a physical attribution is made. It can describe irrational systems, false conjectures, obstructed inquiry, missing evidence, and failed attempts.

The **creative-episode class** consists of base interpretations with an actual instance of (G) connected to a critical episode. The **knowledge-creation class** consists of base interpretations with an actual instance of (EK). The **recursive-capacity class** additionally attributes (RC) to a continuing organization. The **universal class** is (U3).

An individual event need not belong to a universally capable system to be originative under (G). Universal capacity need not be demonstrated by the event. The class definitions preserve that distinction rather than treating a small creative act as a certificate of personhood, consciousness, moral standing, or universal intelligence.

# Results and discriminating constructions

## Equivariance under genuine recoding

Suppose all carriers, component relations, role bindings, maps, histories, question contracts, and attribution indices are transported along bijections preserving their structure. Then the truth of (E), (G), (P), and (EK) is preserved.

**Proof.** The bijections carry satisfying valuations to satisfying valuations in (O). Images and compositions in (F), (C), and (A) commute with those bijections. Critical contrasts and non-vacuity are preserved. The historical order, represented bindings, repertoire membership, and equivalence at the fixed grain are preserved, so construction and newness have the same witnesses. Obligation truth and its produced change are preserved. Each displayed conjunction therefore has the same truth value. ∎

The result does not apply to arbitrary compression, loss of event identity, a changed boundary, or a coarsening that identifies a relevant distinction. Those operations are not the stipulated bijections.

## A complete output description need not determine explanatory organization

Let \(P\) be a projection from full organization models to an observed input–output description, and let \(\phi\) be a fixed account-claim about a candidate and question. Suppose \(M_0\) and \(M_1\) satisfy

\[
P(M_0)=P(M_1),
\qquad M_0\models\phi,
\qquad M_1\not\models\phi.
\]

Then no function of \(P(M)\) alone agrees with the accounting predicate on both models.

**Proof.** Equal inputs to a function have equal outputs, while the required outputs differ. ∎

Parallel and priority wiring provide a concrete instance when the projection retains only endpoint values and the question concerns the active route. The same argument applies to attribution from identical emitted text and to semantic use inferred from delivery logs.

The theorem identifies missing information in a projection. It does not say that no physical or organizational evidence can ever establish the distinction, or that inventing a new mathematical notation is necessary to express it.

## A conditional bridge from argument dependence to explanatory work

Suppose an application \(u\) is an actual explanatory route for \(p\), with support \(W_u\in\mathsf S_{E,p}\). Suppose removal of \(d\in W_u\), under the stated restriction operation and the same interpretation, prevents that route from accounting for \(p\). Then \(\{d\}\) is a critical block for \(W_u\).

**Proof.** These are precisely the two membership conditions in (B). ∎

Without the explanatory-route premise, inferential essentiality does not imply explanatory work for the same question. The shadow-to-height calculation can essentially use the shadow length while doing no production work on the pole's height. The conditional bridge is useful; the unconditional identification is false.

## No promotion from record state to truth

Take two base models with identical receipt sets and appraisal events, but different interpretations of an unverified application premise. In one, the premise holds; in the other, it does not. The record states are the same and the account-claim can differ in truth.

Therefore record-state equality does not entail equality of semantic satisfaction without the relevant application premises. This is compatible with reliable checks of narrower record facts. It is not an argument that quotations, calculations, or experiments are epistemically useless.

## A nonempty finite episode interpretation

Consider a stipulated investigator whose initial represented theory contains the additive two-balances equations and an unsupported current use of \(b_B=0\). Its initial repertoire lacks the represented connection between the measurement fibre and the non-identification of mass. Its physical control organization can combine equations, represent a vector, relate a null direction to a questioned feature, and change a working use. Those prior general operations are not stipulated to contain every result they can produce.

In the episode, the investigator constructs \(v=(1,-1,-1)\), verifies \(Av=0\), and binds the nonzero mass component of \(v\) to the live identification question. It also binds \((0,-1,1)\cdot v=0\) to the offset-difference question. The response withdraws the unsupported reference use and makes the fibre explanation deployable. A counterfactual calibration row removes that particular null direction and changes the represented conclusion. A content-preserving recoding changes neither the represented dependence nor the response's use of it.

The mathematical account is the fibre construction already proved. The newly constructed binding and its actual use witness (G) at the stipulated grain and baseline. The response belongs to a critical episode. The repair obligation is possession of the limitation account and withdrawal of the unsupported identification route; the protected obligation includes retaining the original readings and the still-open mass question. These witness (P) and (EK).

A toy physical module may realize these finite symbolic operations with explicit working storage and replenished resources. Such a module is a relative consistency witness for an episode interpretation, not evidence that a scripted demonstrator, an actual language model, or a human brain has been experimentally shown to satisfy the class. The investigator's representation and control assumptions are part of the witness and are not derived from printed labels.

## Recursion does not entail universal reach

Construct a family of organizations that can represent and criticize arbitrary finite chains of methods concerning one declared domain, with return to their actual uses, but whose owned processes and all admitted extensions lack a representation route to a second independently specified domain. The recursive condition holds in the first domain; (U1) fails in the second.

This separates recursive structure from universality. Removing the barrier requires an organizational account, not merely permission to write a new topic name. Making every current rule editable also does not, on its own, establish that the missing representation route exists.

## Finite performance and universal attribution

A finite collection of successful episodes does not entail universal capacity without organizational premises. Where the model class admits both an organization continuing generally and an organization matching those episodes but stopping at an unobserved barrier, the recorded outcomes do not distinguish them.

This does not prohibit a finite **argument** about the architecture from establishing a barrier or supporting a universality conjecture. The unsupported step is from a finite outcome sample alone to an unrestricted capacity claim. A proof about an organization and a count of its successes are different evidence.

## Neither probability nor computability is secretly derived

None of (E), (G), (P), (EK), or (U3) depends on an empirical-frequency update or a merit maximization. No probability-of-truth theorem follows from the fact that a constructor or a hypothesis has worked repeatedly.

The relations can have finite or infinite interpretations and can be decidable or undecidable in particular presentations. No conclusion about their computability follows merely from being recursive, critical, explanatory, or physically represented. Conversely, a computational realization is not excluded merely because it is computational.

# Why a new branch of mathematics is not yet required

The recurring difficulties are largely failures to distinguish objects, indices, directions of dependence, or quantifier strengths. Sets of structured relations can distinguish redundant routes, collective infinitary support, interference, semantic roles, controlled histories, and modal capabilities. Non-computability does not require a different foundation. The present class uses existing mathematics while changing what the mathematics is asked to represent.

There is also published work expressing constructor-theoretic tasks and their implementations using sets, relations, and symmetric monoidal process theories. That supplies a concrete reason not to infer a need for new mathematical foundations merely from constructor theory's counterfactual language. The possible novelty here is a proposed **semantic organization of familiar mathematics**. # What could defeat this class

## A structural account that still does not explain

The most important challenge is a fully specified candidate satisfying (E), with true target anchoring, the original question preserved, substantive contrasts, and no conclusion smuggling, which nevertheless provides no explanatory account. That would refute the sufficiency claim. A genuine explanation that cannot satisfy the conditions under any interpretation preserving its actual organization would refute necessity.

Necessary mathematical truths and non-causal explanations are demanding cases. A variation that changes an axiom is a well-defined mathematical comparison, not necessarily a possible physical intervention. But the availability of some edited theory must not be used to pretend that every proof thereby answers every mathematical why-question. The declared query and the real organization of the argument remain essential.

These challenges concern the constitutive conjecture, not a missing proof of a theorem asserted to follow from set theory. The distinction matters: a definition can be exact while being a poor theory of its intended subject.

## Representation and apparent reason use

The explicit representation interpretation is not eliminated. If the fidelity and integration conditions admit a system whose apparent reasons are merely disconnected decorations, the reason-use account is too weak. If they exclude a genuine inexplicit or distributed understanding, it is too strong. No output-only test settles the matter.

The formal model must keep enough internal organization to make these criticisms specific. Merely attaching an interpretation label to a fixed response policy is not the required construction or realization. The substantive question is whether the represented distinctions actually participate in its uses and changes.

## Progress can be mis-scoped

Equation (P) is intentionally local. A dishonest choice of protected obligations can conceal severe losses. That does not make the local conjunction mathematically false; it makes an expanded claim of overall progress unsupported. The proposed knowledge-creation condition adds an adequate, newly constructed account on the actual repair route precisely to prevent unrelated novelty, mere confidence, or an endorsement from supplying the missing epistemic achievement.

A defensible case of knowledge creation that contains no such contribution would challenge (EK). A case satisfying every clause while only producing a mistaken epistemic repair would challenge the account or the bridge from it to the obligations. It must not be dismissed by inventing a favorable new grain after the fact.

## Normative content is not derived by a change of notation

The class does not derive objective aesthetic facts from non-normative data. A relation of genuine aesthetic reasons is a declared substantive input when such facts are claimed. This is a limit on the present explanation, not an excluded domain and not a concealed assertion that approved art is objectively good.

A more reductive theory must supply and defend that bridge. The present document supplies neither a proof that such reduction is impossible nor a promise that a new branch of mathematics would achieve it. It states exactly which part of an aesthetic success attribution depends on it.

A counterexample to (M1), (M2), (I2), (O1), (T2), or the retention fixed-point result while all stated assumptions hold would expose a mathematical error. Finding that real inquiries do not satisfy those assumptions instead restricts the application; it does not refute the conditional theorem.

# Mathematical checking performed for this document

The displayed finite monotone identities were checked by exhaustive enumeration of all nonconstant monotone Boolean support families on two, three, and four commitments. There were respectively **4, 18, and 166** families. Critical membership equalled the union of minimal supports, and whole-set indispensability equalled their intersection, in every enumerated case. Exact symbolic arithmetic checked the balances matrix's rank as **2**, its null direction as \((1,-1,-1)\), the unchanged rank after three copies of the readings, and rank **3** after adding the calibration row \((0,1,0)\).
