# What miniReason can establish

The project explores Mini's configuration space and tests the designated [FW5 explanatory-construction source](sources/FW5-explanatory-construction.md). Language expression, formal feedback and template chains are subset studies. Prose, Lean-oriented and non-Lean proposals are legitimate specimens; correct capture and successful syntax are not admission gates. Engineering results and semantic attributions answer different questions.

The stored ECS 2.0 PDF is **preserved unchanged** as a historical artifact. Conjecture–Criticism Harness v1.3 remains a mechanism reference. ## Claims and their dependencies

A delivered answer is evidence of delivery. A schema check establishes an interface fact. An exact verifier establishes a bounded proposition under its contract. Stronger claims require the corresponding source conditions.

| Claim | FW5 conditions and source section | Evidence that does not suffice |
|---|---|---|
| Account | Anchoring; fidelity including structural equality and edit composition; question fidelity including the respect; non-circular dependence; non-vacuity. See “A structural answer,” equation (E) | Correct output, plausible mechanism words or a successful parser |
| Reason use | A role-preserving map from the represented objection into an active response route, with content-governed recoding and change contrasts. See “Reason use is causal organization, not an output comparison” | Prompt inclusion, a citation, self-reported usefulness or endpoint differences alone |
| Understanding/deployment | Represented content integrated into owned problem-directed uses, relevant bindings and discriminations, availability as a conjectural target, and the stated retained-capability conditions. See “Realized use rather than logical omniscience” | Storage, a generic simulator or hypothetical use by a future reader |
| Origin | Attempt AND historical New AND Build. Build needs attributable nontrivial binding construction relevant to attempted use. See “An originative explanatory act,” (G) | A fresh string, copying a functioning solution, or an incomplete prior archive |
| Repair | At least one failed obligation becomes satisfied; protected prior successes remain; the contribution produces the change. See “Obligations rather than a hidden score,” (P) | One fixed example, temporal succession or undisclosed losses |
| Created explanatory knowledge | Creative critical episode, attributable epistemic repair, originative adequate contribution, contribution membership in Result(Δ), origin event no later than the creation event, subsequent deployment and active ProducesVia connection. See “Created explanatory knowledge,” (EK) | Unrelated novelty in a successful run or a proposed rule merely archived |
| Recursive critical capacity | Every admitted finite target chain has an owned continuation preserving roles and operative return. See “Target closure and return,” (RC) | One completed return, a queue entry or permission to edit parameters |
| Universal/constructor capability | Independent domain and enabling quantifiers, owned construction, physical possibility and retained capability. See constructor and universal-class sections | Any finite benchmark or single originative event |

Anchoring is one of the five Account conjuncts. Fidelity includes both (F) and (C); splitting them while omitting anchoring misstates equation (E). Meaning, component roles, question and contrast scope are fixed across the comparison. The target answer cannot be copied into a boundary or renamed as a law. Baseline consistency and at least one substantive commitment-changing contrast are required.

A false conjecture can be originative. Origin does not require truth, permanent retention or completed criticism of that output. A complete critical episode may end in retention, rejection, suspension or continued uncertainty. Invalid objections can be understood and used; validity and use remain separate. These distinctions prohibit a single creativity/correctness/closure field.

## Declare the interpretation before the evidence

Name the relevant source claim, represented occurrences and role bindings, target organization, component edit semantics, question and respect, grain, attributed boundary, history/continuity, enabling tools and resources, contrasts, and any normative relation invoked. These are criticizable interpretation claims, not facts made true by a registry entry. Declare unused dependencies rather than inventing them.

The usual attributed boundary includes model, Mini, configuration, admitted tools and operative state. Operator-provided language primitives, decoders and finished information are enabling contributions. Model-only attribution needs a separate argument. Public artifacts reveal their content and configured use, not hidden native reasoning or an exhaustive account of model internals.

Changing the question, grain, anchors or protected obligations makes a new claim. It cannot rescue the same assessment after adverse evidence. Historical New concerns the earlier deployable repertoire under stipulated continuity; a new directory or unseen test instance does not establish absence from that repertoire. Reconstruction, transfer, acquisition and first origin must be distinguished.

## Construction, use and operative return

Choose a downstream use whose need for the proposed relation has an independent argument. An identical local observation compatible with two states requiring different answers is a useful ambiguity witness. A ready-made correct rule supplied as a menu tests selection or transfer; it does not establish unrestricted construction.

Preserve candidate bytes, the proposed binding, its material realization and subsequent use. Compare the same proposed content on the active use route with identical archived content unavailable to that route. Also preserve full-source reconstruction controls: withholding information is a distinct intervention, and cannot establish a Mini advantage over a fully informed baseline. Unknown or inadequate constructions remain results; an operator must not insert a reference solution and credit it to the model.

Return must concern the same parent's use, interpretation, method or attention under a stated correspondence. Suspension can be an operative result. Relevant alternative subsidiary results must permit different use-states unless only one use is applicable. Recoding, changed-content and irrelevant-carrier contrasts help assess content dependence; they do not replace an interpreted active route. Redundancy can conceal use in endpoint invariance. One finite instance cannot prove (RC).

Freeze repair obligations and protected uses. Expose losses outside the protected set. A failed prediction concerns the candidate together with auxiliaries and interpretation; criticism can land on the task, translation, checker, evidence or attention practice. Prose criticism remains legitimate when an executable route fails.

## Support, variation and measurement

FW5 allows nonmonotone, redundant and infinitary support families. Collective work need not reduce to singleton contribution; a finite monotone theorem applies only under its premises. Its preservation result fixes organization, interpretation and variation family. Reach or edit-survival counts supply no numerical warrant. ECS 2.0's intrinsic family determined through anchoring is a separate hypothesis, not a FW5 conclusion.

Calls, tokens, elapsed time, exact task outcomes and configuration differences can describe finite engineering effects. A scalar novelty/progress score cannot adjudicate content. Capture alarms concern behavior under declared assumptions, not truth or creativity.

## Testing the theory itself

A sufficiency challenge must satisfy all five Account conjuncts with true anchoring, original question, substantive contrasts and no conclusion smuggling, while an independent case argument establishes absence of explanation. A necessity challenge needs a genuine explanation for which no interpretation preserving its actual organization satisfies the conditions. Failure of one encoding is insufficient.

Mathematical, non-causal and interpretive cases are promising stress domains. A tedious proof is not a counterexample merely because another is illuminating. Conversely, changing the why-question into an easier output question cannot protect a failed candidate. Record the strongest attack, defense and exact unresolved premise.

Keep representation/integration and knowledge-creation challenges separate: decorative reasons admitted as operative, genuine inexplicit understanding excluded, all formal (EK) clauses passing despite an independently defensible absence of genuine epistemic repair, or genuine creation excluded. Proposed gap-filling amendments are separate criticizable contributions. Neither model agreement nor an exact-output oracle substitutes for an independent explanation argument.
