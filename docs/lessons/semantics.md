# Semantic lessons

ECS §5.2 requires sensitivity to relevant content, not merely causal carriage of an artifact ID. A content-preserving recoding and a content-changing contrast on an active route are stronger probes than observing that a port was populated.

ECS §6.2's novelty relative to a prior deployable repertoire cannot be decided for an LLM's entire hidden pretraining history. A run can establish a narrower chronological construction trace without settling that broader question.

The construction design review exposes an initialization issue: a prior criticism can already contain the repair later attributed to a new response. Include the whole supplied criticism and both language declarations in the baseline when inspecting what a later construction adds. Equal access to that material controls differential information across arms, but does not make a WHL-focused starting issue an unbiased comparison of intrinsic language capacity. Queuing and selecting its unresolved content remain decisions to investigate, not certifications of bearing or repair.

E015 distinguishes source custody from accurate attribution during revision. Every raw occurrence remained intact, yet one revision adopted a changed position while attributing it to its original J, and a later promotion attributed a current criticism to the earlier E009 episode. Conversely, Mini-native supplied a qualified textual correction while preserving an independently checked availability example. These are different observed mechanisms, not a single improvement score. See INT-001 through INT-003 and the E015 substantive review. Criticism exposure and a plausible response relation still need controlled recoding/content-change tests before stronger reason-use claims.

E013 shows that a supported criticism can coexist with an unsupported one in the same occurrence. A revision can fix endpoint polarity while keeping a definition too weak to express attainment, or separate decision adequacy from compatibility while accepting an unproved restriction on structured answers. Review the commitment actually stated and its finite counterexample; do not credit a whole revision from one correct change. See INT-004–INT-006 and the E013 recovery supplement. Expression failures may be introduced by an interpretation of a language rather than by its declared resources.

E016 shows that a use-stage answer can recover correct finite consequences from explicit task data despite tension in the returned account. Separate an account's commitments, their attribution and the final task answer. A correct final number cannot establish that the account caused the answer; the frozen no-return control directly addresses that local necessity claim. All criticisms remain fallible, including operator review.


## 2026-09-19 — Holes in the explanatory-construction semantics found by two independent witnesses

Evidence: the [adjudication](../reviews/2026-09-19-fable-cross-examination/15%20Adversarial%20cross-examination%20-%20findings%20and%20adjudication.md), sections A, E3, F and G, against the [standalone rewrite of FW5](../reviews/2026-09-19-fable-cross-examination/10%20Claude%20Fable%20Semantics%20-%20standalone%20theory.md). The rewrite is not FW5; several findings carry over because they concern shared definitions, and each is marked as such in the adjudication. Two models with different training reached the same list independently, which is evidence about the text, not about either model.

Representation defined by "a faithful transport with selected or constructed provenance", with construction defined by "a represented target", is a definitional cycle; the first constructed representation is impossible or the order is not well-founded. Stratify by layer: the primitive layer's content identity is not the representation predicate.

The organisation a physical occurrence instantiates at a grain is a parameter, not a consequence of physics as stated: it must return ports, components and every component relation, an occurrence can instantiate several organisations at one grain, and nothing constrains admissible grains. A "two primitives" claim that hides a decomposition oracle and free grains is false as written; count the organisation interpretation as a declared parameter and constrain grains.

"Kind is nothing over and above signature under admitted changes" makes "(F1) entails same kind" analytic; the corollary that "an anchoring condition adds nothing" is question-begging against an opponent who holds that a kind-label discriminates where no admitted change does. The steelman wins on the full physically admitted contract: anchoring is component fidelity on that contract, and there are two grades of adequacy (account at C; anchored). Historical kinds (predicates on history) are a second kind of kind that signatures on a contract cannot express.

Two candidates satisfying the fidelity and question conditions are not "one account": the claim fails for different decompositions and for different active commitments; a read-only memory satisfies all four conditions unless the anchor map is required to be a decomposition. "Surprise requires an incomplete history" unpacks the definition of surprise and is not a derivation. Underdetermination of a selected transport off its history is "no guarantee", not fallibility, and needs a richness hypothesis on the population.

A contract must be able to contain declared counterfactual edits with stated meaning (impossibility explanations, historical contrasts) and must not be able to exclude only the embarrassing physically admitted edits: non-circular dependence catches total protection only, and objectivity needs a separate contract-adequacy condition. The transport orientation in the representation predicate and in the account predicate are inverted relative to each other as written.

Neither frozen experiment instantiates a definition of the semantics as stated; each tests proxies (prediction accuracy, non-regression, input dependence, code path) that the design named with the semantics' words ("faithful", "provenance", "needed"). Experimental labels must not collide with the semantics' condition names, and every proxy must be declared as one.
