Non-Lean Semantic Class: **Rival-Story Semantics (RSS)**

RSS is a non-Lean, noncomputable semantic class for the frozen warehouse prose. It is not a proof assistant language. It is a way of assigning meanings to statements about a replicated warehouse snapshot by treating the snapshot as a collection of rival stories rather than as a database. It is deliberately noncomputable: it does not provide an algorithm for resolving conflicts, and it treats any demand for a single answer as a projection that must be justified outside the semantics.

**Primitives**

1. **Story**: a story is a claim about what is promised for a booking. A story is not a record. Records are evidence for stories. A story has a booking, a revision, a kind (hold or release), a quantity, an expiry, and a provenance (which delivery or set of deliveries it is drawn from). Provenance is not ordered; it is a set of delivery events.

2. **Bag**: an unordered collection of deliveries. A delivery is an event that makes a record available. Deliveries are not ordered, may be repeated, and may differ in logical time if they come from different replicas. The bag is the raw material, not the story.

3. **Coordinate**: a capacity and a logical time. A coordinate can be shared or replica-relative. RSS treats coordinates as parameters of a viewpoint, not as absolute facts.

4. **Viewpoint**: a pair (coordinate, bag). A viewpoint is what a reasoner sees. Different viewpoints may disagree about logical time, capacity, and which deliveries are present.

5. **Conflict**: a situation in which two or more stories for the same booking at the same greatest revision are not exact duplicates. Conflict is not error; it is material.

6. **Liveness**: a story is live under a viewpoint if it is not superseded by a higher revision for the same booking within that viewpoint, and if its kind and expiry are considered under the viewpoint's reading of expiry.

7. **Reading**: a reading is a policy that decides how to treat expiry, releases, duplicates, and ties. Readings are not part of the snapshot; they are supplied by reasoners. RSS does not choose a reading. It compares readings.

8. **Projection**: a function from a viewpoint and a reading to a single answer (for example, a single availability number). Projections are allowed but not privileged. A projection is always a loss of information.

**Relations**

- **Exact duplication**: two deliveries are exact duplicates if all record fields and provenance are equal. Exact duplicates add no authority.
- **Near-duplication**: two deliveries share booking and revision but differ in kind, quantity, or expiry. Near-duplicates are candidates for conflict. RSS does not decide whether they are duplicates or alternatives; it records the disagreement.
- **Supersession**: for a given booking, a story with higher revision supersedes a story with lower revision. Supersession is structural within a reading; whether it is structural across readings is a matter of conjecture.
- **Expiry effect**: under one reading, an expired hold reserves zero but remains superseding; under another, an expired hold is deleted and lower revisions can become live. RSS represents both as readings, not as facts.
- **Release effect**: under one reading, a release at a lower revision cannot cancel a hold at a higher revision; under another, cancellation is possible. RSS represents both as readings.
- **Alternative compatibility**: two stories from different bookings may be compatible or incompatible depending on unstated physical resources. RSS marks this as invisible unless a viewpoint includes a resource model.
- **Negative availability**: availability is a derived quantity that can be negative under a reading. RSS treats the sign as a signal, not an error, and treats the set of reservations as more informative than the sign.

**Combinations**

RSS combines primitives into **accounts**. An account is a viewpoint, a reading, and a set of accepted conjectures. Accounts are not merged. Disagreement between accounts is a first-class object. An account can be criticized by another account, and the criticism targets a specific element: a record, a rule, a booking history, a snapshot, a coordinate, or an assumption.

RSS also allows **inquiry states**. An inquiry state is a collection of accounts plus a set of open questions. Open questions are not solved by RSS; they are carried forward. A conjecture is open if no account has committed to it or refuted it. A commitment is an account's acceptance of a conjecture. A refutation is an account's production of a counterexample or a derivation of inconsistency within its own reading.

**Interpretation**

A statement in the frozen prose is interpreted in RSS by locating its primitives and relations. For example, "duplicate deliveries convey no additional authority" is interpreted as: exact duplicates are inert in all readings; near-duplicates are not covered by this statement and remain candidates for conflict. "A higher revision supersedes lower revisions" is interpreted as: within a booking, supersession is structural under all readings; across bookings, it has no force. "Availability can be negative" is interpreted as: under readings that select reservations, derived availability may be negative; RSS does not treat this as an error. "People may disagree about whether uncertainty should be retained" is interpreted as: different accounts may adopt different readings, and RSS does not rank them.

RSS interpretation is noncomputable because it does not specify an algorithm for selecting a reading or resolving conflicts. It specifies what would count as an interpretation, and it allows multiple interpretations to coexist.

**Conjectures, commitments, refutations**

- A **conjecture** is a proposition about an account or a class of accounts. It may be about a reading, a rule, a record, a snapshot, or an assumption. Conjectures are not required to be true.
- A **commitment** is an account's acceptance of a conjecture. Commitment is local to an account. Different accounts may commit to incompatible conjectures.
- A **refutation** is one of: (a) a counterexample within an account's own viewpoint; (b) a derivation of inconsistency within an account's own reading; or (c) a demonstration that a conjecture depends on an assumption that another account rejects. Refutations are local to accounts and do not force global agreement.

**What RSS permits**

RSS permits the representation of uncertainty without forcing resolution. It permits multiple readings of expiry and release to coexist. It permits criticism to target specific elements. It permits negative availability as a signal. It permits the distinction between exact and near-duplicates. It permits the distinction between a rule criticism and a data criticism. It permits the idea that the snapshot is a merge of replica views, without deciding union, intersection, or single-view.

**What RSS may obstruct**

RSS may obstruct efficient decision-making because it refuses to produce a single answer. It may obstruct comparison between accounts because it lacks a common metric. It may obstruct automation because it is noncomputable and requires human or external choice. It may obstruct reasoning about physical resources because it does not include a resource model unless one is added as a primitive. It may obstruct reasoning about time across replicas because it treats logical time as viewpoint-relative without a mechanism for aligning viewpoints. It may obstruct the very idea of a snapshot as a single object, because it treats a snapshot as one viewpoint among many.

**Unresolved expressive limitations**

RSS cannot express the exact provenance of a delivery unless provenance is added. RSS cannot express physical resource sharing beyond what an account includes. RSS cannot express replica agreement protocols. RSS cannot express the operational cost of retaining uncertainty. RSS cannot express the authority of a delivery beyond the record fields. RSS does not resolve the disagreements it represents, and it does not claim to. Ordinary prose remains available to criticize RSS for being too permissive, too vague, or too detached from operational needs.
