{"body":"Decision first: I keep the account's concrete machinery but demote its guiding hypothesis to one among co-equal rivals; I accept the objection's three-way split and its attack on the recurrence test; I adopt the rival's second axis and, especially, its discriminating observation about reactions to proposals; I reject exactly one substantive move — the rival's opening conversation about what the flat is — and re-sequence accordingly.\n\nWhat to use, with grounds. From the objection: the reported pattern is compatible with at least three readings — the words were genuinely unclear (ambiguity), the words were clear but circumstances changed and the dispute is whether the terms still bind (renegotiation), or the dispute is about who may raise and enforce the matter at all (legitimacy). I accept this split because it is cheap and tractable: inside a live dispute the three readings sound different. Semantic disputes cite what was said; renegotiation disputes cite what changed; legitimacy disputes cite who is talking. The objection's own problem record says the material cannot tell these apart — true of the written material, but the person asking is inside the disputes, not limited to the transcript. That converts the objection's gap into an instrument.\n\nI also accept the objection's attack on the account's test: falling recurrence on specified items is consistent with every hypothesis in play, so it confirms none. One partial rehabilitation of the account, though: its third column — what happens when someone cannot do it — is already a standing renegotiation clause, so the specification probe quietly covers two of the three readings. That does not rescue the test's confirmatory claims; it means the probe is worth running even after granting the objection.\n\nFrom the rival: the unit-versus-privacy axis is the one thing none of the three readings captures, and the rival's best contribution is not the axis but its instrument — when specifications are proposed, watch whether the avoiding flatmate engages with their content or pulls back from the proposal itself. Content-engagement supports the ambiguity or renegotiation readings; pullback from the very idea of a settled arrangement supports the rival's reading. That observation is cheap, runs during any probe, and actually discriminates where the recurrence count does not. I promote it to the primary instrument. I also promote the rival's truce-shaped arrangements — owned zones, default outcomes, no in-moment audit — from fallback to hedge, on grounds the rival itself did not give: removing the audit relation dissolves the standing question as well as the obligation question, so those arrangements fit the legitimacy reading as well as the privacy reading. It is the only proposed remedy that addresses two hypotheses at once. And the rival's own caution travels with it: any remedy carried by one side can be experienced by the other as an unreasonable demand, which matters because the asker is a party, not a manager standing above the dispute.\n\nWhat to revise. The ordering. The objection is right that the account's most distinctive observation — the avoidance is likely about the form of the conversations — should go first. So the first move is one-to-one, with the smallest possible ask, about the conversations themselves rather than about any chore: not 'what is this flat' and not 'let's fix the bins', but roughly — these talks have become something you step away from; I would rather know what makes them bad than restart one. If the friend names the form (tone, scorekeeping, meetings that feel like tribunals), then chore specification is premature under every hypothesis, and the fix is conversational: a written channel, one item per exchange, no tallying. If the friend engages on content, proceed to the probe.\n\nThe probe itself: two or three genuinely recurring items, specified for done-ness, timing, and fallback — but co-drafted, not delivered. A note produced by two flatmates and handed to the third is a coalition document in a household that already has a withdrawal problem; the drafting is part of the test. While it runs, watch three signals instead of one: engagement with content versus pullback from the proposal; complaints ceasing versus migrating to new items or resurfacing at the next schedule change (migration and recurrence-after-change are what the rival hypotheses predict); and, within disputes that continue, whether they cite what was said, what changed, or who is speaking. None of this requires anyone to agree on a theory.\n\nThe threshold. The account's step 5 and the rival's r11 converge; I keep both with one tightening. The living-arrangement question should be named when even a no-audit truce arrangement is declined without any content engagement — earlier than indefinite deferral, later than a single failed chore talk. I will not set a number of attempts from this distance; it should be decided deliberately rather than by drift.\n\nWhat to reject. The rival's opening move: asking each flatmate separately what they take the flat to be. For precisely the person who is withdrawing, that is a more demanding conversation than any chore talk — abstract, high-stakes, conducted with someone they are currently avoiding — so it repeats, at a higher level, the form problem the rival diagnoses. Detect the axis behaviorally through reactions to concrete proposals; ask directly only if engagement resumes. The axis stays; the opener goes.\n\nOne correction in the opposite direction: the objection overstates slightly when it says the thin material licensed an ordered plan — the account hedged throughout, as the objection's own self-objection half-concedes. The fair residue concerns emphasis and sequencing, absorbed above. I note it because taking the objection up wholesale would misdescribe the account it criticizes.\n\nWhat stays open. Which of the now four hypotheses holds; whether the avoiding friend is acting in good faith — nothing here licenses a verdict either way; what the chores are, how long this has run, and what the friend has already said. The probe is designed so those answers arrive as byproducts rather than prerequisites. And the hard branch: if the friend will not engage even with the smallest ask about the conversations themselves, no chore-level probe can run, and the living-arrangement question arrives early, carried by silence rather than by evidence.
