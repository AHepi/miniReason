The rival's own r9 flags the last of these. So the rival's test is not decisive either; it is evidence on a second channel, which is progress, not resolution.\n\nFourth, what follows. If neither test is decisive on its own, the useful question is not which hypothesis is prior but which first action is least costly if the hypothesis behind it is wrong. Ranked that way, the moves change order. The separately-convened, narrow conversation with the withdrawing flatmate is cheap, reversible, does not enact a settled position about what the flat is, and yields information on both channels at once - content-engagement and the reaction to being asked at all. The written note is not cheap in the same sense: it enacts a unit-with-obligations reading before that question is settled, and on a lopsided arrangement it produces clearer paperwork over the same imbalance. That is not an argument against writing anything down; it is an argument that the note goes second. The terminal question - whether the living arrangement still fits - appears as the account's step 5 and the rival's step 4, and I would neither force it from a chore dispute nor keep deferring it indefinitely. But the objection's o3 applies here too: a threshold stated as a number of attempts is a scheduling choice, and naming a number in advance is a way of borrowing precision the material does not have.\n\nFifth, what I am least sure about. The rival's diagnostic question - ask each flatmate separately whether the flat is a shared project or three lives sharing a kitchen - is itself a proposal that enacts a frame. It asks for an abstract self-report that many people cannot give about their own household, and a \"three private lives\" answer might reflect fatigue with the question rather than a considered view. I would rather learn the axis from concrete incidents - what the last kitchen argument actually felt like it was about - than from the ontology question directly. That is a refinement of the rival, not a rejection of it.\n\nWhat would move me. If the separately-convened conversation is refused outright, the withdrawal is probably not about the form of any particular negotiation, and the burden shifts to the relationship or the living arrangement. If it is accepted and the content is engaged, the ambiguity reading gains. If it is accepted but content is not engaged, the rival's axis is the better bet. If recurrence falls after a spec note tried later and stays down across a schedule change, that is real support for the account. If it falls and reappears on new items, the objection wins.\n\nOn tone: I do not think any of the three has earned a plan.
