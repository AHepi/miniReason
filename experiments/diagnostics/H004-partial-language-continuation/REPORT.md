## What was learned

**Criticism can itself become the error that needs correcting.** In the prose late chain, cycle18 correctly notices that a sentence classified as truncated by cycle17 is actually complete; cycle19 retains that correction. Earlier gates also record retraction and restoration of substantive projection counterexamples. These are concrete targets and responses across time, not merely a tally of criticism labels.

The same chains also carry unsupported criticism into working use. A recurring move uses the critic's current empty target slot to accuse a previous response of lacking evidence that the previous response actually received. Current absence can limit the current appraisal; it cannot establish historical absence. WHL19 improves the scope by explicitly withdrawing a route as a present ground and leaving cycle16's actual access unknown, but that does not validate the original provenance accusation.

**Accurate custody does not guarantee accurate use of custody.** WHL conversion18 falsely calls its complete cycle17 input a truncated prefix. Conversion19 states that input was complete while retaining the criticism about its supposedly missing ending. RSS conversion20 falsely calls its verbatim cycle5 input a later reconstruction. Root checked the original response and decoded request: the full original body, including R1–R8 terminology, was present unchanged. These are participant errors, not missing-file or transport failures.

**Every final arm computes the contract's fresh D set {3,6} and protected A set {-2,3}; explanatory defects persist.** All report the corresponding intervals [3,6] and [-2,3], while several reverse lower/upper labels or contradict the numerical results in prose. Prose20 first recognizes two distinct A payloads with equal effects, then denies that either case has such a collision. WHL conversion20 falsely predicts that deleting an expired hold would revive a lower revision despite the remaining greatest-revision release. RSS20 gives the wrong maximum reservation sum after using the correct one.

The WHL equality discrepancy is a sharper check than the contract answer alone. WHL's written hold rule remains active at expiry equality; the task contract reserves zero. On D, WHL would permit A effects {0,2}, so the availability set would be {1,3,4,6}. RSS conversion20 notices the discrepancy but omits the release alternative. WHL20 correctly gives A's coded effects [2,0], then announces the wrong whole-case set {6,8} while its own subsequent arithmetic yields the missing values 4 and1. Thus identifying the disagreement does not ensure correct use of it.

**Some specific representation findings are useful.** WHL conversion20 explicitly diagnoses the reversed bound-function names. WHL15 locally corrects its own duplicate-list claim by reading the actual filter, obtaining [3,3,8] as a list and {3,8} as a set. RSS conversion17–20 preserves the distinction between limitations of a representation and limitations of the whole process using it. These local findings do not establish a policy winner.

**Formal vocabulary can also hide a mistaken admission rule.** WHL18–19 calls the prose claim about creativity “typed wrong” because R1–R8 do not supply a creativity property. The contract is not a closed type system. Prose claims and criticism of assumptions are legitimate inquiry targets. D1's inference is unsupported; that does not make its expression illegitimate. Root rejects this proposed ground.

## Interpretation under the designated FW5 source

[FW5](../../../docs/sources/FW5-explanatory-construction.md) separates origin, successful explanation, repair, present deployment, and historical creation. It allows partial/distributed representation and later re-presentation of a target. Forgetting does not erase an earlier creative event; present access still matters when a particular criticism is attempted.

The study supports investigating continuity as a means of later error discovery. It does not establish perfect memory as a requirement. Nor does error make every contribution non-originative: FW5 permits false originative conjectures. Historical newness and the attributed construction/use organization have not been established here merely by obtaining custom wording or a fresh file.

The root supplied the contract, deliberately mixed sound/unsound memos, cases, return schedule, use policies and partial-admission rule. DeepSeek supplied the responses. Mechanical custody and root repair of the continuation boundary must not be credited to the model. Many sound MemoD verdicts rest on positions already explicit in the task.

Both original language specimens were available to every arm. This was a comparison of use policies on shared material, not exclusive language expressivity. There was no matched native-reasoning, no-criticism, or active-versus-archive content intervention. Correct later arithmetic and temporal succession do not establish that criticism caused a repair under FW5.
