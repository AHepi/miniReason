The task says: if the new problem is one of scope, say what would have to be supplied to investigate it without manufacturing a policy. The projection problem is neither. To investigate it without manufacturing a policy, and under the materials I actually have, RSS and R1–R8 would need to supply:

1. a criterion distinguishing a projection that is information-preserving for a *stated* inference from one that merely coincides on the present data;

3. a marker of coincidence — a parameter or observation recording that the projection and the object agree *here* and that no uncoincident case has been tested.

Absent these, the projection problem stays open. Nothing further need be supplied for B6(a)'s scope demand beyond what cycle 7 already named: a resource model as a primitive, absent from RSS and not added by the contract.
