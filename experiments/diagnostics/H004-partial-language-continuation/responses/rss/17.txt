## The criticism I now make against my previous response

The central defect in my previous answer is a **provenance/attribution failure on the axis the block names**, and it is worth stating precisely because it is *not* the failure my previous answer spent most of its length diagnosing.

My previous response framed the whole investigation as follows: it claimed that D1, D2, D4 and D6 "share one move, which is the *opposite* of the move analyzed in the previous cycle's Memo C," and asserted that "C1, C2 and C4 inflated a *local* result into a *global* one." That passage does three things at once. It (i) asserts the existence and content of a prior memo C; (ii) attributes specific clauses C1, C2, C4 to it; and (iii) makes a comparative claim ("the opposite of") that depends on the correctness of (i) and (ii). None of the three is supported by the supplied material for this cycle, and the reopened-targets stanza explicitly says no earlier target is re-presented. Under R7 — "Distinguish what a source states, what is observed, what follows under an assumption, and what is proposed or disputed" — what a source *states* is Memo D; the Memo C material was recalled, not re-presented. My previous answer flagged in its preamble that it would "not attribute sentences to hashed texts I cannot read" and would "label … recomputation as mine." But it then ran a comparative argument of exactly that kind about an unread, un-re-presented prior memo. That is the same class of move the block is probing — treating a working interpretation as if the recorded source said it — performed by the very response that claimed to be keeping the two apart.

**Why the criticism is warranted.** The block's instruction is explicit: "This block investigates late error discovery through actual earlier targets. Their mechanical re-presentation supplies evidence; it is not evidence of unaided recall." A comparative claim against an unre-presented memo C is precisely a claim advanced on unaided recall with no re-presented target to check. And it fails my response's *own* declared discipline: I wrote that where I recompute a case I will "label the recomputation as mine rather than attributing it to a reopened stamp." The Memo C comparison was not labeled as a recomputation; it was presented as an established prior result.

**The consequence.** The comparative framing ("D1, D2, D4, D6 are the opposite of the move analyzed in Memo C") must be **withdrawn as comparison**. This is a correction to my previous answer, not to Memo D, and I attribute it here rather than silently repairing the earlier occurrence.
