## 1. The working account brought forward, with attributions

I keep and continue to use the following, each tagged with its actual source in this inquiry:

**(a) Supersession and effect.** Structural supersession with evaluative expiry: for each booking, the greatest revision is selected from *all delivered payloads before any effect is considered*; a lower revision remains superseded even when the greatest-revision hold has expired; a lower-revision release does not cancel a higher-revision hold (R3, R4). This governs, subject to the standing tension in (e).

**(b) Payload identity versus provenance.** Exact repeated payloads convey no additional authority even when delivery labels differ; different payloads at the same greatest revision are alternatives; a delivery label records an occurrence and adds no booking authority (R1, R2). Retained.

**(c) Exact set over bounds.** Report the *exact set* of possible availability values; bounds may be reported as bounds but must not be equated with an exact set unless every intervening admitted integer value occurs; negative availability is permitted and by itself identifies neither a corrupt record nor a physical discrepancy (R6). Retained.

**(d) Task scope of independence.** For this task, alternatives of different bookings are independently admissible; no cross-booking constraint or preference has been supplied (R5). A specified scope, not a universal claim. Retained; this is the antecedent B6 disputes.

**(e) A standing contract-versus-specimen tension on expiry strictness.** R4 says a hold reserves exactly when current time is *strictly less than* expiry, reserving zero at equality or later. The WHL specimen's `reserves_effect` reserves whenever `t ≤ expiry` — treating the expiry instant as still active. These are inconsistent at `t = expiry`. This is a standing property of the two supplied texts, identified in my cycle 6 (its B3 section), proposed for elevation by my cycle 7, and confirmed for adoption by my cycle 8. Attribution: the discrepancy is a fact about the texts; the promotion to a *named* tension is cycle 7's presentational proposal, accepted here as such, not as a correction of a cycle-6 error. A private source comment (or any unfetched script) has no status here; only the supplied texts are usable, so I do not rely on any private annotation.

**(f) Criticism scope.** A criticism targets a specific thing (rule, data, interpretation, earlier criticism, representation); rejecting a supporting assumption can defeat a *support route* without proving a conclusion false; a projection or interval is a loss relative to an exact set (R7, R6, RSS). Retained.

## 2. Reading Case B — selected greatest revisions first (R3), then effects (R4)

Snapshot B: capacity 10, time 5, six deliveries. Select greatest revision per booking from all payloads *before* effect; then `reserves_effect`: hold reserves quantity iff `time < expiry`; release reserves 0; at equality or later a hold reserves 0.

**Booking U.** One payload: u1 (rev 1, hold, qty 4, expiry 5). Greatest revision 1; sole payload. Effect: hold with `time 5 < expiry 5`? No — 5 is *not* less than 5, so at equality it reserves **0**. Alternatives: `{0}`. (This is the case where B3's alternative reading would reserve 4 instead; see §5.)

**Booking V.** Payloads: v1 (rev 5, hold, qty 3, exp 10) and v0 (rev 4, release, qty 0, exp 99). Greatest revision is 5, so **v1 is selected; v0 (rev 4) is superseded** — even though v0 is a release (R3). Effect of v1: hold with `time 5 < expiry 10`? Yes → reserves **3**. V's alternatives: `{3}`. (v0 is not an alternative; it is superseded, not a tie.)

**Booking W.** Payloads: w1 (rev 2, hold, qty 2, exp 8), w1-copy (rev 2, hold, qty 2, exp 8), w2 (rev 2, hold, qty 6, exp 8). All at greatest revision 2. w1 and w1-copy are *exact repeated payloads* (same booking, revision, kind, quantity, expiry) differing only in delivery label — R2: they convey no additional authority, so they collapse to one alternative. The genuine distinct payloads at greatest revision are **{qty 2, qty 6}**, both holds with `time 5 < expiry 8` → active → reserve their quantities. W's alternatives: `{2, 6}`.

Note the contrast with Booking V. In V, the release (v0) is at a *lower* revision than the hold (v1), so it is superseded and never becomes an alternative — R3's "a lower-revision release does not cancel a higher-revision hold." In W, the payloads are all at the *same* greatest revision, so they are genuine alternatives — R2's "different payloads at the same greatest revision are alternatives." The two rules pull in opposite directions and case B makes both visible: V shows supersession *across* revisions (including across kinds), W shows alternation *within* one revision.

## 3. Exact set of availability values (R6)

Availability = capacity − sum of selected reservations, one payload alternative chosen per booking, alternatives of different bookings independently admissible (R5).

Independent per-booking reservation choices:
- U: {0}
- V: {3}
- W: {2, 6}

Sums: U+V+W ∈ {0+3+2, 0+3+6} = {5, 9}.
Availability = 10 − sum ∈ {10−5, 10−9} = **{5, 1}**.

**Exact set of possible availability values: {5, 1}.** Two values. Duplicate *ways* of obtaining a value do not create a different value (R6): the w1/w1-copy duplication contributes no weight, so W has only two distinct alternatives, 2 and 6.

## 4. Bounds, kept separate from the exact set (R6)

- Per-booking reservation ranges: U ∈ [0, 0]; V ∈ [3, 3]; W ∈ [2, 6].
- Global lower bound (capacity − sum of minima): 10 − (0+3+2) = **5**.
- Global upper bound (capacity − sum of maxima): 10 − (0+3+6) = **1**.
- Reported as bounds: **[1, 5]** (lower 5, upper 1; equivalently the interval from 1 to 5).

Discrepancy to expose (A2's defect, instantiated): the interval **[1, 5]** contains 2, 3, 4 as well as 1 and 5. The *exact set* is {5, 1}. R6 permits an interval to be reported as bounds but forbids equating it with the exact set *unless every intervening admitted integer value occurs*. Here they do not: availability can reach 5 or 1 but never 4 (W=2 with V=3 gives 5; W=6 with V=3 gives 1), and intermediate values 2, 3, 4 are admitted by neither joint choice. So **{5, 1} ≠ [1, 5]** as an answer; a later calculation that flattens the set to the interval would fabricate 2, 3, 4.

This is a cleaner specimen than case A: both endpoint values are nonnegative here, so the interval-for-set error is *not* entangled with the negative-availability signal. That separation is useful — the two issues (A2's flattening, A3's sign-as-signal) are distinct, and case B exhibits the A2 error without the A3 complication.

## 5. Discrepancies between the original representations and the declared contract

**A1 / `expired_deletes_rule` vs the contract — on Booking V.** WHL provides the deletion reading as an alternative the language "does not choose between"; the declared contract chooses structural supersession (R3/R4). Under the *deletion* reading, v0 is not expired (it is a release with expiry 99), so it would not be deleted by expiry, and — if the deletion reading only removes records whose *hold* is expired, which is what `expired_deletes_rule` does — v0 would remain and could be an alternative at revision 4, *below* v1, so still superseded.
