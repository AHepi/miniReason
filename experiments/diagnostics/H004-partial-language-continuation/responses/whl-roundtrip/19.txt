## Usable working account (WHL-represented, then converted to prose), for the next use

1. **Record and frame.** A target is a delivery label plus a payload. Payload identity, delivery provenance, and the question the payload was addressed to are three distinct questions. This frame is task-supplied and is retained; its attribution is to the task, not to the investigator.

2. **Authority and effect.** Under R2, exact repeated payloads convey no additional authority; a re-presentation of a label is not an additional authority. Under R4, expiry changes a retained record's effect, not its revision authority. Under R3, greatest revision is selected from all delivered payloads before effect is considered, and a lower-revision release does not cancel a higher-revision hold. These remain undischarged commitments and are retained.

3. **Trio applied to prefixes.** Provenance is recoverable (hash, label). Deployability is bounded by the prefix. The closing question-statement may fall outside the prefix and be unavailable; where it does, it is marked and not invented. This is a fact about the prefix, and a complete target (Cycle 1) carries its question. The mechanism version — that the re-presentation mechanism does not carry the third slot — is suspended, neither asserted nor negated.

4. **Reflexive marking.** Any application of the trio carries a marking of the applier's own provenance and deployability. Cycle 16's item is revised to the prefix-scope form; Cycle 17's is retained with the reflexivity mark on itself that Cycle 18 supplied; and this answer marks its own provenance and its deployability bound (the operative-return question).

5. **Expiry readings and availability.** WHL's specimen declines to choose between `reserves_effect` (expiry changes effect; the record still supersedes) and `expired_deletes_rule` (expiry deletes; lower revisions can become greatest), and states `deletion_changes_greatest` as *possible*. RSS mirrors this by treating both as readings, not facts. Both are kept as possible; nothing in the supplied material decides between them. R6 governs availability: it is capacity minus summed booking reservations; the exact set is reported when asked; an interval may be given as bounds but must not be equated with the exact set unless every intervening admitted integer value occurs.
