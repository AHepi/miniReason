## Criticism 1 — C1: the `exact_duplicate` move is a category slippage, not an application of R2

`exact_duplicate` in WHL is defined on `Record` as equality of its *five* fields — booking, revision, kind, quantity, expiry. It does not carry delivery provenance. Delivery *labels* live at the snapshot-delivery layer (R1), not in `Record`. The prior response wrote that X and Y are "`exact_duplicate`s *in the evidential sense* — same content, copied provenance." That is a reconstruction WHL does not license without an extension: WHL has no provenance field in `Record`, so "copied provenance" cannot be represented as a sixth field to be equal; provenance would have to be re-encoded as one of the five existing fields, which would misrepresent its status. R2 speaks of *payload* identity and *delivery label* as different questions. The prior response collapsed the distinction by reading "copied intermediate file" into payload identity and then invoking R2 as if R2 addressed the copy relation.

Why this matters. The conclusion ("one route, not two") is *right in substance* — shared derivation defeats independence — but it is *attributed to the wrong contract clause*. R2 is about duplicates of the record payload adding no *booking authority*; it is not about evidential independence of two reports. The correct resources for the prior response's own conclusion are (a) R7's instruction to distinguish what a source states, what is observed, what follows under an assumption, and (b) the ordinary notion of conditional independence of evidence, plus (c) R1's remark that a delivery label records an occurrence, not additional authority. The prior response got (c) roughly right and then over-extended (b) into a WHL definition it does not have.

Consequence: the criticism's *verdict on B1* stands (B1's claim of two independent routes is defective). The *grounding* changes, and the change matters for later cycles because a reader could otherwise think "WHL's `exact_duplicate` covers evidential copying," which would license using it as a general-purpose identity test for reports — a use WHL does not support because `Record` has no provenance.

Retained: B1 is defective; provenance is not authority. Revised: attribute the defect to R1/R7 and the independence notion, not to R2's `exact_duplicate`, unless the language is extended to carry delivery provenance inside a represented object.

## Criticism 2 — C2: the "tautology" fork is a false dichotomy, and the projection primitive is being asked of WHL where WHL legitimately abstains

That fork excludes the *most likely* reading, which is neither: the reader is being used as *evidence of* delivery history, and the claim is *evidential over-reach*, not a semantic tautology or an outright category error.

The prior response did notice this class ("display inflated into delivery history"), but then added the meta-claim that "WHL's absence of a projection primitive ... structurally invites" the conflation, and said it "is a limitation of WHL worth stating." That is an over-reach in the opposite direction. WHL's design notes explicitly warn that the snapshot is a single list and that replication is not primitive. The prior response treated an explicit abstention as a defect with a corrective direction, which is exactly the move the contract's R7 warns against when it distinguishes what a source *states* from what a reader supplies. Why this matters.
