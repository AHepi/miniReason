**A2 — defective, retained unchanged.** Under R6 an interval may be reported as bounds but must not be equated with an exact set unless every intervening admitted integer occurs. For the exact set `{−3, 2}`, the interval `[−3, 2]` also contains `−2, −1, 0, 1`; A2 does not establish that these are attainable, so substituting the interval "exactly" and reusing it downstream is not harmless — it can yield answers outside the true possibility set. **A4 — defective, retained with a scope note.** A4 treats different delivery labels on the same payload as "independent confirmations" giving the common reservation more authority than a single conflicting alternative. **A6 — defective, retained with a sharper failure-mode statement.** A6 claims that shared capacity "already imposes a rule forbidding simultaneous high alternatives from different bookings." ## Consolidated working account

I encode the relevant claims in WHL vocabulary and render them back to prose, checking the conversion against the actual sources for added or lost commitment.

1. **Authority.** For each booking, form `greatest_revision_in` from all delivered payloads first. Expiry never removes a record from this comparison; it only affects `reserves_effect` (R3, R4). The reading in which an expired hold is deleted so a lower revision can become greatest is statable in WHL as `expired_deletes_rule` / `deletion_changes_greatest`, but WHL does not choose it, and the contract excludes it for this task. Using it must be attributed as a reading choice, not presented as the contract's rule. (A1 correction; contract authority, WHL neutrality, both stated.)

2. **Payload versus provenance.** `exact_duplicate` is identity of `(booking, revision, kind, quantity, expiry)`. Distinct delivery labels do not confer authority; near-duplicates are candidates for alternative status, not corroborations. Payload identity and delivery provenance are different questions (R1, R2). (A4 correction, with the RSS-silence scope note.)

3. **Reservation effect.** A release reserves `0` regardless of its other fields; a hold reserves `quantity` exactly when `logical_time < expiry`, otherwise `0`. Expiry changes a retained record's effect, not its revision authority (R4). This is `reserves_effect`, and it is the contract's rule here.

4. **Alternatives and independence.** At the greatest revision for a booking, each non-identical payload is an alternative; exactly one is selected per booking; alternatives of different bookings are independently admissible and no cross-booking constraint has been supplied (R5). There is no global choice function in WHL; `possible_reservations` is per booking. (A6 correction.)

5. **Availability.** Availability equals `capacity − Σ selected reservations`, and may be negative (R6). The exact attainable set is `{ capacity − Σ_b q_b : q_b ∈ possible_reservations b }` over admissible per-booking choices. The WHL bound functions `availability_lower_bound` and `availability_upper_bound` sum per-booking extrema; they are guaranteed only to bracket the exact set, not to equal it, because independence across bookings does not imply the per-booking extrema are jointly attained. The bound interval equals the exact set only when the exact set is itself an integer interval containing every intermediate admitted value — the R6 condition — which is a snapshot-specific fact, not a property of the bound functions. Report bounds as bounds; derive the exact set from the admissible combinations; do not present an interval as exact absent that check. (A2 correction, extended in Cycle 2 and tightened in Cycle 3.)

6. **Criticism discipline.** Distinguish what a source states, what is observed, what follows under an assumption, and what is proposed or disputed; a defect in an interpretation does not by itself discredit all its records (R7; A5 preserved).

7. **Open items.** Whether a specific snapshot's bounds coincide with its exact attainable set is snapshot-relative and not settled a priori (R6). Which reading of expiry or release is in force is fixed here by the contract, but WHL does not fix it, and the contract/WHL disagreement on this point is exposed, not resolved by the sources themselves except by their own stated precedence. Uncertainty is retained, not collapsed.
