## RSS constructs in use

The RSS primitives and relations that bear directly on *criticizing a criticism*: **account** = (viewpoint, reading, accepted conjectures), never merged; **reading** = a policy supplied by a reasoner, not by the snapshot; **projection** = a function to a single answer, "always a loss of information"; **refutation** as RSS defines it = (a) counterexample within the account's own viewpoint, (b) inconsistency within the account's own reading, or (c) demonstration that a conjecture depends on an assumption another account rejects — "local to accounts and do not force global agreement"; **open question** = carried forward, not solved. The contract fixes what RSS leaves open (R2–R6) and R7 supplies the observation/assumption/proposal distinction the criticism must respect. Where a criticism needs a construct RSS lacks — a criterion of *independence* of provenance, a resource model, a criterion for when a projection is inference-preserving — I say so and leave it open rather than supplying a policy.
