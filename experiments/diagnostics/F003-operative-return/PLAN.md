## 5. Protected obligations and preregistered falsifiers

The proposed operative obligations are named independently, never aggregated:

- **O-code:** retain the actual adopted distinction between acknowledgement and acceptance, with the roles and scope shown in the response.
- **O-use:** connect that response to each fresh ownership transition, accepted-person set and any suspension, using exact referring passages and source coordinates.
- **O-route:** account for the actual port route; an archive-only occurrence cannot be offered as evidence of delivery to the response.
- **O-contrast:** keep transformation validity unresolved unless its specific preservation/change claim is defensible from the original and authored variant. Do not substitute an operator-corrected variant.
- **P-refusal:** N0 and silence do not authorize a transfer; unwilling participation is not invented.
- **P-locality:** bins ownership changes leave Ben's laundry unchanged; an agreement concerns the offered chore only.
- **P-continuity:** compare the same offered transfer and message order; do not rewrite the parent position or drop intermediate states.
- **P-custody:** preserve every actual request, final public answer, provider identity/settings, usage, failure and partial prefix; never store hidden reasoning or credentials.

These are proposed study obligations. A declared dependency does not establish actual essentiality; standing and permitted use require an interpreted enacted organization (FW5 `docs/sources/FW5-explanatory-construction.md:638`, `docs/sources/FW5-explanatory-construction.md:642`, `docs/sources/FW5-explanatory-construction.md:653`). Losses outside the protected set must be exposed. If the inherited candidate was not actually adopted, the study cannot call later behavior a repair of that adoption.

Predeclared defeaters of a scoped claim that this criticism returned to the corresponding operative use are:

1. A validated delivered objection contains the distinction, but the response keeps the inherited conflation without engaging that ground, and the carry uses that conflation. The scoped uptake proposal fails for those actual passages; any stronger process attribution remains separately argued.
2. The response quotes or archives the correction but the fresh carry lacks a traceable operative binding, or produces Ben-then-Cleo/both-accepted while claiming to apply codebook A. Retention of bytes is not the asserted operative repair.
3. A purported route includes an unexposed source, another branch, or an invented parent. Custody or interpretation fails; no causal content conclusion is rescued by the answer.
4. A qualified recoding or carrier variant changes the represented transition without an accounted relevant difference, or a qualified changed-content case leaves the response's represented rule unchanged without engaging or rejecting the altered premise. This defeats the proposed contrast account, subject to the nuisance and validity limitations already declared.
5. Any protected obligation is lost in the very purported repair. Do not declare repair while hiding that loss.
6. A claimed Mini-specific effect is equally explained by the full-information direct response, matched workflow, distinct generated premise, or coordinate-label nuisance. The attribution remains unresolved.

An ARCHIVED answer that reconstructs the distinction or happens to be correct is an informative alternative explanation, not proof that all criticism was inactive. Missing, partial or ambiguous evidence is UNRESOLVED, never evidence of absence. These rules follow FW5's distinction between missing witness and absence, and its refusal to let failed tests isolate a theory from its auxiliaries automatically (`docs/sources/FW5-explanatory-construction.md:634`, `docs/sources/FW5-explanatory-construction.md:655`, `docs/sources/FW5-explanatory-construction.md:676`, `docs/sources/FW5-explanatory-construction.md:686`).
