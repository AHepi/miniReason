{"body": "## Rival framing: the question is a projection, not a thing\n\nThe prior account does bookkeeping and reclassifies. I want to try a different move: refuse the framing of the original task at the level of *what kind of object an answer would be*.\n\nThe original question — \"what happens to information about the physical state of matter that forms a black hole when the black hole evaporates?\" — presumes that there is a determinate, single-valued quantity (\"the information\"), attached to a determinate event (\"matter that forms a black hole\"), with a determinate transformation (\"what happens to it during evaporation\"). Each of those three presuppositions is doing independent work, and none of them is forced by the physics alone as stated.\n\nSo my rival pose is: **the question names a projection of a broader state, and asks about the trajectory of the projected shadow.** Whether the shadow has an answer depends on whether the projection is well-defined at both endpoints, and whether there is any physical process that selects one such projection. In the black hole setting, \"the information about the physical state of the infalling matter\" is naturally read as a coarse-grained selection from the joint state of matter plus gravitational field; the \"outgoing radiation\" is another such selection. The original question asks whether these two selections must agree in some quantifiable sense, as a function of time. That is a question about *relations between projections*, not about a conserved substance being shuffled around.\n\nThis is not the same as saying \"the question is ill-posed\". It is saying: it becomes well-posed only when you state (i) which projection is being tracked, (ii) what instrument/environment defines it, and (iii) whether the dynamics forces one projection to encode the other. Those are precisely the three things that standard framings tend to leave implicit, and the honest answer depends on them.\n\n### Why this rival helps\n\nThe previous account separates accessibility-vs-existence from an exactness clause, and flags a pivot premise. Under the projection framing, those two trackers acquire a natural common parent:\n\n- **Accessibility-vs-existence** becomes: is the projection operationally definable by some physically allowed measurement at the late time? If not, the question of \"where it went\" has no operational meaning.\n- **Exactness clause** becomes: granting a projection, in what sense must the late-time projection reconstruct the early-time projection — state-vector level, density-matrix level, or some weaker statistics?\n- **The pivot premise** becomes: is there anything in the coupled dynamics of matter + geometry that *selects* one projection pairing as the physically privileged one, or is the pairing an artifact of how we choose to coarse-grain?\n\nThat is a reframing, not an answer. It is also testable in the weak sense that if no projection is selected by the dynamics, the original question has no single answer *and that fact is itself the answer to a nearby, better-posed question*.\n\n### Difference from the previous account\n\nThe previous account says the questions form a family and calls that a *candidate positive claim*. I go further: the projection framing makes the family structured rather than merely plural, by naming the three coordinates (projection, instrument, dynamics-forcing) along which the members vary. That is a stronger claim than \"it's a family\", and correspondingly more exposed to being wrong. I mark it as such.\n\n### What I do not do\n\n- I do not claim any particular projection is the physically correct one.\n- I do not resolve the pivot antecedent.\n- I do not claim the projection framing is the only non-reifying reading; it is one rival.\n- I do not carry the p.carry.* provenance markers forward as physics. They are unrelated to the present question and I have no direct view of them in this invocation.\n- I do not close the original task.\n\n### What is new here relative to the prior account\n\n1. A reframing of the task as a question about **relations between projections**, with three named coordinates.\n2. A claim that this reframing makes the \"family\" structured rather than merely plural (bolder than the prior account's version of the same observation).\n3. An explicit dissociation from the prior account's provenance bookkeeping as unrelated to the physics.\n4. Continued refusal to select among (a)/(b)/(c) or to force the pivot.\n\nStill not an answer.
