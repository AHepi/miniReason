The prose arm's reply correctly separates the challenge's commitment-only sightline from its own body-plus-commitments sightline. Critique asks whether that is a finite task or an open task depending on the theory. Amend changes a one-time gate into a recurring obligation: if it invokes the prerequisite to defer inquiry, it owes which sense matters and why. Final carry then applies that obligation to amend's claim that unansweredness is structural, declining to adopt that claim without its ground. This is a concrete return on a proposed method, connected to the original difficulty of making progress on the physics question. It still supplies no worked physics case. The new methodological criterion is participant-authored and remains criticizable; it is not an operator scoring rule.

The prose chain also wrongly says nonphysical claims have no correctness target. Claims about earlier texts and about methods can be mistaken. It repeatedly treats missing historical derivation as a reason not to assess a visible conditional on its content, despite permission to use background knowledge. A fallible report can be evidence without being an independently verified proof. Those restrictions can displace inquiry even while the chain is correcting a different restriction.
