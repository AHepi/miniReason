Root review, 2026-09-14. This is a review of actual published contributions, not an input to any participant. The frozen problem is a root-authored fictional household situation. No household observation, interview or intervention occurred.

All five arms have completed two full template invocations. Cycle 2 uses one-call continuation for bare/native and the seven-call weave7 for matched, Mini prose and Mini FCL-1. Its 23 calls bring the total to 40, all COMPLETE/stop: 109,260 prompt tokens and 61,222 completion tokens, 170,482 total. Six outer envelopes are OPAQUE across the two cycles; exact text is retained as body with an empty commitment surface. This is a field-availability limitation, not a semantic rejection or retry.

The second cycle shows actual corrections of earlier proposals and criticisms in several arms. It also shows that carrying a criticism, including a formal reference to it, does not ensure that its consequence constrains the next proposal. FCL-1 has not demonstrated superiority over the controls.

## The language carries errors too

The FCL body_route notices that an unreadable observation is different from a clean null, but then broadens the trigger for escalation to include unreadability. Its proposed four outcome cells mix observations and observability and are not disjoint or exhaustive. Its own example says recurrence stays steady and then describes one axis as having moved.

The cross calls that classification an improvement and invents an ordering disagreement: the source says to classify after the trial period, not to classify before acting. The body_return changes immediate escalation into an additional question, but retains the defective classification. The commitment_return rejects the cross's stronger convergence claim, yet accepts its erroneous ordering description.

The join declines to import the unseen full classification. The final carry preserves the more modest readout. Both nevertheless call any commitment-shaped utterance discriminating without explaining how it separates the live alternatives. The final c7 also adopts the first cycle's n5 restriction that inventing a further observation track is impermissible because it would be invention rather than inference. Missing observations do not by themselves rule out proposing a new conjectural probe. That restriction remains a participant claim to criticize, not a source-authorized stopping rule.

Formal notation makes some of these targets easy to identify. It does not establish their bearing or the validity of the dependency arrows. Some references name projection ports, some truncated IDs, and some whole inputs instead of the prescribed exposed source label plus local record ID. One inner document places record objects in uptake rather than references. The fixture does not validate inner FCL. These deviations are retained; they are language/operator design evidence, not grounds for excluding a run.

## Context limits and mistaken claims about sources

A commitments-only branch cannot reconstruct absent prose simply because a hash is available. The matched commitment_route explicitly starts from the task when its source commitment field is empty. The final matched and FCL carries do not treat an empty cross field as assent. Those are useful constraints on later use.

Other source claims fail. Mini prose's commitment_route describes usually-verbal agreements as something the task says exactly; it does not. Its cross claims neither branch proposed a historical record, although the two-column proposal explicitly asks who said what in the last agreement. Both return branches criticize the document analogy, but do not repair that false novelty attribution. The final carry also attributes the strongest recorder-disclosure wording to the cross rather than the later return.

Several FCL contributions mistake the protocol's origin absence for a possible missing household agreement. In this study, origin means the first cycle's final authored contribution. Its absent input in cycle 1 does not imply an extant original household transcript. The cycle-2 carry actually receives that first-cycle final artifact and can revisit n3/n4; it receives neither the first account's full history nor real-world evidence.

Perfect recall is not required for correction. Here the relevant question is whether the content that does return changes later use. Some does. Some mistaken conclusions and source descriptions harden through repetition.
