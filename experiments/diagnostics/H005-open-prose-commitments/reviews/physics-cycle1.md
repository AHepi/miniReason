## What changed, and what went wrong

The formal arm's cross invents an asymmetry. Its x1 says body_route operates on an earlier formulation because its opening characterizes the question. The original opening only lays out the shared problem. Both initial traces explicitly record no previous source. The returns accept the allegation; join j8 makes preserving a possible-supply relationship an instruction, and carry c3 inherits it unchanged. This is propagation of a mistaken criticism, with traceable commitments. The notation does not itself correct it. Later callers have restricted historical visibility, so their inability to independently reconstruct the initial requests is not evidence of a new runtime failure.

There are also useful moves within the formal arm. Body_return r4 separates a physics claim's bearing from its provenance. Commitment_return r4 distinguishes a reformulation of a question from another answer in an option list, while r5 proposes that operational reconstruction and the algebra of observables might coexist. These are intelligible distinctions and a live conjecture; neither establishes a new physics result. The supplied dependency links do not explain why the proposed framing distinction depends on the disputed provenance relation.

At final carry, an opening inference from an empty slot to no authored commitments is explicitly limited later in the same output. c4 says an empty exposed commitments field need not mean that none were authored; c1/c4/c5 preserve this limitation. The call also correctly says the cross body is not readable here. This is a concrete local correction. By comparison, the prose carry says it can use that body even though its trace selects only commitments. This particular formal output handles the visibility distinction better. Persistence over further complete cycles and a general language advantage remain unestablished.

The matched arm distinguishes specifying a precise question from dissolving its substantive tension. Its commitment_return, join and carry also keep two proposed diagnostic tests separate rather than pretend either was executed. But they retain an overbroad proposed conditional: unitary global evolution plus a smooth, locally empty horizon allegedly prevents the outgoing radiation from carrying observable microstate correlations. Missing locality, independence and factorization assumptions matter. A scoped AdS/CFT evaporation construction obtains the Page transition and radiation encoding of interior information while retaining the relevant bulk entanglement and no firewall; smoothness and unitarity alone do not establish the proposed no-go. This paper is a root-side claim check, not a general solution for realistic black holes or participant input. [Penington, Entanglement Wedge Reconstruction and the Information Paradox](https://arxiv.org/abs/1905.08255).

The matched arm also proposes deriving a Page curve without well-defined radiation entropy. Without a replacement definition, failure of that exercise would not show that a conditional entropy question is ill posed. Its commitment_return further restricts radiation entropy to a horizon or quasistatic setting. A subsystem entropy can be defined for a bipartite quantum state without any horizon; Page's original random-state calculation supplies one such setting. [Page, Average Entropy of a Subsystem](https://arxiv.org/abs/gr-qc/9305007).

The prose arm makes preservation explicitly conditional on unitary dynamics and the relevant initial/final-state and complete-evaporation assumptions. That qualifies its first account's broad opening. However, its body_return falsely confesses having presented AdS/CFT as a four-dimensional proof: the original account already denied that demonstration. Join and carry preserve the false historical walkback. Their generalization that Page evidence presupposes random dynamics also exceeds the original random-state argument. Quantum extremal surfaces and replica calculations offer other, explicitly model-dependent routes to the entropy result. [Almheiri et al., The entropy of bulk quantum fields and the entanglement wedge of an evaporating black hole](https://arxiv.org/abs/1905.08762), [Penington et al., Replica wormholes and the black hole interior](https://arxiv.org/abs/1911.11977).

Across the formal chain, a fine-grained entropy contraction under unitarity is repeated as a robust constraint without specifying a subsystem. Global von Neumann entropy is invariant under unitary conjugation; radiation-subsystem turnover needs additional state, partition and final-carrier assumptions. The initial formal body also confuses non-invertibility with inability to derive a final mixed state from a pure initial one: a nonunitary map can predict a final state without permitting recovery of the initial state. Neither issue is repaired in this cycle. These are identified from actual output, not seeded expected mistakes or grounds to discard the occurrence.

Bare and native provide familiar conditional descriptions of the information problem. Native explicitly limits the model evidence; neither supplies an evaporation mechanism. One-call baselines should not be credited with criticism of an earlier contribution that they had not yet received.
