# H005 prospective study: open prose through complete template invocations

Root design, REC-20260914-B. Participant material is [material.json](material.json); the [FCL-1 proposition](../../../docs/reviews/fcl1-language-proposition-2026-09-14.md) and [commitment-source review](../../../docs/reviews/commitment-interface-source-2026-09-14.md) explain the hypotheses. No provider output was available when this design was written.

## Problems and exposure

The four initial problems concern a household chore dispute, information after black-hole evaporation, responsibility for motives one did not choose, and social mixing alongside isolation. Their exact prose is frozen in the material. None supplies a target answer, a merit measure, an expected error or a rubric. No external evidence packet or preselected “correction” is supplied. Participants use the exposed prose and their background knowledge. Root may later check factual claims against primary sources; those sources are not retrospective participant inputs.

The prompts explain the experimental interfaces and ask stages to work, criticize or respond. These are the proposed templates themselves, not an implicit grading standard embedded in the problems. A stage may retain a conjecture, reject an objection, find another problem, or remain uncertain.

## Three distinct template structures

The complete connectivity, field selection and exact stage instructions are in the material. Every model node authors one body and one commitment string in a single call. There is no compulsory body layout.

| Template | Calls per complete invocation | Connections and selective views |
|---|---:|---|
| fork5 | 5 | Account branches to an objection with both fields and an alternative with body only; both return to a response. The completion sees the response, selected earlier commitments and the original cycle's completed account when available. |
| return6 | 6 | Reopened account → commitment-only challenge → reply → criticism of that criticism and its use → amendment → completion. The reply also sees the full reopened account; the criticism stage sees previous commitments. |
| weave7 | 7 | Independent routes start from the previous body and the previous commitments. A cross-examination sees both; each route separately responds; a join receives those responses; completion can revisit the original account. |

A cycle completes only after the whole chosen template completes. A sibling branch is not a separate cycle. The final node is the template's completed contribution. The first invocation has no earlier artifact; absence of an exposed view is explicitly not evidence that a past event did not occur. On cycle two, the origin can be the same artifact as the previous completion; the trace will preserve that fact. Original contributions are never overwritten by their later summaries.

## Why these configurations, in this order

FW5 distinguishes the existence of a criticism, its bearing, its actual use and a resulting repair. It also allows the interpretation, method and earlier criticism to become targets, and does not require complete memory. These motivate separate criticism/use routes and a selected return to earlier artifacts. The commitment source motivates making proposed applications and dependence accessible separately from the associated prose.

| Domain | Cycles 1 → 2 → 3 | Prospective mechanism hypothesis |
|---|---|---|
| Daily task | fork5 → weave7 → return6 | Practical disagreement can concern both an account of a situation and what an agreement would require. Separate perspectives may expose that difference; recombining body and commitment routes may reveal a mistaken assumption; the return can question the proposed remedy or criticism. |
| Physics | weave7 → return6 → fork5 | Competing explanatory accounts may share words while relying on different theoretical uses. Separate routes and subsequent cross-examination may expose an unsupported bridge; criticism of the criticism can question an alleged impossibility or evidence claim; the final fork can develop and compare an alternative consequence. |
| Philosophy | fork5 → return6 → weave7 | Rival positions can disagree about the question or a term's role. Returning to the grounds of an objection may expose an equivocation in the objection itself; separate later views can test whether the revised commitments still support the prose account. |
| Sociology | weave7 → fork5 → return6 | Aggregate descriptions and the relationships attributed to people need not coincide. Separate accounts may make different causal commitments; a rival route can question those commitments; a later return can reopen the evidence or method used to prefer an explanation. |

These hypotheses select routes, not correct domain conclusions. None assumes the named mistake occurs or must be found. No random template mutation is used.

## Five arms

1. **bare:** one direct, thinking-disabled call per cycle, continuing its own earlier public body and commitment interface.
2. **native:** the same one-call structure with native reasoning enabled at low effort; hidden reasoning is never stored or exposed.
3. **matched:** the same multi-call topology and selected field content as the Mini arms, rendered directly outside Mini, with free-prose commitments.
4. **mini_prose:** canonical Mini compiler/reducer/port rendering within the declared memory-backed fixture, with free-prose commitments.
5. **mini_fcl:** the same fixture, topology and selected field views, using FCL-1 commitments.

The prose arms receive the same conceptual distinction between commitments, dependency, mention, criticism and use. Their authors express it in ordinary prose. The FCL comparison therefore concerns a formal carrier, not exclusive access to the idea of a commitment interface.

Matched direct calls control for extra calls and selected information. Their direct rendering differs from Mini's rendered brief; any difference is part of the declared condition. This study cannot attribute a difference to an entire durable Mini scheduler that did not execute.

## Execution and resource boundary

Run the four problems as separate five-arm comparisons, initially for three complete invocations each. The three multi-call arms each make 18 calls per problem; bare and native each make three. Thus the initial schedule permits **240 unique provider calls**, assuming no transport-stopped chains. These are 60 complete invocations across five arms and four problems, not 240 cycles.

At most five independent requests may be active in one coordinator process. A ready wave gives each ready arm a slot before filling remaining slots with independent sibling nodes. Actual dependencies remain ordered. At most one problem's five arms are active at a time. Root publishes and verifies ready inputs and completed parents before dependent requests.

All calls use the existing DeepSeek Flash provider, an 8,192 completion-token ceiling and 180-second timeout. Native low effort is selected because the recorded E026 high-effort call consumed its entire allowance without a public answer. Actual usage, returned model, finish reasons and delivery problems are evidence; the ceiling is a resource boundary, not a desired answer length or quality measure.

Preserve partial public output, malformed envelopes and erroneous formal syntax. When an envelope is unparseable, exact returned text remains an opaque body and the commitment field is recorded as unavailable, never reconstructed by the harness. A transport failure or empty public output can block a dependent invocation; that does not show semantic failure. Never silently retry an attempted coordinate. An interrupted coordinate without terminal evidence must be audited before any new action.

The helper and focused offline checks are pending root acceptance. Its plan must pin material, helper and canonical runtime; the actual records must expose artifact labels, selected fields, parent hashes and exact payloads. Neither old observations nor frozen source will change.

## Interpretation and possible fourth/fifth invocation

Root alone reviews substantive outputs. The unit of argument is a particular change: what was alleged, what grounds were available, what subsequent use actually changed, what remains disputed, and what useful commitments were lost. This is not a sum of successful corrections or a correctness ranking.

A model's self-description is a claim to investigate. Parser success, agreement with root, adoption of a standard answer, more objections, novelty of wording and longer memory do not establish repair. Conversely, a wrong proposal, invalid objection or unexpressed relation does not disqualify the run. Obligations relevant to a repair claim may emerge from the inquiry and can themselves be questioned; they are not a hidden preloaded rubric.

After the first three complete invocations, root will judge the language hypothesis against both Mini prose and no-Mini evidence. If a particular unresolved criticism, dependency or return failure makes a further invocation informative, freeze that next template and its purpose prospectively, up to five total cycles for the selected chain. A changed template is a new invocation, not a retroactive repair of an old one. No fourth/fifth call is justified merely by a desire to obtain a favorable result. If the comparison does not establish superiority, report that result and its limits.
