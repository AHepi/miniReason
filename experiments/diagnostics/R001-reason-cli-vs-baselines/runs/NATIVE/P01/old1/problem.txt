P01

A source prepares a row of six sealed positions.

First, one of three source modes is selected:

- Mode S1 has probability 1/4 and places exactly 1 amber marker.
- Mode S2 has probability 1/2 and places exactly 3 amber markers.
- Mode S3 has probability 1/4 and places exactly 4 amber markers.

Conditional on the selected mode, every subset of positions of the required size is equally likely to be the amber set.

After the row is made, an auditor selects one of the actual amber positions uniformly at random. The auditor reveals only that the selected position belongs to the set consisting of positions 1 and 2. The auditor does not reveal which of those two positions was selected.

Given this disclosure, determine the exact conditional probability that position 6 contains an amber marker. Give the probability as a reduced fraction and explain the conditioning.

Work from the prose only. Do not use code or retrieval.
