P06. Seven sample trays

Seven distinct sample trays labelled A, B, C, D, E, F, and G must be placed from left to right into slots 1 through 7, one tray per slot.

The placement obeys all of these rules:

- A is immediately before F.
- The slot number of C is exactly two greater than the slot number of F.
- G is immediately after C.
- B is somewhere after G.
- For any two trays X and Y, define their inclusive span as abs(slot(X) minus slot(Y)) plus 1.
- The inclusive span of A and B equals the inclusive span of D and G.

Determine the unique left-to-right tray order and give enough working to establish uniqueness.

Work from the supplied prose only. No code execution, calculators, external tools, or retrieval are available.
