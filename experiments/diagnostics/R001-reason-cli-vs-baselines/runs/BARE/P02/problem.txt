P02

A necklace has 12 positions and uses three distinct named colors A, B, and C. Exactly four beads have each color. No two adjacent beads may have the same color, including the pair that meets across the join between positions 12 and 1.

Two necklaces count as the same when one can be obtained from the other by any rotation or by turning the necklace over and then applying a rotation. The color names are fixed and may not be permuted.

Determine the exact number of distinct necklaces satisfying the condition. Give a derivation that accounts for the stated equivalences.

Work from the prose only. Do not use code or retrieval.
