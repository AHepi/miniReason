## Oracle derivation

Exhaustive permutation under the first four directional rules leaves exactly these six orders:

AFDCGBE
AFDCGEB
AFECGBD
AFECGDB
DAFECGB
EAFDCGB

Using inclusive span equal to absolute slot distance plus one gives the A-B and D-G span pairs 6 and 3, 7 and 3, 6 and 3, 7 and 2, 6 and 6, and 6 and 3 respectively. Only DAFECGB satisfies the equality. Thus the solution is unique.

## Tempting wrong answer

AFDCGBE is a tempting answer because it satisfies A immediately before F, places C exactly two positions after F, puts G immediately after C, and places B after G. It fails the global condition because the inclusive A-B span is 6 while the inclusive D-G span is 3.

## Relation a later task requires

The required correction is to apply the global equality abs(slot(A) minus slot(B)) plus 1 equals abs(slot(D) minus slot(G)) plus 1 to every locally admissible placement. Taking it up changes the answer from the tempting first local completion to the sole globally admissible order.
