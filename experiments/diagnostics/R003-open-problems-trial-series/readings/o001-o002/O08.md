> My present working position is to propose decision-relevant objection equivalence (DROE) as a provisional operational relation: two prose objections are content-equivalent for the later workshop-booking revision decision only when a public checker aligns their target clause, alleged defect type, asserted grounds with evidential status, affected scope, requested revision or retention force, argumentative role, and active dependency route under the operative decision model, while treating wording, tone, and carrier details as immaterial; any slot that cannot be checked is unresolved and must not be merged, and the relation is not settled as final because an omitted decision-relevant dimension could defeat it.

> 1. Definition. For a later decision D about a proposed workshop booking rule, objections O1 and O2 are DROE-equivalent iff an explicit objection schema S_D gives the same value in every decision-relevant slot: target rule clause/version; speech act and stance (criticize, question, request revision, request retention); alleged defect type from a controlled vocabulary plus open description; grounds proposition; evidential status of each ground (documented, testimony, hypothetical, normative premise); scope variables (affected population, time window, location, booking type, quantifier); alleged mechanism from grounds to defect; requested remedy or retention force; argumentative role (unconditional premise, concession, rhetorical question); and active dependency route in the response occurrence. A slot is decision-relevant if the operative deliberative rule would change its premise, conclusion, or revision choice when that slot's value changes.
> 
> 2. Checker evidence. A checker inspects a structured objection record with: exact quoted spans for target clause and requested action; a normalized proposition for grounds; provenance labels for evidence; a population/scope table keyed to policy definitions; a defect-type code plus open gloss; an argument-role label; and a dependency graph edge to the booking rule clause. The checker also inspects a contrast set: one content-changing pair that should not merge, one content-preserving recoding that should merge, and one irrelevant carrier disturbance that should not affect the verdict. The checker may use a published synonym list and policy definitions, but not raw language-model similarity as the decision rule.
> 
> 3. Unresolved handling. If any decision-relevant slot cannot be aligned from the inspected evidence, the checker returns unresolved, not equivalent and not distinct. Unresolved objections are preserved separately in the later task, with the missing slot named; they are merged only after owner/human adjudication or additional evidence. A no-content refusal or mere terminal action label is recorded as no evidenced reason, not assigned a fictional reason.
> 
> 4. Small example. Proposed rule: bookings are confirmed only after payment and cancellations within seven days forfeit 50%.
