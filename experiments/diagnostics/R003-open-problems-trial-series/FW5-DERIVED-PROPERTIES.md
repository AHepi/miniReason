# FW5-derived properties of error correction

Derivation memorandum, 2026-09-17 UTC. No experiments selected or run.

## 1. METHOD

The result is twelve **conditional properties**, not twelve claims that criticism necessarily improves reasoning. Some constrain reason use, some local repair, and others the stronger attributions of origin, explanatory knowledge creation or recursive capacity. Their antecedents matter. A correction can be real without being historically new, originative or evidence of a universal capacity. FW5 explicitly separates these classes (L1180-L1196). The orchestrator decides which, if any, to test.

**Entailment.** A property is retained only as a consequence of a quoted definition or explicit commitment with its scope intact. Compatibility, useful engineering practice and a comparative mechanism hypothesis are identified separately. The source itself limits its claim:

> **Constitutive conjecture.** Explanatory creativity can be characterized by the construction of question-relevant dependency representations, their content-sensitive use and criticism, and their physically grounded provenance. The definitions below make that conjecture precise. The conjecture is not a theorem about every possible mind. A genuine explanatory achievement that cannot satisfy the structural conditions would count against it.

FW5 L33.

> That does not remove the need for substantive theories of the world. A physical law, the meaning of an expression, and a normative reason are not derived from a transcript by set theory. Their interpretations are declared inputs to a model, and applications of the model are conjectures. The distinction is essential: a complete mathematical definition of a model class is not complete knowledge of which model describes reality.

FW5 L39.

The inferential form is: if this occurrence really instantiates the specified FW5 relation under the fixed interpretation, its defining conditions must hold. This is a necessary-condition claim, not a prediction that a prompted model will instantiate it. The associated empirical risk is the constitutive conjecture: an independently defensible genuine achievement excluded by every interpretation preserving its actual organization, or a fully satisfying construction that nevertheless misclassifies it (FW5 L1364-L1380). Failing one convenient encoding is insufficient. Never redefine genuine correction as passing the very FW5 clause under test.

The predictions below are conditional case patterns. FW5's base class expressly includes irrational systems and failed attempts (L1192), while retained inquiry can permit false conjectures and provisional stopping (L968). It therefore supplies no unconditional forecast of which R002/R003 arm will improve, or comparative advantage over bare/native reasoning, without additional implementation and interpretation premises. Most retained properties constrain an attribution; this limited predictive yield is itself a finding.

Before an assessment, record the question/respect, target occurrence, boundary, grain, continuity, permitted resources, role bindings, prior situation, repair/protected obligations and contrasts. Give an independent case argument for correction: what was wrong, what changed, why the new position removes that defect, and what practical or explanatory difference follows, with rival readings retained. Do not let a reader's verdict, oracle label or supplied framework vocabulary decide it by fiat. Missing evidence is unresolved. A later change to these terms creates a separately identified assessment (FW5 L140, L170, L591-L593, L787-L800; PURPOSE L7).

**Failure taxonomy used in every property.** A *clause challenge* needs the independent achievement argument and the condition's failure, with the antecedent and relevant interpretation defended. A *missing definition* means FW5 has not supplied the needed application criterion. An *unsupported mapping* means an available criterion has not been connected to these actual events. An *operational failure* means delivery, parsing, execution or resources prevented the intended observation. A model making a mistake allowed by FW5 is not a contradiction of FW5. A definition's truth by stipulation cannot be empirically falsified; its adequacy for the intended subject can be challenged (FW5 L1368; PURPOSE L7).

## 2. PROPERTIES

### EC01 - Active content dependence

**(a) Statement.** An error-correcting episode attributed to use of an objection must place the represented objection on an actual, role-preserving route to an operative response, with nonconstant dependence under declared relevant contrasts.

**(b) Entailment.**

> For token-level claims, the physical interpretation of a history supplies process occurrences with their input and output ports, the port-to-port connections actually instantiated, and the component relations applied there. An **active route** is a connected subnetwork of those actual occurrences joining the named represented input to the named operative result. Its designation must satisfy the corresponding component relations and role bindings; it is not inferred from the presence of a similar sentence in a record. For reason-use and contribution claims the subnetwork must also have a nonconstant dependence on the relevant represented distinction under its declared contrasts. A dead branch, a cancelled signal with no downstream role, or a carrier detail that changes while the represented reason does not change cannot supply that dependence. Contingent alternatives and collective blocks remain available, so this condition does not equate contribution with single-variable necessity at the full endpoint.

FW5 L601.

> A response uses a reason when the represented content of the objection participates in the organization's deliberative transition. The response state includes interpretations, provisional uses, methods, and continuing questions, not just a terminal action label.

FW5 L626.

The response can change a method, interpretation, provisional use or continuing question; it need not change the terminal answer. Nonconstant dependence concerns the represented distinction and a suitable route or collective block, not necessarily the marginal effect of deleting one sentence. Equal final answers cannot establish non-use (FW5 L330-L332, L632).

**(c) Recorded hold/fail pattern.** Bind RT L17/L26/L31-L39: quoted target and defect connection, actual objection bytes delivered to RETURN, disposition's substantive reason, old/new operative relation, and a later same-question derivation that uses that relation. A supported occurrence identifies why replacing the new relation with the old changes that derivation, or why a relevant alternative objection changes the interpreted response. A failure witness is an established disconnected objection port or a canned response policy invariant over all *relevant* declared content contrasts, including its internal response-state roles. Prompt inclusion, a claim of uptake, one unchanged endpoint, a tail-edit flag or one insensitive contrast alone leaves attribution unresolved. Host routing shows availability; it does not show the opaque model used it (FW5 L628-L634, L640).

**(d) Conditional predictions.** R003 O01: a change from 'critics agree' to a discriminating inquiry must affect a particular proposed comparison or interpretation of its result; O04: a strategic-booking objection must affect an allocation decision or its defended retention. Decorative criticism predicts no such reason-governed difference. R002 decomposition: if a returned correction is credited, its binding must participate in the later STEP/SYNTHESIS or other identified operative result; mere accepted-step carriage is insufficient (RT L77). A useful explanation repair with unchanged numerical answer remains possible.

**(e) What failure challenges.** Independently established objection-based correction with no such route under any faithful interpretation challenges FW5 L601/L628 as necessary for reason use; a model passing those requirements with demonstrably decorative reasons challenges their sufficiency (L1372-L1374). Correction by fresh independent solving is not that counterexample: it defeats attribution to the objection. *Missing definition:* no defensible represented distinction or operative rule for the prose case. *Unsupported mapping:* route inferred only from text presence. *Operational:* no delivered return/use. None alone falsifies the clause.

**(f) Cost/current instrument.** Existing fields support a provisional route account and obvious non-delivery findings, at zero new calls. A discriminating behavioral condition needs a frozen common parent plus an operative-versus-archived objection intervention, with equal information apart from the declared route and matched resources; EC02 specifies complementary content contrasts. Opaque internal dependence still needs a substantive realization account. **With change** for the full causal test; not certified by current trace labels.

### EC02 - Three-way reason contrast

**(a) Statement.** An error-correcting episode claimed to use an objection cannot treat a content-preserving recoding, a relevant change of grounds or defect, and an irrelevant carrier disturbance as one undifferentiated input change wherever those distinctions are claimed.

**(b) Entailment.**

> Formally, the realization supplies a response suborganization \(D_s\) with represented target, grounds, defect connection, and response-role ports. A reason-use witness is a structural map from the represented objection organization into that suborganization such that content-preserving recodings preserve the interpreted transition, relevant changes to the grounds or alleged defect have the changes specified by the system's operative deliberative rule, and the mapped objection is on an active dependency route of this response occurrence. The map must preserve internal role bindings, not merely the endpoint string.

FW5 L628.

> The operative deliberative rule can be wrong. **Understanding and using an invalid objection does not make it valid.** The definition concerns whether the system used the content, not whether it responded ideally. Its contrast contract must include a content-changing case, a content-preserving recoding, and the distinction between a change in the objection and an irrelevant carrier disturbance wherever those distinctions are claimed. These are semantic comparisons, not a mandatory battery of physical tests.

FW5 L630.

Thus a reason-use witness must preserve the interpreted transition under genuine recoding, implement the operative rule's stated response to changed content, and separate carrier effects. The rule need not be correct. This is not a theorem that any paraphrase preserves content or that every changed reason changes the conclusion; the nonconstant condition is EC01. FW5 explicitly calls these semantic comparisons, not compulsory physical tests. Its global recoding theorem assumes transport of the whole relevant structure, not just changing a prompt (L1202-L1206).

**(c) Recorded hold/fail pattern.** RT L45-L48 must name exact canonical objection, paraphrase and role correspondence; a substantively changed ground/check with its predicted transition; and a carrier-only perturbation. Freeze question, prior state, resource and response interpretation. Record returned reasons, changed relation and use under each condition, leaving mapping disagreement unresolved. A supported pattern preserves meaning/role-level response under recoding and responds to the changed grounds as specified. Failure is a reproducible, semantically consequential divergence under a defensibly genuine recoding, or an irrelevant carrier difference controlling the alleged reason-dependent transition under the claimed invariance. A one-off stochastic endpoint mismatch is not this finding. A task recoding disagreement is first evidence about task access, not about objection use (RP L90-L94).

**(d) Conditional predictions.** R003 O05: equivalent formulations of an objection to causal identification should preserve the interpreted limitation; genuinely distinguishing new evidence may change which proposed investigation discriminates the accounts. Merely renaming community labels should not supply that evidence. R002: checked inverse maps can expose a numerical/derivation discrepancy, but the returned objection must separately pass a reason-level correspondence reading. A stall switch's new lineage or visibility must be disclosed, not called pure recoding.

**(e) What failure challenges.** A genuine reason-using correction whose actual organization cannot preserve the stated semantic contrasts challenges L628-L630 necessity. If a claimed paraphrase loses a distinction, the test challenges the paraphrase mapping, not FW5; inexplicit sensitivity outside the chosen grain also requires investigation before exclusion (L154, L1372). *Missing definition:* no operational equivalence criterion for the specific prose. *Unsupported mapping:* two messages called equivalent without bindings. *Operational:* missing companion response or failed schema. Carrier effects can defeat a proposed realization without refuting the constitutive conjecture.

**(f) Cost/current instrument.** **With change.** R002 currently records interpreted reason counterfactuals; its task variants are not this intervention. R003 has no justified recoding maps (OP L52). A physical local test could reuse one frozen parent and compare its canonical response with three independently receipted response branches: genuine recoding, changed content, carrier-only. Preserve a later-use branch per condition where required. This is a proposed resource unit, not a statistical guarantee or authorized dispatch; replicated/noise characterization would require its own allowance.

### EC03 - Standing coherence is application-specific

**(a) Statement.** An error-correcting episode that claims coherent continued use of a particular argument must retain enacted license, applicable scope and standing for every genuinely essential premise of that application.

**(b) Entailment.**

> Standing is the system's enacted permission to use a content in a particular application and respect. It may be explicit or inexplicit. An appraisal can change it without having earned prior certification. Generation alone does not logically entail it, but generation and provisional adoption may occur together in one physical event. Separating their meanings does not require separate timestamps or separate machines.

FW5 L638.

> For an argument application \(u\), let \(\operatorname{Prem}(u)\) be its declared essential premises, including interpretation and scope premises. Let \(\operatorname{Live}_j(d;u)\) indicate standing in appraisal \(j\), and let \(\operatorname{Lic}_j(u)\) and \(\operatorname{Scope}_j(u)\) indicate the enacted license and scope judgment. Define
> 
> \[
> \operatorname{Usable}_j(u)
> \iff
> \operatorname{Lic}_j(u)\land\operatorname{Scope}_j(u)
> \land\forall d\in\operatorname{Prem}(u),
> \operatorname{Live}_j(d;u).
> \tag{K2}
> \]
> 
> The actor can violate this coherence condition. The model then represents an error in its appraisal. A record declaring a dependency does not establish that the dependency is actually essential; an error in that declaration is another criticism target.

FW5 L642-L653.

K2 is a definition of usable application and a coherence condition the actor can violate. It is not an empirical guarantee that actors obey it, nor a claim that every genuine correction has a fully coherent surrounding theory. Declared essentiality itself can be mistaken. Generation and adoption may coincide; no extra 'approval' stage follows from K2.

**(c) Recorded hold/fail pattern.** Use RT L17, L23-L26 and L31-L40 to name argument u, essential premise d, exact withdrawal/scope change, disposition and subsequent operative derivation. Holding: the affected application is suspended/repaired, d receives a separately defended reinstatement, or the conclusion uses a genuinely different licensed argument. Failure of the *coherence claim*: d is explicitly withdrawn for this application, the later derivation still essentially uses d, and nothing restores its standing. A `taken-up` label followed by unchanged essential use is evidence for this discrepancy; a merely carried status is not a new decision. Implicit standing may need substantive interpretation, so silence about d is not proof of withdrawal.

**(d) Conditional predictions.** R003 O05: withdrawing an identification premise should remove license for the argument that uniquely attributes recovery to that cause; a narrower conditional explanation can remain. O04: rejecting a 'one booking per resident prevents capture' premise should affect the policy argument relying on it. R002 decomposition: if a current step's essential input loses standing, an agreeing use label cannot restore it; the next step must not be read as coherently licensed merely because prior bytes were accepted. The current immutability boundary may instead stop the occurrence (RP L202).

**(e) What failure challenges.** A correctly interpreted violation establishes the actor's appraisal error, expressly allowed at L653. Even if the episode corrects another error, this alone requires FW5 to relinquish **nothing**. A challenge to K2 as an adequate model of rational/coherent use would need an independent argument that the same application remains legitimately usable despite actual loss of an essential premise, with no alternative support or scope change. That attacks the essentiality/standing model, not the Boolean equivalence. *Missing definition:* no case-specific standing criterion. *Unsupported mapping:* dependency listed but not essential. *Operational:* truncated later step. These remain distinct.

**(f) Cost/current instrument.** **Yes, bounded contradiction audit** with current quoted claims/dispositions/step dependencies, zero new calls, where the record explicitly identifies withdrawal and continued reliance. Positive completeness about all implicit premises is unavailable. More systematic tests would add an application-to-essential-premise correspondence record and a later use targeting the withdrawn premise; the correspondence would remain criticizable.

### EC04 - Elimination does not confer truth

**(a) Statement.** An error-correcting episode cannot justify the falsity of a conclusion solely by withdrawing one argument for it, or justify a theory's falsity solely from a failed joint prediction without addressing its auxiliaries and interpretation.

**(b) Entailment.**

> Suppose a system withdraws an essential premise of \(u\). Equation (K2) no longer licenses \(u\). Another argument \(v\) for the same conclusion can remain usable. The conclusion is not thereby false. Withdrawing a criticism likewise does not establish the truth of its target.

FW5 L657.

> A machine check can establish a limited proposition such as equality with source bytes or validity of a derivation relative to formal premises. Its interpretation can have real consequences. A passing execution cannot make the program's specification, the model of the experiment, or the claimed relevance immune to prose criticism.

FW5 L661.

> For a test whose reasoning is
> 
> \[
> T\land B\land I\ \Longrightarrow\ O,
> \]
> 
> an established \(\neg O\) yields
> 
> \[
> \neg(T\land B\land I).
> \tag{K3}
> \]
> 
> It does not yield \(\neg T\) without additional premises about \(B\) and \(I\). Here \(B\) can include auxiliaries and \(I\) the observation interpretation. This is not an argument against testing. It states exactly what the test contradicts and where further criticism can matter.

FW5 L663-L676.

This forbids two inference forms, not conclusions reached through additional grounds. Withdrawing an objection also does not prove its target. Bearing requires an account of the alleged defect relative to the actual target (L609-L622); a valid limited check can matter without certifying its relevance. Criticism of a passing check remains legitimate.

**(c) Recorded hold/fail pattern.** RT L17-L25/L31-L36: identify the exact tested conjunction, expected outcome, actual redo/host value and the return's inference. Record which premise is withdrawn, what alternative support remains, and whether the conclusion is independently refuted. Holding: return limits the inference to the defeated route or supplies the additional premises needed to localize failure. Failure: it quotes only that route's defeat and asserts the opposite conclusion, or treats host pass/fail as settling an untested specification or relevance issue. A correct endpoint does not repair an invalid justification. No check, a false computation, changed inputs and a misinterpreted result are different findings.

**(d) Conditional predictions.** R003 O05: identical measured abundances do not by themselves identify the operative causal state; objections to one explanation leave rival causes and measurement assumptions open. O06: exposing one unsupported claim to unique optimality need not forbid a defensible provisional curriculum choice. R002 decomposition: rejecting one derivation must leave a separate valid route available; use-seat agreement is not host execution. In a separate CHECKER occurrence the actual stdout can settle its finite calculation while the code's model remains challengeable (CLI L100-L104).

**(e) What failure challenges.** An actor committing either fallacy is described by FW5, not a refutation of it. A genuine correction can accidentally accompany that fallacy. To challenge the prohibition one must defend the alleged inference without the missing premises; this would dispute the logical/dependency account, not merely show a useful answer. A verified correct checker whose application is wrong challenges its mapping or specification, exactly as L661 permits. *Missing definition:* unknown applicability of a prose/empirical check. *Unsupported mapping:* oracle result assigned to a different claim. *Operational:* timeout or unreadable output. None is a negative semantic observation of the absent result (L686-L688).

**(f) Cost/current instrument.** **Yes**, as a scoped inference audit of objection, redo, disposition, checker identity/result and alternative derivations, with zero new calls. No new truth oracle is needed. Testing whether explicit alternative-support presentation changes response would require separately matched prompts and resources; it is not mandated by FW5.

### EC05 - Build requires actual binding construction

**(a) Statement.** An error-correcting episode credited with Build must contain an attributable, use-relevant nontrivial binding construction in its actual subhistory, rather than only transfers of an already operative organization.

**(b) Entailment.**

> An elementary **transfer** is content-preserving carriage of an already integrated organization at the stated grain. An elementary **binding construction** changes or establishes a relation between represented parts in the recipient's operative organization. These are classifications of actual transformations under the interpretation, not intrinsic types conferred by a model's invitation or by a host event label.

FW5 L716.

> Define \(\operatorname{Build}_{\beta,\ell}(s,c,h,e)\) when an actual subhistory owned or controlled by \(s\), delimited at \(e\), prepares a represented organization for an attempted explanatory use of \(c\), contains a nontrivial binding construction relevant to that use, and is not merely a composition of content-preserving transfers of the already operative organization. A structural account of that subhistory must connect the constructed binding to the resulting use. Electricity supply, incidental causal ancestry, and ownership of an output file do not by themselves meet the condition.

FW5 L718.

> A learner may receive a complete exposition and nevertheless construct the bindings through which it becomes usable to that learner. That is reconstructive construction, not historical firstness. A relay may deliver the same exposition without such construction. A physically faithful copy of an already functioning cognitive organization, if permitted by the relevant physics, must not be pronounced a fresh invention merely because the recipient's address is new. The interpretation must distinguish transfer of an operative organization from its reconstruction.

FW5 L722.

A construction witness identifies incoming carriers, already operative organizations, constructed/reconstructed bindings and their resulting representation (L714-L720). A fixed algorithm can perform such construction; determinism, deduction and search neither establish nor disqualify Origin (L769). Thus 'designer supplied the procedure' does not entail 'designer supplied every use-specific binding'. Receiving a complete exposition can still involve reconstruction. Build is necessary for Origin, not for every Repair.

**(c) Recorded hold/fail pattern.** Link RT L11-L13/L17/L26/L33/L37-L39 and L72-L77 to all actual supplied content. State the relation between represented parts before and after, the owned operation establishing it, and its connection to attempted use. A supported Build account exhibits that transformation in the public work and explains its actual route, admitting opacity. A concrete failure is a completely observed relay that only copies an already integrated relation at the fixed grain while claiming it constructed the relation. Merely producing a new sentence, using a template or retrieving source material settles neither side. Public prose asserting 'I constructed' is not the physical-semantic witness.

**(d) Conditional predictions.** R003 O03 directly asks this distinction: an adequate account should separate transfer of an operative organization from the recipient's reconstruction and expose uncertainty about actual operations. O02 may show a newly established distinction serving a later inquiry without proving historical novelty. R002: a step can construct a problem-specific dependency from accepted results, while copying an accepted result into SYNTHESIS can be transfer; neither step IDs nor a correct synthesis alone establish Build.

**(e) What failure challenges.** A genuine *originative construction* excluded despite its actual organization being faithfully represented challenges Build's necessity in G; an independently demonstrated mere relay satisfying every Build condition challenges sufficiency (L33, L1372-L1374). A useful transferred correction need not be originative, so a genuine correction without Build does not itself refute FW5. *Missing definition:* the reading gives no task-independent decision rule for 'nontrivial' or which bindings count at a grain. *Unsupported mapping:* visible derivation mistaken for the actual constructing process. *Operational:* missing incoming/step records. Keep these separate.

**(f) Cost/current instrument.** **With change** for a discriminating construction attribution: add provenance annotations distinguishing supplied organization from produced bindings, plus a transparent transfer comparator and a reconstruction case under the same target/use interpretation. Such annotations alone are not proof. Reading extant public step records costs zero calls and can locate candidates, but cannot recover hidden construction history. No demand to store native hidden reasoning is implied.

### EC06 - New is historical absence, not fresh wording

**(a) Statement.** An error-correcting episode credited with historical New cannot have a structurally equivalent content already in the attributed continuing system's earlier deployable repertoire, including its initialization.

**(b) Entailment.**

> Fix a system boundary, grain, history, and continuity criterion before assessing an event. Let
> 
> \[
> R_{<e}(s,h)
> =
> \bigcup_{\xi\text{ before }e}R_{\beta,\ell}(s,\xi),
> \]
> 
> including the initial repertoire within the stipulated continuity. Define
> 
> \[
> \operatorname{New}(s,c,h,e)
> \iff
> \neg\exists d\in R_{<e}(s,h),
> \ d\equiv_\ell c.
> \tag{N}
> \]
> 
> The equivalence is structural at the stated grain, not string equality or similarity. Forgotten prior understanding belongs to \(R_{<e}\) when the claim is first creation by that continuing organization. Reacquisition can therefore be an achievement without being first creation.
> 
> Acquisition concerns absence from the immediately prior deployable repertoire and presence afterward. Newness concerns the whole stipulated historical baseline. Construction concerns provenance. These relations can come apart.
> 
> An incomplete archive leaves a historical claim uncertain. It does not make the absent content historically new. Initializing a new system boundary with an already functioning organization must include that organization in its baseline rather than manufacture novelty by forgetting its initialization.

FW5 L728-L750.

New quantifies over the whole stipulated earlier repertoire, not just the immediately preceding prompt or session. Deployability is ability to use content, not storage or all logical consequences of current knowledge (L696-L708). Old equivalent deployable content defeats New; incomplete evidence of it does not establish New. This is not a requirement that every useful repair introduce new content.

**(c) Recorded hold/fail pattern.** RT identity/boundary and exact source exposure fields must bind c, its structural equivalence at grain, continuity and historical baseline. Holding can be established in a transparent bounded system with an exhaustively justified repertoire model and genuinely absent equivalent content, together with later deployability. A single demonstrated prior deployment of equivalent c by that continuing system is a failure witness for the particular New claim. A matching string is inadequate without equivalence and use; a failed recall probe is inadequate evidence of absence. RT cannot reconstruct proprietary pretraining or earlier latent capacities.

**(d) Conditional predictions.** R003 O03: partly known history predicts an unresolved first-origin attribution even if a specific acquisition or reconstruction is observable. O01/O02 fresh formulations may support useful distinctions without firstness. R002: a newly emitted derivation step can be a new occurrence while its content was previously deployable; repeated successful use after construction is deployment, not repeated first creation (L972-L983). Naming a new run or resetting context cannot manufacture New for a boundary that retains initialized competence.

**(e) What failure challenges.** Prior equivalent deployment refutes a New attribution, not FW5. Genuine correction despite prior knowledge is compatible with the explicit reacquisition distinction. A genuine *historically first* creation that necessarily has equivalent prior deployable content would challenge N's criterion, grain/equivalence or the proposed connection to origin; ordinary reuse is not that case. *Missing definition:* empirical repertoire membership and content equivalence for this system. *Unsupported mapping:* prompt-visible archive equated with all prior capability. *Operational:* missing records, not absent knowledge. Unknown history must remain unknown.

**(f) Cost/current instrument.** **No** for full historical New of current model-inclusive R002/R003 boundaries. Zero-call provenance reading may falsify a specific claim with a known prior witness. A transparent initialized toy organization plus a fully specified continuity/repertoire account could test the restricted attribution, but it would be a different system claim. No finite battery of prompting closes the model's unknown historical baseline.

### EC07 - Origin requires attempted use as well as New and Build

**(a) Statement.** An error-correcting episode credited with an originative explanatory contribution must include an actual attempt to address a recognized difficulty, historical New and Build, while neither truth nor completed criticism is necessary for that originative act.

**(b) Entailment.**

> An attempted account is a represented organization actually used to address a recognized difficulty, whether or not it satisfies (E). Write this relation as \(\operatorname{Attempt}(s,c,p,h,e)\). Its interpretation includes the active question and the content's operative role; a task label alone is insufficient.
> 
> Define
> 
> \[
> \operatorname{Origin}_{\beta,\ell}(s,c,p,h,e)
> \iff
> \operatorname{Attempt}(s,c,p,h,e)
> \land\operatorname{New}(s,c,h,e)
> \land\operatorname{Build}_{\beta,\ell}(s,c,h,e).
> \tag{G}
> \]
> 
> No truth, universal capacity, permanent storage, or completed criticism of this very output is a conjunct. A false explanatory conjecture can be originative. An account of an inherited theory's defect can be originative even though the original theory was not authored by the critic.

FW5 L754-L767.

G is a conjunction, so neither a fresh string nor a useful artifact to an external reader supplies the missing attempted use (L769). Conversely an incorrect conjecture may satisfy G. Calling something an originative act and calling it a successful correction make different claims. A creative critical episode connects an Origin contribution to its inquiry; it need not criticize that same contribution afterward (L775).

**(c) Recorded hold/fail pattern.** RT L10-L13/L17/L26/L31-L39 supplies the recognized difficulty, prior target, operative relation and actual attempted application. Add the separate EC05 construction and EC06 historical witnesses. Holding of the full attribution requires all three; uncertainty in any remains uncertainty in Origin. Failure of Attempt is positively evidenced where content is only emitted/copied into an unused archive and no problem-directed operation at the attributed boundary employs it. An answer contract named 'conjecture' supplies only an invitation. A false checker result defeats adequacy in the tested respect, not G by itself.

**(d) Conditional predictions.** R003 O02: trying a new distinction in a concrete re-expression could be an Attempt despite failure to resolve the opening; a vocabulary list with no operative application does not establish it. O03 should keep attempted construction, firstness and successful correction separate. R002: a false proposed step can be a meaningful attempted account; if later rejected, its episode history is preserved. A final correct synthesis does not retroactively make every intermediate conjecture adequate or new.

**(e) What failure challenges.** An independently defended originative explanatory act lacking Attempt, New or Build under every faithful interpretation challenges G's necessity. Correction alone with no New or Build does not. To challenge G's sufficiency, establish all three and independently show why the episode is not originative; disliking mechanical generation or small significance is insufficient under L767-L769/L1196. *Missing definition:* what recognized difficulty and operative role amount to for this prose. *Unsupported mapping:* task label substituted for Attempt. *Operational:* no actual attempt delivered or subsequent behavior unavailable. None automatically settles the constitutive claim.

**(f) Cost/current instrument.** **No** for full G with the current model-inclusive historical boundary (EC06); **yes** for the narrower public Attempt audit at zero new calls. A task-specific use can strengthen that component, but no extra use call cures unknown New or supplies missing Build. No truth gate or mandatory separate generation/adoption timestamp should be added in FW5's name.

### EC08 - Completeness requires a target, objection and response, not success

**(a) Statement.** An error-correcting episode presented as a complete critical episode must contain a recognized difficulty, an available prior target, a conjectural objection and a content-sensitive response yielding a situation, but completion cannot stand for improvement or resolution.

**(b) Entailment.**

> A complete critical episode contains a recognized difficulty, a represented target available before the criticism of it, a conjectural objection, and a content-sensitive response with a resulting situation. The objection need not have actual bearing, and the response need not improve the situation. An incomplete engagement is represented without being called complete.
> 
> A creative critical episode is a complete critical episode containing an originative contribution under (G) connected to its inquiry. That contribution can precede or follow criticism of a different, inherited target. Conjecture-before-criticism remains a target dependence, not a global three-stage schedule.
> 
> Closing an episode is an engagement decision. It may accompany acceptance, rejection, suspension, disregard, or a decision to investigate elsewhere. It does not prove that the problem is solved, and the episode can be reopened or absorbed into another.

FW5 L773-L777.

Target-before-objection is a semantic/causal dependence, not a mandatory global three-stage schedule. The target may be inherited or inexplicit (L55, L857-L859). An unchanged answer, reasoned rejection, suspension or even harmful uptake can conclude a complete critical episode. No objection with actual bearing is required for completeness.

**(c) Recorded hold/fail pattern.** Use RT L10-L12/L17/L26-L35/L41/L50/L57, with parent/child links. Holding: locate all constituents and their causal connection, assess response content-sensitivity using EC01/EC02, and identify resulting use/appraisal/question state. Failure of a completeness claim: a positively established absence of a target before its supposed criticism, or a run that terminates before any response. Missing public articulation of a target is not proof of no inexplicit target. Empty objection opportunity, unavailable critic, parser label and `slot_chain_present` alone do not make a complete episode (RP L72; CLI L132). A `closed_without_propagation` episode can be complete yet lack R002's later-use witness.

**(d) Conditional predictions.** R003 O06: rejecting the demand for a uniquely best curriculum and making a provisional actionable choice can close an episode while the value conflict remains; it is not automatically a Repair. O05 unresolved causal identification is similarly compatible with completion. R002: a full local step episode can exist before a later step's delivery failure; no complete whole-problem episode or synthesis is inferred. The A2 plan reports such later delivery/resource failures (RP L238); this memorandum does not re-adjudicate their contents.

**(e) What failure challenges.** A genuine *complete critical correction* with no prior represented target, no objection or no content-sensitive response would challenge the necessity of L773 if inexplicit/distributed realizations are genuinely excluded. A correction obtained without any criticism is not a counterexample to that conditional. Lack of improvement, or completed rejection of a valid objection, is permitted and requires FW5 to relinquish nothing. *Missing definition:* the episode boundary or recognized difficulty lacks a supported application. *Unsupported mapping:* one call equated with one episode. *Operational:* failed critic/return makes observation incomplete, not substantive criticism false.

**(f) Cost/current instrument.** **Yes**, for structural completeness and incompleteness audits using existing chronology, target, disposition and outcome fields, zero new calls. The content-sensitive component retains EC01/EC02's causal limitations. Additional fields are not needed merely to record suspension or failure; do not demand every complete episode be an improvement.

### EC09 - Repair changes a fixed obligation without losing protected success

**(a) Statement.** An error-correcting episode credited as local Repair must produce a failed-to-satisfied change in at least one fixed repair obligation while retaining each previously satisfied protected obligation under a stated cross-representation interpretation.

**(b) Entailment.**

> Let \(O\) be the explicitly claimed repair obligations and \(P\) the protected obligations for a comparison, both fixed for that comparison. Their definitions need not predate the discovery historically; they must not shift inside its assessment. Define a local repair by contribution \(\Delta\) as
> 
> \[
> \begin{aligned}
> \operatorname{Repair}_{O,P}(\xi,\xi';\Delta)
> \iff{}&
> \exists o\in O\,[\neg o(\xi)\land o(\xi')]\\
> &\land\forall r\in P\,[r(\xi)\Rightarrow r(\xi')]\\
> &\land\operatorname{ProducedBy}(\Delta,\xi,\xi';O).
> \end{aligned}
> \tag{P}
> \]
> 
> Here \(\operatorname{ProducedBy}\) is a construction account of the actual change in the relevant obligation-bearing organization, using the history and structural account conditions already defined. It is not satisfied by temporal succession alone. A transport supplies the interpretation of \(o\) and \(r\) across changed representations; without one they are not silently compared.
> 
> Equation (P) does not rank alternatives. It states that a particular defect was repaired, that declared protected achievements were not lost, and that the contribution produced the change. Losses outside \(P\) must be exposed. The result is local progress, not an all-things-considered verdict.

FW5 L787-L802.

The existential improvement, universal protected-preservation and ProducedBy conjuncts are all necessary. Obligations are specified predicates, not an aggregate merit function (L783-L785). Improvement need not add accepted claims: recognizing underdetermination can remove a false identification claim while leaving the original numerical question unresolved (L810). FW5 protects the declared P, not every conceivable good consequence; R002's choice to protect all already-correct requested facts is a study rule (RP L92).

**(c) Recorded hold/fail pattern.** RT L21/L25/L33-L40/L49/L52 must state O and P, why their predicates fit this difficulty, each relevant before/after truth claim, the old/new transport and the contribution producing the change. Holding: a defensible false-to-true obligation transition, all old protected successes retained, and a supported causal contribution. Failure of the scoped claim: no such transition, one named protected success becomes false, or a positively established independent change with no relevant contribution. Mere temporal order is not ProducedBy. An endpoint patch retaining an erroneous derivation fails a protected derivation obligation; it is not automatically failure of *every possible* Repair claim.

**(d) Conditional predictions.** R003 O04: a repair of strategic capture that destroys a protected newcomers' opportunity does not satisfy that chosen P, even if utilization improves. O06: adding uncertainty while abandoning the requested actionable next-term decision is a loss if that obligation was protected. O05: a defensible account of non-identification may repair an unsupported causal claim. R002 decomposition: corrected current-step output plus corrupted accepted dependency cannot count as protected repair; a correct final number with an unrepaired required proof remains inadequate to that proof obligation (RP L78/L86/L220-L222).

**(e) What failure challenges.** An independently genuine *repair in exactly the declared respect* with none of P's kinds of attributable obligation change challenges P's adequacy. A trade-off judged good overall while losing a protected fact does not refute this explicitly local relation; FW5 declines an overall ranking (L804/L1378). Selecting P after seeing the loss is a changed assessment, not a rescue. *Missing definition:* disputed legitimacy or truth criterion of an open-ended obligation. *Unsupported mapping:* changed framing silently compared as the same predicate, or succession called production. *Operational:* absent later use or checker result. A real harmful transition is a substantive adverse observation, not mere instrumentation failure.

**(f) Cost/current instrument.** **Yes**, for scoped obligation/derivation/loss readings from current fields at zero new calls. Full causal ProducedBy retains EC01's limitation. R003 needs explicit case-level predicates and competing readings; its sealed briefs cannot be an answer oracle (OP L56-L64). An intervention on the purported contribution would strengthen attribution. No sum, weighting or success count is required.

### EC10 - Created knowledge must participate in the repair

**(a) Statement.** An error-correcting episode credited with created explanatory knowledge must contain an originative, adequate and subsequently deployable result whose relevant binding actually helps produce the epistemic repair.

**(b) Entailment.**

> Practical repair alone is insufficient for knowledge creation. In particular, an obligation such as “increase our confidence” must not make a false belief into knowledge. An **epistemic obligation** in this definition requires a correct structural account to be deployable in a specified problem-use respect, or requires the correction of an operative use through such a deployable account. Accounts of a defect, a scope distinction, a mistaken question, or an impossibility are included by taking that relation as the specified question. An endorsement count or an ungrounded satisfaction report is not such an obligation.
> 
> A contribution creates explanatory knowledge in the stated respect when it belongs to a creative critical episode, produces a repair under (P) of at least one epistemic obligation, and contains an originative, adequate, subsequently deployable contribution that participates in producing that repair. A creative but false side-remark cannot borrow credit from an unrelated correction in the same episode. The new content can be an account, an account of a defect, a revised interpretation, or an explained limitation.

FW5 L814-L816.

> \[
> \begin{aligned}
> \operatorname{CreateEK}_{\beta,\ell,O,P}
> (s,\Delta,h,e)
> \iff{}&
> \operatorname{CreativeCriticalEpisode}(s,\Delta,h,e)\\
> &\land\operatorname{Repair}_{O,P}(\xi,\xi';\Delta)\\
> &\land\exists o\in O_{\mathrm{ep}}\ \exists c,p_c,e_c\,\bigl[\\
> &\qquad e_c\preceq_h e\\
> &\qquad\land\neg o(\xi)\land o(\xi')\\
> &\qquad\land\operatorname{Origin}(s,c,p_c,h,e_c)\\
> &\qquad\land\operatorname{Account}(c,p_c)\\
> &\qquad\land c\in\operatorname{Result}(\Delta)\\
> &\qquad\land\operatorname{Deploy}_{\beta,\ell}(s,c,\xi';U_c)\\
> &\qquad\land\operatorname{ProducesVia}(\Delta,c,o;\xi,\xi')\bigr].
> \end{aligned}
> \tag{EK}
> \]
> 
> Here \(O_{\mathrm{ep}}\subseteq O\) is the set of epistemic obligations just defined; \(\preceq_h\) includes equality as well as causal precedence; and \(\operatorname{ProducesVia}\) requires the construction account of the repair to contain the relevant binding of \(c\) on its active route. These are additional conditions, not inferences from temporal co-occurrence. The indices \(\xi,\xi'\) identify the episode's stated comparison situations. For a community attribution, \(s\) denotes that composite and its integrated use, not an unspecified future reader. A result merely stored for a hypothetical reader can be a valuable artifact without establishing this possession claim.

FW5 L820-L839.

EK joins Origin, Account, membership in Result, deployment and ProducesVia for the *same* relevant contribution, with origin no later than creation. A false side remark cannot acquire credit from a separate correction. A correct account in a saved file for a hypothetical reader does not establish possession at the stated system boundary. Epistemic obligations include explained scope, defects and limitations, not only a solved original question.

**(c) Recorded hold/fail pattern.** Identify c in the returned result; give its problem/Account argument (target anchoring, fidelity, question fidelity, non-circular dependence, non-vacuity: L172-L228); bind EC05-EC07 witnesses; name the epistemic obligation repaired and the exact later use and relation in RT L35-L39/L77. Holding requires each link, not an attractive isolated novelty and a separate correct answer. A clear failure of the *claimed EK route* is a documented correct repair wholly produced by inherited content while the purported new c is an unused appendix, or an inadequate c doing the alleged explanatory work. Lack of public later use alone is unresolved deployment, because FW5 admits internal semantic use (L696-L708); the harness's closing-only status is narrower.

**(d) Conditional predictions.** R003 O01/O03: useful revisions do not entail created knowledge if the alleged new distinction is idle or its history unknown; a distinction that changes the proposed discriminating inquiry supplies a candidate active link. O04/O06: a new explanatory framing should make a specific difference to the actionable decision/criticism route if credited with its epistemic repair. R002: a new corrected binding must matter to downstream step/synthesis under the same premises, not merely coexist with a correct result that another unchanged route supplies.

**(e) What failure challenges.** Independently genuine *knowledge creation*, not merely useful correction, without any such adequate-originative active contribution challenges EK necessity. A construction satisfying every EK clause yet independently shown to make only a mistaken epistemic repair challenges Account or its bridge to the obligations (L1380). Do not infer that counterexample merely from unimportant content; local significance is explicitly limited. *Missing definition:* deployability/epistemic obligation not operationally grounded. *Unsupported mapping:* contribution, origin and repair witnesses refer to different contents. *Operational:* absent synthesis/use. Unknown New blocks certification but is not disproof.

**(f) Cost/current instrument.** **No** for full EK certification in current model-inclusive occurrences: EC06 and the realization/Account dependencies remain open. **With a named contribution-to-use mapping** the existing fields can expose unrelated-novelty or archive-only attribution errors without new calls. A prospective active-versus-archived contribution condition plus same-question use can test that narrower link; it cannot establish historical New by itself.

### EC11 - Recursive scrutiny needs operative return

**(a) Statement.** An error-correcting episode invoked as a witness of recursive scrutiny must make the scrutinized use, method, interpretation or attention state reachable by a relevant subsidiary result, with an alternative use-state possible unless only one is applicable under the contract.

**(b) Entailment.**

> At situation \(\xi\), an aspect \(d\) of the organization's represented activity is **scrutinizable** when there is an owned, physically admitted continuation in which a description of \(d\) becomes a represented target, conjecture and criticism can be directed at it, and the result can affect its subsequent operative use. The scrutiny concerns the same \(d\), under a stated interpretation, not a similarly named surrogate.
> 
> Return relevance is a capability of the transition organization. There must be a path from a subsidiary result to the parent's relevant use, method, interpretation, or attention state. At least one alternative subsidiary result in the contract must permit a different use-state, unless the contract itself establishes that only one use is applicable. A decorative transcript channel permanently disconnected from the operative state does not satisfy the condition. No requirement says a failed objection must change the conclusion.

FW5 L1050-L1052.

> Recursive critical capacity requires closure under every finite continuation of such scrutiny within its declared domain, with role preservation and return. Resources may depend on the finite depth. The quantifiers are
> 
> \[
> \forall n<\omega\ \forall\text{ admitted target chains of length }n,
> \quad\exists\text{ an owned enabling continuation}.
> \tag{RC}
> \]
> 
> The target chains include criticisms, interpretations, methods, and prior appraisal practices. They are not restricted to object-level claims. Their admissibility is fixed by the domain and the operation of taking a represented use, or a criticism of that use, as a target. It is not defined as “chains the candidate already succeeds at.” The condition includes every such finite chain in the declared domain; it does not authorize silently deleting a chain because it concerns the actual evaluator. Equation (RC) does not require an actually completed infinite regress or simultaneous infinite storage. No internal perfect truth predicate is required: a statement about the system's own adequacy remains a represented conjecture, not automatic metatheoretic disquotation.

FW5 L1054-L1062.

One witnessed return is a local necessary ingredient; it does not establish RC's every-finite-chain quantifier. Scrutinizing an evaluator or method is not outside the domain merely because the present harness cannot change it. Conversely no rule requires every failed objection to change the conclusion. The parent can provisionally retain or suspend the same use for represented reasons.

**(c) Recorded hold/fail pattern.** Bind RT parent/child identity, target quote, subsidiary objection/check, disposition and actual parent request/use in L10-L13/L26/L31-L41. Declare the same-target correspondence and an alternative subsidiary finding that would alter the relevant use-state. Holding: a concrete returned result reaches and substantively changes or grounds retention of that state under the contract. Failure of a local *operative-return capability claim*: a mechanically or substantively established permanent disconnection, or a policy forbidding every outcome from affecting that target. One rejected criticism, a resource stop or one unchanged endpoint cannot establish such incapacity. Stall switching is an instrument change, not proof of recursive semantic return.

**(d) Conditional predictions.** R003 O01: criticism of an inquiry's evaluation method must be able to revise that method's actual proposed use, not just append caveats. O06: a criticism of the objective must be able to change the provisional decision procedure while maintaining the explicit need to act. R002: criticism of a previously accepted step encounters an immutable-state boundary; under A1 it is recorded as unresolved rather than silently rewriting it (RP L202). That is a concrete limit of the present route, not a refutation of FW5's possible recursive class.

**(e) What failure challenges.** A genuine recursively critical practice whose faithful organization excludes such return challenges L1050-L1052 necessity. A claimed RC model that excludes evaluator-directed chains solely because it fails them contradicts its own L1062 domain premise. An ordinary genuine correction without recursive scrutiny need not meet RC. *Missing definition:* same-parent interpretation or admissible target domain unspecified. *Unsupported mapping:* a similarly named surrogate treated as the parent. *Operational:* unavailable return/step. No finite set of successes certifies RC, and a finite bounded failure does not prove all enabling continuations impossible (L1072-L1074/L1258-L1260).

**(f) Cost/current instrument.** **With change** for a tested return to an already accepted method/step: add a separately versioned parent revision/suspension route, preserve the old history, and contrast at least two relevant subsidiary results. Current fields can document the local boundary at zero calls. No finite call budget establishes the full RC quantifier. This is not a request to modify A1/A2 or execute a successor.

### EC12 - Enabling conditions cannot define success into existence

**(a) Statement.** An error-correcting episode cited for capacity must have independently meaningful enabling conditions, and an episode cited for origin must attribute supplied contributions to their actual source.

**(b) Entailment.**

> A capacity claim names the background learning, evidence, time, memory, tools, communication, and maintenance it permits. It also names the achievement at issue.
> 
> Understanding a supplied explanation may permit its complete communication. Reconstructing an understanding absent from the recipient requires the recipient's construction of the relevant use organization. Originating an unsupplied solution must not be witnessed by silently providing a complete functioning solution or a competent replacement thinker.
> 
> The restriction is not that the environment contribute no knowledge. Inquiry always has background. The restriction is that the contribution credited to the system must actually be made by that system under the declared boundary. A receipt of a complete explanation can contribute to a later genuinely creative reconstruction or extension, but it cannot establish historical origination of what was supplied.
> 
> An enabling condition is inadmissible if its only description is “the conditions under which the system succeeds.” It must identify physically and semantically meaningful resources and contributions independently of that success claim. It may be discovered during inquiry; it cannot hide the desired achievement as a condition.

FW5 L1095-L1101.

> Let \(\operatorname{Exec}_\Theta(\pi,z,i;\chi)\) be the set of maximal executions admitted by the physical process theory and task-completion contract when the protocol starts in constructor state \(z\), receives task input \(i\), and operates under enabling conditions \(\chi\). Those conditions specify resources, communication, maintenance, and the disturbances against which a capability is claimed. They do not filter executions according to whether the desired answer occurred.

FW5 L920.

The two exclusions have different scopes: success-conditioned enabling descriptions are inadmissible in capacity claims; supplied contributions must be attributed to their actual boundary in origination claims. Specified resources, carefully constructed tasks and supplied explanations are not forbidden. The text permits complete communication for understanding and creative reconstruction. Nor is L1101 an extra conjunct of each Repair or EK occurrence; EK's separate active-route condition is EC10.

**(c) Recorded hold/fail pattern.** RT L9/L13/L26/L55-L56/L62-L64 must preserve exact input exposure, task, material sources, outcome-independent resource contract and external contributions. Holding of this exclusion rule: the named condition has meaningful content independent of its outcome, and supplied answers/procedures receive explicit attribution. Failure of a capacity argument: its domain or enabling set is only 'the instances where this system succeeds', or the credited unsupplied achievement arrives as a complete functioning solution from an unacknowledged external author. A prior difficulty-based admission rule is not automatically that fallacy; its selection boundary and claim must remain explicit. A deliberately constructed toy problem is not inherently inadmissible.

**(d) Conditional predictions.** R003 O01: a criticism-specific advantage remains unsupported without matched multi-call/information conditions; that matching is a mechanism-identification proposal, not an FW5 theorem (OP L94). O03: distinguish the designer's delivered procedure from the recipient's actual new bindings. R002: calibration-selected ceiling cases delimit a stratum; a decomposition completing one of them is finite package evidence, not universal capacity. A2's larger allowances and repairs change enabling conditions and cannot be hidden in a pure decomposition claim (RP L248-L254).

**(e) What failure challenges.** A useful correct episode on a contrived task does not challenge this condition, and successful copying can be acquisition without origin. A defensible capacity attribution requiring only outcome-selected enabling conditions would challenge L1101's adequacy; evidence of genuinely originating what was fully supplied would challenge the provenance distinction, after distinguishing reconstruction. The critique's claim that task 'rigging' alone defeats an individual EK event does not follow from this passage. *Missing definition:* an independence criterion for a disputed enabling description. *Unsupported mapping:* operator contribution omitted from the boundary. *Operational:* incorrect exposure/resource records. Observed resource limits constrain this implementation, not FW5's whole possible class.

**(f) Cost/current instrument.** **Yes**, for the narrow exposure/selection/resource attribution audit, zero new calls using frozen wires, plans and histories; not a test of universal capacity. A causal criticism-versus-repeated-solving comparison requires a separately budgeted neutral continuation control matched in problem, seats, calls, ceilings and public-history allowance. No new success threshold or ranking follows.

## 3. WHAT FW5 DOES NOT SUPPLY

These are missing *application criteria or evidence*, not an assertion that every named relation lacks a mathematical definition. FW5 openly treats representation, physical interpretation and normative relations as primitive data (L1152-L1176). The distinction matters: supplying those inputs is substantive additional work, and a proposed operational definition must not be credited to FW5 or silently used to absorb adverse evidence (PURPOSE L7).

**GAP1 - A usable identity test for open prose across recodings.** The closest passage is:

> A grain fixes which structural distinctions count as differences for the attribution at issue. A genuine recoding is an isomorphism of the retained structure at that grain. Coarsening is not automatically an isomorphism. Bundling two commitments into one can change the answer to a question about individual contribution while preserving an answer about their joint organization.

FW5 L154.

It defines genuine recoding structurally, but supplies neither the retained structure for O02/O06 nor a procedure for discovering the relevant roles, reference and scope. Equal strings, similar sentences and matching conclusions are explicitly insufficient (L144-L156). A task's declared inverse map addresses that task, not automatically an objection's meaning. An agreed local correspondence is a criticizable additional interpretation, not identity certified by a parser. This is the central unresolved input to EC02/EC06.

**GAP2 - The concrete use task for a terminal prose conclusion.** The closest definition is:

> For a content organization \(c\), a use task specifies requests to employ its represented relations in a declared family of problem contexts and the corresponding internal or external semantic outcomes. The requests can include interpreting a component, following a dependence, relating an implication to a question, or treating the content as a target of criticism. The outcomes are changes in represented organization, not necessarily sentences.

FW5 L696.

FW5 does supply a broad definition: use may be internal, and treating content as a criticism target can count. It does not supply the particular requests, semantic outcomes, retained capability or realized integration for a specific final answer with no observed continuation (L698-L708/L839). Thus 'no downstream call' is not proof of no use; conversely a hypothetical future reader is not current possession. The harness needs an independently motivated use or a substantive account of internal use, neither invented solely to make its chosen answer pass. This affects EC01/EC07/EC10.

**GAP3 - Independence of a critic.** The closest criticism passage is:

> A criticism has a represented target \(z\), an alleged defect \(\delta\), grounds \(g\), and a proposed connection from \(g\) to \(\delta\) relative to a question. A defect is a specified failure condition on the target or its application: inconsistency, a false dependency, a missing distinction, an unsupported reference, a failed task, a misapplied rule, or another condition whose interpretation is supplied.

FW5 L609.

Grounds and a target connection specify criticism; they do not define statistical independence, independent derivation, outside-the-basin access or model-lineage diversity. Actual process ports matter at L599-L601, and distinct sufficient receipts at L682, but neither entails that a second model has independent errors. Ruling 10 (L16) is a declared engineering rule about lineage; RP L20 itself calls the basin idea a mechanism proposal, not a FW5 theorem. Two derivations in a single prompt or two provider labels cannot fill this gap. FW5 supplies no prediction that cross-lineage criticism must outperform same-lineage criticism.

**GAP4 - How to identify the operative rule and its relevant contrasts without tailoring them to the result.** FW5 L601 requires 'nonconstant dependence on the relevant represented distinction under its declared contrasts'; L628 requires that 'relevant changes to the grounds or alleged defect have the changes specified by the system's operative deliberative rule'. Those are real constraints, and the dead-branch exclusion is substantive. They do not identify that rule or its contrast family from public responses. A rule written after observing a failure could redescribe nearly any transition; an independently justified prospective correspondence is therefore needed for EC01/EC02. Finite endpoint comparisons alone cannot distinguish all organizations (L1210-L1224). This is an unsupported application if the rule is simply asserted, not automatically a flaw in the formal definition.

**GAP5 - Recognition of actual standing and essential premises.** The closest caution is:

> The actor can violate this coherence condition. The model then represents an error in its appraisal. A record declaring a dependency does not establish that the dependency is actually essential; an error in that declaration is another criticism target.

FW5 L653.

K2 is exact once Lic, Scope, Live and Prem are supplied (L642-L650). It does not tell a reader how to determine them from a model's disposition text, nor whether a listed premise is truly essential or a represented step is merely post hoc. An explicit withdrawal/continued-use conflict is readable; an exhaustive implicit standing account is not provided. EC03's current audit is consequently narrower than full standing attribution.

**GAP6 - An application criterion for nontrivial binding construction.** L718 says Build 'contains a nontrivial binding construction relevant to that use'; L716 defines a binding construction as changing or establishing 'a relation between represented parts in the recipient's operative organization'. These state what must be shown, but not which relation and grain makes a particular rewrite nontrivial, nor how to establish it inside an opaque model. There is no licensed significance threshold to supply instead. A transparent relay/construction contrast may provide an argument. Knowing the algorithm was designer-written does not establish the absence of new bindings (L722/L769). EC05 is therefore not decidable from a fresh result field.

**GAP7 - Access to the historical deployable repertoire.** The closest explicit limit is:

> An incomplete archive leaves a historical claim uncertain. It does not make the absent content historically new. Initializing a new system boundary with an already functioning organization must include that organization in its baseline rather than manufacture novelty by forgetting its initialization.
