C13 — Cyclic constrained multiset words

Givens:
- Reading cyclically, no four consecutive symbols may spell ABCA; occurrences may cross the last/first boundary.
- Positions are labelled: rotations and reflections are different words.
- Equal symbols may not be adjacent, including the last and first symbols.
- Form a length-16 word with exactly 6 A, 5 B, and 5 C symbols.

Question: How many words satisfy all conditions? Give a recurrence/state description that includes the cyclic closure checks.
Work from the prose only. Do not use code or retrieval. If the information is insufficient, say cannot decide and name the missing derivation.
