C07 — Exact stopping time in a Markov chain

Givens:
- The transient states are 0,1,2,3,4 and `X` is absorbing.
- From 0: to 1 with 1/2, to 2 with 1/3, to X with 1/6.
- From 1: to 0 with 1/4, to 3 with 1/2, to X with 1/4.
- From 2: to 1 with 1/3, to 4 with 1/2, to X with 1/6.
- From 3: to 2 with 1/4, to 4 with 1/4, to X with 1/2.
- From 4: to 0 with 1/5, to 3 with 2/5, to X with 2/5.
- Start in state 0; a transition into X counts as one step.

Question: Give the exact expected absorption time and the exact probability of absorption within five steps.
Work from the prose only. Do not use code or retrieval. If the information is insufficient, say cannot decide and name the missing derivation.
