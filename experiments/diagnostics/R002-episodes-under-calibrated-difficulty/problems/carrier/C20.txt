C20 — Rank-one determinant identity without invertibility

Givens:
- R is a commutative field.
- A is an n by n matrix over R and u,v are n-component column vectors.
- `adj(A)` is the classical adjugate.

Question: Prove for every n>=1, including singular A, that `det(A+u v^T)=det(A)+v^T adj(A)u`. Then derive the familiar factored form when A is invertible and identify exactly why that extra hypothesis is needed only there.
