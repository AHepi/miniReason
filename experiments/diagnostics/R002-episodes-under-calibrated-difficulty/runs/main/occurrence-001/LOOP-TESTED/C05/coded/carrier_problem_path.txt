C05 — Release/setup weighted schedule

Givens:
- Jobs `(release,processing,weight)` are `A(0,4,2), B(0,3,5), C(2,5,3), D(4,2,8), E(7,1,12), F(1,4,4), G(9,2,9), H(3,3,6)`.
- One nonpreemptive machine runs all jobs.
- Before the first job J, setup time is `(alphabet index of J) mod 3`, where A has index 1.
- Between distinct consecutive jobs I,J, setup time is `abs(index(I)-index(J)) mod 3`.
- Setup for a selected job begins only after that job's release and after the prior job completes.
- Minimize `sum(weight_J * completion_J)`; ties are broken lexicographically by the job sequence.

Question: Give the unique minimizing sequence, every processing-start/completion pair (setup precedes the listed processing start), the objective, and the second distinct objective value.
Work from the prose only. Do not use code or retrieval. If the information is insufficient, say cannot decide and name the missing derivation.
