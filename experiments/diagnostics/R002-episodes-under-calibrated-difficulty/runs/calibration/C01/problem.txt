C01 — Selected-evidence Bayesian chain

Givens:
- Source A is chosen with probability 2/7 and contains 2 red and 4 blue cards.
- Source B is chosen with probability 3/7 and contains 4 red and 3 blue cards.
- Source C is chosen with probability 2/7 and contains 5 red and 3 blue cards.
- Cards are drawn uniformly without replacement from the chosen source.
- The first three revealed colours, in order, are blue, red, blue; all three cards are removed.

Question: Give (i) the posterior probability that Source C was chosen and (ii) the conditional probability that a fourth draw is red. Give reduced fractions and show the likelihood normalization and the final mixture.
Work from the prose only. Do not use code or retrieval. If the information is insufficient, say cannot decide and name the missing derivation.
