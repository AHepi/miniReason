C19 — Telescoping rational identity

Givens:
- n is a nonnegative integer.
- An empty sum is zero.

Question: Prove for every such n that `sum(k=1..n) 1/[k(k+1)(k+2)] = n(n+3)/[4(n+1)(n+2)]`. State why every division is valid and include n=0.
