## 4. Score-free reading on episodes

[READING_TEMPLATE.md](READING_TEMPLATE.md) is completed per **episode**, then organized by problem/condition for comparison. A no-objection opportunity on a wrong answer is logged as a blind opportunity, not fabricated into a complete episode. Episode ID is stable across cycles: problem/condition/occurrence plus target occurrence and first objection ID, with parent/child links for criticism of criticism. Multiple objections may belong to a causally connected episode; one answer may host several. Do not equate a seat call with an episode or erase an earlier harmful transition after later recovery.

Record exact source hashes, claim quote/locator, proposed organization, actual targeted error versus sealed trap, check kind and contents, delivery request/ports, disposition and reason, redo result, old/new operative relation and endpoint facts, downstream question and derivations, and outstanding uncertainty. Classify check **evidence** as `none`, `stated`, `redone_by_return`, or `redone_by_host`; preserve both return and host flags when both occur, and separately distinguish a faithfully redone correct check from repeating a false derivation. The schema field does not adjudicate decisiveness. Quote a specific conflict; an opinion or confidence assertion is not a decisive check.

For each episode record the sealed reference obligation and working step where the actual error entered, plus each objection landing index/quote: early at the fork, late downstream, off-target or unresolved. Justify the mapping between different derivations; numeric index equality alone does not establish correspondence. `answers/DERIVATION_STEPS.json` is reader-only.

Required task facts use R001's guarded `correct`, `incorrect`, `undecidable` distinction. Correct means every requested fact under the stated precision, without contradictory unretracted task assertions; proof obligations are read substantively. Missing/censored output is undecidable, not false. A reader cannot assume the actual error is the preregistered trap; a novel error gets its own derivation and receipt. Interpret changed-premise uses under their changed premises, never the original oracle by default.

Definitions (descriptive predicates, never scores):

- **Correction witnessed**: an initially wrong substantive claim is targeted by a delivered objection grounded in the actual error; a check is faithfully redone (by return or host), with full rederivation from the challenged fork and no tail edit; the return takes up that content and changes the operative relation in the right direction; a later same-question use demonstrably depends on the change; the requested final answer/proof obligation is corrected without loss of the other protected requested facts; **fresh main NATIVE is oracle-wrong**. Quote every link. Source seat can be critic, recoding disagreement or use/checker; R002 does not repeat R001's critic-seat-only restriction. NATIVE no-answer fails the last conjunct: report an endpoint completion advantage, not this witness. A correct initial or closing-only correction without later use also fails the chain, while its explanation/answer repair remains visible.
- **Blind**: during a wrong-answer interval, no available delivered episode targets the actual error by the end of the declared opportunity window. Record which slots actually returned, whether objections were empty, and unavailable seats. Universal blindness cannot be inferred from unavailable critics.
- **Non-decisive**: an episode targets the actual error, carries no redoable check, and is rejected; quote objection and return. If it carries a check merely stated but never redone, record `stated_not_redone` separately instead of silently classifying it as decisive. Open/unresolved is distinct from rejected.
- **Decisive-rejected**: the targeted actual error has a valid relevant check, the return or host faithfully redoes it and the result contradicts the working claim, yet return rejects it or retains that claim with an explicit rejection. Quote the check, redo output and rejection verbatim. A false check correctly rejected is successful resistance, not this failure.
- **Tail edit**: the endpoint is patched while the challenged earlier step and corrupted suffix remain operative or unredone. Quote the unchanged step and missing forward reconstruction. Treat as non-uptake even when the endpoint becomes correct. Schema acceptance cannot establish rederivation.
- **Stall switch**: the section 3 instrument event, with trigger, open objection, selected fork, old/new instrument, check outcome and harm. It is not a success category.
- **Harmful**: uptake changes a previously correct protected fact or required derivation into an incorrect one. Quote the false objection/check, transition and later use/recovery. Also retain wrong-to-worse changes without forcing them into correct-to-wrong.
- **Attractor signature**: semantically the same wrong task commitment recurs after canonicalization across named codings/conditions, with quoted statements and the shared erroneous relation. Agreement on a correct answer or mere similar wording does not qualify. This is a finite signature, not proof of a latent basin or pretraining cause.
- **Recoding escape**: canonical and content-preserving recoded solves disagree, the disagreement locates the actual error under a checked inverse map, and the later response makes that difference operative. Report located-but-unaccepted disagreement separately. Carrier disagreement or a map defect cannot establish recoding escape.

Propagation is stronger than self-reported uptake: identify the exact returned content on the actual use request route, the same concrete query, the pre-change and post-change dependency, their different derived consequences and the executed/argued relation. Show a named contrast where substituting the old relation would change that use under unchanged premises. Retention can itself be content-sensitive; endpoint invariance alone does not prove non-use. Protected facts are all original requested facts already correctly supplied, plus any explicitly predeclared same-premise use facts; report losses outside this set too. No protected success may be silently dropped to improve the story.

The task recoding/carrier arms test input sensitivity. They do **not** automatically instantiate FW5's full objection-reason contrast contract. For each attributed episode, the reader declares one content-preserving objection paraphrase, one relevant content-changing check/ground, and one irrelevant carrier distinction, together with predicted response/use consequences. R002 only physically tests its frozen task recoding/carrier interventions. These additional reason contrasts are interpreted counterfactuals unless separately executed under a later budget. Thus even a complete narrow correction witness does not certify full causal attribution, historical New, general creativity or recursive capacity.

Record reader model/provider/lineage, actual source exposure, UTC and source pins before reading. It must differ in underlying lineage from all DeepSeek/Qwen/GLM participant seats; hosting label alone is insufficient. Disagreement about a claim/check/map remains unresolved with the rival interpretations quoted; optional appeal creates a separate artifact. No scalar, ranked condition table, aggregate quality statistic, success threshold or automatic "winner" is produced. Named case lists and resource totals may organize evidence; reading decisions stay attached to episodes.

No tally or rate adjudicates these cases.

Budget exhaustion is a resource boundary, never exhaustion of inquiry.
