For a source with `r` red and `b` blue cards, the ordered evidence has likelihood
`(b/(r+b))(r/(r+b-1))((b-1)/(r+b-2))`. Multiplying by the priors gives A `2/35`, B `12/245`, and C `5/196`. Their sum is `129/980`, so C has posterior `(5/196)/(129/980)=25/129`.

After the evidence, a source has `r-1` red cards among `r+b-3` cards. Mixing `1/3`, `3/4`, and `4/5` against the normalized A/B/C weights gives `224/387`.

Trap: multiplying marginal colour probabilities as if the three draws were independent, or conditioning only on the existence of a colour, changes both posterior and fourth-draw mixture. The executable oracle evaluates exact ordered likelihoods and executes the with-replacement faulty route.
