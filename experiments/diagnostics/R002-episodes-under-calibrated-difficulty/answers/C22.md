# C22 sealed answer

```json
{"problem_id":"C22","P(x)":"x^4"}
```

The binomial expansion gives `(x+1)^4-x^4=4x^3+6x^2+4x+1`, and P(0)=0. If Q is another solution, R=P-Q satisfies R(x+1)=R(x). A nonconstant polynomial cannot be periodic: its finite difference has degree one less with nonzero leading coefficient. Thus R is constant, and R(0)=0 makes it zero.

Trap: integrating the cubic termwise solves a differential equation, not the finite-difference equation. The script checks coefficient identities and the kernel certificate in bounded degree; the human degree argument supplies universality.
