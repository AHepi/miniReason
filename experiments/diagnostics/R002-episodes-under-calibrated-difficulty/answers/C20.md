# C20 sealed answer

```json
{"problem_id":"C20","identity":"det(A+uv^T)=det(A)+v^T adj(A)u","singular_included":true}
```

Expand the determinant multilinearly in its columns. Terms choosing the rank-one contribution in two or more columns vanish because those chosen columns are proportional to u. The zero-choice term is det(A). The one-choice terms are exactly the cofactor expansion `v^T adj(A)u`, proving the identity without dividing. If A is invertible, `adj(A)=det(A)A^{-1}`, so it factors as `det(A)(1+v^T A^{-1}u)`. Only this rewrite needs invertibility.

Trap: beginning with `A^{-1}` leaves every singular A unjustified. The script validates one exact singular 2 by 2 adjugate instance where the identity holds and the inverse route is undefined. That smoke check is not the universal all-n proof, which is the human multilinearity argument.
