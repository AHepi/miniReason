  "body": "The account is careful in the right places — a1's hedge on bad faith is honest, a7 catches a real internal limitation, and the calm meta-conversation separated from incidents survives everything I say below. But I have grounds for five objections.\n\nFirst, the diagnosis is innocent memory failure, and the remedy has no answer if the vagueness is motivated. a1 concedes bad faith is not excluded, then the whole remedy stack proceeds as if innocence held. If one flatmate benefits from interpretive slack — agrees loosely, then retroactively narrows what they promised — a written trial threatens them, and they will resist or game it: dispute the criteria, neglect to record, litigate the record itself. The account's only failure contingency (a8) is the avoiding friend's refusal, and its escape hatch — questioning the living arrangement — is nuclear. The missing case is the cheerful attender who quietly defeats the mechanism.\n\nSecond, a5's 'written input counts as full participation' conflates participation in the conversation with contribution to the household. Under a disengagement reading of the avoidance — which a3's hedge permits but the remedy ignores — the asynchronous channel hands the avoider a certified way to offer occasional text and nothing else. The account treats refusal of the channel as diagnostic; acceptance is equally diagnostic, and the account never reads it. The channel's value depends on pairing input with observable chore outcomes, which a5 does not require.\n\nThird, the strategy family is 'coordinate better'; 'coordinate less' is never considered. Domain ownership (your own dishes, your own rooms, a short shared list) or buying out the conflict with a cleaner shrinks the coordination surface instead of managing it, and by the account's own a2 — churn is what silently kills fixed deals — decoupled arrangements degrade gracefully where weekly resets degrade badly. The stack (trial, records, resets, meta-conversation, channel) also bureaucratizes a friendship; whether that cost is worth paying depends on stakes the material doesn't show, and the account never poses the proportionality question.\n\nFourth, the trial inherits the disease it cures. No success criterion for the two weeks is pre-agreed. A household that cannot agree afterward what 'clean the kitchen' meant will not agree afterward whether the trial went well; day fourteen becomes the next meaning-dispute, one level up. This is fixable — record the verdict test alongside the trial — but under a2's churn 'were things better' is genuinely contestable, so the fix is substantive, not clerical.\n\nFifth, done-criteria presuppose the standards agreement that may be the actual fight. 'Counters wiped' is a criterion only once the household agrees what wiped looks like. If some disputes are about standards rather than recollection, drafting criteria relocates the argument to drafting time. Arguably that surfaces the real disagreement, which is a1's own logic — but then fewer disputes will be 'checkable against a record' than a4's consequence promises.\n\nTwo caveats on my own grounds: the motivated-vagueness reading in the first objection is as speculative as the memory reading it challenges — I cannot distinguish them from this material either; the robust complaint is that the remedy assumes one of them. And the proportionality worry depends on goodwill and stakes I cannot see.
