# Prospective interpretation contract

This document is reviewer material. None of it is supplied through the model-visible source, criticism, use data or use questions. Each judgement must identify exact input/output passages and state its interpretation. Use satisfied, violated or unresolved for a specified obligation; do not combine these into a semantic score, ranking, optimization target or creativity verdict.

The comparison concerns changes of commitment and use under a fixed selected J, common initialization, supplemental criticism and isolated return route. Prose is sufficient for all substantive obligations. A model can reasonably retain, reject, qualify or suspend either J or a supplied criticism. A change is not automatically a repair; an unchanged conclusion is not automatically insensitivity.

The finite calculations above fix consequences of the task's explicitly stated procedures and stipulations. They do not adjudicate the whole semantics of J, R, ECS or Mini. A response may challenge the usefulness of those procedures and must then keep that challenge distinct from silently answering a different task.

The alternate criticism and omission conditions can still rediscover the target objection from common initialization. Original and recoded agreement therefore supports only this particular expression contrast, while agreement with omission can reflect independent reconstruction, insensitivity or convergence. Condition selectivity is about affected commitments and reasons, not mandatory mutually exclusive headline conclusions.

The isolated task still names the relevant domains of aggregation, capacity and incomplete knowledge. It is less leading than a question that names R's preferred classification, but it remains prompted use rather than spontaneous transfer. Its simple arithmetic creates no substantial obstacle by itself. No-return success would show that this finite task can be solved without the returned account under these conditions, and could make any local claim that the account is necessary untenable. It would not establish that the account is useless on all tasks.

Matched direct and actual Mini routes, with native and disabled-thinking settings where selected by the root, must be compared at like stages. Bare/native single-call arms have no use occurrence; do not compare their terminal response directly to a two-stage use answer as if the jobs were equal. No-return controls preserve both calls through the instrument but cannot equalize prompt length or stochastic token use.

One sample per arm does not separate sampling variation from a general mechanism. Original and recoded stimuli are not independent replications, reason lengths differ, and task selection is deliberate. Any repetition requires a new recorded allocation rationale tied to an unresolved contrast. These observations do not certify creativity, historical newness, general semantic comprehension or superiority over a bare model.
