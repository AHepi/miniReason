# Open-ended languages for inquiry

## Purpose and governing question

This program investigates whether AI participants can construct, communicate with, criticise and revise languages that preserve distinctions in human prose while supporting ideas and problems not anticipated by the initial configuration. The object is not to maximise compression, solve a fixed benchmark, fit a predefined ontology, or produce a formal certificate before a contribution becomes admissible. Compression is a possible achievement to investigate, not the rule that decides what may be thought or retained.

The governing question is: can a language become a resource for finding and expressing previously unavailable distinctions, including distinctions that change what the inquiry is about, without its initial grammar or the experimental machinery becoming a veto on those distinctions?

The current user's requirements govern this program. Earlier miniReason experiments supply observations and possible criticisms, not an obligation to retain their task domains, optimisation assumptions, schedules or operational admission conditions. This program is a research proposal, not a report of completed language experiments or a proof of expressive universality.

## What must remain open

An opening may be a perplexity, a disagreement, a story, an apparent inconsistency, an unfamiliar experience, or an incomplete attempt to say something. These examples are not a taxonomy of admissible problems. A participant need not supply a target function, finite search space, correct answer, executable test, agreed meaning, decidable predicate or complete problem statement.

The current question, the proposed language, interpretations of its expressions, relevant evidence, criticisms and standards of criticism may all become objects of inquiry. Changing the question is not automatically progress, but it is not automatically evasion either. A criticism can itself be mistaken. A contribution can be significant without settling the issue. Prose remains available when a proposed notation is inadequate or not yet understood.

The configuration supplies communication opportunities and a record, not a complete theory of legitimate thought. Slot names invite contributions; they are not semantic types. Human-authored instructions still influence the process and must be attributed as interventions. Calling a prompt permissive does not make it causally neutral.

## Distinctions needed to interpret results

Expressibility concerns whether a representation can carry a distinction under a stated interpretation. Reachability concerns whether this participant, with this context and these resources, actually finds such an expression. Communication concerns whether another participant can recover or use it. Origin concerns how a new resource came about. These are different claims. Failure to find an expression in a finite run does not establish that the language cannot express it; writing a new symbol does not establish that another participant understands it.

Productive recombination of already supplied meanings is also different from conceptual expansion. A system can generate unfamiliar combinations while retaining exactly the experimenter's ontology. Conversely, an apparently small reinterpretation can change what counts as an entity, a cause or a relevant difference. Reports should describe the actual change rather than infer conceptual novelty from unusual spelling or a compositionality score.

The previous conversation's suggestion of a DEFINE operator is useful but insufficient by itself. If a new symbol only abbreviates an expression whose interpretation was already fixed, substitution removes the symbol without adding semantic resources. Conceptual expansion may instead involve new evidence, a new explanatory role, a changed interpretation, a newly distinguished relation, or a practice of use that was not already fixed. These possibilities are questions for investigation, not a mandatory semantic kernel.

A further distinction is between the constructed notation and the whole system interpreting it. An English escape passage can preserve a meaning that the compact notation cannot yet carry. That is a legitimate mixed-language contribution. It is not evidence that the compact notation alone expressed the meaning. Likewise, shared model knowledge can help a reader fill a gap without proving that the transmitted message contained the missing distinction.

## Relation to existing work

Telegraph English rewrites prose using structured fact lines and approximately forty relational or logical symbols. Its authors report strong key-fact question-answering retention at reduced token counts. This provides a useful symbolic re-expression comparison, not a demonstration that every meaning or future conceptual distinction is preserved. The present program does not adopt its inventory as the permissible ontology. See Arbuzov and colleagues, 2026, [Telegraph English](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.04426), arXiv:2605.04426.

GlossoGen reports compositional and morphologically productive languages arising in a multi-agent scenario, with efficiency pressure among the identified conditions. That is relevant evidence for language emergence and transmission. The present program deliberately does not inherit an efficiency reward: whether a compact language develops without sacrificing open inquiry is itself at issue. The reported scenario does not establish unrestricted future conceptual expressivity. See Stengel-Eskin and colleagues, 2026, [GlossoGen](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.01491), arXiv:2609.01491.

Chaabouni and colleagues distinguish generalisation to novel combinations from measured compositionality, and report that the latter can help transmission to new learners without being necessary for generalisation in their experiments. This motivates testing new readers separately from new combinations. See [Compositionality and Generalization In Emergent Languages](https://aclanthology.org/2020.acl-main.407/), ACL 2020, DOI:10.18653/v1/2020.acl-main.407.

Andreas, Dragan and Klein's [Translating Neuralese](https://arxiv.org/abs/1704.06960), 2017, arXiv:1704.06960, investigates translation through similarities in listener interpretations in task environments. This is useful for designing interpretation probes. A task-relative interpretation criterion should not be silently promoted to a definition of all possible meaning.

These are limited primary-source anchors, not a claim of exhaustive literature coverage or historical novelty for the present proposal.

## What the repository's earlier experiments contribute

The [E004 packet review](../../docs/reviews/E004-language-packet-review.md) identifies source-conditioned mappings embedded inside proposed language declarations. A later response could therefore retrieve a supplied solution organisation rather than independently construct one. New comparisons must distinguish shared conventions from source-specific hints and answer-bearing exemplars.

The [H004 terminal report](../../experiments/diagnostics/H004-partial-language-continuation/REPORT.md) records useful corrections alongside false criticisms and provenance errors. It also rejects a participant's attempt to dismiss a prose creativity claim because the supplied formal vocabulary lacked a corresponding property. That is directly relevant to the current design: absence from a proposed language is a possible limitation of that language, not a host-level prohibition on discussing the subject.

The same report explicitly does not establish a superior language, reliable autonomous repair or an advantage from additional cycles. Those outcomes remain open here. This program neither edits the old observations nor adopts their numerical tasks as the boundaries of the new inquiry. The sources were inspected at commit 712be48267e973cfa001848c71a2ab3428becf53.

## Two connected research modes

The discovery mode uses open-turn, critical-return or language-workshop templates. Its participants can change the problem, vocabulary, explanatory account or interpretation. There is no reward signal, target compression ratio, mandatory improvement or automatic semantic winner. A contribution is retained because it was produced through the authorised communication route, not because a judge approved its meaning.

The comparison mode takes an explicitly identified snapshot of an emerging practice and asks a narrower question about that snapshot. Holding its bytes fixed for a particular comparison identifies what was supplied; it does not forbid criticism or future revision. A participant can propose an extension during the comparison. The report then distinguishes using the supplied practice from changing it rather than discarding the proposal.

The two modes must not be collapsed. Unlimited adaptation during a claimed controlled comparison obscures attribution. Conversely, requiring the entire discovery process to obey a permanently frozen language defeats the research question. Local comparisons return criticisms and observations to discovery without becoming admission exams.

## Beginning an inquiry without supplying its answer

Begin with material whose unsettled aspects are preserved. For example: 'Two descriptions seem to concern the same thing, but every way I have of combining them loses something I cannot yet name.' No concealed target ontology or uniquely correct interpretation accompanies this opening. The example is a starter, not the defining problem of the program.

Use open_turn to see what participants do without prescribed argumentative roles. Use critical_return as a separate configuration that offers conjecture, criticism and return, while allowing any of those activities to question the procedure itself. Compare actual conversations, not merely the number of role-labelled artifacts. A scripted role sequence is an operator contribution and cannot be credited as an emergent reasoning strategy.

Introduce language_workshop when a representational issue is under investigation, or begin with it when that is the opening concern. Participants can supply examples, descriptions of usage, partial conventions or unfamiliar notation. They need not first declare a complete grammar. The package may be abandoned or expanded. A longer expression that retains a newly noticed distinction is not a failure because it is longer.

New topics and materials may arrive after the initial language proposal. Their authors should not be required to translate them into that proposal before submission. Preserve the original material. Where the materials are deliberately withheld for a comparison, record precisely from whom and for what comparison, rather than treating withholding as a universal operating rule.

## Investigating combinatorial and conceptual reach

First examine a practice on unfamiliar combinations of meanings already used in its examples. This can reveal productive recombination, but it does not settle conceptual expansion. Then examine situations that challenge a distinction the current practice assumes away, or for which it supplies no settled interpretation. A participant may discover that the apparent problem lies in the question rather than the grammar.

A useful record follows a particular difference through time: what was initially conflated or difficult to say, what expression or interpretation was proposed, what another reader understood, what criticism arose, and how subsequent use changed. These are narrative loci for inquiry, not required fields whose absence disqualifies a contribution. The record should admit disagreement about whether any new distinction was actually constructed.

Where a proposed extension matters, preserve a snapshot before it and another after it. Present matched new uses to readers supplied with each snapshot. Where possible, include a prose-only account of the same material. Examine whether the alleged new distinction actually affects interpretation, explanation or use. A better result may reflect clearer instruction, more context, a model's prior knowledge or additional examples rather than an expanded language; those alternatives need specific examination.

A new domain should not merely rename familiar entities while preserving the original relation structure. At least some challenges should be written after inspecting what the language appears to assume. Other challenges should be authored independently of it, reducing adaptation to the researchers' favourite weaknesses. Neither class is an exhaustive test of future meanings.

## Investigating preservation without deciding all meaning in advance

Use blind_roundtrip for a local communication comparison. The encoder sees the supplied material and can transmit an expression together with whatever conventions it considers necessary. The reader sees only that current transmission. The comparator sees the source, transmission and reading. It may identify a lost distinction, an introduced assertion, an unresolved ambiguity, or an inadequacy in its own comparison.

This route isolates supplied prompt content, not everything the model knows. A truly independent provider session must also be stateless with respect to the source. A delegate that maintains hidden conversation state would invalidate the blinding even when Forge's ports are correct. Distinct models can be studied later, but model identity itself changes the experiment and should be reported.

Literal roundtrip reconstruction is neither necessary nor sufficient for every claim of semantic preservation. A paraphrase can differ in words while preserving the relevant distinction. A reconstruction can resemble the source while silently changing scope, uncertainty or who is responsible for a claim. These examples are useful search directions, not a complete representation schema or a mandatory list of semantic dimensions.

Ask concrete questions when an actual ambiguity arises. For example, if a reading turns an undecided possibility into a definite assertion, preserve the two passages and examine the difference. Do not replace that examination with a similarity threshold. A critic's accusation is itself a conjecture, not an automatic refusal or an accepted diagnosis.

## Conditional results that constrain interpretation

The following arguments describe limits on claims. They do not define which contributions the system admits.

**Abbreviation alone does not establish semantic expansion.** Assume a new symbol has a fixed definition in the old language and every occurrence can be replaced by that definition without changing interpretation. Every new expression using the symbol then has an old-language counterpart. Under these assumptions the extension can improve convenience and compression, but its introduction alone is not evidence of a new expressible distinction. The assumptions can fail when interpretation or grounding changes; that is why those changes must be investigated rather than excluded.

**A meaning-relevant collision limits a fixed decoder.** Suppose two source cases require different interpretations under a particular defensible distinction, but an encoder sends identical messages and gives the decoder identical additional information. A deterministic decoder must return the same result in both cases. It therefore cannot reproduce both different interpretations correctly. A stochastic decoder has the same conditional output distribution and cannot guarantee the required distinction in both cases. The defensibility and scope of the distinction remain open to criticism. This is a local counterexample method, not a claim to possess the complete space of meanings.

**A finite positive record does not distinguish universal coverage from certain untested failures.** Given a finite set of observed uses, a rival account can agree on those uses while failing elsewhere. The record alone cannot rule out that rival. A broader universality argument would need independently defended assumptions about expressive construction, interpretation and extension; passing more cases does not supply those assumptions automatically. Negative counterexamples can still be highly informative.

**Transport inclusion is weaker than understanding.** An injective encoding of nonblank textual replies into a JSON body, with exact source retention, prevents particular host formatting rules from deleting those replies. It does not prove that a model understands them, that the text expresses its intended meaning, or that later reasoning makes appropriate use of it. The supplied implementation establishes a route to examine those questions, not their answers. Blank replies are preserved separately and labelled as non-contributions rather than credited as reasoning.

## Compression as an observation, not an optimiser

When a language happens to be compact, report the representation's size together with the cost of supplying its grammar, conventions, examples, references and decoder context. Count tokenisation using the actual target tokenizer when available. Character counts, byte counts and provider token counts answer different questions and should not be interchanged. Include incompressible or expanded cases rather than selecting only favourable passages.

For a declared corpus and decoder supply, one possible descriptive accounting is:

\[
R = \frac{\sum_i |\operatorname{encoding}(s_i)| + |\operatorname{shared\ conventions}| + |\operatorname{required\ examples}|}{\sum_i |s_i|}.
\]

The size notation denotes the explicitly reported accounting unit. Reference retrieval and any additional decoder context must also be charged when supplied. A value below one would describe compactness under that accounting, not semantic adequacy or creativity. No threshold on R decides whether a contribution is retained, a problem is permitted, or a language is continued. Costs already embedded in a pretrained model remain a separate dependence, not something this formula makes disappear.

A representation whose compactness depends on retaining the whole source for the decoder is not source-independent compression. It may still be useful as an index. A representation that becomes longer to preserve an unforeseen distinction may be a useful expressive extension. The discovery process must remain able to pursue it.

## Comparisons and causal attribution

Compare open_turn with the role-structured alternatives using the same supplied materials and reported provider settings. For a claim about structured interaction, add a matched multi-call prose conversation rather than comparing several calls only against a single answer. Where native reasoning is examined, report the actual model and supported setting; do not assume that matching a public output cap matches hidden computation.

For a claim that a particular criticism caused a later change, fork from the same earlier snapshot. One branch receives that criticism in its active context; another does not. Keep the rest of the declared supply matched as far as practicable. Preserve both branches and examine their actual later use, including cases where the criticism is wrong. Such withholding belongs to this explicit local comparison, not to the general discovery template.

The delivered four templates do not automatically implement every such intervention or conduct a study. They provide the open discovery routes and a bounded source-isolation route. More specialised forks require separately identified manifests or a future comparison driver. That driver must not turn a local comparison outcome into an optimiser for all subsequent inquiry.

Use repeated independent occurrences when investigating reproducibility. Record authoring, model, decoding, material and routing changes rather than treating every difference as a language effect. Quantitative summaries may describe a defined comparison, but individual disagreements and counterexamples remain available. Do not combine everything into a universal creativity score or rank languages by a concealed weighted objective.

## Resource boundaries, memory and continuation

A finite process needs an execution envelope. The delivered generator requires the operator to choose a cycle count and optionally call and completion-token limits. These bound this occurrence; they do not classify its question as well-defined, solved, failed or exhausted. There is no automatic semantic stop condition or content-derived attention ranking in the templates.

By default, the discovery roles receive all previous participant artifacts, while exact source copies are presented without repeated historical duplicates. This avoids silently replacing the source with a summary. It does not solve context growth. When full context exceeds a provider's capacity, record a resource interruption, preserve the record and select an explicit successor arrangement. Do not silently discard supposedly irrelevant details and then claim unrestricted access.

A successor may use a participant-written memory description, an index or a proposed compressed language as an additional representation, but retain the originals as the reference record. A retrieval route, when implemented, should make specific unavailable material recoverable without requiring it to fit the current ontology. The supplied code does not yet implement autonomous retrieval, cross-process resume or dynamic host-topology replacement.

Writing a new grammar or problem in prose does not require granting the model executable control of the host. Changing permissions, code, provider allocation or file access is a separate operator action. This separation protects open semantic content without allowing that content to rewrite the machinery that handles it.

## Reporting and concrete deliverables

Each investigated occurrence should preserve its input bytes, actual configuration, public participant contributions, supplied conventions, context access and resource outcome. Interpretation reports should trace particular passages and uses, including mistaken criticisms, unresolved disagreements and discarded hypotheses. They should distinguish model-authored ideas from operator-authored tasks, examples, repairs and interventions. Missing responses or usage remain unknown rather than reconstructed as completed work.

The first substantive deliverable is a set of inspectable language practices and inquiry histories, not a selected winning language. Another is a collection of locally argued preservation failures or conceptual extensions, accompanied by the comparisons that bear on them. A further deliverable is criticism of this research program itself: situations in which its distinction between discovery and comparison, or its chosen observation practice, hides something important.

The implementation deliverable is deliberately narrower. Four generated Forge templates, a source-copy machine seat, a public-text transport wrapper and offline tests remove identified host-level obstacles to investigating open subjects. The audit and usage guide state the remaining limits. No paid language experiment is launched by generating configurations, and scripted routing tests are never reported as evidence that an AI has achieved reasoning, creativity or universality.

The program is successful as an inquiry only insofar as it exposes and engages with substantive problems in constructing and using expressive resources. Even that formulation is open to criticism. It is not a machine-checkable admission rule, and it must not become one merely because it is written here.
