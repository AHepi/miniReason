More loops produced no repaired final artifact. ## What was model-controlled and what was host-controlled

The model proposed routes and child packets and made all eight saved continuation decisions. The host replaced **all four first-pass route proposals**, enforced admission, executed workers, assembled recorded output and ran the fixed checker or configured critic. Thus a full route/spawn/assemble/verify sequence is not evidence that Flash independently chose or implemented every stage. Actual maximum fan-out was1, with depth1 outer tasks; UC4 reached a decomposition planner but no nested leaf. Multi-agent synthesis and successful nested recursion are not demonstrated.

UC1's initial route proposal was substantively apt: direct_answer **"would under-serve the multi-part build-and-verify structure"**. The host nonetheless chose it. Later, Flash chose engineer_patch even though the sealed task had none of its required documents/allowed files/test commands/behavior contract. The scope guard disallowed manufacturing those inputs. The result shows an interaction between model choice and instrument design, not pure model inability. See [UC1 reading](readings/pilot-uc/UC1.md) and original route event `runs/pilot/UC1/events/a0003.json`.

## Artefacts the owner can use

**Blender:** the240-frame shot specification fixes staging, lenses, targets, motion and timing. A video operator can use it as a manual blocking brief. The script passes a scalar to rotation_euler through prohibited setattr, aims the camera at its shot positions in two shots, never keys camera rotation and does not establish shot holds or drone exclusion. The structural verifier explicitly says **"Standalone AST/API verification and fallible review remain required before conformance."** Those checks were not performed by the run. Judge43 later ran the pinned standalone checker offline on the saved script/spec: it refused `setattr` as a banned name and unapproved direct call; this does not establish Blender runtime behavior. This is plain bpy under a local Blender 5.2 pin, not the requested unspecified latest plugin. [Script/spec and reading](readings/pilot-uc/UC1.md).

**Story:** HTV-5 identifies **"a causal and motivational link from failure and disappointment to the final reframing"**, grounded in **"hiding her disappointment and saying"**. The analysis separates explanatory roles from fruit/animal/wording substitutions and gives competing accounts, breaking variations and refuters. HTV-3 overstates physical separation/cessation as necessary; some prose labelled exact evidence changes punctuation. The owner has a useful analysis to criticize, not merely a plot summary. [UC2 reading](readings/pilot-uc/UC2.md).

**Intent:** all eight cases include exact input, hypotheses, supporting phrases, a discriminating check and boundaries on action. The refund/approval cases correctly separate requested mechanism from underlying goal. But **"The user is the source of the problem"** is not supported by **"from customers"**, the synthetic-chat option is a possible negotiated alternative rather than an evident meaning of "real", and the Legal case misses the concrete consequence of front-loading approvals before Thursday if the constraint is real. Numeric confidence is uncalibrated. [UC3 reading](readings/pilot-uc/UC3.md).

**FW5:** four mappings target quoted L601, L123/L140, L661/K3 and L773/L634, name record anchors, reject naive label equivalences and preserve defended narrower claims. That is useful initial adversarial mapping, but most attacks are absence-of-field arguments; the artifact itself says **"Source-clause challenge: none."** It does not establish necessity/sufficiency counterexamples. An author claiming this harness instantiates FW5 would owe the requested application mappings and contrasts; the constitutive conjecture has not been refuted or confirmed. The source is a pinned excerpt packet plus a scripted arithmetic projection, not full FW5 or a live critical episode. [UC4 reading](readings/pilot-uc/UC4.md).

## One proposed change and stopping reason

Offer **host-validated executable repair actions**, selected by ID, in place of free-form spawn/plan reconstruction. P-A5 currently offers a reference menu; that does not ensure a complete action has satisfiable template prerequisites or the right source namespace. This one interface change should expose a valid bounded repair option while keeping immutable evidence behind references. It would address the observed gap between recognizing a defect and dispatching work that can repair it. This report proposes the change; it does not implement it or launch a successor.
