This is an over-strong necessity, not a new event in the supplied story.

The accepted structured quote list and four checker anchor substrings do not certify every quotation in the answer. This is a quotation-precision defect, not a newly invented event.
- Several REFUTERs describe a different story that removes a stipulated premise, rather than challenge the necessity across preserving variations. That is a legitimate conditional failure case, but weaker than showing which variation preserves the same explanatory job while breaking the claimed requirement.
- Candidate A receives detailed variations; B-E receive briefer rejection arguments, not individually developed preserving/breaking pairs as requested. The narrator's initial "ripe" versus the final "not ripe" is present, but the critical distinction between self-protective devaluation and a reliable new observation could be made more explicit.
